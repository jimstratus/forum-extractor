Posted March 31, 2018

This scenario starts out with a distress call being received by the sector fleet (line) that is protecting the Mon Calamari sector in the Outer Rim Oversector. They are warned ny their superiors that it could be a trap and to wait until reinforcements arrive, so as not to leave the Mon Cal shipyards and system undefended but that order is ignored and they are lured into the trap. The question is, how did the enemy sneak into the system in the first place, (and from the method they used, how did they use their route undeterred as well). Who are they? What is their end game? What do they want?

Unlike other introductions I am not going to post a long introduction that practically lays out the entire scenario. Otherwise it would spoil the surprises in store for those of you who have not gotten into the EOTIR Cloud Drive scenarios folder and read the synopsis document already (cheaters). Normally we ask everyone who is interested in participating in the scenario to "sign up" by replying in this post. I suppose I will ask you to do this, however I have already designed this scenario with all the current active/semi-active members in mind. I would ask for sure if you do not plan on participating, to post here. If that is the case, you may have to be NPC'd, and we guarantee you will not die if you are NPC'd (or other negative actions).

This topic will also be used to discuss the scenario, questions, comments, etc,,, before, during, after, or as we go along. I will be providing special information to individuals via PM that concerns them and their future post, such as vital intelligence or other details and facts they will need to know that you will find out when they post. This is a SEMI turn based scenario, not exact. but we will try to stick to the program somewhat. If there are actions that require chance as to whether or not something should happen to your character, object, or mission, or its success, we have dice rolls available for each post, should they be needed as a plugin

This scenario has a lot more to it than i have let on so far, I have only given the beginning, but it encompasses other things as well, to allow other characters to join in. I'll give you one more spoiler, we even have a kidnapping, requiring a SAR mission.
