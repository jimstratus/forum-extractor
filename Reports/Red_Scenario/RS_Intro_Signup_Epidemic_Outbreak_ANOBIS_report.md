This scenario starts out with an outbreak of an epidemic, or virus of some kind on a planet that is somewhat distant but close enough to be a key planet in trade, etc.

Bacta (the chemical that cures and kills just about any disease and heals and not harms humans, etc.) is expensive and the producers are no longer producing it in mass quantities due to peace-time and not producing enough of it even for the time present.

The planet in question contacts the Ministry of Health (Imperial Republic) for help in getting control of the outbreak and subduing it and preventing it from spreading further.

There is a little known piracy (privateering) and occasional interuption of trade by pirates working for no known person in particular besides themselves.

The Ministry of Health sends out a team and shipment with minimal military escort to the planet in question to assist in the situation and files the appropriate reports with the appropriate entities.

The Naval Operations Division of the Ministry of Defense reports to Chief of Naval Operations Admiral Diego Cavaleiro that their escorts have failed to report in. No contact is able to be made between the ships and Naval Headquarters. Admiral Cavaleiro contacts his superior officer and files a report with Director of Intelligence Lord Admiral Eric Jackson.

Minister of Defense Ieyena Cohean recieves a report from one of her division chiefs (Admiral Cavaleiro) and instructs her secretary to get more information and establish contacts with dignitaries.

Meanwhile, the Commission for the Preservation of History in Life (on behalf of the Ministry of Health its sub-ministry) contacts the Imperial Republic Security Bureau and, using its authority as a SuperMinistry and the Chairman's position to order an investigation (as all investigations concerning this first starts with the IRSB's jurisdiction). (The IRSB will discover that there are traces of external forces involved (fringe groups not inside the IR) and contacts MoD to inform them of this and sends all their data to IRI to compare data and coordinates all future actions with them as it now falls under IRI jurisdiction and then will be a dual-investigation).
