## Post 2 by Selina Vadcasta on 2018-02-02T17:55:12Z



With a glass of punch in hand, the State Security Advisor continued to scan the ballroom. The promotion ceremonies were now complete, but it would be a long while before Selina would make it to bed. If the woman would truly let go of the reigns and let others do their jobs, she could go to bed far earlier. That wasn't Selina though.  
  
Eye contact with strategically placed people continued before she moved. Selina situated herself at the bar. It was the spot that attracted everyone at some point in the evening, other than the refreshers. There were always those that lingered at the bar, and those that situated themselves, as she made a point of doing. Past experience had taught Selina that the bar was where almost everyone let their guard down and emotions flow freely. Any predator would wait at the watering hole.  
  
The short blonde leaned back against the bar and sipped her punch. A couple of her own mental guards came down, to open her mind to the emotions of those around her. The Ballroom was secure, so there wasn't an imminent sense of danger, or fear from those assembled. That made it much easier to pick out those with mixed emotions.  
  
The young officer sitting two seats over, twirling his glass, seemingly in deep thought, was having an internal struggle. Perfect. Now to wait.  
  
Those situated between them lingered for a quarter of an hour before moving on. The rest of her glass she drained before turning to the bar, setting the glass on the bar top and side stepping to the seat next to him. Selina offered the young officer a smile before ordering a refill.  
  
"Long day?" she inquired of him. He nodded in response."Yeah," she agreed.  
  
A fresh glass was set down in front of her. Selina smiled and thanked the bartender. Sipping slowly, she waited. And waited. Not a talker, it seemed. That suited Selina just fine. She wasn't in the mood to entertain this evening.  
  
A soft thud of her glass to attract attention, and his gaze. A quick but gentle probe for mental walls or shields found nothing. Not that it would have done more than delay her. No, this was better. He'd never even know she was there if he didn't know to shield. A brief smile crossed her features before beginning mimicking his twirling of his glass with her own.  
  
Too distracted to notice her. It was too easy. Selina slipped into his mind as a shadow. In his mind she saw a number of cargo manifests. His thoughts flowed. Valuable but not essential cargo was missing. A number of them from ships en route to or from Byss. No reports to explain the shortages. Should he report it? Perhaps investigate further before reporting.  
  
Selina understood his internal struggle. He was young, and she had no doubt that he would continue to dig. She left him at the bar with his thoughts, and without finishing her drink.  

Three weeks later.  

Research hadn't taken long. Missing cargo usually led to a rodent. Or several. The Governor, an Admiral and pirates. His Majesty wouldn't like it at all. While she didn't have all of the details, she had concluded that there was no report of the pirating, and the Governor had previously been covering his tracks. His partner was not nearly as smart. Especially if a Junior officer was able to uncover the treachery so easily.  
  
Arrangements had been made to retrieve the Governor in the middle of the night, and he was currently en route to Coruscant from Byss for his own protection. And probably his demise. It was entirely up to the Supreme Ruler. The Pirates, and other conspirators on the other hand were out of her hands.  
  
Today was the day. Selina had been hovering in the Palace situation room for hours in anticipation.
