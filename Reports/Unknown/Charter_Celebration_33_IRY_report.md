It did not help her to feel safer though, since Jared was not there to truly protect her. She knew that he had much more important things to do than to look in on his... wife. She sighed in relief when she saw Jared. At least it was just him.

Jared looked up at the camera that was pointed at him and then pointed to the door, he was wearing a deep green uniform that bordered on black; on the right side of his chest was his new rank bars and cylinders along with his medals and a new stripe of leadership. He had been promoted along with given new orders and the orders were what worried him at the moment since he hadn't told Diandra yet and doubted she knew yet.

The door opened up a second later. Diandra stood before him in a pair of loose, flowing pants and a tank top that hugged the curves of her waist. He knew his next drop was going to be on Korriban, he was ready for the next drop and also planned on fully bringing hell down around the ears of the sith.

Diandra looked confused for a long moment. She knew that he was playing about the whole 'milady' thing, so he wasn't socked as he had expected to be. She hadn't had time to prepare, she hadn't slept properly. She knew that she probably looked tired and horrible. It took hours for her to get ready for events like this.
