"Will I get to visit?"

Revan turned his head to regard his only child as the Delta-7 reverted to realspace within the Naboo system. Smirking slightly, he offered the child a slight nod of his head. **"Yes, Jayden. There are simply other things we are presently responsible for elsewhere. Your sister has things in the Cluster well in hand."** Needless to say, Revan's trip to the Cluster had been fleeting. He recalled the day of Nixy's coronation, but he had deemed it better at the time that he not openly reveal his presence to anyone - be they friends or family. Even with his most recent trip, his only real purpose had been to collect Jayden. Naturally that required a...level of interaction with the new Queen Mother. **"It is time we both fully embraced our destinies and duties in this life."**

"What do you mean?"

Revan laughed softly. **"You'll see son. You'll see."** Activating his identification code, Revan casually began piloting the vessel into an orbit around Naboo. Several fighters from the RSF flew to intercept and escort them in.

"What about the Imperial Republic?"

Revan arched a curious brow at his son, somewhat thrown off by his question about the galactic government. **"What about it?"**

"Well...are you going back?"

Sighing, Revan shrugged his shoulders slightly. Did he have any intention of returning to the government? No, not really. It represented far too much of what he simply did not agree with in this life, this galaxy. Then again, there would never be the perfect government. The Imperial Republic did well keeping the citizens of the galaxy safe, but there seemed to be facets of its leadership that had lost sight of the true purpose of the Imperial Republic. It was little more than a dictatorship doing a poor jocracy. That, however, in and of itself was not what Revan took issue to. Perhaps it was simply the fact that he'd been so heavily involved in its leadership during a time of great disturbance. Admittedly, his wife had not been the best administrator of the government's policies and commitments to the galaxy. Then again...he married a Sith, so what did that say about *his* judgment abilities?. **"That...is not my decision, Jayden."**

Jayden could tell by the tone in his father's voice that it was simply best he did not pry into the matter. Within moments, the Escort Shuttle was beginning the initial stages of its re-entry interface. Before they dipped into communications blackout, Revan dispatched a message to be relayed to Selina back on Coruscant. It simply informed her that they had returned to Naboo. It was there her training would continue - should she choose it to be. Revan had elected not to return to Coruscant as he'd previously stated to her.

## Post 17 by Revan Centurion on 2009-06-08T15:51:22Z



“Is there anything I need to know for when we arrive there Revan?"

Revan mostly shrugged in response to Selina's question. **"What do your feelings tell you?"** Truthfully, there was always something more that a person could or should know. The difference came in the simple fact that nobody could know everything before it happened. Whether Revan was keeping something from Selina was irrelevant. Complacency had no place in either of their lives.

Leaning his head back against the cushion of the seat, Revan closed his eyes. **"You'll have to pardon me...I haven't really slept soundly in a long time. We should arrive within a few hours. Try to behave yourself while I'm asleep."** Revan winked jokingly before closing his eyes.

-----------

*Six hours later*

Revan slowly began to stir to the soft beeping sound of the vessel's proximity alarm. The modified Delta-7 had actually been designed to require minimal input from the pilot in a wide array of flight conditions. Eventually, the alarm ceased and the computer simply took over guiding the vessel down to a pre-determined set of landing coordinates. Stretching his arms slightly, Revan loosely grasped the control column and deactived the automatic flight control system just as the vessel slipped into the lower limits of Dxun's atmosphere. A mere five minutes later, and they had landed in a large clearing. Unfastening his restraints, Revan slowly came to his feet, instinctively fighting off a strong wave of darkness that washed over him.

It had indeed been a while...
