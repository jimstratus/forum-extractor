*Chambers of the Imperial Republic Senate*

It felt like ages since High Councilor Firecam had walked through these halls. A special session of the Senate had been called, and the High Council's presence was requested. There had been those attacks against the Throne and His Majesty's Chief of Staff, and rumor had it that these acts of war might be fully retaliated and result in a formal declaration of war by the combined Senate and High Council. He had been in retirement for some time, though still a permanent member of the High Council, he participated in the private meetings they held, though had not long since made a public appearance.

Locke looked around, marveling at the majesty and architecture that seemed to slip his mind when he had visited more frequently. Moments such as this were few and far between, so he took his time making his way to the appropriate chambers. The storm on the horizon was fierce enough that someone such as himself had been called back to observe and possibly offer advice and comment as necessary. He chuckled quietly to himself, knowing that it was a price he had to pay for his exploits as a fleet commander. Checking his datapad for the current time, he quietly entered the back of the large chambers, taking a seat in the High Council area and waited for the current session to start. He was soon joined by a longtime friend and old counter-part, Cognatus, the former Chief of Army Operations, now commanding the combined Armed Forces. It was good to see him again. Locke had a feeling that things were about to change in a big way, and that he was unsure of how this war would unfold. Dene took a seat next to him, and he noticed Dene had an anxious look on his face.
