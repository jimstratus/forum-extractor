## Post 3 by James Stratus on 2009-01-10T06:58:09Z



The High Prince arrived shortly after his Executor. He assisted her directly in overseeing the preparations for the Balle Royale. This was not normal protocol, but it was a special occasion and they had both taken a personal interest and seen to its planning with careful deliberacy. The Prince had assigned Lt. General Bodost of the IRSB to supervise security preparations and the security of the event itself. While he would be attending the ball, he would also be on duty. The Prince had made special preparations prior to the ball, and even spent some a considerable amount of time on Kuat at the Stratus Family's Palace there, not to mention all the visits to Naval Headquarters and the Kuat Drive Yards. It was unusual behavior for the High Prince. He usually left all such matters to other individuals, including his brother. He came next to the Executor, who was standing and admiring the setup in awe.

**"Well?"** he asked politely.

**"I think the preparations are just about complete, my lord. Our teams have done incredible work."** The two often used formality in the presence of others outside their personal staff and other close associates.

**"I agree, Executor Treyson. A job well done indeed. I think..."** he said with a quiet, excited voice, **"that it is finally ready. All that is left now is for me to get a date. I would bet that I am the last individual to find a date for the ball."**

**"How much are you willing to bet, Your Highness?"**

**"I'll bet ten million credits."**

**"Dang, your about to get rich pretty fast. You better hurry up and get a date."**

**"I know, Tavria,"** he said **"believe me, I know."**
