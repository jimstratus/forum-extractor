Praetor Kaidlen Shan made sure that the newly married and promoted Emperor and Empress of the Imperial Republic got off on their honeymoon. She could have gone along, as the primary security for the Imperial couple. However, Kaidlen did not feel comfortable with the idea. The security detail that she had sent would be plenty. After all, the ones she had chosen to go along were the best.

Sighing, the cat-like woman walked into her apartment within the Imperial Palace. There was a minor, almost unnoticeable tug within her. It was nothing new to her, Kaidlen had felt it for a long time. For the most part, she kept it buried, which wasn't hard. Her various duties were more than sufficient to do just that. It was rare for her to have any downtime, her duties and positions were that numerous. When she did get a respite, the tugging she felt within caused her to want to go search it out. Kaidlen wished that one day to go and follow it, to see where it would lead her. Maybe it would take her to the people she had been born to, to the planet she had been born on. She sighed again. She would not follow the tugging within her this time. She still had duties to perform, after all. And if there was one thing that could be used to describe Kaidlen, it was that she was extremely dutiful.
