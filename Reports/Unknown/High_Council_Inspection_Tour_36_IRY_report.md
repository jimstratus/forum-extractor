## Full Content



The High Prince had called a meeting of the High Council. It was scheduled to meet at a later date but he pushed the meeting up, as he had other business to attend to, many things to do to prepare for the upcoming wedding. It was coming up fast. He was the first to arrive, though he naturally did not enter the actual high council chambers themselves, but waited in a private lounge that could convert to an office instantly while he waited for the others to arrive. He would be the last to enter, so as to make a grand entrance, as was protocol. Sometimes the Chancellor and/or Executor would go around to the same door he came through and join him and wait in his private waiting area when he permitted it, as friends and collegues.

One by one the members of the High Council arrived, including the Praetorian, Director-General Applebee, and lastly, Prince Marc Stratus, who represented the Royal Family (and the Royal Supreme Court, for that matter). He was also served as the District Governor of the Capitol District. He also waited for a special guests, a senior member of the Grand Admiralty, and his Deputy Chief of Staff who he had invited to attend this meeting. Once Grand Admiral Taftican and Lady Klone had arrived he was ready to start the meeting.

Everyone was seated at the table at their appointed seats, talking to their neigbors, and he enabled the chime feature on the door as it slid open to announce his entering the room. He was followed by Executor Treyson. The Supreme Chancellor was already in the room, speaking with his fellow High Council members. Everyone grew silent and rose from their seats as the High Prince walked in. As he and the Executor sat down, everyone else followed suit.

**"Thank you everyone for coming. Let's get started shall we? Our first order of business is that I am declaring that the Sullust system from this day forward shall be a member planet and system of the Regasterra. Lord and Lady Klone are hereby appointed to Regasterra Stewardship of Sullust, with Lord Klone acting as Lord Steward and Lady Klone as Steward. Normally, it is natural after being appointed to a Stewardship to immediately leave for the system and inspect the place to which one has been assigned and make any necessary changes and improvements. While I will be sending you both to Sullust shortly after this meeting, you will not be going their directly. It has been brought to my attention that there has not been a High Council member that has gone through the Rinma Trade Route in quite some time on an inspection tour. Grand Admiral Taftican tells me that High Councilor Cohean was the last one to go through the route on official business and that was quite some time ago. Grand Admiral Taftican has kept things well in order but we still like having the High Council keeping an eye on things and having a firsthand account of what is going on in our systems and reporting back to this Council upon their return of their findings, as well as making any necessary changes to local governments or military units along the way that are needed on behalf of the High Council with the full authority of the High Council, seeing as this is an official High Council assignment, and you are their representative for this mission.**

**Therefore I, High Prince Stratus, on behalf of the High Council, do assign you, Lord Klone, to represent the High Council, and assign my Deputy Chief of Staff to represent the Office of the Supreme Ruler, in visiting the planets and systems along the Rinma Trade Route on an official High Council Inspection Tour. Lord Klone, you will be going not as Director of Intelligence, but as an Imperial Republic High Councilor. A Deputy Director from your office will be appointed as Acting Director in your absence from Coruscant. Once the tour is complete you will go to the system and planet of Sullust and do the same, where you may stay for a period of one month, and appoint a planetary governor to act as your representative leader in your absence, who will send you regular reports. At which time you will be expected to return to Coruscant. You will be allowed to travel to Sullust to fulfill your responsibilities there from time to time and check in with the governor you have appointed, whom you will maintain constant contact with regardless of your galactic location.**

**Grand Admiral Taftican, the Rinma Task Force is hereby ordered to escort Lord and Lady Klone on their inspection tour. You will be taking your orders from Lord Klone, as a member of the High Council.**

**Now, let us move on to our next item of business, I have decided that I want all members of the Throne and the majority of this Council at my wedding. And in order to follow the letter of the law in accordance with the Imperial Republic Charter, a member of the Throne must be present on Coruscant. Therefore we must appoint a Regent from among this body to remain behind, they will satisfy the requirement of being a member of the Throne. I would like to skip the order of succession and appoint Lord Admiral Firecam, who has already filled that role before. The reason being is I would like the rest of you there at the wedding, and also the Lord Admiral already has been Regent before and therefore it will be an easier transition. Not that I don't want you at my wedding also, Admiral Firecam, I just can't have it both ways. Someone would have to stay, and most of you have to be there for security reasons alone. All in favor of this, please indicate.**

**There was some discussion and eventually there was general majority agreement, though not unanimous.**

**Now, on to the next item of business, we have been having a lot of pirate attacks lately, and I want us to step up our response as well as our intelligence....."** The Supreme Ruler went on in detail and then continued with each item of business until the High Council meeting finally concluded.

## Post 8 by Lan Klone on 2020-08-14T19:04:45Z



As the *Prometheus* made its way through hyperspace, Lan poured over the dispatch he received several times, committing the information to heart. The Emperor entrusting the Cloning Project to Lan while on the tour filled him with a sense of honor and invigorated him to ensure that tour proceeded without any hitches. Between each planet they visited on the trade route, whenever Lan and Terrisa weren't socializing with the crew of the Protector-class vessel, they would review the information for the planet they were heading to next.

---

**---Abregado-rae---**

At Abregado-rae, Lord and Lady Klone and Grand Admiral Taft inspected the numerous factories on the planet that exported various gadgets and technological advances for the Imperial Republic. The three noticed that one of the factories, the second one that they inspected, wasn't hitting its production quota based on the shipping manifest for a shipment to Coruscant. Lan called on a couple members of his Intelligence protection detail to run a basic investigation into the missing merchandise, delaying their departure from the planet. During that time, Terrisa and Admiral Taft traveled the areas surrounding the factories, interacting with the locals in an attempt to look for a unique cloning template to use for the Cloning Project, while Lan interacted with the factory workers, doing much the same with his royal guard detail never too far behind. The investigation into the factory revealed an interesting discovery. The Intelligence agents discovered that the missing tech was being loaded onto a freighter that didn't appear in any flight manifests by a small group of factory workers, led by the factory's manager. The group was arrested and transported to Coruscant, where the workers were sent to the Arrth-Eno Prison Complex and the manager was sent to the Justicars' Tower for Intelligence agents to interrogate.

**---Thyferra---**

On Thyferra, reeling from the incident on Abregado-rae, the three went into the Bacta refinery facility expecting another scandal. They were pleased to find that there were not apparent shortcomings in the facility, the staff meeting its production quota consistently. The facility's supervisor gave Lan a list of potential candidates for the Cloning Project from her staff, though none seemed promising, even after consulting with Terrisa and Admiral Taft. Lan and Terrisa met with the planet's governor to get an overview of the political situation in the world, but there wasn't much to report.

**---Eriadu---**

While on Eriadu, Terrisa opted to remain on the *Prometheus* while Lan and Admiral Taft visited the trade planet. At one of the many commerce centers on the planet, the two ran into an interesting individual, a man clad in simple robes. The man turned out to be a Shadow Jedi, sent to Eriadu to compile a list of individuals who would be suitable for the Cloning Project. While the Jedi did provide Lan with a list of those he believed may be a good fit, he did direct Lan’s attention to one name in particular, stating that the person in question seemed to show a high degree of loyalty to the Imperial Republic, and seemed to be Force Sensitive, though they were more than likely unaware of it. The two returned to the battleship, deciding to discuss how to proceed with the list in private, along with Terrisa. While none of the three were adept in the ways of the Force, they knew the importance of it, and decided it was very well worth considering.

**---Sullust---**

Admiral Taft and Lan both went about inspecting the various factories and shipyards on the planet while Terrisa met with the planetary governor to get an idea of what to expect when they are on-world. The Grand Admiral and High Councilor found nothing out of the ordinary at the facilities, so Lan went to meet up with his wife while the Grand Admiral returned to her flagship, not wanting to intrude while the Klones learned about the world they had stewardship over. As Lan entered the government building, he couldn’t help but overhear the quiet utterances of anti-Imperial sentiments from inside, though they clammed up upon seeing the High Councilor.

On the shuttle ride back to the *Prometheus*, Lan sent a holo-message to IRSB, asking them to send agents to investigate the governance staff of Sullust for a plot against the Imperial Republic. After further consideration, he sent a directive to the Directorate of Counterintelligence, telling them to send agents to work with IRSB on the investigation.

**---Sluis Van---**

While in the orbit of Sluis Van, the *Prometheus* docked with the shipyard in the planet’s orbit, allowing the military vessels onboard to be repaired and undergo maintenance. Lan, Admiral Taft, and Terrisa took a shuttle, along with a military escort, to the various battle stations as part of the inspection tour. While for the most part, most of the battle stations were operating at peak conditions, one of the engineers in charge of maintaining the stations reported that a number of the stations have experienced a number of system failures over the last year. Though they were always able to fix the issue quickly, due to the frequency of the system failures in multiple stations, they suspect it might be sabotage.

While waiting for the ships to be repaired, Lan went about investigating the mysterious sabotages, Admiral Taft and Terrisa assisting where they could. Though no leads turned up by the time the ships were repaired, the *Prometheus* started its journey to Coruscant, and Lan left a couple of the IRIS security agents on the stations to continue the investigation, sending another directive to DCI to send more agents to assist.

---

Back on Coruscant after the tour, Lord and Lady Klone traveled back to the Royal Palace alongside Grand Admiral Taft, to the High Council chambers. Lan and Admiral Taft gave their report on the inspection tour before the rest of the High Council present, leaving out the details pertaining to the Cloning Project.
