## Full Content



Revan's boots echoed lightly against the marble flooring of the Imperial Palace. He had walked the corridors of secrecy and power that catered to the efforts of the Imperial Republic's Intelligence Service. However, these days he did not walk them as an outright member of the organization. His purpose within the entirety of the government was...far more vague. At least...to those that did not need to know the truth. In the end of the day, he supposed that it mattered extremely little. His name would be synonymous with many things for several years yet to come.

Arriving at the section of offices dedicated to senior level management, Revan swiped his access card and entered the eight-digit alphanumeric code. With a soft hiss, the reinforced doors slid open, sealing immediately behind him. Dressed in the mix of silver and black indicative of his family estate on Naboo, Revan quietly and swiftly made his way to the office of the new Director of Operations. At present, IRIS was without an active Director. Crystal Reins, however, remained Acting Director until such a time as a new one was appointed by the Supreme Ruler and confirmed by the Senate. It was a necessary level of beaurcracy for a Republic. Revan chose to ignore the measures that served to do little other than cripple the organization's effectiveness.

For now...he contented himself to meet this new addition to IRIS' senior staff. The High Councilor maintained an interest in the workings of IRIS even if he chose not to be directly involved in its political web of relative madness.
