**Coruscant**

**Imperial Palace**

***Private Inner Office of the Supreme Ruler***

Seated across from the Emperor James Stratus in a informal private setting, in his inner office, were Lady Diandra Devin and Lord Admiral Jared Quinn.

"So...I take it you two enjoyed your leave of absence?" he asked.

"Yes, very much so, my Lord" Lady Devin said with a knowing smile.

"To the point we would like to extend it...long term...." Lord Admiral Quinn finished the thought for her.

"I see. I was expecting as much. One can experience burnout after serving at the top for extended periods of time. It is high pressure. I imagine you two will enjoy being mostly-civilians again."

"Mostly civilians?" Lady Devin asked.

"Lord Quinn is still a permanent member of the High Council. Traditionally, government officials who hold permanent Council seats who decide to retire retain those seats unless they resign from the High Council separately, as it is an entirely separate thing. I would hate to see you do that. You are also both of the nobility. So you are hardly civilians. Your noble status will follow you, but other than that you will be able to take that long break. We will cut down on your Council portfolio and assignments as well though you would still need to attend most of the meetings, at least by holo. If a regency event is triggered, you can always yield to the next in line if it should fall to you. So if you agree to stay on in that capacity, we will do our best to accommodate your desire to step back from public service for the most part. Certainly from your military commission and ministry position."

"Alright. I'll think about the High Council post for a while longer then. For now, Your Majesty, I respectfully request that you accept my resignation from the post as Grand Minister of Defense, and approve my retirement from military service."

Lady Devin interjected "and I request that Your Majesty accept my resignation as your Chief of Staff. It has been an honor serving you, my Lord, but I wish to retire from government service."

The Emperor nodded. "Very well. It is with deep gratitude that I accept your resignations from your respective positions. Lord Admiral Quinn, your retirement from military service is also approved. I hereby grant you an honorable discharge from the Armed Forces. From now on you shall be known as Lord Jared Quinn, or Lord Admiral Jared Quinn (ret.). Thank you both for your service to me and to the Imperial Republic."

They all rose from their seats and the two bowed to the Emperor, with Lord Quinn offering a final salute to the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces.

After they had left, the Supreme Ruler began reviewing personnel files of several high command officers that were highly placed in the Ministry of Defense, and sent out several summons to each of them to come meet with him at the Palace for a 1-on-1 interview/meeting, along with a group meeting with Lord Admiral Firecam and some of the Senate and High Council Defense Oversight members. He reached out to Lord Admiral Firecam and gave him instructions regarding travel arrangements and then went back to his other work.

**Coruscant**

**Imperial Palace**

They had arrived on schedule. Dressed in their class A formal military dress uniforms, one by one they each met privately with the Emperor in his main office, with Lord Admiral Firecam, Grand Marshall Burton, and Praetor Overmind having the rare privilege of being able to observe both audio and video. The Emperor then met with Lord Admiral Firecam, Praetor Overmind, and Grand Marshall Burton over lunch. Afterward, they were all summoned to the Palace Throneroom together. Praetor OVERMIND, Lord Admiral Firecam, Grand Marshall Burton, Grand General Stanton, High Admiral Kopishke, High General Dravaskas.

They filed into the Throneroom, then came to attention, until the Emperor signaled them to stand at ease. He finally began to speak.

"There have been some recent vacancies at the within the Ministry of Defense within the High Command. As you already know I have appointed Lord Admiral Firecam as Interim Minister of Defense. This has created a vacancy in the position he normally occupies. Grand General Wexley Stanton is hereby appointed to the position of Acting Deputy Minister of Defense for Operations. Taking over as Acting Chief of Staff for the Imperial Republic Army, I am appointing High General Sebastian Dravaskas. While the current titles suggest these may be temporary, if you perform well in your duties they could very well become permanent.

Now, High Admiral Kopishke, step forward." As she stepped forward, Emperor Stratus held out a rank plaque with 12 tiles, three blue and three yellow on top, with three blue and three red on the bottom. The rank plaque of a Grand Admiral. "I now bestow upon you the rank of Grand Admiral, with all the rights and special privileges that are granted by the Imperial Republic Charter. Congratulations, Grand Admiral Kopishke."

"Now, you all have work to do. Return to your respective headquarters, and get to it. Transportation has been arranged. Dismissed."
