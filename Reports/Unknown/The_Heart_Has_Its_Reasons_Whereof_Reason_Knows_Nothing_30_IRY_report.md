(Before Dene is accused of treason)

[Aurielle's](http://www.denimblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/hayden-panettiere-diesel-matics.jpg) skin was coated in a thin layer of perspiration already before they began, and she wished she had removed the jacket. But there was no time now. Her blonde hair fanned out as she spun and thrust out with her lightsaber. It sizzled as it came into contact with Dene's blade. The pale blue and the silver blades made a wonderfully hypnotic sight as they parried with one another.

"I see," Auri grunted. Dene had just walked her through correcting her stance as they fought. He was still, after all, her teacher and mentor. Auri had switched her feet as he said, and was not made to step back as she blocked his attack. But she had to move again. Blocking more, learning to not die by the saber in combat was something she was not proficient in. Normally, she was empathic. today, she was sore. And grumpy.

Aurielle HATED combat lessons.

"Let's make a bet," she said, moving to deliver two blows to shake things up. Dene blocked them easy enough, but it didn't stop her from trying to throw him off. "If I win this one, you buy me ice cream tomorrow instead of practice. If you win, what do you want?"

## Post 5 by Aurielle Stratus on 2011-07-22T16:29:46Z



Aurielle pushed back, but some things were an uneven match. While her stance was strong, her stature was much less than that of her battle mate. Thin beads of sweat began to show on her skin, the only sign she gave that this exercise was causing strain. But she would not complain too much. Secretly, any time spent with Dene was like a suspension from her pathetic circumstance. She could be just Auri. Not Princess Aurielle, not Senate Liason. But Auri, just Auri, with her Dene.

"My goal is not to harm, Master Dene. My goal is to prevent you from harming me," she spoke, being bluntly honest. Because it was a truth that when training, she would not learn if she were not being honest with her teacher and mentor. "In all things, a swift death may mean no answers. Were I trying to capture you instead of kill, what good would harming you do?"

She used the Force to give her block enough power to thrust him back, and she dropped low to the ground, swinging her leg in a wide arc as her hands pressed against the floor to steady herself as she aimed for the back of his knees with the intent to knock him over.

## Post 7 by Aurielle Stratus on 2012-08-20T15:52:02Z



"Ah, but even without my hands, I can command the Force," Auri countered, smiling back. When they rested from their sparring, she proved this point by focusing her energy on a single stone about the size of a coin resting by the water's edge. She lifed it with the Force, bringing it to rest in the space in front of her chest, circling much like a planet's orbit, around her invisible sun.

The stone moving with her, she sat at the edge of the water. While the stone continued in its orbit, her eyes focused on Dene. He made focusing hard. That's why she used the stone. It gave her mind reason to focus and not to act out of turn. "I am not a fighter," she relented. The truth between the two made speaking easier. "I abhor battle, and violence. It doesn't gve credit to my family name."

None of her names, anyway. Organa. Jackson. Stratus. All three, strong and proud. She was the weak link. "I wish there was a way for me to be comfortable in fighting, but there just isn't."
