***Corruption and Incorruption***

This is an official scenario that will be written into the timeline. It begins in the month of Anak, 36 IRY. Background information is being provided for reference. Additional information will follow as appropriate timing allows.

About this scenario: Critical military supply shipments to Anaxes from the Contrumm shipyards are being intercepted and hijacked too easily by pirates. The Imperial Republic Intelligence Service (IRIS) discovers a plot arrangement where shipment route and inventory are being leaked to pirate gang and military escorts fail to stop the raid. IRIS-DCI (Directorate of Counterintelligence) believe local officials are involved.

## Post 4 by James Stratus on 2015-12-29T11:24:15Z



**\\*\* PIRATE CAPITAL SHIP MAELSTROM \*\*/**

Aboard the pirate dreadnaught *Maelstrom*, pirate chieftain Captain Slate Commor turned to his first mate Lt. Adra Delys. **"What news from our contact?"**

"**The supply convoy will pass through these coordinates skipper,"** she said pointing to the starmap, **"and will be under escort until they reach this point"** indicating to another section of the starmap **"and then the escort will shrink off into a third and will be ripe for the taking."**

**"You know I don't like that name,**" Slate said, referring to the term "skipper". **"Chief or Captain will do."**

**"Anything yo say Captain."** The pirate chieftain was ex-military and as such instituted a system of military ranks below his and that of his lieutenant, or second in command of the pirate gang. A supply of military grade weaponry and crucial medical supplies was on its way from Contrumm to Anaxes. It was the third shipment they would intercept, and thanks to their 'contact', they knew exactly where and when to hit it, and with what size of force they needed to overpower the military escort that accompanied it. The *Maelstrom* was not the only pirate ship that they had heavily modified with military-grade weapons at their disposal. He had a feeling this stroke of luck would not last for long, eventually their contact's masters would catch on and do something about it. Slate Commor was preparing his gang for a retreat back to base after this run and then planning a permanent move to another base that their contact did not know of to avoid entanglements with the Imperial Republic Navy.

**"Stay sharp everyone.**" Slate ordered. Currently in hyperspace, they were about to enter realspace with a gravity well generating ship that would also pull the Imperial Republic supply convoy out of hyperspace. They had stolen the gravity well generator from one of the previous raids and had retrofitted it to a dreadnaught dedicated to interdiction only due to the amount of power required to operate the gravity well.
