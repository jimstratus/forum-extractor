It had been several months since Phoenix had been left on Hapes to rule in her Mothers stead. Now as things progressed it looked as if her Mother wasn't coming back and it was up to her to rule. No matter, this was her time now and she could not wait for the events to come.

She sat at her desk going over preparations for the up coming ball. She was patiently listening to the coordinator ramble on about why she had picked a pewter silver over a shimmery gold. Phoenix tried to stifle a yawn as she nodded her head. **"Very well Adena, I appreciate all your hard work. The High Prince and his entourage should be here within the week. Please have the final menu on my desk by tomorrow morning. I do not want any more changes to be made at this point in time."** Adena bowed her head, **"You are dismissed. I have also made room for The High Princes own security. Everything is in order." Phoenix nodded. **"And the Navy? Are they prepared to escort the High Prince?"**

"Yes ma'am they are standing by." Phoenix again nodded. **"Very well. Thank you for your time and work Rhowen. Now I understand why my Mother relied on you so heavily. I really appreciate you staying on."** Rhowen smiled. "Think nothing of it. This is your time now Phoenix, you will be Queen Mother in a matter of days."

**"I don't know if I should be scared or excited."**

Rhowen winked at her. "Maybe a little of both is required." Phoenix smiled. **"Yes perhaps you are right."** If Rhowen was about to say something more she never got the chance. At that moment Jayden came running into the room. "Nixy, I don't know what to wear!!!" Phoenix frowned. **"Well that's no good!"** she dismissed Rhowen with a smile and a simple nod before turning back to Jayden. "Well lets go check out your closet shall we?"

Jayden had come to stay with her at the Palace when Revan and her Mother left. They never spoke of their mission, but Phoenix had an idea they were looking for her two little sisters. That was a story in its self, and definitely for another time. Since then, Jayden had become her charge, and she took that responsibility to heart. As she followed Jayden to his room, she couldn't help wondering if her mother would come to the coronation.
