In the Royal Imperial Throneroom, holocams and a few reporters stood with anxiety over the event about to take place. The room was full with various security details, high ranking government officials, and other special guests. The High Prince rarely ever reporters in the Throneroom, though this time he had made a small exception, allowing five of the news agencies to send one representative. Many new security-types, all clad in a royal blue and purple uniform, stood at attention in an assembly. Everyone awaited the arrival of the High Prince and who they guessed would be the new leader of an organization, rumours pointing to a new Royal Guard organization.

Back in the Private Office of the Supreme Ruler, the High Prince went over his notes and continued his work. He would soon be joined by the Executor and Minister of Defense, and the new Commandant. This was a happy occasion. General Bodost and his teams were already in place, and the General was undoubtely preparing for transition of security for the Throne level and royal residential and office areas of the Palace. Though they would stay on until the recruitment process was complete, they would slowly phase out from primary duty, though there was still going to be an IRSB unit assigned to the Palace at all times, as were their units from every security agency of the Imperial Republic assigned to it.

## Post 6 by James Stratus on 2009-02-11T07:43:46Z



The High Prince nodded and smiled as his secretary walked in. **"Looks like its time, Colonel Shan,"** he said, with a hint of excitement in his voice. **"It is your time to shine."** He looked around to those in the room. **"Let's move, folks."** He led the way and their was applause and salutes as the double doors opened and the High Prince and his entourage entered the Throne Room. Uniformed Palace Security Agents, Imperial Republic Security Agents, and a group of uniformed men and women clad in purple and blue combat suits, standing at attention. When the High Prince and his group came to a halt at the center of the Throne itself, silence came all around the room. The High Prince's voice was booming and authoritative.

**"Recent events have led us to some misfortune. It has brought specific issues of internal security to our attention, which, I am pleased to say, have been corrected, though the process is still somewhat ongoing it has met with huge success, though some casualties. It is therefore necessary that we reorganize and re-establish a unit and team which we once trusted with the safety of some of the most critical personnel of the Imperial Republic and critical operations as well. We must form a fresh view of this organization. As of today, we are formally creating the organization of His Majesty's Royal Guard. Their uniforms as you see to my right are new and fresh, a new design, a new name, a new trust, a new start, and.."** there was a short pause, **"a new commandant."** He motioned for a woman clothed similar to the new Royal Guards though a bit more distinguished approached from behind. **"I present to you Colonel Kaidlen Shan, who I hereby appoint as Commandant of the His Majesty's Royal Guard, effective immediately. I would also like to make mention of Captain Koru Tav, who has served us well, and should be honored for his loyalty and exemplary service. He shall serve as the Attachment Leader of my personal guard unit, also known as Captain of the Guard. The new organization shall have the full authority of a security agency of the Imperail Republic with special powers neccessary to carry out their responsibilities to maintain the safety and security of key government personnel. The recruitment project is assigned to Captain Tav and Colonel Shan, and they will begin immediately. Without further adieu, I will dismiss all present at these procedings, as we all have a lot of work to do, I am sure you all understand. Thank you."**

With those final words, the High Prince and his entourage departed the way that they came, and the armed units snapped to formation and marched out, taking positions as assigned, the guards marching out to a small assembly room where they would meet with Colonel Shan and Captain Tav for a briefing.
