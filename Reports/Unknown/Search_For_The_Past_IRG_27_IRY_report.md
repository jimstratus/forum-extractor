Colonel Kaidlen Shan paced within her new office, as she reviewed files of the membership of the newly instated Royal Guard. There were so many new faces within the ranks of the Guard. Too many new faces. She wondered what had happened to the members of the Guard that she had trained with, before the High Prince had left for five years. Many of them had vanished during Layha's tenure as Empress. Not that it was Layha's fault. Kaidlen had been aquainted with the Hapan woman for a long time now, and knew that she was not behind what had eventually happened. Well, she was planning to fix that. Those members of the old Royal Guard that she could find would be invited back to join the new Guard. It was the least that she could do for those people who should have never been replaced like they were. Unfortunantly, it was not a task that Kaidlen could delegate to someone else, even though it should not fall on the new Commandant's shoulders. None of the new Guard members knew any of the older members, so it was left to Kaidlen to search for them. However, she would not be searching alone. The Colonel wanted to test and learn more about Koru Tav, the new Captain of the Guard, and essentually her second in command. If they were going to be working together, eack of them needed to be able to rely and trust completely the other.

**"Aislyn, when Captain Tav arrives, show him right in. Except for him, no one else please, excluding His Majesty, who is always free to see me whenever he wishes."** Kaidlen ordered, after activating her comm. **"If Princess Auri shows up looking for me, let me know. I'll come out to talk with her. I'm not really expecting her to search me out today though."**

**"Yes Colonel Shan. I'll see to it."** Aislyn's voice replied.

The Colonel deactivated the comm, and went over to where a holopic rested. It was an image of her as a child, with her 'father'. With a smile, she turned it off, hiding the image it displayed. The holopic really should be in her apartment, not her office, but Kaidlen felt it was safer there than anywhere else. Sitting down, she pulled out a datapad and started listing names on it while she waited.
