Life rarely goes the way it is expected to. This was a lesson that many would learn over and over during their lifetimes. It was also a lesson the Ieyena had faced more times then she liked. After almost five years of assisting her father with his battle against an unidentifiable illness she had watched he slip out of this life in his sleep.

Once again she found herself in her official residence on Coruscant, but unlike the countless time she had spent here in the past she found no comfort in it's cold and lifeless walls. She had dedicated her entire life in being everything to everyone and in the process had lost sight of who she truly was. This truth was now all too obvious in her minds eye. Yet again she had returned to the only life is had known. But this time she wounded if it was the life that she truly wanted.
