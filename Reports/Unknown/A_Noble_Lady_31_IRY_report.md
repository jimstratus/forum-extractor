**Royal Palace of Coruscant**

*After the Turbulence*

The High Prince had moved from his office to the Royal Throneroom, where official proceedings generally occurred, though in this instance it was empty, save himself and hiding guards. He sat on the Throne and meditated. Much had happened recently, and there was much to do, many decisions to make. Some difficult, some easy. Executor Treyson had returned to Coruscant to tend to many similar such duties and decisions, and was now joining the High Prince in the Throneroom at his request. He nodded as she came in and took a seat. She rarely bowed in private, though did so slightly on occasion. She was the only one other than the Royal Family that did not. She was his second in command, and partner in running the government, though she answered to the High Prince, and to him only. **"Ah, Tavria. Thank you for coming."** He handed her a "Gold Card" which was the term used for the datacard with all the authorizations and approvals of fiscal assets and spending he had just made. There was also the "Red Card", regarding military and security action approvals and authorizations, and "Blue Card", dealing with diplomacy activities, and the Grey Card, dealing with all other government items of note. After she reviewed it briefly and nodded, expecting to see approvals and declinations in specific areas of note, she put it away. She knew it was not why she was here. He then handed her a "Purple Card" datacard, which she was fairly familiar with though it was used infrequently. It was the datacard containing decisions on high promotions, awards, and other such commendations. He watched her eyebrows raise momentarily as she reviewed it and then she nodded in agreement.

**"Yes,"** said she, **"I think this is very appropriate. When do you plan to make this happen?"** she asked.

**"Now,"** the High Prince answered. He keyed a button on the comm. **"Major Tav, have General Shan and Miss Devin meet me in the Throneroom at once."**

## Post 16 by Diandra Devin on 2009-09-10T17:09:00Z



(edited)

It hadn't taken very long for Diandra to return to her office. She was just beginning to start the paperwork that would allow Quinn to "take" his "vacation" with her. As she did the paperwork, she pulled out her communicator and pressed a button on it. It immediately dialed Quinn's number and gave her the signal that it was waiting for him to pick up.

On the other end, Jared was buried under paperwork. He was going over things concerning the merging of the two imperial republic military branches, and there was still a lot to be done before the official merger could happen. The events of the past month had pushed it back even farther. He heard his com beep twice with an incoming signal.

"Quinn," he said. "What I can I do for you?"

Diandra grinned and sat back as she heard his voice. "Well, Lieutenant Colonel, you can stop whatever you're doing and talk to me a for a moment. I've got some news for you."

Jared wondered what Diandra needed. They hadn't had a chance to go to dinner that night or any other night since then. He'd been shipped off to Berchest shortly after everything concerning the general was wrapped up, and that is where he sat at the moment.

"I'm listening." He sounded tired and worn out as he sat there in the makeshift office he had.

"You'll be getting new orders in a few hours," she said. "I think you'll be very interested in knowing what they are, and I think you'll be absolutely delighted when you get them." She couldn't help but give a little laugh as she worked on the forms before her. "Oh, and I do have some news to share with you as well. I thought you might want to know before the reporters get ahold of it."

He found himself a bit annoyed from her vague answers. He was tired and had been dealing with paperwork all day, so it didn't help his mood. "Out with it, Devin." He leaned back in his chair as he waited for her answer.

Her voice sounded a little hurt as she spoke next. "C'mon, Jared, don't be so grouchy with me. How do you ever expect to get another date with me if you're going to be an old grouch?" He could hear a pout in her voice, though she continued.

"I was promoted," she said finally. "And... you should call me Lady Devin from now on. In public, at least." She paused for a moment and then sighed. "I don't suppose you'd be interested in hearing about your new orders."

Jared kicked his boots up on his desk."Well then, Lady Devin, I am curious about them since I was given orders facilitate the merger." He also began to wonder what her being a lady meant about his chances with her. She had almost been out of his league before, but now she really was.

"Well..." she said. "As I said, I was promoted. I was also... well, rather ordered on vacation. Three weeks of it. I was told that I could take anyone I want. I'm taking you." She laughed now. "Just think... you, me, alone on a tropical beach... You can finally take me out to dinner -- with no interruptions."

He lifted a brow as she spoke of them being alone. "Are you sure you want to be seen on a tropical planet with the likes of me? Especially alone?" His tone teasing slightly as he sat there waiting for her response. The idea appealed to him, but somehow he doubted they would be alone completely given how much protection members of the throne usually had.

Diandra laughed and shook her head before she lowered her voice slightly and leaned over her paperwork, the comm close to her mouth. "Hey, if you don't think you can handle the sight of me in a bikini, just say so. I'm pretty sure that his Majesty would allow me to change my mind, especially if I told him that you were revolted by the thought of my swimsuit body." She filled out a few more lines and then she spoke again. "Last time I saw you, you were pretty built, so I don't think we'd be alone. The beach girls on Spira would take one look at you and you'd be like fresh bantha meat."

He sat there listening to her and chuckled slightly. "Well now doing that would be a insult to you. As for me going, if there are new orders for me I do believe I won't have much of a choice will I?" He was curious why she wanted to go to Spira. It was a beautiful planet from what he had heard, but he also had heard it was rife with people and tabloid photographers.

"See, that's the beauty of it. You're being ordered to accompany me so that you *can't* say no." He heard her laugh again. "That, my dear sir, was completely his Majesty's idea. I went along with it because I knew it wouldn't give you a chance to say, oh, find an excuse to stand me up." She was teasing, of course. It just seemed that every time she finally said yes to get him off her back, some situation would pop up that would keep them from going out together. It had finally aroused her curiosity enough to begin wondering what sort of date Quinn would be.

Jared sighed as she spoke of him trying to stand her up. "Now why would I ever do that Lady Devin? If anything, I would think it would be you standing me up rather then the other way around." He was trying to think if there was anything else they needed to discuss before he returned to the doldrums of his work.

"Jared! I realize that I'm... not always the most friendly person. Alright, fine, I can be downright cold and boring," she said. "But, if there's one thing I'm not, it's a breaker of promises. If I agree to meet you for dinner, I'll meet you for dinner." She grinned a little and put down her pen. "Now, then," she said. "I should get back to work before my co-workers think that you've somehow seduced me from Berchest."

This brought a chuckle from him as she spoke of her being boring and cold. A light hrm coming from him when she said he seduced her."Well in that case you should get back to work. Send me the vitals on this trip and I will meet you there." Jared knew a vacation sounded amazing at the moment since he'd been jumping from operation to operation since becoming a officer.

"Will do," she said. "I'll send them along with your new orders, soon as I get the paperwork finished." She bid him goodbye and then ended the call before she went back to work, thinking about and looking forward to the next few weeks.
