**Centurion Estate**

**Outside Theed, Naboo**

Revan was sitting quietly in his private study. The sun bathed the room in warm light from the skylights and large side windows. Outside the study, Revan could see the beautiful gardens that his servants ensured were meticulously tended to. Seated in front of Revan, were the various other Lords and Ladies who all had families that were at least in some part controlled by Centurion Industries and thusly...Revan himself.

*"Lord Centurion, surely you realize how urgently your leadership and expertise is needed in this situation? Not only are we currently in the midst of huge commercial endeavors, but our economy has never been improving at such an exponential rate. If the people of Naboo felt that there was no one to lead them, chaos would shortly ensue."*

Revan sat tall and rubbed his chin as he thought. While he was by no means the oldest noble on Naboo, he had, somehow, managed to come to be one of the most prominent even with his somewhat recent, extended absence. Despite these thoughts, he spoke with the confidence and poise they had all come to respect. **"I understand what is on your mind, but how am I too make such a decision lightly? Surely you understand that such a transition requires coordination on many levels... Do you think the Imperial Republic will be happy about this change? I've served the Imperial Republic, and I can tell you now that this is all highly unorthodox. It has only been hypothesized on paper...that doesn't mean it will work."**

One of the younger nobles was next to speak, *"Lord Centurion, we all have heard the rumors of your involvement with..."* Revan cut the man off with a serious glare that discouraged him from continuing his statement. Revan slammed his fist onto his desk. He was tired of people forcing him to take an unnecessary trip down memory lane. **"She stays out of this..or you consider relocation.... I want to do what is right for Naboo just as much as anybody in this room...hence my hesitation. Now...I will speak with the council about the growing situation. Her assistance will be useful.. These developments stay between us...we need not start a panic about the Queen's condition. Is that understood?"** Revan watched as various heads nodded.

He then waved a dismissive hand and nodded his head slightly. **"Very well, you are all dismissed."** When they had left the room, he sank into his chair and rubbed his temples slowly...wondering how Jayden was doing his first day at school. *Maybe we should have continued to drift amongst the stars a bit longer...* Five rather long years had gone by since Revan resigned from the Imperial Republic. At that time, he'd been deeply conflicted. Still, it was as if he could barely recognize any part of the government he once served. Having increased in both intelligence and patience, Revan knew the time would soon come where he would be forced into action. For the sake of his young son, he simply hoped that wasn't...very soon.

*Two Days Later*

Revan was seated in a comfortable seat before the Queen's executive assistant at her estate on Naboo. For the time being, he was serving as a caretaker for her office and duties...until the aristocracy and her executive council reached a decision.

*"Lord Centurion,*

*Thank you for coming to see me today sir. I have here...documents that the Queen had wanted me to ensure you received on your next visit to Theed. You will find that it is completely legal and signed by not only the executive council but the majority of the aristocracy as well."*

Revan eyed the man curiously. The Queen's executive assistant wasn't a noble and enjoyed little change in status due to position. It was akin to being a secretary really... Revan took the documents from the man and quickly began scanning them. His expression gradually changed from one of intrigue to mild confusion. Closing the folder and placing it back on the desk, he rubbed his chin before looking at the assistant. **"Is this for real? I can understand the aristocracy...most of them work for me anyways...but...unanimously by the council?"**

*"The Queen had it drafted just after your most recent departure and appointment as her Chief Advisor."* Of course, recent was a term of large approximation in this case.

Revan shook his head slightly, **"There are numerous nobles from Naboo currently in the Queen's service. In fact...many of them are more influential than myself...heck almost all of them are."**

*"That is not our concern. This change is already effective..and it only relates to the duties you will hold on Naboo. It's a testament to your dedicated service to the Queen and the people of this planet despite your...other obligations that tend to arise."*

Revan nodded slowly before rising from his seat and shaking the assistant's hand. **"So I suppose this means the council is attempting to force my decision prematurely? Fine...I will set things in motion. Do be so kind as to inform the council that I am most displeased...and their failure to at least heed my requests...orders as the Chief Advisor...WILL be dealt with. The situation is fragile enough without them trying to manipulate people to bend to their will."** Revan watched as the assistant stood from his desk and bowed respectfully. *"As you wish, Lord Centurion."*

Exiting the office, Revan passed through the corridors, nodding to various personnel. He would need to meet with the Royal Council sooner than expected...on top of that it would appear he needed to call a meeting of the aristocracy...very soon...

## Post 3 by Revan Centurion on 2007-10-28T04:59:38Z



*One Day Later*

Revan had been extremely busy over the course of the past few days. Ever since his arrival, he had been feeling the pressure from his various subordinates. He had just returned to his estate from visiting the Queen at her private retreat near the lake. It would appear that her condition was not improving at all. The Royal Medical staff gave her a very low chance of survival.

Sitting at the desk in his personal study, he looked out the huge array of windows at the rainy weather outside. On his desk were two documents, the first was the document given to him yesterday by the Queen's executive assistant. The contents of the document were not entirely unexpected, but he still had been avoiding the "increase in status" for personal reasons. A part of him was still unsure about embarking down this path. It would mean many things for his life... The second document had just been drafted earlier that morning at the Queen's private retreat following a brief meeting with the Queen's medical staff and the Royal Council. Revan had given them a severe lecture about their actions...he was NOT impressed.

As he continued to stare out the window, he wondered what the future would hold in store for himself, his family, and the people of Naboo. However, it was time to stop contemplating his ability to lead and simply trust in his experience and the opinions of others. He quickly logged onto his terminal and began typing a message to Selina. The message was transmitted to her office.

*Lady Vadcasta,*

*I require your presence at my estate with the utmost urgency. There are a number of developments that we need to...discuss. I look forward to meeting with you in the near future.*

*Lord Revan Centurion*

Revan knew she would want to know what was really happening with the Queen. To this point, it had been kept completely secret. Oddly enough, Selina was also an advisor to the Queen, though not a member of the council. Though not a native of Naboo, Selina had managed to accelerate to the upper rung of Naboo society - with Revan's assistance. A powerful executive in the corporation that Revan controlled, she'd made the transition into politics rather seamlessly. Still, she hated politics just as much as Revan. A necessary evil, to be sure. Revan could not say that he much approved of the system the royalty of Naboo had used in the past. It was certainly one of the things he intended to discuss with Selina when she arrived.

## Post 5 by Revan Centurion on 2007-10-29T17:28:01Z



Revan's air limo came to a subtle halt inside the expansive garage attached to the rear of the estate. Revan hadn't really been one for the public eye as of late, and it was something he was...not too interested in becoming used to once more. Stepping out of the air limo, he nodded to the two family guards waiting for him. **"Gentlemen, I trust all is well?"**

The senior guard bowed slightly and replied. "Of course, My Lord." The guards were devoid of any real emotion, most of them having served with Revan during his time as Director of IRIS. Ironically enough, the head of his security was also his corporation's Chief of Staff, Selina Vadcasta. After all, he'd yet to meet anyone better suited for the task.

Revan nodded his head and gave the man a slight smile as he walked through the doorway leading into the estate's interior. Having been decorated completely on his own or by his Great Uncle, Revan enjoyed the privacy of not having the inside of his estate used as a model for others. Additionally, every feature was tailored to his specific tastes and needs. Naturally however, it was not at all sparse in decoration. In truth it was rather...ornately decorated. While Revan did not exactly feel a need for all the luxuries of the rich and famous, he did recognize that maintaining appearances had its uses. Besides, the more recent alterations had been done by Selina during the three months following his resignation that they remained on Naboo. The moment Jayden was born, they were sure to distance themselves for the destabilizing Imperial Republic. *Hah...Imperial Republic. It isn't a Republic at all anymore. It's just a really crappy imitation of Palpatine's Empire, lead by an individual that can barely control their own life let alone an entire government.* In all honesty, Revan had been amused when he learned of the Empress' 'changes' within the government. It bothered Revan significantly...mostly because he knew what needed to be done. It was part of who he was...what his responsibility in this galaxy remained. **"Balance..."** It existed in all things, and the Empress had forgotten that. Then again, perhaps she simply did not care. For her sake, Revan hoped it was the former. The latter would lead to nothing other than failure.

As Revan turned the corner, he was immediately greeted by one of his aides. "Welcome back, My Lord. Is there anything you require?"

Revan shook his head. **"No. Where is my son?"**

"In the main living room. Lady Vadcasta is with him."

Revan raised an eyebrow slightly. **"Selina? She's here already?"** Before the aide could respond, Revan waved a dismissive hand. **"Nevermind, thank you."** Quickening his pace slightly, Revan made his way towards the area of the estate where his son and Selina waited. Upon entering the room, Revan had the severe sensation that something rather sharp was being hurled at his head. Remaining calm, Revan simply sighed as he stopped in place and raised a hand nonchalantly. Glancing off to his left, he smirked when he saw Jayden standing there and a very sharp piece of glass mere inches from Revan's own head. **"Still angry about going to school then, Jayden?"** The five-year old boy folded his arms across his chest.

"Why do I have to go? I'm smarter than all of them *anyways*."

Revan laughed slightly as he grabbed the piece of glass and set it on a nearby table. **"Jayden, contrary to what you may think, you do not know everything. Nobody knows everything. Trust me, I've been around for a while. If you're smarter, than make yourself useful in helping others to succeed. Personal success is--"**

"...secondary to the natural balance and order of life," Jayden replied mockingly. "Yeah dad, I know. You *always* say that. If you're so smart, why can't you come up with different sayings?"

Revan smiled slightly as he walked over to Jayden and pulled him into a tight hug. **"Your father is just as much a student of life as you are, Jay."** Revan came to his feet as he heard Selina approaching. He smiled at her for a moment before turning back to Jayden. **"Go run along and have some fun. I want your homework done** ***before*** **dinner this time though."** Jayden made a face before sighing. "Yes father." After embracing Selina's legs, Jayden took off down the hallway. **"And no more using the force for menial tasks. It nulls your senses!"**

There was no reply, but Selina started to chuckle lightly as she reached out to touch Revan's arm. "Revan, don't you think maybe you're a bit tough on him?"

Revan turned his gaze to Selina with a somewhat confused look on his face. **"What? No? The galaxy is not a playground and he needs to learn that. If he keeps going around like a crazy person, he's going to get himself or someone else hurt. That boy has way too much of his mother in him."**

Selina smirked slightly. "Oh yes...I'm sure that's one hundred percent Layha's personality right there."

Revan rolled his eyes slightly. **"Please Selina, we both know I was young and incredibly stupid."**

Selina wanted to make a comment about how much good Revan had done and how they never would have met had it not been for that, but she chose to remain silent. Revan really did not seem to enjoy taking those trips down memory lane. For five years now, she had watched him bury his anger and his sadness in training and study. Sure, it did him credit. He was arguably one of the most successful men she knew, in multiple ways. At the same time, it was holding him back. Holding him back from taking on the role she knew he was destined for. "Yes well...you're still kind of stupid." Revan cracked the slightest of smiles. "Anyways...what did you call me here for? My time is money you know, your money."

Revan shook his head as he laughed slightly. **"Don't remind me. Anyways, surely you've heard the news?"** Selina nodded. **"Well, I need your help. It falls to me to make some...reforms, and I can't exactly do it alone."** Revan glanced around before motioning for Selina to follow him. **"Come on, we shouldn't have this conversation out in the open. Can't really be sure of who to trust these days. Needless to say...I think it's time we got back to doing what needs to be done for the galaxy..."** Selina's expression brightened, hopeful that maybe Revan was going to finally start coming around. It had been some time since she'd seen the Revan she fell in love with. Of course, Revan knew nothing of that...or at least pretended not to know.

A war with Hapes right now is the last thing she needs. She's already on the verge of ostracizing herself from the rest of the galaxy based on her somewhat recent policy and civil rights amendments. I've not done anything to betray the Imperial Republic, thus I'm not truly concerned. Am I not allowed the opportunity to speak with my Queen or a fellow Head of State?"**

Detrich merely grumbled. The sound elicited little more than a smirk from Revan as he stepped aboard his modified Escort Shuttle. It was, very likely, one of the few ships that he knew Imperial Republic sensors would have difficulty tracking. After all, most of the IR's advanced technology and present operating procedures were developed under a time period of constant preemptive offensive action. Still, a blockade of the cluster. Revan had to admit, that was indeed a daring move. After all, Revan had been in command of the Hapan military just prior to his resignation from the Imperial Republic. They knew more about the IR military than most were likely aware by this point. *We all have our contingencies...*

As Revan, Detrich, and one other member of Centurion's guard boarded the escort shuttle, the loading ramp closed behind them. Quickly settling into his seat behind the controls, Revan smiled to himself as he once again slipped into the simple pleasure of piloting his own spacecraft. Within mere moments, the shuttle was rocketing towards the edge of the system and then entering hyperspace.

*"Imperial Republic Blockade, this is Count Revan Centurion of the Hapan Court. I respectfully request permission to pass through the transistory mists to enter the cluster. I have important business to attend to on Hapes. I'm forwarding identification details now."*

Pressing the button to transmit his clearance information, Revan simply waited to see if he would be allowed to pass. Naturally, he was getting through one way or another. However, he really did not feel like having to exert himself unnecessarily.

## Post 9 by Revan Centurion on 2007-10-30T00:03:12Z



Revan merely smirked before nodding to the Detrich in the co-pilot's seat. **"Alright Detrich, engage the stealth mode systems."** 'Stealth' mode was perhaps a bit misleading. Revan himself had not really looked into the addition of a cloaking system for the vessel. Sure, the technology definitely existed, and he'd had access to it in the past. The problem was, a cloaking system made a lot of the spacecraft's onboard ECM equipment effectively useless. In a ship of the escort shuttle's size, Revan was worried about being seen by the naked eye. Chances are if you were close enough to do that successfully, it was too late anyways. Instead, he'd adapated an advanced electronics suite which reduced the spacecraft's emissions spectrum severely - except for those in the infrared band. For that, Revan was more than capable of masking his entire ship through other means.

"Stealth mode activated, cancel activation." The computer confirmed the activation of the new flight mode and also repeated the order to cancel said activation should it be required.

Smoothly, Revan applied full thrust to the spacecraft's sublight drive. Sure, the Imperial Republic's military was impressive, but the technology used to create Revan's own ship tended to go beyond the concepts of normal military grade equipment. They could follow, but they'd be lost through no fault of his own in due time. Keying the communicator, he sent his reply. **"How kind of you. I will comply, but I can't be responsible for the inability of your pilots to keep up should it come to that. You needn't worry, I'm going directly to the Fountain Palace and then leaving again."** It was the last communication Revan would make to any Imperial Republic forces until he was leaving. It took only moments for the spacecraft to slip beyond the natural boundary that was the transistory mists. Arriving on the other side, Revan initiated the sequence to enter hyperspace once more. The Cluster was extremely large, and it was a wonder that Layha was foolish enough to try and blockade the entire thing. That would take more military resources than most military commanders would even attempt to spare. At least...it would if the goal of the blockade was to be completely and utterly effective. Fortunately, the advent of advanced targeting systems allowed for fairly good coverage without involving a lot of ships. The down side to this of course was...smaller ships could sneak through without so much as breaking a sweat. For the second time, the *Legatus* shot off into hyperspace.

---------------------

The short ten minute series of hyperspace jumps ended with Revan's ship coming out of hyperspace just outside the gravity well of Hapes. Activating his code clearance once more, he quickly piloted the shuttle down towards the Fountain Palace. Enroute, he opened his comlink and sent a brief message to Palace Security. **"This is Count Centurion. Inform the Queen Mother that I would very much enjoy an audience with her as soon as possible."** Reaching out with the force, Revan removed any inclination to deny his request. He doubted the Queen remembered him much. After all, he was no longer a necessary part to her goals. Still, it was possible she could accomplish much through him. Caralon was and always would be a resourceful woman. One not to be trusted, yes, but useful nonetheless... Turning his head to Detrich, he nodded slightly. **"Deactivate the stealth mode."** The soft beeping of the computer was confirmation enough that the spacecraft had been reverted to normal operation.
