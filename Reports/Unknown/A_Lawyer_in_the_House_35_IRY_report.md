James Stratus was relaxing in the arms of Dana Lance on the couch, both in silk night-gownish robes, when Marc walked in.

All this by Jim's own command, for she had defied his direct orders during the Battle for Kuat and endangered the mission and the life of herself and her future offspring. But why was this coming to his mind now...it had been 17 years. Had Marc come to talk about this?
