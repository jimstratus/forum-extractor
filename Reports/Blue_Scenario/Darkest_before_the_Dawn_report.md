It had been two years since the events of that fateful night, he had spent two weeks afterwards scrapping up scraps and trying to find a place to live; from there he found other ways to make money on the side. Some included using his fists to make a living in illicit fighting rings, but fate stepped in once again as he worked in the storeroom of the store he worked in during the day. Unknown to him as a large Zabrak walked into the area he was working in, the creature had been sent to escort him to Don Giska Ora'rey; the bothan had been watching the young man for a few months and was pleased with his progress. **"Boy, you come with me to see Don Ora'rey."**

Jared's head snapped up at the mention of the bothan who ran the fighting rings in Cornet at the moment, his first reaction was to run out the back door but he also knew that the Zabrak would probably be on him before he could even bother to turn the corner.**"Whats Giska want with me?"**

The Zabrak didn't bother to answer as he motioned for Jared to follow him before walking out of the storeroom, Jared followed the zabrak and sighed slightly as he mind turned to his mother and little brother and sister; he had been giving her as much money as he could afford. It was one of the reasons he had entered the fighting rings.

As they went outside he was greeted by the sight of a luxury airspeeder with a the bothan crimepin sitting in the driver's seat**"In Quinn, we have much to speak about.** Jared climbed into the other side of the airspeeder before they took off heading south towards the training center he used.**"I want you to fight for me Quinn, your a good human who has a good head on his shoulders.**

Jared looked over at the bothan and sneered slightly.**"Get to the point Giska, I've got to get back to work.** He didn't want his boss to catch him not there since it could very well mean he'd lose his job and only way he had to support himself since the money he made fighting in the ring went to his mother to help with his sibilings.

**"Quinn, do not test me or bad things could happen."** It was at that moment that Giska turned on a small holoscreen aboard the speeder and the image flashed of Jared's family as Giska smirked looking at him.**"Now then, I want you to fight for me.**

He looked over at the bothan and sneered knowing the furry sentient was holding all of the cards at the moment, Jared didn't like the idea of fighting for the bothan but knew if he didn't something bad would happen to his family even though Giska didn't say it outright. **"Fine Giska, I'll fight for you.** Jared didn't like the idea of fighting for Giska since he had heard stories of what happened in the fighting rings that Giska personally oversaw, but he reminded himself at the moment those were just rumors passed around between fighters.

Shortly after agreeing to fight for Giska he was dropped off outside the training center and given a address to be at that night, Jared then returned to the flat he rented to relaxed before he headed off to the address; unknown to him at that was the fact that it would be the stage of his first true fight for his life. He arrived at the address in question around fifteen minutes early only to be greeted by the Zabrak that had nearly dragged him out of the store earlier.**"Come Quinn, tonight you truly fight."**

Jared was curious what the Zabrak spoke about, but his answer came a moment later as he walked into the back of a large room filled with other fighters only to see a Gran being taken out the door in a clear bodybag; the cold hand of fear gripped his spine as he realized exactly what happened here. He was only given a few moments before his name was called and he stepped up to the gate that led into the ring.**"Come on Giska, you never said anything about this..."**

Giska ignored Jared's words and only gave the signal that sent Jared stumbling into the ring where he saw his opponent who growled at him before stalking around the ring, Jared thoughts whisked away as he tried to dodge the Cathar's vicious attack only to be swatted across the ring like a gnat. He hadn't expected anything like this but something in the back of his mind told him he'd better fight or he'd end up like the Gran he had seen in the bodybag and fight was exactly what he did, as the cathar approached him Jared lashed out with a punishing blow to the catlike creatures solarplexus before swinging around the bring the durasteel toe of his boot into contact with the cathar's kneecap.

The spectator's stood with a cheer as the cathar let out a scream of pain, Jared himself backed up and clutched his midsection that was still bleeding from the cathar's claws.**"You die here human."** The cathar spoke before lashing out at Jared who was just in the reach of it's claws which raked over his leg sending Jared to his good knee as pain shot through his leg and up it seering any thoughts from his mind except survival.

The fight continued like this until the cathar finally slumped to the dirt floor in exhaustion and bloodloss, Jared leaned against the duracrete of the ring wall clutching his midsection in agony; he looked down as he pulled his hand away only to see blood seep from the four gashes he had received from the cathar who now laid in the middle of the ring. Jared barely registered the gate opening again as he hobbled towards it, the Zabrak smirking at him as he allowed Jared to pass and enter the backroom where he landed on a table while trying to maintain conciousness.

Nirium was used to seeing violence. She was used to seeing death and hatred; she did, after all, work in the same rings that Jared now worked in. She like many others there did what she could to survive, and it was not always the most pleasant of work. "Why?" He sneered at her. "Gonna go in the back finally and do some real work?"

"No," she said. "But I imagine that Don Ora'rey doesn't want to lose his new prize fighter to blood loss."

Her boss grunted and set her free. She jumped easily from her dancer's platform and headed into the back room where she had seen them lead the poor boy. It only took Nirium a moment to find him. There were three deep gashes across his chest and left side; a nasty scratch on his right arm; turning him over revealed some already developing bruises on his back. Nirium bit her lip and hoped to the gods that she could fix the poor boy. She wasn't a doctor, or even a medic, but living where she lived, some medical knowledge was mandatory. What she knew was basic at best; disinfecting things and stitching up wounds if need be, but that was it.

Nirium had seen far too many young men like him go into the ring and never come out again. She could barely remember the first time she had seen it; the shock had been so great that a much younger Nirium had fainted on the spot. More than a few of her friends had gone into the ring, and like the rest, had not survived. She refused to let yet another young man die in such a way. Besides, there was something about the boy that called to her.

There was only one thing to do. With a nod to herself, Nirium rolled him onto his back again and gently patted his face.

"Boy," she said softly in his ear. "Wake up, boy. We need to get you out of here, else you'll die on this fraking table."

Rain pattered on the roof and the window panes of the apartment, making a sound that most would find calming, if not down right sleep-inducing. Nirium, on the other hand, found it just the opposite. It was maddening. She stood at a window inside her room, wrapped in a towel, holding aside the sheer curtain that gave her some privacy from the outside world. Still fresh from a shower, beads of moisture ran down her lithe body, ignored by her for the most part. A wet tendril of hair fell into her face and she brushed it back behind one ear. She was deep in thought, her expression one that she wore only in private.

Jared was not there to see her, and therefore, she let her more morose feelings show through. It was the rain that had brought them on, and it was the reason that she hated the rain. Today wasn't *The Anniversary*. It wasn't long off though; *The Anniversary* pained her, and she figured that it would continue to pain her for the rest of her life. After a long moment, she turned away from the window and let the curtain drop back into place.

Off came the towel; it landed on the single bed not far from her on her left. Nirium took up a bottle of sweetly scented lotion from the dresser to her right and poured some of it into her hand. As she spread it on her still moist skin, her thoughts drew her deeper into her private misery.

"Ara, Jared," she whispered. "I hope that you get out of this hell hole soon."

She frowned at herself and settled down on the edge of the bed to spread the lotion on her long legs. Despite the fact that she was considered plump, Nirium was well toned and flexible. Several months had passed since she'd met Jared for the first time, and since then, they had become good friends. Or so she liked to think.

Giska, after discovering that his newest death match fighter was staying with one of his better dancers, had been delighted. At first, being together had only been a means of keeping an eye on him when he was wounded, as it had been that first week they knew each other. Then, it became a job from Giska himself. A way to spy on him, to know if he was going to run, or worse, rat that slemo bothan out. All this in return for never having to turn another trick again. It was an opportunity that she hadn't been able to refuse. She just prayed that Jared was smarter than that.

So, she suggested at one point during their short relationship that he move in with her. It was natural, since he was staying with at her apartment more often than he was staying at his own. He had accepted and they had moved together to a nicer side of the Blue Sector. They had their own separate rooms now, but Nirium had to admit that she missed sharing a bed with him.

Not that he ever tried anything with her, aside from occasionally throwing an arm across her midsection in the middle of the night and cuddling close.

She smiled at that thought as she replaced the bottle of lotion on her dresser and pulled on a lacy set of smallclothes and on top of that, a loose pair of pants in a soft gray color and a light blue tee shirt that clung to her curves. Her feet were left bare. Nirium picked up a brush that sat next to her lotion and began to drag it through her thick, tangled tresses. She winced once or twice, but never made a sound.

Her thoughts still revolved around her private pain. She couldn't help it. *The Anniversary* was coming up, and it was just around the corner. Three weeks, and she feared that she'd have to relive the pain of that day. She'd have to work, too, she knew, and Jared would no doubt be in the rings. That, of course, just made everything worse.

"Ara, give me strength. If you can't lend me strength, then at least lend me some sanity for the coming days."

Her brush worked quickly, and her fingers even more quickly. In a few moments, a thick, still damp braid hung down her back, secured by a bit of string. After a moment, she stood up from the bed and moved into the sparsely furnished living area. There were five doors there; one led to the main hallway, one led to the fresher, one to Jared's room, one to hers, and the last one, made of clear glass led to an uncovered patio of sorts.

She walked over to this one and opened it, then stepped outside into the drizzle. The rain hit Nirium's skin, bathing her anew; she stood there like a fool and let it wash over her as she stepped up to the flimsy railing and leaned against it, her gaze far off as she watched for Jared to come home. A sudden thought came to her; it was peaceful and it made her smile.

For once, Nirium didn't mind the rain.
