Jared stepped off the transport and breathed in the air of Coruscant, he had been cooped up in a ship for almost a week now do to his transport from Kuat to here but at the moment his mind was on business as he headed down to grab a speeder bike if he could; he nodded to one of the sergeants who was manning the hoverpool.**"Need a speeder bike Sergeant, any up to snuff?"** The Sergeant looked at Jared for a moment and then motioned to a pair of speederbikes that were still shining at the moment.**"Fresh out of the crate Major, just aching to be broken in properly.""**

It didn't take Jared long to saddle himself upon the back of the new XR-15 Shadowhawk speeder, he felt the flightgrade repulsors hum to life and the low whine of the controls; it had been nearly a year since he'd last rode one. His hands wrapped around the controls before sliding them foreward, the bike screamed out away from the huge landing facility of the imperial army upon coruscant. Jared weaved through traffic causing a few hovertaxi driver's to shout obscenities in their native tongue which he didn't hear as his booted foot flicked downwards sending the speederbike towards it's limit quickly.

Soon enough he was settling the speeder bike down in one of the landing areas before pulling out the keycard and sliding it into his inside coat pocket, from his back pocket came his uniform hat; he looked up at the great palace before striding towards the entrance way. He was there to get the flags and medals for those who had fallen of his unit, it was something he hoped to never do but knew it had to be done.

As if she had read his thoughts before he had even thought them, Diandra appeared to his left at the next requisition window. Truth was, despite herself, she liked Jared. He had guts, and that impressed her, though she would never admit it to herself or to anyone else, either.

Giving him another small, warm smile, she headed for the lifts again to go back to her office. Lunch was waiting and she was eager to get her work done so that she could go home on time.
