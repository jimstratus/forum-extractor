Jared had only a few days before he was shipped off to Fondor to take command of the Fondorian Task Force, it seemed so odd to him that they would assign him a large force given his specialty was small unit actions and covert operations; Of course he hadn't had time to look over the Task Force's dossier. He had spending most of it with Diandra given how shaken she with what had happened to her on Rhen Var. But at the moment he was in the command training dojo, he knew even though he was being given a command which meant a desk that he'd be on the front lines whenever he could. Jared was never one to sit by idly and let others fight in battles he knew he should be in as well.

He wore a simple pair of bdu pants and a grey tank top as he worked on his striking; his left hand was still partially bandaged from the abuse it had taken on his last op. But never the less, he worked through the pain and used it to focus his mind as he stuck and jabbed the training unit; it wasn't like fighting a force user or even another well training individual but it helped to keep his skills sharp. As he worked the unit his mind drifted back to the conversation that he had with Cognatus and his innate ability. War was on the horizon and by Jared's own assumption the republic needed whatever edge it could get given the way they had been defeated nearly without a loss by enemy forces at Rhen Var.

His train of thoughts distracted him long enough for the training unit to land a solid blow to his midsection which sent the Admiral reeling backwards for a moment before he quickly spun and lashed out with his good leg before following it with a hard blow to the solar plexus section of the unit which disabled the unit for the moment, He hadn't seen dene since the attack on his manor but he had seen reports that the warlord was safe. But still Jared was bothered by the rather precision attacks by the still mysterious enemy they faced, he had stationed two of his old unit on a permanent security detail for Diandra and also to keep him appraised of her condition given one of them was Horns and the other was Bit.
