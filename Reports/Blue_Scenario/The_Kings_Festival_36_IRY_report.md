**KINGDOM OF KUAT**

His Supreme Majesty High Prince James Stratus and their Royals Highness Prince Marc Stratus and Princess Tavria Treyson cordially invite you to the annual King's Festival at the Kuat Royal Palace, a long-time honored tradition that began with King James Stratus the First (the High Prince's grandfather and former King of Kuat).
