**Armed Forces Command**

**Coruscant**

Dene was in his office, taking the day to catch up on paper work. He was distracted by the thought of food, he was starving. He shook it off as he filed his reports, he looked on the floor next to him, his pet Tigger, the tuskcat lay purring, fast asleep. **"Lazy, just lazy. What am I paying you for?"** He joked with the cat, its yellow eyes looking at Dene curiously before heading back into a daze.

A moment later Dene's eyes got big when he noticed a "Urgent" folder sitting under the stack of papers. He quickly opened up. His left eyebrow went to the roof. "What in the holy prince is this?" Dene pointed the papers at the cat and then tossed them on the table. He read off the topic, **"Dene Cognatus, head of Kitchen Staff."** He sighed and laid his head on the table. **"Lame."**

A few moments later he threw them into an incinerator and looked around to see if anyone noticed, he grinned. He noticed another document on his table. **"Dene Cognatus, is still head of Kitchen Staff."** He sighed and looked around once more before burning it. Successful once more Dene shifted his eyes around the room. Not able to detect any other scrupulous documents.

He walked to his secretary's desk. **"Mary, I am going out for lunch, take care of Tigger will you, he needs one fresh recruit every three hours."** Dene smiled. Mary began to freak but Dene stopped her and pointed her to the bag of 'cat food.' He chuckled. **"Ok?"** He said. She nodded **"Yes Head Chef."** and went back to her busy work. Dene stopped for a moment, **"What did you say?"** he looked at her furious. **"I said yes sir."** she said. **"....Ok..."**

Dene grabbed a steak sandwich and some water and sat down in the Command Center *of the* restuarant, *of which he was Head Chef and head of Kitchen Staff. He always dreamed of being in the military. That's why he wore a chef-general uniform and made his workers where similar attire. Those loyal to him called him General, and those he did not care for called him Chef Cognatus, others simply called him boss. And what a pain in-the-ass boss he was.*
