Tears of anger cutting down his face, he pushes deeper through the woods. His mind racing faster than his legs could carry him, *\*How could our families have given up so easily? Shae is ALIVE! I have to find a way to bring her home.\**

Bursting out from the tree line he finds himself amongst the buildings of Aloriya, the neighboring city to the Capital. Forcing himself to stand and walk upright, he put on a more noble air as he walked through the countless Nekoisans. Pressing forth through the crowds, he starts making his way toward the space port, hoping to use his name to help him off planet he steps inside. Stopping for a moment at the banking kiosk, he withdraws as much as he can without setting off the security alerts before continuing to the ticket broker.

Forcing a smile, he steps up to the counter, **”One for the station if you would please.”**

**”Okay sir, I’ll just have to verify your passp……”** The young Nekoisan woman’s voice trailed off as she looked up at whom she was servicing. **”…I…I’m sorry your highness. I’ll have you booked immediately.”**

**”Please, it’s okay.”** Liudahl tried unsuccessfully to calm her down as she hurriedly shuffled through the paperwork to get him aboard. Cursing herself inaudibly, she pushed a digital code sheet through the small opening, her hand shaking enough that it made it slightly difficult to grab. **”I thank you miss.”** Once he had a hold on his pass, he turned toward the indicated port and boarded the large transport, taking a seat in the back, hoping to draw as little attention as he can, though it wasn’t hard with the sparse population of the ship.

Gazing through the small viewport next to his seat, he watched the various colors of the fading jungle, the late morning sky and finally atmosphere as they broke into the dark night of space. While his heart told him that he was moving in the right direction, he couldn’t help but regret leaving his family. Under his breath he spoke the short prayer that his father had taught him and his brother during their first hunt:

**“Maker, grant Thy protection**

**And in protection, strength**

**And in strength, understanding**

**And in understanding, knowledge**

**And in knowledge, balance**

**And in balance, the love of of all existences.”**

As he continues, his eyes begin to well up, **”I’m sorry Taad, Maamr; this is something I have to do. Someday I hope to return to you, but until then, please be safe.”** The planet far below looks like a mere spec as his view swings around where his destination fills the viewport. Letting out a heavy sigh, he watches as the station gets closer and eventually surrounds him as they pass into a large landing bay. As soon as he feels the transport touch down, he stands ready to disembark along with the handful of other passengers.

As he stepped out into the station he could see that there was little done here other than trade. It was almost more of a place to stop and refuel than anything else, though there were a few places involved in active trade. Thankfully there was even a small shop that carried the essentials for crewmen and pilots that worked the various ships. Watching around to make sure no one had raised attention to him, he ducks inside. After gathering up a few things, a spare change of clothes, some traveling rations and duffel to put them in, he took them to the counter. Spreading out the credits to pay for what he’d already placed on the counter, he nodded his head at the display case behind the clerk, **”How much for one of the knives up there?”**

**”They’re thirty credits for the smaller ones, fifty for the larger.”**

**”One of the larger ones then please.”** Counting out another fifty credits, he slides everything into the duffel, folding the knife into its handle. Throwing the bag over his shoulder, he waves his thanks to the clerk and starts walking through the station until he comes across a fully equipped fresher. Hanging the bag on a small hook next to one of the showers, pulling out the knife he stepped inside, carefully making sure that the water wouldn’t come on. He folded out the knife and rubbing his thumb across the blade checked to see that it was sharp enough, then grabs a chunk of his long mane, sliding the blade underneath and up until he had roughly sliced through. Knowing that he needed to changed his appearance, it still caused him to shiver as his hair released and fell to the floor, he continued cutting until the white hair signifying his nobility was cut down to little more than a scraggly flat top.

Folding the knife, he slips it into the waistline of his trousers before shaking violently to knock off any strands of the freshly cut hair. He folds and places his tunic into the bag as he scoops it up, stepping over in front of a wall of reflection panes where he again pulls the knife blade out, taking it to the short patches on his chin and jaw line and trimming them back to short stubble. He turns from side to side, appraising himself of his work, **”Not exactly the best I’ve looked, but it’ll have to do, at least until I get away from Nekia familiar space.”**

Stepping back out into the main area of the space station, he makes his way back toward the landing bay with the hopes of cutting out the middleman and digital trail involved in buying passage. As he steps in, it soon becomes apparent that he had missed many of the leaving ships, as there were only a few still parked aboard, one lifting off even as Liudahl stepped out onto the decking. There was a ship toward the magnacon field that still had its loading ramp down, a pair of Chiss worked to gradually load the handful of crates as he approached. As one of them returned to the ramp from inside, he notices Liudahl, quickly drawing a Charric and aiming it at him, stopping him short a good half dozen meters away.

**”Ho friend, I only ask to be allowed to accompany you to your next destination.”** The Chiss looked at him questioningly, motioning with the blaster for him to leave them. Slowly he reaches into his trouser pocket to bring out a handful of cred chips, holding them in his outstretched hand for them to see.

The other Chiss soon appeared standing next to his cohort, muttering something he assumed to be in their language before turning to him, **”A thousand credits will get you as far as Csilla, and you have to work too.”** He swung his hand out over the stacks of crates surrounding the ship.

**”I have no problems with that.”** He steps closer, tossing his bag onto one of the crates before squatting low to pick it up. His arms straining against the weight, he feels his claws extend into the wooden bottom of the crate. As he steps past the two Chiss into the ship, he lets the crate slide into place with the stacks of others and starts toward the ramp again where the two others were pushing stacks up into the ship with a hover cart. With the extra help from Liudahl, the ship’s cargo area is soon piled high with crates, leaving only a small walk space to get from the ramp to the access hatch for the rest of the ship. As the Chiss who’d first pulled the blaster hit the control to raise the ramp, he shot Liudahl an approving nod, the engines firing up where the other Chiss, apparently the pilot, was getting the ship warmed up and ready for departure. Climbing through the hatch, he makes his way to a small lounge area, setting his bag in the corner of the small couch; he sits down and rests his head against the back. Peeking through the entryway into the cockpit, he watches as the inside of the landing bay disappears to starlight and then bursting into streams of light as he feels his first pull of hyperspace.

## Post 2 by Liudahl Oura on 2011-08-13T08:24:00Z



Nausea tugging at his insides, Liudahl remains seated firmly on the couch, his eyes clenched tight to help qualm his stomach. Just as the feeling had started to subside and risk slitting his eyes open, what little of the forward viewports he could see reverted back in to tiny pinpricks of light. The compensators did what they could, but as the gravity fields shifted again, he could feel himself start to lurch as he struggles to keep down what little was contained in his stomach. **”Ugh, I hope it isn’t always like that.”** As the ship slides into place on a cargo platform, he wipes a bit of saliva from his lip as he stands and prepares to help unload before bidding the Chiss adieu.

Once the ship has been emptied, he counts out the credits to the assumed captain who seems very pleased at their recent profits. As the ship pulls away from the platform, Liudahl turns and disappears into the crowd, pushing his way through the various unfamiliar faces. His stomach begins to rumble, having not eaten since before he left home, he shifts his attention to finding something to eat. Struggling through the signs all written in Cheunh, he manages to decipher one that appears to hang over a small diner.

As he steps inside, he’s met by a server droid who greets him in common, and seats him along the inner wall. **”What would you like?”** Trying to keep his regal air suppressed, he looks at the droid, **”Bring me whatever the soup of the day is.”** The droid nods, making some sort of acknowledged whistle as it toddles off to place the order. Liudahl allows himself to relax a bit as he looks around, taking in the sights he thinks to himself *\*Sure is a lot different than the palace. What they tell you in class fails to really describe how things are.\** In a matter of a few moments, a hearty sized bowl of an aromatic, meaty stew is placed before him on the table. His attention changing to his freshly delivered meal, he fails to notice a brightly colored Houk sitting near the exit, staring at him over the top of a large mug of steamy liquid.

Finishing the last few bites of his stew, Liudahl stands and makes his way toward the counter where he promptly pays and steps back out into the bustling street, stopping for a moment to decide which direction to go. Though he constantly feels a tugging from within, he’s unsure of exactly where to go to follow that pull. Finally settling on a path, he turns to start back toward the space port, hoping to catch some news as to what to expect. Turning a corner he steps out of the crowd, the pathway here less used. Happy to have found some open ground, he lengthens his stride hoping to cover faster ground, but a thwack to the head stops him short. As he turns to check what hit him, he comes face to face with the Houk from the diner, or more appropriately two of them as his vision doubles, goes blurry, then dark as he falls heavily to the ground. **” ’Et’s get yer loaded up b'for som'n sees.”**

## Post 3 by Liudahl Oura on 2011-09-05T11:05:23Z



Roughly scooping Liudahl up, the Houk grunts heavily, flinging the limp form over his shoulder. He casually continues toward the spaceport, **”I tol’ yer yer shoulda stopped long timgo! Yer nev’r could hol’ yer liquor, why ed you start soarly anyway? What’m I goin to do wit yer?”** Continuing the overplayed charade through the light crowd at the port, the Houk pushes through to a private hangar bay. As the portal closes behind them the façade drops, **”Oi, ‘at was a pain in th’arse.”**

Feeling the pull of gravity shift through the darkness, Liudahl struggles with his mind trying to regain consciousness as he’s hastily tossed upside down. Hearing the gruff sound of the Houks voice cut through into his mind, he can’t help but to wonder what he’s going on about. Only subtly aware of those around them, he tries to reach out to one of them, but finds his arms unmoving. The swinging movement of his abductor’s walking forces his head to swim, stopping for a moment as the hum of the lift rings in his ears. Finally feeling himself start to come around, the gravity tugging at his body shifts again as he falls to the floor, plummeting deeply into darkness again as his head bounces from the floor plating.

Crossing over to the lone ship centered in the hangar, he punches a small panel underneath which triggers a small loading lift to drop. As he steps aboard, he hits the retract button, causing the platform to slowly rise back into place. Once he clears the floor, he steps forward toward a row of force cages and keys open the nearest. Dumping Liudahl to the floor inside, he smiles before reclosing the cell, **”Aint sure what yer are, but yer’ll make ol’ Kaine alotta cred.”** Smiling, he turns and disappears and in a matter of minutes, the floor begins vibrating as the engines roar to life. The Houk can be heard talking through into the cell bay, though his voice is barely more than a mumble, it could easily be assumed that he is talking with the port authorities for flight clearance. Seconds later the small freighter shakily lifts from the deck and turning cuts through the magnacon field into the darkness of space.

As the ship cuts exits the hanager, it angles toward the edge of the system; a high pitched whine cutting through the air as they surge into hyperspace. Satisfied with his jump programming, Kaine steps into the room just outside the cell bay where Liudahl lay slumped in a pile. His loud footsteps can be felt as they echo throughout the ship as he bustles about the room before paying Liudahl another visit. He squared up to Liudahl’s cage and takes a long drag from black bottle, **”Still sleepin’ eh?”** He kicks the cage shell, sending sizzling arcs of energy across the barrier field, **”Guess I ‘on’t know meown strenth. Well yer best wake up b’fore we gets to Selvaris.”** He takes another drink as he steps moves down the row of cells, **”An ‘ows our other ‘guest’ t’day? Comfy?”** His guffaw rings against the metal plating of the walls, a bit of his drink sputtered from his mouth toward the other cage before sizzling into vapor.

A raspy voice rises from the lump of rags on the cage floor, **”Why don’t you jump out an airlock.”** The dusty pile stirred as his head rose to glare through the energy field at their captor who laughed in return, throwing back his head.

**”B’then ere’d be no one left ta fly this crate. Yer’d bein a hellofa pickle then eh?”** Launching into another deep belly laugh, his taunting is cut short by a loud chirp echoing from the cockpit and through the various comm speakers in each room. **”Wellp, Iguess our time isat ‘n end.”** A low rumbling belch erupts from Kaine’s gullet as he turns to disappear into another part of the ship.

As the fog begins to lift once again, the thundering belch resonates against his head creating a loud buzzing within Liudahl’s skull. Slightly stirring, he forces his eyes to slit open for a second, quickly closing them against the light created by the energy field of his cage. As he shifts his position in attempt to stand, he leans a bit too hard against the field causing the fur on his elbow to singe and fill the small area with a light haze of smoke. As he retracted his arm, he felt the momentum rapidly slow, tipping him once again into the sizzling cage door before he can shift into a more stable stance. Still resting on his knees, he braces himself against the floor with one hand, gingerly examining the back of his head with the other. Wincing, he traces a gash the length of his finger along the upper side, accidentally brushing away some of the clotting where it had started to scab over. Steadying himself against the back wall, he holds his wound closed with one hand, speaking more to himself than anything, **”Where am I?”**

Flashes of his short time on Csilla fill his mind’s eye before settling on the colossal Houk. Slowly the foggy memories of being carried aboard a freighter return before his reverie is cut short as a gravelly voice rings out not far away, **”A quick flight to indentured service I’m afraid, and not the voluntary kind.”** The form on the floor a few cells down stirs once again, stretching to full height, the rags falling into place as his tattered clothing. He shuffles as close to Liudahl’s cell as he can without touching the barrier, his long inclined head wobbling around in attempt to see through the blurred view of stacked energy fields. **”You’ve never heard of Kaine? You must not be from the usual pickings. Kind of suprising really, I would imagine you’d be missed; not being a junker.”**

*\*Junker? Kaine?\** **”There’s no one to miss me. I doubt at least.”** Straining to see through the distortion, he struggles against the light in attempt to see whom it is he was conversing with. **”How long was I out? The way my head pounds; it feels like quite a while.”**

**”I’m not completely sure, but it’s only felt like a few hours since we took off.”** The co-captive shuffled around a bit again before starting, **”I’m called Chindo”**

Kaine’s voice can be faintly heard rumbling from the cockpit as he clears his ship to land. A few moments later as the ship passes through the atmosphere and low enough for the reflected blues and greens of the rapidly passing oceans and forests, the colors dance across the opposing wall of the cell block, though they fall muted by the yellow glow of the energy barriers. Another minute or so has them landing in a small clearing near the edge of a city largely hidden in the trees, the buildings and walkways wedged tightly between the trees along the forest floor.

The light faded away as the ship dipped beneath the canopy to touch heavily on the duracrete pad followed by the loud crack of arcing electricity as Kaine cut the engines, the only sound audible now is a low hum from the cell generators. As the loud thumping of footsteps grows louder, Chindo can be heard shuffling back into his pile of rags. Kaine, filling the doorway, looks with great self satisfaction at his stock. **”Well; Iguess yer d’cided ta wakeup aftrall. Good! Yer looks better fo th’ buyers ‘at way.”**

Liudahl turns to face his captor, finally clear headed, **”Let me free! My p….”** Thinking back to what teachings he’d had about hostage situations and slavery, he changed his thinking about revealing his heritage. *\*It’ll only give me a higher price tag.\** **”I need to be let free.”** His voice changed from commanding to being more negotiating. Kaine chuckled lightly at the ill formed attempt at bargaining for freedom, **”Oh I’ll let yer go aight; for the right price.**

Anger boiling up inside, Liudahl balled his fist, slamming it against the inside of the barrier causing his knuckles to crack and begin to bleed, the outer ends of his fur smoking into singed curls as he emphasized is his demand, **”You WILL let me free!”** His outburst eliciting little more than a hard laugh as Kaine turned his back to his captives, calling back over his shoulder, **”Don’ yer go an’wheres.”** The self appraised genius of his retort causing him to break out in increasing laughter as he disappeared down the loading platform.

Walking into the tree line, Kaine wound his way down a familiar path to his favorite cantina, the Karoth’s Mir. Waving at the man behind the counter, he’s met with a smile and a nod toward the back corner where the light seems not to reach. One of the waitresses soon slid a short, fat glass of bright, nearly glowing yellow liquid onto the table with a wink before hustling off to serve the handful of other patrons. As he took his first drink, a small human slid into place across the table carrying a similar glass of thick brown liquid. **”I understand you’re in a very specific area of trade. . . “** Their conversation continues loudly over several more drinks, the two seeming more and more as friends than members of a business deal.

Still fuming, Liudahl searches his cage for anything he might be able to break into to work himself free, though from the apparent state of the ship and quality of the cells, he can tell where most of his funds were going. Finding little else to try, he braces himself against the solid portions of the back corner and lifts himself up to pull open the light panel in the ceiling. Breaking the light open, he yanks on what wiring he can get in his grasp, stripping away the wrapping with his claw to expose the bare metal. **”Here goes nothing.”** The commotion caused by his trying to escape at this point caught the attention of his fellow captive who attempted to watch from his cell, adding only a pessimistic **”Good luck.”** before slinking back into a pile.

Shaking his head at his co-captive’s cynicism, he starts twisting the wires together, connecting the exposed areas to test several different combinations. The first sending sparks through the other cages as the lights in each top explodes into a shower before flickering into darkness. Another try seemed to boost the energy to the barriers, their hum growing in volume and the opacity increasing to a near solid state. Combination after another producing varying affects, many that remained unseen from within the cell area before he finally dropped back to the floor with a deep sigh. Hearing Chindo stir again, Liudahl cut him off, **”Not a word. . . “**
