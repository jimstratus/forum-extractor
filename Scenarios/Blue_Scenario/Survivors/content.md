## Post 1 by Jared Quinn on 2010-09-26T21:01:55Z



Jared had busy since delivering Diandra back to Coruscant, the official vote for war had meant the republic military complex went from an active standby to full alert and units were being brought up and mobilized for the coming days. Jared himself had been busy with his new unit, it meant a lot of changes including his handler and what his duties were. Gone were the days of him just training with the soldiers he'd fought with for so long, they were sent out to other units as executive officers or command officers at his request. He had been promoted in a quick ceremony and given his new assignment, it was something so far off the grid that even IRIS and Commandant Cognatus didn't have access to all of the files.

He had thrown himself into his training and preparation for the upcoming operation since it meant going after the Sith again, something Jared was more then prepared for; He wanted blood and would extract it by blaster or fist if it came down to it. He hadn't seen Diandra since he'd officially delivered her to the royal palace so she could be under guard by the royal guard as well as by two of his unit who he had given orders to watch over her, Alot had happened since their vacation on Spira and most of it bad.

After hitting the showers once he finished his workout routine and training Jared found himself shrugging on his uniform coat over a simple black shirt and a pair of BDU pants before he headed out into the light drizzle that was falling over the city sector, it made the great buildings shimmer and glisten as they caught the lights of the city. He was headed towards one of the many parkways since he needed to head towards the civilian sectors, his destination was one filled with Senators and high ranking officials within the government. It took him the better part of two hours until he finally arrived at the apartment block he had in mind, security was especially high at the moment within the sector. A pair of IRSB officers moved to stop him from entering the block given his uniform coat didn't carry any rank badges on it given his latest assignment; that was unit he flashed his security badge and they allowed him in easily enough.

The ride up the lift was short and he was alone for most of it except for one of the Corellian senators who had shaken his hand during a Gala a few years ago. Jared knew of the man and knew which sectors he represented, the senator had tried to make small talk with Jared given how Jared had become a sort of poster child for what Treasure row citizens could become. He had no interest in speaking with the politician and made it apparent as he leaned in one corner of the lift until the lift stopped at his floor, from there he headed down the long hallway where a pair of black encased soldiers stood at alert. **"At Ease men."**

With that Jared knocked on the door twice before he ran his hand through his wet hair and sighed slightly, he had been giving Diandra some time to recoup after all that had happened to her. Now he needed to speak with her and explain he would be gone for a bit and he couldn't tell her anything about what he was doing, he hoped she would understand given the situation and the climate in the republic.

## Post 2 by Diandra Devin on 2010-10-11T03:38:20Z



Diandra was still rather... shaken up over everything. She had a right to be, too. Undoubtedly the apartment complex that she lived in was heavily guarded now that she was home and safe. Even Nirium had needed to go through several extensive background checks. At the moment, she was out, having known that Jared would be coming to spend some time with Diandra. At his knock, Diandra checked the viewscreen outside her door.

"What's the password?" She says, her voice shaking. She sounds frightened -- if only because she's still not quite used to being home. It doesn't help that she also fears that maybe... just maybe this might all be a dream, another hallucination. Maybe she was still on Rhen Var, in that seemingly doorless cell. Diandra doesn't open the door until Jared gives a password. If he doesn't give the pass, he's got a barrage of questions to answer that only he would know the answer to. And if he can't answer them... well, he might not be getting in to see her at all.

## Post 3 by Jared Quinn on 2010-10-11T03:56:58Z



He sighed as she asked for the password, he leaned against the door with his head against the cold Durasteel of the door that separated Jared from Diandra; His hand reached up and tapped the com button for a moment.**"DQ."** He hoped that was the right password given his exhaustion and want to just lay out and tell Diandra everything would be alright, but he also knew he couldn't guarantee everything would ever be alright again.**"Diandra, it's me Jared."**

He looked up at the remote camera so she could see it truly was him, his worry lines were clear as day around his eyes and mouth. He also had a air of exhaustion and weariness that seemed to be bone deep at the moment, the look came from the fact that he'd seen her taken away and then found her as he had.
