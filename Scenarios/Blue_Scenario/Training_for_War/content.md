## Post 1 by Jared Quinn on 2014-03-12T19:53:38Z



It had been barely a month since the events on Rhen Var and the republic was still preparing for war, at least that was what most civilians believed. In truth the more clandestine arms of the Republic had been busy at work including Admiral Quinn and the unit he'd built for such operations. But now he was back on coruscant and staring at the cylindrical device that sat in a case on his desk, the words Dene had spoken to him aboard the prince's ship about training still rang in his ears. It had remained in the back of his mind and he knew with the sith returning the republic would need all the help they could get, of course it meant stepping away from the fight like he normally did.

But it was something he knew he needed to do so he could protect the one's he cared for, finally he looked away from the case and then tapped his desk com transceiver and brought up it's virtual Rolodex; within moments he found the name he was looking for and then had the unit send a signal. He didn't know if Cognatus would pick up or not, but finally he heard the sound of it going to a recorded message. Jared waited an then sighed as it finally beeped to signal it was ready to record.**"Dene, it's Jared. I've been thinking about what you said on the ship; Your right and I'd like to meet and discuss training."**

With that he cut the transmission before he settled into the chair behind his desk to continue his day to day duties, he wondered if he would have been able to protect Dia rather then allowing her to be abducted aboard the ship when they'd been assaulted. Of course he shook his head and forced the thoughts from his mind, doubt meant questioning things better left unasked.

## Post 2 by Dene Vye Cognatus on 2014-03-17T04:16:16Z



Dene had retired from military life, he had served the Imperial Republic for nearly a decade. His career originated as an Interim position as Army Chief of Staff when he was a Brigadier General. He had worked up to eventually lead the Imperial Republics most diverse, critical and most advanced military ever seen in its history. Dene was overseeing the rebuilding of his personal manor that was nearly destroyed when Sith assassins blew it up. Dene was ensuring the contractors were building everything per his personal design when he heard his holo-recorder ringing. He couldnt get to it in time but he quickly checked the recorded message. It was his Executive officer and successor who left the message, he pressed the button. Jared appeared in holo-form **"Dene, it's Jared. I've been thinking about what you said on the ship; Your right and I'd like to meet and discuss training."**

A smile fell across his face. He pressed the record button **"Jared, good to see you, very well meet me at the jedi temple ruins, dont bring anything or anyone."** Dene prepared his speeder and headed to the Jedi temple, the abandoned ruins of the once mighty Jedi council. About half an hour later Dene had landed at the temple pads and went into the chamber, the halls were dark and dusty. Dene crept into the shadows awaiting Jared.

## Post 3 by Jared Quinn on 2014-03-18T01:41:35Z



Jared received Dene's response in short order on his communication's suite, he watched the holo and sighed slightly. The old ruins had been abandoned for quite some time and he didn't plan on going unarmed, he picked up his sidearm and strapped the weapon to his hip before he grabbed his uniform cap. As he stepped out of his office he looked over at his secretary. **"Miss Castillon, I'll be out of the office for the rest of the day. If it is absolutely urgent I will have my com on me."**

With that he made his way out of the massive military office complex and out into the surrounding base, his trip to the motor pool only lasted a few moments before he requisitioning a speeder bike; it'd nearly five years since he last rode one but they hadn't changed that much since he was a Recon soldier back on Fondor. It was around twenty minutes after Dene arrived that the Jedi Master would likely hear the familiar sound of the military grade speeder bike approaching before it landed next to his own personal speeder. Jared's familiar figure stood on the landing pad for moment before he began moving towards the dusty and rubble filled hallways of the Jedi Temple.**"Dene, I'm here as you asked."** Jared was still nervous given the ease at which the sith had nearly killed Dene as well as others under the royal guard's protection, his hand naturally drifted down the the butt of his recon pistol as he unhooked the strap across the back of the weapon incase he would need it.

## Post 6 by Dene Vye Cognatus on 2014-03-19T02:08:34Z



Dene was pleased, he had always felt Jared would follow down this path, he had forseen Jared's future, dark times were coming indeed. Dene felt like he had lost a lot when he was nearly killed. It was his worst injury he had ever received in all his years of war. **"I am pleased Jared, come with me."** Dene started to walk in front Jared entering into the dark halls. **"These halls once stood for something, right now the symbolism is dim but existant."** He reached for his lightsaber and lit the silver/blackish blade revealing a drawing, on the drawing showed a grand master, a master and several apprentices or knights re-entering the halls of the Jedi. A once sacred chamber that only the worthy could enter. Dene had only been once he had earned his place in the Jedi archive but he had been told that he would rebuild an order.

Dene walked to the chamber entrance, he stopped his lightsaber was reattached to his belt and he stood near the door, it was still sealed. Dust was proof that it had been years since it was opened. You must enter and if you are deemed worthy you will be accepted as a New Order Initiate. If you are deemed unworthy your powers, even though untrained will be drained from you permanently."** Dene said with a straight face, his hood covering up most of his face.
