***March 30th - A house on Coruscant***

\*Loke entered the center of the room along with Curt and Faroon, Loke started to speak\*

"You all have decided that the IR has not done justice for us, whether it be not enough money for the poor, lack of jobs in certain parts of the galaxy, or even if you just don't like Stratus, you have all come here tonight to support a Revolution, or as some would call us a rebbellion. Most of us outside of those who work in the IR are known as people of the political party *RAG* most of you because your smugglers, but you will no longer be called that, after July 1st you will be called the *Reform Faction* we shall proclaim it with our bombs, our action, and our fist. We will leak out intel reports on purpose as to tip off Stratus, Rishi shall be our target, most of you feel they are traitors for giving up the privateering industry. We shall blame them as a easy target and when Stratus does not look, we shall rip him up from the inside, all Intel Agents get a hold of every piece of information you can, we might get locked out after the blasts, our Spec Ops members have been notified of what to do, and we are planing our espionage as we speak. Not only will July be the month of our Revolution, but of our FREEDOM!!!"

\*The crowd cheered as Faroon and Curt looked at Loke's amazing leadership, he had always been able to command respect, but this was amazing smugglers and IR officers working together for a common goal, Faroona nd Curt were sure this might work\*

***Midnight July 1st - Coruscant IRSB HQ***

\*Loke Niamba is looking at data records as he orders one of his officers over to him\*

"Take this datacard to Agent Faroon, and tell Curt to go with the plans as set up" Loke said

"Yes sir" his officer replied

***12:05 July 1st - A street corner near the palace***

\*Curt walked up to the IRSB officer\*

"He got it already?" Curt said

"Yes, I was amazed myself, he must have gotten in the good with General Oho"

"Let me see it"

\*Curt put the datacard into the datapad and looked at the plans to most the ships and IRSB set ups, and even some added information like schedules for a few throne members as to track where they are at what time. Secretaries are very feeble minded he thought.\*

"ok let's go we don't have much time if we are gonna pull this off"

\*curt took off running down to a hover car at the end of a alleyway\*

***12:15 - The Senate***

\*A briefcase was sitting in the seat of the senator from Rishi, a relatively small planet that joined the IR only recently, and was known for its population of smugglers. As the Janitor came by her put the briefcase beneath the desk and entered the briefcase code activating a small bomb inside, not big enough to topple the building, but would definately cost the IR in rebuilding costs. As the janitor left the room he quickly left the building and hopped into curts hovercar which was waiting outside about 5 miles down the road the Senate glowed red as smoke fumed out. Curt looked over at the Janitor\*

"Good job, Faroon, here is the Datacard from Loke, he got us good information, shall I rally the intel and spec ops for part two of our plan?"

Faroon grinned "Yes, with these specs of the ships the IRSB staff and even these schedules everything should go nicely"

***12:30 - Palace***

\*Megan D'soto ran into Stratus' quarters and told him what happened at the senate, meanwhile Daniella's room was disturbed as Wadowski came in and reported the urgent news. As the alarms started sounding after Intel confirmed it was not a fire but a bomb explosion Layha awakened, as did General Oho, the two (while in seperate rooms) both received holovids of a intel agent telling them of what had happened in the senate, and what the speculations were. Loxxiv was asleep as the sound blared in his room one of his agents came running in and explained to him what had happened. Now with the Throne and Division leaders known there were only two left, Diego and Ieyena, their ships were messaged with a holovid from another intel agent explaining what had happened and that they make no rush to the planet, it was the same agent that had given Curt the intel information.\*
