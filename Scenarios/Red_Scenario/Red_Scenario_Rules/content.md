## Post 1 by James Stratus on 2004-02-03T19:05:23Z



A red scenario is defined as something that will be permanently affected. If you are in a red scenario, any events that happen in this scenario to you or around you will affect you in all future role-playing. If you die, you are dead - you have to start a new character and start over. If you are injured - etc, you are injured. If you lose your ship, you lose your ship.

If your friend dies, your friend dies.

Red Scenarios are done only when authorized and are always organized and every step and post must be pre-approved before it will display. (This forum is moderated - posts will not be displayed until approved by moderator).

You must signup to participate in Red Scenarios, post only when allowed and when appropriate - no side comments or side things taking away unless scheduled - use the "Discuss Scenario" topic for discussing it.

**Rules to be updated and revised as necessary. -**
