# Red Alert: The Rebel Situation

*Posted by General Tarkin - 2025-05-01*

The Imperial fleet has detected rebel activity in the Outer Rim territories. Initial reports suggest a small rebel cell has established a base on Dantooine. Emperor Palpatine has ordered an immediate response to this threat.

I have dispatched a scout team to verify the intelligence before committing our full forces. We cannot afford another wild bantha chase based on faulty intelligence.

---

*Posted by Colonel Veers - 2025-05-02*

Scout team Alpha has returned with confirmation. The rebel presence on Dantooine is larger than initially reported. They appear to have established not just a temporary outpost but a full operational base with defensive capabilities.

Recommend deploying the 501st Legion with air support to quell this rebellion before it spreads further into the sector.

---

*Posted by Emperor Palpatine - 2025-05-03*

The Senate must not learn of this rebel base yet. We will handle this situation with Imperial efficiency and discretion.

General Tarkin, you are authorized to use whatever means necessary to eliminate this threat. Ensure no survivors remain to spread their ideology of chaos.

The mission must be complete by the end of the standard week. I expect your report on my desk personally.
