## Post 11 by Ibram Chamberlain on 2009-02-03T03:27:04Z



(edited)

Ibram was lying in his bunk on the Coruscant garrison. It has not been long since the annual ball, and Ibram was still reminiscing about the night. Recently, he had received a package from his folks detailing on how he was missed and they hoped he was enjoying finally being able to be in the Army. There was a unique envelope from his uncle, and Ibram carefully opened it. In it, lay a small unsuspecting metallic piece. It simple looking, and it looked it had been worn through the ages. A silver chain that outshined its piece attached it. With the small package was a small note. It was handwritten, and the writing was precise and neat. Ibram read the lines, and did so many times. Like a relic, he slowly picked up the seemingly obscene piece. This little object was in Ibram's hands but they felt seemingly numb. This pendent belonged to Ibram's grandfather's father, and Ibram's grandfather wore it all the time his uncle said. He had given it to his brother-in-law at his death, so it had been in the other family's possession taken care by his Uncle Gavin. Gavin wrote that he forgot to give Ibram the piece, and said it brought good luck, and that Ibram would need it more than Gavin.

Ibram slowly pulled it on around his neck, and his fingers were trembling. Carefully he notched the chain around his neck and looked towards a mirror. It hung almost perfectly around his neck, and Ibram felt a little chill go up his spine. With a renewed sense of purpose, he got ready for the next deployment. He locked up his footlocker and got his pack together. All sat on his bunk at the Coruscant garrison, in a neat pile and arranged neatly. He pulled out his data pad and he had received more news on the deployment. However the 82nd's trip hadn't been scratched and Ibram was summoned to a Company meeting hosted by CPT. Casx. All Casx had to say to the group is that the 20th was temporarily shifted in deployment to some special training or something along those lines. Casx had finished the briefing with that all too recognizable finely groomed beard bearing his famous wry smile that said he was up to something, for he gave it on occasion that had importance. He had given the same smile when leaping out of the Navy landing ship when he jumped off the side into the unknown. With little ado, the 20th loaded up on a smaller transport and made its trip to a waiting fleet.

In his seat, along with his newfound comrades, Ibram grabbed the pendent and played with it in his hand. Soon it was revealed that this large warship was known as the ***Terror*** and it was one of prides of the Imperial Republic Fleet. This wasn't hard to grasp, it was majestic and powerful upon first glance. Many wide-eyed soldiers, including Ibram gawked upon the impressive size of the ship. Slowly, the transport docked in the internal bay and the troops of the Big 2-0 Company, 82nd "Indefatigable" Brigade unloaded into the mass of troops. Ibram just followed Casx while he argued with the Navy officers on board. This was typical, but it was also entertaining. So, the big 2-0 waited on board the ***Terror*** awaiting where to go.
