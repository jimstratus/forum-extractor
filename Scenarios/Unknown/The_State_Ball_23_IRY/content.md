## Post 3 by Revan Centurion on 2008-02-26T01:31:16Z



Revan sighed lightly to himself as he looked out across the Coruscant sky lanes. From the balcony of his quarters at the palace, Revan could see clear across the area known as Imperial City and well out towards the commercial district of Coruscant. Off to his right, he could see a sizable amount of the industrial district that was home to the vast majority of manufacturing interests on the planet. The sun had already set on Coruscant, and the brilliant array of lights that never seemed to be extinguished appeared as jewels in the distance. Dressed in an all black suit and a black overcoat with crimson lining, Revan Centurion stood in silence. It was the evening of a celebration, and he'd never been more confused and, in truth, concerned. While it was a happy occasion, the belated annual celebration of the Imperial Republic's inception, Revan was apprehensive. Military forces had somewhat recently returned for an extended operation in the Outer Rim, and a number of individuals had returned from the Hapes Cluster with Layha. Revan knew what had happened, but he'd not spoken a word of it...there was little reason to.

As he turned from the magnificent view of Coruscant, Revan was greeted by one of the royal guards. **"What is it?"**

"Message from one of our advance readiness units."

Revan grumbled slightly, knowing that the message was likely a very simple status update that had little bearing on anything. Still, the ARUs that had recently been instituted amongst the Royal Guard certainly had served an important purpose during the trials and tribulations of the recent past. So much had been accomplished...so many organizations swept away...so many good men and women lost. Perhaps it was one of the very few reasons that he committed so much of his being to this job. That and the fact that he knew how important it was to have a father you could look up to. After all, Revan never really had that. In fact, the closest thing to a father to him had attempted to kill him not more than six years ago. Revan never wanted to but Jayden in that position. **"Very well, anything critical or are things progressing as usual?"**

"Status is normal sir. I've had specifics routed to your datapad should you choose to glance at the full report later."

Revan nodded to the Protector. **"Very well, thank you."** Nothing else was said as the royal guard fell into step behind Revan. Once he had exited his quarters, he was joined by another royal guard. In silence, the three individuals walked the short distance to a private lift which would access Layha's quarters within the palace directly. Once they had indeed arrived, Revan motioned for the guards to wait by the lift. Walking down the long corridor, Revan arrived at the door leading inside Layha's quarters and knocked twice. **"Layha...are you seriously** ***still*** **not ready."** Revan smirked slightly to himself, but it was a nervous smirk. In an attempt to distract himself, he briefly ran a finger over the object in his right pocket.
