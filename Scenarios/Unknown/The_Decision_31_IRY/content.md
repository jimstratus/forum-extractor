## Post 2 by Jaylen Myst on 2009-11-21T16:36:25Z



Jaylen hissed silently in frustration as he replaced the archive file back into its proper place and proceeded to the next promising file was located. Travelling up the stairs to the second floor, he gazed at his surroundings in amazement. He was in the Jedi Archives, a location rumored to have the most information in the known galaxy. He shook his head and found his way to the correct aisle. He quickly found the correct file among the wall of color and headed to the closest station to where he could upload ad view the information. He proceeded to skim the contents for any hope of that jewel of information that had been eluding him.

Jaylen ended the file with a low growl and clenched teeth. He pounded his fist on the table in frustration. He took out the file, perhaps harsher than intended, and pushed the file back into it's proper place. He went to sit down in one of the corners which he had claimed on the top floor of the archives. The silence was soothing.

He rubbed his forehead as he felt most of the frustration leave him. He took a breathe as he walked to the railing to look down onto the few people milling through the electronic library; yellow eyes passing over a person who had just entered. He closed his eyes and exhaled slowly.

*Three weeks I've been at this. Three weeks on nothing. Nothing! Nothing on the name of a planet. Nothing on the name of a species. Nothing on the names of individuals.* As he was allowed, Jay would come to the archives to do research to see if he could find anything of value that would give him more detail of his past life. He found that he could remember everything except the details he truly wanted to remember. He knew the sound of each of their voices, yet had no picture to connect them to. They lived on a planet of land and water with both cities and countryside. It was...Adumar. Finally, he had something to go on. *I guess the three weeks weren't all wasted.*

He looked once again to the aisle and aisles of files. Suddenly weary, he chose to leave that thread for another day and became content to watch the few people on the lower levers go about their business.

## Post 3 by Selina Vadcasta on 2009-11-25T22:58:53Z



Selina narrowed her eyes as she walked through the Jedi Archives. It seemed that someone was greatly displeased and incredibly frustrated. She quickly replaced the item she'd claimed what seemed like ages before and silently sought the source of the frustration, moving swiftly but quietly past the endless rows of archived materials.

It didn't take her long to determine that she knew the source, at least enough to recognize his mind when she brushed it gently. Selina narrowed her eyes briefly as she wondered what he might be searching for in the Jedi Archives. Still, she wasn't rude, so she wouldn't pry into his mind to find out. Of course there was another reason that she would not invade his mind, for they seemed to be developing some trust and that was a great way to break it instantly, whether he was aware of her presence in his mind or not.

A few minutes later she came upon Jaylen, who seemed to have had enough for the day. She came from behind him silently and with a smile “Hello Jaylen.” She left the greeting short and without questions, for there would likely be plenty of time for that soon enough.
