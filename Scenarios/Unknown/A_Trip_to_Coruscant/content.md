## Post 1 by Sean Kennedy on 2004-04-22T04:48:07Z



\*Lord Kennedy walked to the command docking bay of the Centerpoint Space Station, secret research and shipyard facility of the Imperial Republic, unknown to even the other agents of the Throne save the Supreme Ruler and the Royal Guard. The red-robed male royal guards, unique to Centerpoint, the traditional style of guards that the Supreme Ruler did not allow into his service but allowed into the service of Lord Kennedy, flanked their lord to a waiting module that connected directly to his flagship, as always, the newest of its kind as it was constantly upgraded where they produced the things, an *Eclipse*-class Mark VI Super Star Destroyer. Since the space station was so huge and the home of everyone in the operation, the Super Star Destroyer, even though it was the biggest of its kind, could be docked directly and inside the station. Standing at the rampway, as per orders, was Lord Murashkin, Lord Kennedy's second in command of the station, and who would assume command during his trip.\*

"Lord Murashkin, I will be taking a trip to the Capital. You are in command until I return. Keep up the production and research as per schedule and as a matter of fact, step it up. I have heard rumours that His Majesty is retiring most of the Imperial design ships or overhauling them completely. I am going to participate in this and see what this is all about."

\*Murashkin raised an eyebrow.\* "Really? This will be interesting indeed, my lord."

"Indeed it will. Carry on." \*Lord Kennedy walked through the command transfer module to his flagship. It would make about twenty hops before making it into Coruscant and such to maintain secrecy of its origin. The *Prince's Pride* alone was hard to miss, and a ship that was even bigger than its predecessor was even harder to miss.
