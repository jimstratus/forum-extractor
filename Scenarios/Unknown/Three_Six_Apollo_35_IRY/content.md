After the wedding, the attendees remained in their seats so that the Royal Family could be safely escorted out after the newlyweds. The Jedi Guard moved in tandem with the steps of the High Prince and the Traditional Royal Guards. Disguised in the attire of the Royal Guards, they made eight in total, two up front, two in back, and two on each side of the Royal First Family members, all who carried their own lightsabers hidden. Leaving their armored shuttlecraft onto the main lawn of the Castle Treyson. The High Prince in center front, just behind a row of two guards walked down the beautiful castle grounds pathway to the castle entrance. Leaving the grand hall, Princesses Nicole, and Ashlee walked with their elder brother the High Prince James.

Walking down a dimly lit corridor, the entourage continued on the guarded route toward the ballroom. Though he did not sense any danger, James felt a sudden separation from the Force. Suddenly an automatic defense mechanism came online, meant to catch intruders, but was misbehaving and rapid turret fire opened on the Royal Family and their guards. Eight lightsabers were already activated deflecting turret fire and a waive of Royal Guardsman from the shuttle rushed forward like an Army with blasters firing. The High Prince's own lightsaber was activated, as was Marc's, Ashlee's, and Aimee's. The Royal Guardsmen began to rush their protectees towards an emergency bunker when suddenly the High Prince gasped and fell to his knees. From his pristine white uniform appeared an increasing blob of dark red blood. He had been stabbed, but not during the attack, in the chaos fleeing after.

**"Shields activate!"** shouted the leader. Rapidly activating his comlink, the Royal Guard captain hit the emergency transmission key and then shouted into it
