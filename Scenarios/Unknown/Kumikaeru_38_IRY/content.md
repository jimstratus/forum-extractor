Emperor James Stratus, sitting in his private inner office sanctum, a SCIF chamber that doubled as a meditation sphere, was trying to unravel the newest mystery of the latest intrusion into his mind. His mind barriers had always been strong, even during his days under the tutleledge of Lord Kennedy. However, there was only one person who could waltz right in without an invitation, at least through the initial few layers. This was because both he and this individual had built them together in training. It started happening months ago, though it was infrequent, as if someone was testing new equipment, newly obtained powers, or making a long distance call. Now they were more frequent, and instead of simple thought projections, she was having entire conversations with him, even to the point of interrupting him at work or in private when he was spending time alone with family. It was becoming damn inconvenient and, quite frankly, alarming. The last thing he needed was for people around him to start thinking their Supreme Ruler was acting odd.

*What's the rush?*

And then the connection was gone. The other Jedi around him had not detected her intrusions or made any mentions of feeling her presence. Her presence was a strong current that could be felt from across the galaxy, especially when it was rooted in a place of power. Only when she withdrew her own presence and had cloaked it purposely had it ever gone out, as it did several months after she resigned from her Throne high office, as his second-in-command. After that, her presence was never felt again, except on a few separate incidents and only to specific individuals. This time, he suspected, if anyone other than himself was feeling her reach, it would likely be the person to whom he was being urged to pay a visit.

*I need to get out of my own head. At least while I can anyway. Find some peace and quiet.*

Oddly enough, he found himself making private arrangements to make a surprise private appearance at the Imperial Ice Rink on Coruscant to watch a figure skating competition. An almost ancient form of art and dance, but something he used to like to watch on his homeworld of Kuat and on Hapes with her. Their short-lived love affair had long been over, though they had remained the best of friends for decades after. The bloodline of both of their Royal Houses even now continued on. He sent a quick message to Kaidlen and Megan, and his private secretary, telling them to clear his schedule for the next few days, and to send a small advance team to the Ice Rink, but not enough to signal that he would be the protectee that would be arriving, just enough to establish security. He also had the *Dawn Treader* moved from the Palace Throne Hangar to a large empty warehouse area. The part that they would not like. The Command Fleet would *not* be joining them. This was a *Black*-level project. Only those of his guard who had Black-level clearances would be joining them for this trip.

When the survivors of his family moved to Hapes, he kept up the skill. It was also a Hapan tradition, and because it was something his mother cherished, Jim wanted to honor her memory.
