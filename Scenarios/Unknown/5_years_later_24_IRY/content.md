The Palace seemed cold and dark. The halls that had once been so alive and bustling were now quiet and almost eerie. Layha made her way towards the Throne Room, two guards followed in close quarters. Her footsteps rung like a bell through the dark and eerie hall. Upon approaching the massive doors that lead to the Throne Room, Layha gave the guards a signal to stop and guard the doors. She alone entered the room and was greeted by only silence.

The Throne Room was rather large. Towards the west wall was a throne that symbolized her reign. It was made out of stone imported from the palace of old. The throne was supposed to be a reminder of what a ruler should be to its people. Layha paid no attention to the stone throne and walked over to the east side of the room. The side where the wall was windows that reached from floor to ceiling. She stood and gazed out over the capital planet known as Coruscant.

Five years... A total of 9 years since Jim had left on his so called crusade. Seven years since the Outer Rim Wars had begun. Six and a half years since her divorce to Kyle Katarn. Six years since the girls had gone missing and five years since she had become the Dark Master.

She ran her hand through her short locks and perused her lips. In the last five years she has completely taken over the IR. She had demolished the Senate and pulled the job of Supreme Chancellor. There was no longer a second Executor and no High Council. All that remained was the core of the Throne. Layha had put limitations on the Throne. It now consisted of the MoD Grand Admiral Overmind and Executor.

Over the last few years she had ordered a mass hunt down and slaughtered any opposing forces to the IR and her rule. In her defense she publicized her concern for the people and her right to see that the "bad guys" were forced to pay for there crimes. The Fringe were the first to be demolished. And to her ever lasting happiness, the DCC were all hunted down like prey and killed. Only Krayt remained, but nothing had been heard from him in years. Rebellions started by the Senate had been hushed and the people who were responsible were taken out.

A door opened and closed with a hiss. Layha said nothing as foot steps approached.

**"I do hope you have some useful information to report this time."** she didn't have to turn to know th officer feared for his life. She could feel it, almost taste it and it made her smile.

"Yes your Majesty. I was given an update on the Outer Rim Wars." Layha turned the smile gone.

**"And?"**

"The Grand Admiral has sent you a personal message. It is to be viewed by you alone." Layha nodded and grabbed the datapad he held out. **"What else?"**

"The Hapan's have not requested any sort of peace talk. In fact there has been no movement from within the Cluster that we can tell." Layha almost smiled. 30 years later and finally her Aunt had tried to steal her seat of power. Layha had reacted like some one squashing a bug. She had ordered a blockade around the Cluster. For six months nothing had gone in or out of Hapes as far as she knew. **"The witch is more resourceful than I gave her credit. She must have some sort of leak. Report to the Captain. Tell her to find that leak and cork it."** The Officer nodded. "One last thing, a message from an old friend." He handed her a holo-recorder. Layha took it and gave him a short nod. **"That will be all for now. You may leave."** he nodded, bowed and scurried from the room. Layha looked down at the recorder and her body began to tremble. She thumbed it on and an image appeared in the palm of her hand. Revan Centurion was in his usual attire of all black. Only his eyes stood out for they were a brilliant green. Layha felt as if those eyes where starring into her very soul.

"Layha," his voice made her shiver and reflexes took over. Layha turned and hurled the recorder at the nearest pillar. The small device hit the pillar and smashed into peaces. Anger rose inside her turning her body warm. She breathed in shallow breathes and fumed. She turned to the shattered recorder and felt both guilt and hatred. She hated the man for doing what he had done. *\*But that is in the past Layha. What is done it done. He isn't apart of your life anymore. He is dead to you.\** She turned from the shattered recorder and turned to the stone throne. Within a few strides she was seated on the Throne of power. In her hand was the report from Overmind. She quickly listened to his updates and familiarized herself with the latest strategies the Grand Admiral had planned. Another hiss of doors opening, Layha didn't look up from the pad as the person approached. **"What is it now my brother."**

"Layha this is ridiculous. I am pleading with you to call off the blockade. Hapes will die without its economic flow. People are sick and starving." Layha dragged her eyes from the datapad to rest on her brothers fair face. **"It is not my fault Auntie Caralon is a fool. She needs to understand I am the ruler."**

The Imperial Republic found itself in a very difficult position a while after High Prince Stratus left Coruscant on his personal quest. The IR was in the good hands of the Empress and her Ministers. However, the shadow of darkness that arrived, was something no one could have foreseen.

It started as raids on convoys, leading everybody to think that pirate groups and smugglers were behind the attacks. Several patrols were sent to the Outer Rim in order to detect and destroy these attackers, but the results were discouraging. No one was found, and yet the attacks continued.

When the high officials of the IR were celebrating the MoD Gala, the Minister of Defense, Fleet Admiral OVERMIND was informed that some progress had been made. The hyperdrive signatures of the attacking ships matched those of Imperial class Star Destroyers. He reported it to the Throne and soon increased the patrolling in the Outer Rim. It was not conceivable to see Star Destroyers attacking convoys since all Imperial era Star Destroyers had been accounted for. The readings were partial, so it was impossible to match them to the existing database in order to pinpoint the exact origin of the ships.

Almost a year passed with no real progress. The attacks continued, but they were less and less often, which stopped the Military for making any progress. Until, one day, their luck finally kicked in. They got a full reading. However, when the investigators ran the information and compared it to the database they had, they were startled. The reading they found was a perfect match to the flagship of an Imperial Admiral, Bel Jones. The problem was that he and his ship were reported lost in action several decades ago, even before the collapse of the old Empire. Besides that ship, two others were identified, also as part of Jones's Fleet.

No answers are found as to how the ship and his dreaded commander are still around, until Admiral OVERMIND is able to reconstruct the whole story based on his personal knowledge and several stories that he had heard when he was a middle officer in the Imperial Navy. Admiral Bel Jones was an excellent officer when he was lost in action. However, there were several rumors about that time that his death had been a cover up operation and that he had taken a large number of ships in order to lauch a secret attack on the rebels, which were starting to get strenght in the old Empire. As he was never heard of again, everybody assumed that he had died indeed and the rumors ended.

When Admiral OVERMIND heard about this news, he consulted the database and realized that 200 ISDs had been lost about the time Admiral Jones disappeared. If three ships had reappeared after so many decades, the chances that the rest of them would appear increased substantially. As a result, the Minister of Defense proposed the Throne to launch a special mission in order to detect the spearhead and stop a possible invasion.
