Use this threat to post your reports.

**This is a new procedure. I will authorize Grand Admiral OVERMIND to make modifications to this procedure as he sees fit and to finalize them after consulting with the Executor or myself. EVERYONE (including myself) will file post-battle reports. Classified versions of the reports may be submitted to me via PM for High Council review. My own report may include some classified material.**

All military officers that were active and participants during the Palace Storm scenario are to submit a post-battle report to their direct superior. *(Ex. Lt Hollis will report to General Cognatus who will report to Grand Admiral OVERMIND who will report to me and the High Council.)* This report should include all losses/gains during the battle, a summary of actions carried out by the individual or team under the person's command, how they feel they performed (what they could have done better or justifying odd actions taken), and any recommendations or comments concerning other personnel they interacted with.

Following these reports, CO's will announce to their unit the unit's overall effectiveness and current standing as a result from the battle. They will also present post-battle awards or promotions at this time. Keep in mind some awards and promotions require authorization from branch leaders and some require authorization from the Throne.

The Supreme Ruler does hereby authorize and delegate authority to branch leaders to make sure that all individuals active (or even semi-active) paticipants in the Palace Storm scenario are awarded Battle Efficiency awards (as appropriate), and the Imperial Republic Active Service Medal (for participating in a major campaign). Other awards will be presented as warranted at a later time or as authorized by the branch leader, the Minister of Defense, or the Throne (as needed). Branch leaders are entitled to present branch medals, though some may require approval from the Minister of Defense prior, and the Minister of Defense has his own jurisdiction over various awards and medals, and others require Throne endorsement. See [www.imperialrepublic.org/awards/](http://www.imperialrepublic.org/awards/) for more information. CO's should see to it that families of those who have fallen in battle are presented with the Obsidian Crescent on behalf of the Imperial Republic to honor their sacrifice. The Throne authorizes branch leaders to take this initiative.

## Post 2 by Dene Vye Cognatus on 2008-12-05T06:31:18Z

Imperial Republic Army

Operations Command

Post-Battle Debriefing

**To: Grand Admiral OVERMIND**

**Cc: Lieutenant Colonel Shan**

**From: Major General Cognatus**

Sir,

Exactly 24 hours ago I received a Code 7 alert from yourself. I was at the time at the barracks of the Coruscant Garrison on inspection. Upon receiving the alert, I immediately mobilized the ground forces, with teams dispatched to the Palace and to other targeted areas. We lost contact with the Palace almost immediately after receiving your alert and we were not able to break through the jamming. We were alerted to a hostile fleet arriving in system, while also alerted that the planetary shields were still down. Before we were able to do anything about it we had three troop transports landing from the hostile fleet. The transports were fully loaded with assault troops and heavy assault vehicles. Their first targets were the planetary shield generators and military targets. Lieutenant Hollis led a strike team to one of the shield generators and was able to successfully regain control of it and defeat enemy forces in that region. I successfully established contact with Admiral Firecam shortly before his declaration of Throne Regency to coordinate our attacks.

We encountered heavy attacks directly in front of the Palace gates. There was much callatoral damage among the surrounding buildings. I ordered an evacuation of all civilians in the area. Many homes and businesses were destroyed. We suffered heavy ground personnel losses in the encounter but were able to wipe out all of the enemy's forces, including the heavy vehicles. Once that was accomplished, I took a small team into the palace. I had to destroy the main gates to gain entry. We met up with Colonel Shan and sent a small team with her to rescue the hostages at the Ministry of Finance level. I traced your movements to the end point and met up with you there.

Overall, we suffered heavy damages to the Palace, and encountered heavy resistance from the renegade guards whom we used to place our trust for the security of the Royal Family. I regret to hear of the even greater losses in orbit, and offer my condolences on your loss of the *Avenger* and of notable personnel such as Captain Rooney of the *Prince's Pride*. We will forever be in their debt. On a positive personnel note, I have promoted 1st Lieutenant Hollis to the rank of Captain for her outstanding performance and leadership of ground troops and successful completion of the mission. She has also been awarded appropriate awards post-battle as instructed by the Throne.

Respectfully,

Major General Dene Vye Cognatus

Upon learning more about the situation I took the inititive to bring my fleet as well as a few additional support ships to Coruscant to assist with the situation. Finally, I offer my condolences for the loss of your flagship. The men and women that served onboard the *Avenger* were some of the best that we had, and their loss will truly be felt. Overall, I'd like to note that our officers preformed exceptionally well, especially considering the circumstances. Response times were above average, and crews preformed their duties with precision and accuracy. I would like to also commend my squadron Commander, Lt Kasra, for his work during the operation. Squadrons under his command also assisted in the rescue and recovery operation of key personel from sunk warships.

A followup will be sent to you within a few hours once the repair times for our damaged ships has been calculated, but at this moment we have enough free ships for a second operation theater if necessary.
