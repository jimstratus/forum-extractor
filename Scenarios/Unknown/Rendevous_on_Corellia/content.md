## Post 1 by Sean Kennedy on 2006-08-14T12:48:29Z



Lord Kennedy stood looking out the viewing port if the secret station known as CenterPoint. It had been his home for years now. On occasions he would venture out into the known Galaxy and appear like a shadow in the night. It had been close to a month ago that he had done so and had made his destination Planet Zero. There he had told James Stratus, one of his own, about a certain opportunity that he would never get a second chance too. He advised James to leave the comfort of his life style and go to seek this knowlegde. It took some convincing, but in the end James had seen it his way. He left his Republic to that of his other apprentice, Layha Solo-Katarn. She was a capable person for the job, In fact she was perfect for a little taste of what real Power is all about. Lord Kennedy checked in on her from time to time. Making sure she was in no need of his influence. She was a strong willed woman and so far she did not need his help, only his guidance. It had been a few hours ago that he had felt her reaching out in the Force, calling for him. He had answered and she had asked for help in the project code named X.

It was several hour later that Lord Kennedy sat and contemplated the urgency of the matter. Now was not the time. But as he stood watching, he knew the time was aproaching and soon he would make like shadow and appear in the night.
