## Post 2 by Kyliann Watson on 2009-09-15T03:54:47Z



Kyliann arrived at her office early as she did almost everyday, a slight yawn escaping her lips as she glanced about her office and spotting a file that had been slipped under her door. She leaned down and picked it up, imediently noticing the words "URGENT" written in bold letters. Quickly she walked over to her desk and sat down, wasting no time in opening the file to read its contents.

After obtaining a solid grap on what the innformation was telling her she gently pushed the file to the side and began to put together a report for both members of the Thone and the Military as it was something that should be delt with as soon as possible. When the report was finished she would look it over once quickly:

![IRISEagle.jpg](http://i43.photobucket.com/albums/e360/RedXtrema/Album2/Numero%203/IRISEagle.jpg)

Ministry of State

Imperial Republic Intelligence Service

Directorate of Analysis

CLASSIFIED REPORT

TO: Military High Command

Royal Imperial Throne

Cc: Sector Governor(s)

From: Deputy Director Kyliann Watson

It has been brought to my attention through various intelligence reports that there has been a signifigant increase in Trandoshan Militia activities as of late. This, the vast increase detected from their starbases, and the major troop mobilization leads to a strong conclusion of an iminent attack.

With what was gathered from the intelligence information it would appear they are focusing their attention on the planet of Telti in the Inner Rim. The reason for this attack is most likely an attempt to sieze the droid production factories that cover the planets surface and/or maybe to make those within the Hapes Cluster nervous as the planet rests not far from their border.

As I continue to gather intel I will be sure to keep you up to date on all findings.

Sincerely,

**Kyliann Watson**

Bureau of Intelligence

Deputy Director

*Imperial Republic Intelligence Service*

When she was certain it was perfect she pressed send.
