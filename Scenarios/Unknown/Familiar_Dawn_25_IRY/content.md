It was a cold dreary day on Kuat. Marc Stratus, Governor-Prince of Kuat, was meditating inside the family's secret temple located inside Mount Dagoon. Mount Dagoon was a lot of things. Very few people in the whole galaxy knew what it was, let alone that it even existed. Even fewer knew how to get to it, and fewer had access. It was not listed or published anywhere nor a tourist attraction. It was part of restricted space. Only passersby knew it existed. The public Kuatis knew it simply as a mountain on Royal territory that was part of an overall huge territory unexplored and restricted. The Force Temple and Fortress was indeed a place Marc visited on rare occasions though more than any other members of the family, being the only one who remained on Kuat. Ashlee was off exploring the outer-rim on dangerous flights and expeditions, and Nicole was on Corellia supervising Stratus Industries and Jim.....well....Jim's status was unknown. Marc had not heard from Jim in years. He saw him off when departed on his long mission to the Kathol Rift and beyond, where he would visit the Aing-Tii monks and others. He had not even thought of Jim for a long time. The Imperial Republic had changed much in Jim's absence. The Dowager Empress Layha Solo had made significant changes in the government and was on the brink of either insanity, or breakdown. She had gone through a lot in her own life that had affected who she was. She had changed. It was not a change Marc was thrilled about, but he still regarded her fondly and as part of his extended family. It was interesting watching events transpire in her life as she took on the role of Throne Regent. The Royal Family had supported her thus far in all her decisions, trusting that she was doing what was best.

As he meditated, a vision came to him. He found himself among the Noghri on their somewhat distant planet. Standing around him were Noghri warriors and leaders of the various Noghri clans that protected the Royal Family. The Noghri race have had an unchallengeable allegiance to the royal family as a result of a great love they developed for them for liberating them from Sith oppression and saving their race from extinction. They are a backup and private guarding Force (“in the shadows”) to members of the House Stratus and sometimes close friends. Four clans are each assigned to a family member to protect, the Overclan being dormant, having formerly protected James until he left – though a few close associates volunteered and insisted on going with him to the Shadows. He saw himself and Noghri warriors traveling to Kuat and arriving in huge forces, being smuggled in to the Palace. What did this mean? He had only one way to find out. He would have to travel to Wayland.

## Post 2 by Revan Centurion on 2008-07-19T21:07:56Z



**Earlier that morning...**

Revan's patience was indeed becoming very thin as he sat in on the operational intelligence brief for the day. It appeared that having been the Director of the Imperial Republic Intelligence Service had done very little to acclimate Revan to the unfortunately bureaucratic process that was intelligence management these days. Between executing all of his various duties and spending time with his family, it was a wonder that Revan even managed to survive the previous day. Yes - he had ways of manipulating the very force of life around him to help sustain him from day-to-day, but that was hardly a practical pursuit. Unlike many of those he had met in the past, Revan knew that staying fit, sharp, and sane required him to use the force as little as possible. If an individual allowed their use of the force to become habit, they could very well lose their innate senses altogether. Still, it was important to keep the mind and body sharp both with and without the force.

"In conclusion, it would appear that the vast majority of our resources are finally beginning to stabilize thanks to the allocation of discretionary funds pulled from previous missi--"

Revan's mind blanked briefly before he was assaulted with an array of images. At first, he struggled to make any sense of the images. Eventually, he was able to focus his efforts strongly enough to turn the streaming images into a coherent picture. As a 'story' seemed to unfold before Revan's very eyes, he paid close attention. He was surrounded by Noghri of all things. Perhaps they were finally there to kill him for his less than enthusiastic attitude towards them following him around in the past. They were talking but did not appear to be speaking directly to him. As the image shifted slightly to the right, Revan's vision centered on Prince Marc Stratus. Marc Stratus was presently the Governor of Kuat. Revan had first met the Prince during a trip to Kuat with Kaidlen Shan. A trip which ended in a rather deadly clash of forces between the IR and the now recluse DCC. As the vision began to fade, Revan could hear one of the Noghri speak something very discreetly to him. "You bring honor to a name that was associated with much pain and bloodshed in days of old, Centurion. We will protect and follow you to Coruscant and beyond for that reason." The image quickly began 'fast-forwarding' through a long journey back to Coruscant where Revan found himself standing at the head of a sizable Noghri contingent.

Opening his eyes, Revan came to his feet as numerous faces stared at him with confusion and concern. **"Gentlemen, I'm afraid I need to depart early. See to it that a copy of the written report is forwarded to my Intelligence Advisor immediately. That will be all."** The individuals in the room quickly came to attention as Revan departed. Once he was in the corridor, Revan turned to his executive assistant, Denise, who was at his side nearly instantly. **"Dispatch a message to Port Authority. I'm leaving to an undisclosed location and I need the emergency departure route clear of all traffic. Send a secure message, personally, to Kuat Port Authority and inform them of my impending arrival. Inform the Empress I'm leaving to see Prince Marc immediately."**

After noting all of Revan's instructions, Denise left his side to carry out his orders. Revan made his way immediately to his personal shuttle, the *Legatus*. It would carry him to Kuat with the most haste and least public notification. His task force would remain behind on this one.

**Several Hours Later, Kuati Palace**

As the *Legatus* touched down gently on the concrete landing pad outside the Kuati Palace, personal residence of Prince Stratus, Revan immediately began shutting down all systems. Standing from his seat, he through his crimson and black overcoat on and descended the loading ramp. The heels of his boots clicked very softly against the concrete landing pad as he made his way towards a number of waiting Royal Guards. **"I need to speak with the Prince, it is urgent."** For now, Revan waited for a response. Revan's own Royal Guards remained vigilant behind him.

The first sentence combined with the first two listed services, transportation and security, were heavily on her mind. She recognized immediately that this was not a regular information datacard, as it appeared to any public user. It appeared to have general and contact information for Stratus Industries various divisions and departments. Being on the board of directors of Stratus Industries, she knew exactly what was on this particular brand of card. They had several of them placed strategically throughout the galaxy, each with unique information about that particular area, including an emergency travel agent contact for the area.
