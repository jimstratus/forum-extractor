The Advocatus and her flotilla had been on an extended deployment around the Outer Rim. Her captain had been very successful in doing recon missions and intercepting IR convoys. In part it was due to luck and in part because the main fleet had been busy lauching quick strikes at different points of the Galaxy, keeping the so called IR Navy busy. But now the signal had been sent that all ships needed to return to Reducto in order to get prepped to launch the first major offensive in the war: the invasion of Sluis Van.

## Post 3 by Khyron Overmind on 2008-02-16T22:28:36Z

A lot of battles had been fought, but none of them were a definite stand by the enemy. They kept on their strike and run tactis, which made if difficult for the IR Navy to send a devastating attack.
