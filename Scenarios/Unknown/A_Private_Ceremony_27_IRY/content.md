## Post 2 by Diandra Devin on 2009-01-26T04:16:14Z



Diandra had not followed her boss on that mini-vacation. She hadn't wanted to, knowing that she would've been a third wheel the entire trip. *Poor guy,* she thought. *He should just go ahead and pop the question.*

She herself had arrived only a few hours earlier on a luxury transport and acquired a room for herself at one of the many lodgings there on Kuat. Yet again, she had failed to bring a date with her. Who would have wanted to go with her anyway? Diandra had few friends, mostly women, and she suspected that if there weren't a lot of hot guys around, they didn't want to go. Thus, she had not extended them an invitation. The few male friends she did have were much too busy, she felt. Then again, neither did Diandra want to appear attached to anyone. The only person she had brought with her was Nirium, her lady's maid.

Diandra stood before the mirror in her hotel room, a tube of lipstick in hand. She leaned forwards and applied a light layer to her full lips before she pursed them and then studied her work. She'd already made up the rest of her face; now all she had left to do was slip into her gown and put her hair back.

Nirium helped her into the corset top by lacing her up in the back. It was an off-the-shoulder gown with a sweetheart neckline; the skirt itself was slender but not form fitting. The top was done in a deep champagne brocade and the skirt was several layers of chiffon in silk in a color that matched the top. The sleeves matched the skirt but were only one layer; they were wrist length and wide, dropping into a point at her knees. When she was dressed, she slipped into a pair of low backless heels before she stood still.

Nirium brushed out her long hair and then secured it with a simple golden barrette in a half-pulled back fashion. It was then that she donned a light sleeved cloak and procured an air taxi for herself to take her up to the space port. It didn't take long for her to arrive; she did recieve several strange looks from people to her manner of dress, but she didn't acknowledge those glances. Instead, she settled in on the next transport to the drive yards. She recognized several others there from the ball and knew them by name, though not personally.

When she arrived, she made her way to the rendezvouz point where she and the others who had been mentioned would meet Stratus and OVERMIND. Diandra found herself a seat and settled down into it to await the Prince and their special guest.
