## Post 1 by Aurielle Stratus on 2012-08-20T04:57:14Z



Aurielle powered off her computer screen and sighed, leaning back in her chair. The days strung along into one never ending bland version of existance now. The depression was lessening though, and school was going well despite her current attitude towards acadamia or any other sort of civilized venture. Shut up in one of Coruscant's finest universities, the Princess looked to be well adjusting to her new life. But the wild blood in her veins made the girl yearn for adventure, just like her mother ...

Ryden had long quit her service, another loved one abandoning her. Auri was too far gone to miss him though. She had a new handler now, an older male with a stern face but an understanding mind. They shared her dorm at Stratus University, his room right next to hers, living together through the changing of the guard. Tanner was watching her and the desk from his position on the sette. "Is all well, Princess?"

"Yes sir, just finishing up," she said with a smile, before being overcome with a deep yawn. Auri stretched up, her sore muscles aching to move about after being so still. "You can go to bed ahead of me, I'm not far behind."

"Very well. Tomorrow is the last lecture for your senate babbling class, right? That woman can make people turn grey just speaking on and on and ..."

"Right?" Auri agreed, laughing politely. It was then that Tanner took his leave, mentally preparing for the next day with joy that her term was coming to an end and they would have a few weeks without school. She heard him muttering into his com device that "mermaid was tucking in for the night," pleased that he no longer felt obligated to stay up with her until she retired first. She waited until he could not hear her, and then she closed her eyes and muttered in more of a hiss, "You may take your leave too, I wish to feel for a moment that I am alone ..."

Insulting the Noghri was probably not the most intelligent move she could make, but what were they going to do? Tell her father? Stratus knew full well by now just how silly Auri thought their presence was, while she was here at school. Reluctantly, she felt the presence of the two withdrawing from the room, and she leaned forward, her elbows on the hard desk as her hands cradled her head. It had been weeks since she had been in the company of her father or her sister, as final exams occupied all free time. But now that they were completed, and last day of lecture was tomorrow, Auri realized just how much she missed two of the most important people in her life.

In a moment of loneliness, she reached out and took her personal data pad in hand, typing out a quick message to her family:

"Dad, sis,

School year is coming to a close, and I have to get off Coruscant before I go crazy!!! Velusia vacation? Please?!? Daddy, I've been very good ...

~Auri"

She sent it out to them as a secured message, waiting before going to bed.

## Post 2 by James Stratus on 2012-08-20T05:36:28Z



High Prince James Stratus was just returning to Coruscant after completing one of many long Inspection Tours of an Imperial Republic Armed Forces Regional Task Force, as well conducting a review of the sector government of the Bright Jewel Oversector in the Contrumm system. It had caught his attention when reports began to surface through COMPNOR and the armed forces of corruption originating from the highest levels of the Oversector government, The Supreme Ruler had a zero tolerance policy and, while normally would allow the Judicial system to handle issues, but when corruption ran so deep and was so complex, direct intervention on the Throne's part was sometimes necessary. Not only did he order the execution of a number of government leaders for high crimes warranting such, he reorganized the entire sector's government and military leadership. He hoped word would spread of his intolerance and would discourage other governors from engaging in such illegal activities as embezzlement, piracy, civil oppression and other abuses of power.

He had just landed at the Palace Hangar Bay and was heading toward his personal quarters when his comlink chimed indicating a personal message had been received. He pulled out his unique datapad and began to access the Palace computer system to retrieve the message. It was from his daughter, Auri, who was currently living at Stratus University's House Dormitories. He had missed her too, very much. After reading it he determined it was indeed time for a vacation, and she certainly needed the downtime, and one could argue he could probably use it too after a very brain-tolling expedition from which he had just returned. It was late on Coruscant, and Galactic Standard Time. He knew his staff, who had traveled with him, were also heading to bed. He therefore entered a message to the captain and commandant of the Royal Guard, Simon Morris and Praetor Kaidlen Shan, notifying them to send advance teams ahead to secure a private vacation spot(s) on Velusia for himself, Aurielle, and Kaidlen, who from his standpoint, would be joining them for the vacation, but from the guard and other security agencies standpoint, would simply be traveling with the Supreme Ruler to an undisclosed location for protective duty. He also sent a Noghri advance team to check the work of HMRG. His significant other, Colonel Dana Lance, would not be able to go as she had remained behind to oversee the final implementations of their last mission. As he began to put his datapad away, he realized he had gotten so caught up in the details that he forgot to respond to the original message from his daughter. He put in a quick reply:

> Auri,
>
> Just arrived at the Palace from my trip. I think a family vacation would be a great idea. Going to bed now. Let's talk tomorrow morning.
>
> Dad
