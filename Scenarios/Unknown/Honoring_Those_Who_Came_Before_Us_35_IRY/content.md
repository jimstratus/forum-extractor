Colonel Bremen, head of Coruscant Palace Security, was in the Situation Room, monitoring every detail of every movement inside and outside the vicinity of the Palace. Outside the Palace and within the outer layers of the Palace were uniformed and undercover officers of the Imperial Republic Security Bureau, as well as undercover agents from other agencies, who also mingled inside the Palace with permission from Praetor Shan. While the Prince's own Jedi Order of the Shadow Affinity, known as the Phoenix Order, was large and widespread; the galaxy needed a stronger and greater Jedi Order of the Light Affinity to be sure. Though Joe was once a member of the Phoenix Order, he had chosen the Light, and Jim wholeheartedly supported him.
