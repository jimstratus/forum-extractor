Admiral Jon Tinkel, Executive Officer of the Royal Fleet, directly supervised the repairs of the *Prince's Pride* and of many of the ships of the Royal Fleet.

## Post 4 by Jenna Deirdre on 2009-08-13T04:51:38Z



Jenna sighed as she looked around the hangar. Even with all the bustle of activity, and the fresh wing of fighters and bombers, she could still see the scars from the battle that had nearly put a fiery end to the *Prince's Pride* .

She sighed again as she popped her canopy and stood, twisting to mount the ladder that a tech had run up against her fighter. She had been ordered out here to take command of a new fighter squadron, Phoenix Squadron to be precise. And here she was, about to take command of what would be her third squadron. The only other person to still be with her from both of her previous squadrons was her XO, Lieutenant Tegan Winnry.

A glance aft of her fighters' port stabilizer told her that her XO had also landed safely, and was similarly dismounting from her fighter. With a nod to her, Jenna dropped the last few feet to the deck. She could tell that the A-Grav was set to something slightly less than one gravity, as was standard for most hangar decks. Lower gravity meant that pilots could reach their craft more quickly, and in a scramble situation, speed was everything.

As she reached up to remove her helmet, a young Petty Officer approached her and saluted.

"Captain Halcyon's compliments, Ma'am. He wishes to see you ASAP."

"Thank you, Petty Officer." Jenna said as she returned the salute. "Let me get my gear stowed and then I'll be up to meet with the Captain." She added as she retrieved her gear from the small storage compartment in her fighter.

"Aye, Ma'am. If you'll follow me, I'll show you and your XO to your quarters."

With a slight hand gesture, Jenna bade him lead the way.

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

"Here are your quarters, Ma'am." The PO said with a gesture to his right. "Your XO's quarters are right next door. The rest of Phoenix is quartered across the hall. Captain Halcyon's Ready Room is on deck two."

"Thank you, PO. Dismissed." Jenna replied, returning the young man's salute. "Tegan," She added, once the Petty Officer had departed. "Once you get your things unpacked, get with the squadron. Let them get to know you a bit, but get a good reading on how good they really are."

"You got it, Boss." The other woman said, already moving down the passageway towards her own quarters.

Jenna nodded, and entered her own. As befitted her rank, she was assigned a small two room suite just across from her squadron. With a deft motion, she tossed her gear bag just so on her bed. She privacy-sealed the door behind her and headed towards the small 'fresher station, peeling off her flight suit as she went.

A few minutes later, and dressed in duty blues, rather than a flight suit, she left her quarters and caught a turbolift up to deck two.

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

Arriving quickly at the Captains' Ready Room, she entered once the Captain had acknowledged her presence.

"You wanted to see me, Sir?"

Before you ask what happened to my previous squadrons, let me tell you. My first command, Wolf Squadron, was part of the First Tactical Fighter Wing, attached to the MoD fleet, and specifically the Avenger. We got reorganized a while after the Togorian Incident, and most of my pilots became either Squadron Execs, or CO's. Lieutenant Winnry and myself were transferred to Ghostrider Squadron, also attached to the Avenger. Half of Ghostrider was onboard when the Avenger was destroyed. Most of the others were caught in the blastwave. After that, Lieutenant Winnry and myself were transferred to Phoenix Squadron.

"As you already know, our fighter of choice is the EID Falcon, for its greater manouverability in combat, and its role as a pursuit-intercept fighter. Unless anyone has any questions, I suggest you all get some sleep. We have a mission tomorrow. Squadron briefing is tomorrow at zero-seven-hundred. Launch time is zero-seven thirty. Dismissed."

With a final nod to her new command, Jenna strode out of the room and across the hall to her own quarters. Once again privacy-sealing her door, she slipped out of her uniform and into bed, following her own advice. After all, tomorrow was going to be a very busy day.
