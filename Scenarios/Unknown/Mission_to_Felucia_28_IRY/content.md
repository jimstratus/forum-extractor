## Post 4 by Timothy McVale on 2008-12-21T03:42:26Z



**"Good luck, Lieutenant."**

and with that, Tim stood up and gave a salute before heading out the door.

His first real mission. Sure he's handled small time crimes, consisting of petty theives, but nothing of this scale. He was a little anxious. He walked a little bit faster to get to his destination.

He arrived were large group of agents were resting between assignment.

"Alright listen up." his voice cracking a tiny bit at the first syllable "We got an assignment on Felucia. Delta squad come with me."

Ten young men looked amongst themselves before getting up and following Tim out the door.

The squad geared up and loaded onto a shuttle. It was a long and quite ride. Tim filled them in with what information he could. Other than that, not a word was spoken.

They finally touched down on Fellucia. Tim stepped off the shuttle. A local agent greeted him at the foot of the ramp. "The Bureau Chief is this way, sir." he said as he began to head of towards the buildings.

Tim looked at his men. "Get set to move out." he turned and followed to meet with the local Bureau Chief.

## Post 5 by Raithos Bodost on 2008-12-21T05:17:04Z



**"Lieutenant McVale,"** Brigadier General Fellin was a tall, clean cut human with dark eyes and darker hair, with a voice that was much softer than his rank and stature would suggest, **"please, come into my office."**

\*Lieutenant McVale followed Brigadier General Fellin into the General's fairly spacey office in the Felucian IRSB building.\*

**"You've already been briefed on the generalities of the mission, but here are some more specifics."** Brigadier General Fellin clicked on the holoscreen in his office as he went through a brief presentation. **"Lieutenant, meet the Keres virus."** The first screen was that of a group of fairly healthy looking members of various species but mostly humans. Within a couple of screens, the scene was much worse; blood seemed to be pouring out of wounds that had formed very quickly, a sight that just got worse through each slide until eventually Fellin stopped at one of each of the earlier victims dead in a pool of blood. At which point, the General clicked the screen to a black screen. **"These are scenes from our first exposure to it, here on Felucia. Just over one day after first contact, the results are universally fatal. It causes the body's vascular system to expand to the point of breaking.However, there's a catch, unless introduced directly into the bloodstream, Keres has difficulty breaching the vascular wall to begin with and simply dies off. Due to the rapid fatality time, work on a cure has always been sort of race between those who can weaponize it to overcome this weakness and those who can cure it. Local medical researchers had been working on a such a cure and had been showing promising signs. The only known copies of the virus in its entirety were being moved from one lab to another here in Kway Teow where there would be more security for the research. That was what was stolen. We fear that unless it is secured immediately, the virus will fall into the wrong hands and our research twisted to weaponize the virus."** Brigadier General Fellin then clicked the holoscreen to a map overall of the surrounding area. **"Based on your predictions, the attackers came from here.**" Fellin pointed to a black dot in the middle of a marsh. **"The Dead Bottle Hall, a local gathering place for pirates and smugglers. Unfortunately, Felucia is plagued by them and we are more or less helpless to shut them down, either too small for us to find or too well protected for us to assault without military support. I can give you whatever support you need and even round up local militia to assist you. Is there anything else you need to know?"**
