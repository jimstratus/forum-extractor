## Post 3 by James Stratus on 2012-07-13T03:38:51Z



Upon entering the Throne Room Audience Chamber, Chief Klone found himself facing not only the Supreme Ruler, but the Acting Director of the Imperial Republic Intelligence Service, Jenov Raveshaw, Marshall Jared Quinn, Warlord Cognatus, and His Majesty's Chief of Staff, Lady Diandra Devin, who had originated the summons.

When the chambers were completely secure and in lockdown mode, he was then interviewed rigorously for several hours by those present, for what he did not know. At the conclusion of the interview Lady Devin dismissed him and he waited outside the Throne Room.

He was summoned again, this time by one of the guards only, and when he entered the chambers only the High Prince was present. He bowed and waited to be spoken to.

**"Chief Klone.** **You are hereby promoted to the position of Underdirector of the Directorate of Operations of the Imperial Republic Intelligence Service. Report to your new office on Monday morning. Dismissed."**
