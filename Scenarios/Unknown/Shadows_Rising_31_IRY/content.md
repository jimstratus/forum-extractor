## Post 4 by Jared Quinn on 2010-03-30T04:01:18Z



(edited)

Jared had rented a ship for the two of them to fly back to coruscant on after their three week vacation on spira, it had been a beautiful trip and they had decided to take a private ship back to Coruscant so they could enjoy each others company for awhile longer. He was standing in the galley at the moment making them a bit of food, the three weeks had been amazing along with being filled with many changes for both himself and Diandra.

Diandra slipped up behind Jared and wrapped her arms around his waist. Being that she was shorter than him, she ended up standing on tip-toe to kiss the side of his neck with a little laugh. **"You woke me up,"** she complained playfully to him as she moved to stand next to him, arms still around his waist. **"You woke me up with all the good smells you're making. Now, then, what are you cooking that smells so divine?"**

He leaned over and kissed her forehead for a moment before he stirred what he was cooking again.**"Just some eggs, with a bit of extras in it."** He grinned down at her as he stirred the eggs again, he wondered what life would be like for them once they settled back into their normal lives.

Diandra grinned wickedly at him. **"Extras, huh?"** Her voice suggested to him that extras were sexy and at the moment, more interesting than anything he was cooking. **"What kind of 'extras', Mister Quinn?"** She reached over then and turned off the stove before inserting herself between him and the food. **"How about we forget the extras and go enjoy ourselves?"** Jared would've found the Diandra he'd known back at Coruscant to be very different from the real one, who stood in front of him now.

As she slipped between him and the stove the ship was rocked by harsh laser and ion fire, any resemblance of relaxation in the special forces officer dissipated as he looked down at her."Get to the bedroom and stay there." He moved quickly towards the cockpit as the ship was rocked by another volley of weapons fire, Jared was trying to discern who was attacking them and why exactly.

The next few moments happened in slow motion for Jared as he felt something attaching to airlock section of the ship they had rented, in a few moments Jared was at the airlock prepared to deal with whoever came into the room. He wasn't wearing any armor and the only weapon he had was his small service blaster, as the airlock opened he opened fire with his pistol sending the first two men to an early grave before he was clipped by the man behind them. Jared knew his choices at that exact moment were nil as he didn't have any tricks up his sleeve so he emptied his pistol into the incoming men before he took a blaster bolt directly to the chest.

Diandra heard the commotion up at the front of the ship. Having her own blaster that Kaidlen had gifted her, she took it from her bags and then crawled under the bed to hide herself, hoping and praying that she would not need to use it. However, if she did, she was going to do anything to keep herself from harm. Jared had never failed her before, but even she knew that without his armor there was more of a chance that he could be hurt. The thought made her throat tighten with emotion until she could barely breathe, but then, as Jared had taught her during those three weeks, she forced her emotions back and took deep, steady breaths. She could not and would not be caught in such a state if anyone other than Jared came to look for her.

One of the men walked into the bedroom while they were looking for something, he then reached under the bed and grabbed around before he felt Diandra's ankle. In the next moment she was yanked out from beneath the bed and staring down the barrel of a long retired blaster rifle."Nice and Easy there missy. Put the weapon down." The fully armored individual knew who she was and knew what kind of price they would get for her safe return.

The first thing that Diandra did was to kick with all her might as he dragged her out from under the bed. She screamed the whole way, demanding to be let go. Then, when she came face to face with the barrel of the blaster, she lifted her own and pointed it at the man's head. "I'm not putting it down," she said haughtily. "But you are going to let me be. Let go of me this instant."

Another of the men walked in then and shot her with a small tranquilizer dart before he picked her up and started carrying her towards the airlock again. They had gotten what they had come for and were going to leave the ship and Jared, during their sweep of the ship he had regained enough consciousness to drag himself to the cockpit and hit the distress signal before collapsing into unconsciousness again.

It was a few days later when Quinn woke up in the hospital on Coruscant, he had been rescued by a imperial republic patrol near their position but without their assailants nearby or any real trace of them on the ship.

## Post 13 by Diandra Devin on 2010-03-31T16:55:23Z



~ Somewhere in the Galaxy... ~

Diandra woke to the sound of a cell door being slammed. When her eyes opened, she couldn't see much of anything beyond the small circle of light that she was in. She couldn't even begin to tell how large the room was. All she knew at the moment is that the circle of light fell on a filthy mattress and that she was on it. She also knew that she was beyond disoriented and furthermore, frightened. She wished that Jared was around, but knew that if the men had come and gotten her that it was likely they'd somehow managed to incapacitate him.

What would they do to here here? What would happen? What if no one ever came for her? What then? Upon thinking that, she began to chastize herself.

*Don't you dare,* she thought to herself. *Don't you dare doubt Jared like that. You know that Jared will be the first one busting down the doors to this joint to get to you. He made a promise, and Quinn doesn't ever break his promises.*

What she needed to do was to start plotting some sort of escape, some sort of plan to keep herself busy. She needed to explore, and find out what kind of room she was in. Very carefully, she swung her legs over the side of the mattress. Her feet touched the ground. Very carefully, she stood and began to feel it all out. Ara knows if there was a pit in the middle of the room to swallow her whole if she wasn't careful!

There. Twenty paces straightforward; she turned when she reached the corner of the room, feeling the slimy wall beneath her hand. They were either somewhere very wet, or they were underground, since no wall that she knew of above ground ever felt like this. She congratulated herself on her observation and then began to pace out the length of her room. Fifteen paces; then she reached the other wall, in which there was no opening for a door. Where then, had the sound come from? She began to feel over the wall carefully, her eyes straining to see in the dark. Somewhere here there *must* be an opening! How else was she put in? Unless...

Diandra looked up at the ceiling then, shading her eyes to look at the circle of light on the mattress that was to be her bed. What if there were no doors? What if they had a hole in the ceiling through which they'd put her? That concerned Diandra. Furthermore, she had not felt over anything that resembled hygenic facilities.

"Ugh," she said aloud, now beyond her fear and rather miffed.

She had paced out the edges of the room; now it was time to search out the middle. Not one to be stupid and walk straight across, she dropped to her hands and knees and felt it out carefully. No loose stones. No holes. Just a smooth floor which she was unable to tell if it were dirt or duracrete. Satisfied that the room was not booby-trapped, she rose, and thanked Ara that she'd thought to dress in a thick sweater and a pair of durable pants. Her shoes weren't the type for climbing -- they were simple ballet flats -- but at least they would be comfortable. She dusted her hands off on the back of her pants and then strode the rest of the way to where the light was.

"You'll be sorry," she called up to it. "You'll be sorry that you ever kidnapped me! Jared Quinn is on his way as I speak, and he will hurt every single one of you, mark my words! If you let me go now and drop me off at a safe point, I can promise you that he won't string you up by your entrails while you're still alive!"

When no one answered, Diandra flopped down on the mattress and pulled her knees to her chest. She would not be despaired. She would not. Jared would come for her, and he would get her out of this stinking hole with its dirty mattress and no bathroom. At least there weren't any bugs. So far.
