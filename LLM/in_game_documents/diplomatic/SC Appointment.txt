Seated in the center of the Grand Dais, the Supreme Ruler of the Imperial Republic stood and moved to the center of the Dias and with the voice of one with authority, began to speak. “We have had both the privilege and honor of having the esteemed leadership of Joesefus Alastar Quick as our Supreme Chancellor for the past thirty-five years. When he met with myself and the Executor and announced his intention to retire I could not believe what I was hearing. It has taken this past week to sink in, and the Royal Gala honoring his service has helped me realize that we have reached a time to move onward. The Force has indeed called Master Quick to serve and take over the Jedi Order of the Light as Grand Master of the Order. In addition, as the founding member of the Imperial Republic High Council, Joesefus Quick will continue to serve as a full-fledged member of the Council as its most senior member. The details of which will be clarified in the next meeting of the High Council.
While his leadership will be greatly missed, I am here today as well as the entire Throne in a united effort to present to you your new President of the Senate and our newest member of the Throne, Minister of State and Supreme Chancellor of the Imperial Republic. Jedi Master and High Councilor Prince Dene Vye Cognatus is hereby appointed Supreme Chancellor of the Imperial Republic, President of the Senate, Vice Chairman of the Imperial Republic High Council, Minister of State, Chaiman of the Council of Moffs, and Grand Duke of the Royal Territories of the Imperial Republic. While he served as Minister of Defense and Minister of War he also served as High Councilor and good will ambassador on many occasions and has worked very closely with his wife, Princess Aurielle Stratus, the Throne Senate Liaison. While Supreme Chancellor and Minister of State may be very different from Minister of Defense, I have every confidence in his abilities and know he will do a fine job. I expect all of you to support Supreme Chancellor Cognatus in his new position and authority as a member of the Royal Imperial Throne.
Your new Supreme Chancellor, Prince Dene Vye Cognatus, will now address you.” The High Prince returned to his seat next to the Executor and the outgoing Chancellor and watched as Dene stood up to address the Senate. The Princess Aurielle, Dene’s wife, was seated next to him, with the Director of IRIS and Minister of Defense, their plus ones and the High Prince’s girlfriend/mistress were all seated in the row behind them.
