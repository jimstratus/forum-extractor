Tonight we say farewell to the Supreme Chancellor as he embarks on his next great journey in life. Looking back, I realize that it was highly, statistically unlikely that Joe and I would ever become as good friends as we have become. I of an aristocratic and Kuati heritage, and my boss a humble, public servant of Corellian heritage, who’s idealist frame of mind, pushes him everyday to make the galaxy not just better, but to push it forward to reach its true potential. Born on opposite side of the economic, political, and social spectrums; in the dark times before the Imperial Republic. Many children grew up like Joe with no real hope of a bright and successful future. Parents knew that their children would grow up and simply wouldn’t have a better life than their own. Joe has been an instrumental force in building the Imperial Republic Dream and restoring parent’s hopes for their children to have a better and more prosperous future. Joe frequently quotes the teachings he learned from his mother, who was a real inspiration for him. 
Legislators of the Corellian Senate weren’t required to vote on the record, which meant that it was a rare occurrence when any vote was on the record, with almost every vote being a voice vote. After one year of extreme reckless spending by the Corellian Senate, the Senators voice-voted to give themselves extravagant pay raises. This happened during a time of severe economic depression in the Corellian Sector, and it constantly frustrated Corellian Planetary Governor Joe Quick. He watched as these legislators were out of touch with reality and consistently failed to represent the interest of the Corellian people. To this day, you can’t find one Senator who said they voted for a pay raise that year. It was at this time he remembered the saying of his mother, “Don’t complain about it, do something about it”. Joe, realizing he couldn’t do anything about it as Corellian Planetary Governor, decided to run for Governor-General of the Corellian Sector.  As Governor-General, Joe worked to combat the corruption and reform the Sector Senate to become the People’s House again.  After Joe’s reforms were put in place, every Corellian Sector Senator has to show their votes on the record. Today, the Corellian Sector Senate ranks at the top of every annual Sector Senate Transparency Index.
From working with Joe over the years, I can tell you many things about him. During trying times of crises Joe turns to face the enemy/situation head-on, it is like a dog who loves to hunt. He is passionate about making government work for and to represent the collective interests of our citizens. I remember certain situations where we thought agreements couldn’t be reached and that peace would be untenable, but Joe’s perseverance and commitment to diplomacy always won out and peace was had. Joe has been a fixture at the Imperial Republic over the past three decades, and we will all miss him as he takes on the new challenges that life has to offer.
I remember learning about history at school, and one thing that always stayed with me was this famous quote, “Great statesmen are the ambassadors of Providence, sent to reveal to us our unknown selves.” Through the course of history you will never get to meet many, Josefus Quick is a Statesman.
