Commissioned by the Throne, the State Security Commission (STASECOM) is a collaborative body of individuals representing major security agencies and interests of the Imperial Republic. Each commission member being from a different department performing behind the scenes in a somewhat secretive way, typically a leading official of an Internal Affairs division of their branch. These individuals are said to wield great power and authority. Known primarily in their branches as "(Chief) Security Officers", they are in charge of the issuance and revocations of security clearances to all personnel whose clearance fall under their department's jurisdiction. They also oversee various aspects of their Internal Affairs departments respectively. Commission members answer to their own chains of command through their various departments, though the chain they report to is usually restricted to high officials and in some cases the branch leader his/herself. The commission is chaired by the State Security Advisor, who all members report to indirectly outside their branch, who in turn reports directly to His Majesty the Supreme Ruler, as a member of His Majesty's Personal Staff. Matters of difficult decision pertaining to higher security clearances or special internal investigations are discussed through this commission, as a joint-task force in thwarting threats to the heart of the government, and apprehending traitors of the same.

 

Within this commission, ranks and titles are seldom used and are generally classified, save that of the State Security Advisor. Only names are known, even among its members.
