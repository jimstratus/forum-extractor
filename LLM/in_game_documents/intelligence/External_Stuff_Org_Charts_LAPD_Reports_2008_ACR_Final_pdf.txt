ANNUAL COMPLAINT REPORT - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
for the Calendar Year 2008
The Annual Complaint Report fulfills the requirements of Consent Decree paragraph 130. The report includes 
information on the types of complaint allegations received, the disposition of those allegations, and the resulting 
discipline, as well as summary information by rank and division of assignment.
A complaint is the first step in the Department’s disciplinary process. Complaints may be made by members of the 
public, or may originate within the Department. A complaint may include various allegations against one or more 
employee, sworn or civilian. Complaint allegations are investigated to determine their validity. Recommended 
findings are made by an accused employee’s commanding officer. Disciplinary action is taken against employees who 
are shown to have violated Department policies regarding misconduct or failure to perform the duties of their 
positions. The type and severity of discipline imposed varies according to the nature of the sustained allegations and 
the accused employee’s work history and disciplinary record. The Chief of Police makes the final determination of 
discipline imposed.

Pursuant to Consent Decree Paragraph 93, Internal Affairs Group shall investigate all complaints which allege: 
unauthorized uses of force; discrimination; improper ethnic remarks; gender bias; unlawful search; unlawful seizure 
(including false imprisonment and false arrest); dishonesty; domestic violence; improper behavior involving narcotics 
or drugs; sexual misconduct; theft; and retaliation.  All other allegations are investigated by an employee's chain of 
command supervisors.

Appendix A
This report was completed to meet the Consent Decree requirements specified in paragraph 130.  
Paragraph 130 requires:
The LAPD shall annually report to the Commission, with a copy to the Inspector General, the 
type of complaint allegations it receives and the disposition (including sustained rate) and 
discipline or lack of discipline resulting from each type of allegation. This report shall include 
both the allegations received and any collateral misconduct discovered during the investigation. 
This report shall list the above information for each type of allegation as well as summarize 
aggregate information by geographic division(department, bureau, area, and district), officer 
rank, and type of assignment.
Explanation of Tables
Disposition of  Closed Complaint Allegations and Sustained Rate by Allegation: Table A lists the 
total number of final allegations made and the disposition of allegations for each allegation type. 
It also lists the number of sustained allegations (those adjudicated as Guilty, Sustained, or 
Sustained-No Penalty) and the allegation sustained rate. Allegations are counted for each accused 
employee. The allegation totals are also repeated in Tables B1, C, E, and G.
Sustained Rate by Allegation Type and Distinct Employees Cited in Complaints: Table B1 lists 
the allegation sustained rate and number of employees (sworn & civilian) cited in complaints.  
The number of allegations may not be equal to the number of employees, as multiple allegations 
may be attributed to one employee in a complaint.  This may also hold true for the employee 
count, as an employee may have multiple sustained allegations, and will consequently be listed 
multiple times (i.e., once for each allegation).
Penalty Imposed By Rank: Table B2 lists discipline imposed by rank.  Discipline is imposed based 
on the totality of the sustained allegations.  For example, if an employee is suspended for 10 days 
for Discourtesy, Dishonesty and Neglect of Duty, it should not be assumed that the employee 
received 3.3 days for each allegation type.
Source of Complaints Closed and Sustained Rate by Allegation Type:
Table C lists the number of complaints closed, the number of sustained complaints, and the 
sustained rate for each allegation type. It also lists the number of complaints closed and 
sustained rate by the source of complaint.   A single complaint will be listed for more than one 
allegation type if it includes allegations of multiple types.
Allegation Summary by Employee Rank:
Table D lists the number of sustained allegations and the total number of allegations made for 
each rank and allegation type.
Allegation Summary by Employee Assignment Type: Table E lists the number of sustained 
allegations and the total number of allegations made for each assignment and allegation type.  
Each employee’s assignment type is derived from division of assignment and rank.
Tables F, G, and H: These tables display information based on the organization of the accused 
employees at the time of incident, which may not be the same organization at the time the
