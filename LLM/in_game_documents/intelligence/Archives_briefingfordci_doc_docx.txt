8 - DIRECTORATE OF COUNTERINTELLIGENCE 

(IRIS-DCI) aka DCI

The Directorate is responsible for the countermeasures taken by Imperial Intelligence against spies and leaks benefiting enemies of the Empire.  

INTORG - Bureau of Internal Organization, responsible for maintaining a high level of security among the chiefs and agents of Imperial Intelligence. Will coordinate efforts to gain competent staff to II as well as advise on security clearance. 

Bureau of Counter Intelligence - Bureau responsible for finding and eliminating enemy spies and agents within the Empire. A secondary agency that will only take active measures should the primary agency, the Imperial Security Bureau, be infiltrated or rendered inoperable. Will under normal circumstances inform the Security Bureau through appropriate channels (ie the Director of II). Will assume a low profile and remain a secret to most members of the Empire and Imperial Intelligence.
