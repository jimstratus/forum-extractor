POSTING MOTOR VEHICLE AND PEDESTRIAN STOP DATA 
 
The policy of the Los Angeles Police Commission and the Chief of Police, William J. Bratton, is for 
the LAPD to become more transparent in its policing.  The attached motor vehicle and pedestrian stop 
data is consistent with this policy of disclosure to the community.  This is the first in a series of 
postings of the motor vehicle and pedestrian stop data.  This posting only includes raw data without 
any analysis.  The analysis and interpretation of stop data is a challenging endeavor.  Most methods 
used by jurisdictions to analyze their data (e.g., comparing the demographics of the people stopped by 
police to the demographics of the residential population as measured by the census) can identify 
disparities in the distribution of police stops and/or searches.  Many of the most commonly used 
methods cannot determine if that disparity is caused by police bias or by factors wholly independent of 
the police.  The City of Los Angeles is in the process of contracting (through a proposal process) 
qualified independent professional services who can assist the City in the analysis of the raw data to 
produce meaningful results based on sound research methods.   
 
The data presented was collected during the period of July 1, 2002, to November 30, 2002.  The data 
currently being collected is based upon the requirements established by the Federal Consent Decree 
governing police practices of the Los Angeles Police Department (See Consent Decree Paragraphs 30, 
32, 104, 105, and 156.) (http://www.lapdonline.org/pdf_files/boi/final_consent_decree.pdf).  Future 
data will be posted semiannually, in February and August. 
 
This posting displays raw data for each of the following operational commands: 
 
· The 18 geographic Areas 
· The 4 traffic Divisions 
· The four geographic Bureaus (consists of the 18 geographic Areas & 4 traffic divisions) 
· Operations-Headquarters Bureau (consists of specialized Los Angeles Police Department 
 Divisions) 
· Citywide totals 
 
Maps of the Area/Division and/or Bureau may be found at:  
http://www.lapdonline.org/community/community_main.htm.     
 
The City’s data collection process, including the types of data collected, will evolve over time.  For 
example, the City is currently in the process of standardizing descent race/ethnicity categories utilized 
in various Los Angeles Police Department data collection activities to facilitate data analysis and 
comparisons.  Current descent race/ethnicity categories being used were originally selected for their 
compatibility with mandatory crime and arrest reporting categories.  It should be noted that for 
pedestrian and traffic stop data collection purposes, officers are required to report their perception of 
racial/ethnic descent of the stopped person, and do not ask for their racial/ethnic origin. 
 
If there are questions, comments, or suggestions relating to the data posted, please contact  
Captain Brad Merritt, Los Angeles Police Department, at 14566@lapd.lacity.org or write the  
Los Angeles Police Department, Management Services Division, 150 N. Los Angeles St., Room 717, 
Los Angeles, Ca. 90012.
