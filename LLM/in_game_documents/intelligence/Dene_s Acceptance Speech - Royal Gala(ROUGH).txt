Dene stands applauding the retiring Supreme Chancellor. He offers and shakes the hand of his predecessor. He walks up to the podium and stares out into the open for a minute. Auri sitting at a nearby table gave a smile to Dene. He gave a slight smile in return and prepared his speech. “Good evening proud citizens of the Imperial Republic. We are here tonight to honor the great and legendary Joseph Quick.” Applause can be heard throughout the hall. “It is my great pleasure to have known the Supreme Chancellor, he was intelligence, wise and first of all he was dedicated to our cause. This man has fought hard to bring diplomacy, peace and organization to our great order.” Dene turned and faced Joseph and raised a glass. “To you sir, thank you for your service, your shoes undoubtedly will be difficult to fill, let alone by a military man.” He smiled. “After thirty years of service you have never faltered nor have you ever backed down. No challenge was to great for you and you always succeeded. The Ministry of State is the powerhouse is it today because of your leadership, your ideas and your passions.” He bowed his head to Joe.
“Now the whole story about the palace being blown up.. some of you know, basically diplomacy failed.” He grinned. “Not many people can say they blew up their office and still work there.”
A few minutes later after the applause began to slow and attention went back to the new Supreme Chancellor, Dene began to speak. “My fellow citizens, my experiences within the Imperial Republic have left me never the wiser and capable and personally I am honored that I was chosen to fill the shoes of such a great asset. I accept the full responsibilities of this office and I swear to all of you, the Ministry of State and other departments under my direct authority will only improve upon the magnificent foundation set forth by my predecessor. With this is in mind I will be making a few changes here and there to improve the Imperial Republic where I can.” Citizens stood, applauding the once famous war hero of the Imperial Republic.
