 
LOS ANGELES POLICE DEPARTMENT 
SEMI-ANNUAL PUBLIC REPORT 
JANUARY 1, 2014 TO JUNE 30, 2014 
 
 
The following information is being provided by the Los Angeles Police Department 
(Department) as a public report on a semi-annual ba sis: 
 
• A listing of audits and inspections conducted and s ubmitted to the Police Commission during 
the six-month period; 
• A listing of pertinent changes to Department polici es during the six-month period; 
• A listing of quarterly discipline reports issued du ring the six-month period; 
• A summary of uses of force (both non-categorical an d categorical) broken down by 
geographic Area and suspect descent during the six- month period; 
• A summary of arrests broken down by geographic Area  and suspect descent during the  
six-month period; and, 
• A summary of field stops broken down by geographic Area and apparent descent of the 
person stopped during the six-month period. 
 
This information is a compilation of data from vari ous Departmental sources, prepared by 
Internal Audits and Inspections Division.  The stat istical data have not been audited by Internal 
Audits and Inspections Division.
