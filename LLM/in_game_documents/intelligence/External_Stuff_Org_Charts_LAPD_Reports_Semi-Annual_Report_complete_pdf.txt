SEMI-ANNUAL PUBLIC REPORT
JULY 1, 2010 to DECEMBER 31, 2010
Pursuant to Consent Decree Paragraph 156, on a semi-annual basis, the Department places a
public report on-line that provi des the following information:
• A summary of audits conducted pu rsuant to the Consent Decree;
• A summary of changes to Department policies related to areas mentioned in the Consent
Decree;
• A summary of discipline imposed by type of misconduct, type of discipline, bureau, and
rank;
• A summary of uses of force (both non-cat egorical and categorical) broken down by
geographic Area and suspect descent;
• A summary of arrests broken down by ge ographic Area and suspect descent; and,
• A summary of field stops br oken down by geographic Area a nd apparent descent of the
person stopped.
Although the Consent Decree expire d in July 2009, the Department c ontinues to place this Semi-
Annual Public Report on-line.  The compilation is  prepared by Internal Audits and Inspections
Division.

Police Commission’s weekly agendas at:
