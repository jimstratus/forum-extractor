Excerpt from Mission Log X5110
While laboring among the Aing-Tii monks in the Kathol Rift, I went on several missions for the monks to discover the pirates in the system and their operations, and to report back my findings. I stumbled upon some slave encampments and ships of people the pirates had enslaved. I had hidden myself from them, and placed myself aboard their ship. Something had to be done to rescue these people. I alerted the Aing-Tii to my location and circumstance through the use of the Force, with special techniques they had taught me. While a Master at the Jedi Arts, and also in Sith teachings, I had set out to explore all aspects of the Force and learn from them. I had promised the Aing-Tii that I would help them with their pirate problem and bring safety to the peaceful people of the system and rid them of their pirate problem, as they did not have much in the way of weapons or protection. I became their protector. In return they taught me what they knew of the Force. I learned much from them. I helped them to build ships and establish security, and helped them to fight off the pirates and free those who had been kidnapped by them. I was on such a mission when I discovered Miss Dana Lance. She, herself, was a prisoner among the pirates, and was in a tight situation. For some reason she caught my attention more than any of the others, and so I attempted to rescue her first. She became very useful in assisting me to free all of the prisoners, take control of the ship, and kill the pirates aboard. She had no where to go. I brought her back with me to the Aing-Tii, and from then on she remained with me, determined to bring the pirates to justice and most important to her, rescue all those who had been captured. She had been taken from her parents very young, and they had been killed in front of her. I have observed her to have excellent combat skills, and took her as an apprentice, to learn the ways of the Aing-Tii, as they would not permit me to teach her in the ways of the Force, and I sense she has limited connection to the Force, if any. The only thing she has been able to do is to sense my moods and feelings, which for some strange reason I have been unable to block from her. I have never had this occurence with any other being, though two have been able to break down the barriers because of closness, but it is as if there are no barriers when she is there. She accompanied me on many trips while in the Outer Rim, and the two of us have formed a sort of bonding or closeness, though we have never spoken of it. We have traveled together and gotten to know each other. She did not believe me at first when I said who I really was, but seems to have accepted it upon my arrival at Kuat. She knows I am powerful, yet she knows I would never harm her. Her loyalty has been precious, and she has been a good friend to fill in gaps that were left open long ago with my departure from Coruscant. I have appointed her to be my Intelligence Officer. She is bright, doesn't miss a beat (unfortunately), and very perceptive. She is really good at reading people. I don't know quite how she'll adjust, especially in this field not having known anyone or anything, but she learns fast and has a photographic memory, otherwise I would not have put her in this position. If she is able to handle this I will consider her for an IRIS Leadership position.
