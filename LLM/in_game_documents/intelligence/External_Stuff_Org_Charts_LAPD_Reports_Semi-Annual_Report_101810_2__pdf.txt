 
LOS ANGELES POLICE DEPARTMENT  
SEMI -ANNUAL PUBLIC REPORT  
JANUARY  1, 20 10 TO JUNE  30, 2010 
 
Pursuant to Consent Decree Paragraph 156, on a semi -annual basis, the  Department placed a 
public report on -line that provided the following information:  
 
 A summary  of audits conducted  pursuant to the Consent Decree;  
 A summary of changes to Department policies related to areas mentioned in the Consent 
Decree;  
 A summary of discipline imposed by type of misconduct, type of discipline, bureau, and 
rank;  
 A summary of use s of force (both non -categorical and categorical) broken down by 
geographic Area and suspect descent;  
 A summary of arrests broken down by geographic Area and suspect descent; and  
 A summary of field stops broken down by geographic Area and apparent descent of the 
person stopped.  
 
Although the Consent Decree expired in July 2009, the Department will continue to place this 
Public Report on -line.  The compilation will be prepared by Internal Audits and Inspection s 
Division.
