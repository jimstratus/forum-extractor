
# 🛡️ EID-Ghost Stealth Recon Starfighter Overview

The EID-Ghost is the most elusive craft in the Imperial Republic’s scout fleet. Outfitted with advanced cloaking systems, long-range sensors, and ECM jammers, it specializes in deep-space reconnaissance, fleet shadowing, and precision strikes without detection.

Designed with stealth in mind, the Ghost forgoes heavy weaponry in favor of survivability, silence, and subtlety. It retains dual missile launchers and ion cannons for strategic engagements but relies primarily on its cloaking device and jammers to evade confrontation entirely.

With its dual-crew layout, the pilot and systems operator can coordinate scans, evasions, and stealth maneuvers simultaneously. The Ghost is a favorite of elite recon teams, intelligence units, and special forces. Most of its missions never make it into the official record.
