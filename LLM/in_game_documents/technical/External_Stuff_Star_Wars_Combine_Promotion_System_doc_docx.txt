Promotion System
(by Commodore OVERMIND)


The Promotion System will be introduced within the Imperial Armed forces of the Empire to allow all officers and enlisted personnel to be awarded for performing and outstanding duty for the Empire and also to sanction those ones who don’t act upon the Imperial standards.

Procedures

The Promotion System relays on the Imperial Merit and Demerit system currently in place. According to that system, an officer or enlisted men receives merit points for performing his duty in an outstanding way. He also gets demerit points if he neglects to perform his duty properly. According to the merits and demerits he will be promoted or demoted accordingly.

It is important to notice that the list provided here is for minimum merit requirements for promotion. If the unit CO of the person believes he’s not ready for the promotion, he has the power to hold the promotion even though that person has already reached the number of merits for a promotion. The unit CO will be the judge if that person is ready or not.

Promotion for Enlisted personnel

In the case of enlisted ranks, the Batallion/Fleet CO will be the one responsible to give the promotions or demotions according to the table. In case of exceptional duty, the Commanding Officer will be able to promote a person to the next rank without the enlisted man having the required merits for such promotion.

For example, if Crewman Test only have 25 merits but he promotes activity within the Fleet and has a remarkable performance during a Fleet RPG, he wouldn’t get the 10 merits he needs to be promoted to Flight Corporal. However, his Fleet CO is able to skip that procedure and promoted him. Once promoted, the new number of merits will be 35. 

The same way if Sergeant Trial only has 15 demerits but he defies and offends his Legion CO in a public forum, then he can be demoted to Corporal without the need for him to score 10 more demerits. Once demoted his new number of merits will be 35 and his demerits will equal to 25.

Promotion for Officers

The promotion to the officer level (O-1) should be achieved after the Candidate to Officer has been recommended by his CO to the Officer’s College (after having reached the required amount of merits, 160). Once in the Officer’s College, he’ll be trained in the several subjects that will prepare him to be the leader an officer should be and also tactics, Branch traditions and many more subjects alike. According to the results of the final exam, the Candidate to Officer could be promoted to O-1 (90% or better) or to O-1 and receive an award if he has an outstanding performance (98% or better).

In case the Candidate to Officer fails the exam (scores below 90%) he won’t be promoted and he’ll have to wait 2 months before trying to take the exam again.

In order to be recommended to the Officer’s College, the candidate should meet the following requisites:

Basic leadership skills
Participation in the unit
Good conduct
Basics on branch tactics

The Promotion of officers to the rank of O-4 and above will be proposed by the CO (once the officer has reached the requisites for promotion) and reviewed by the Branch HQ that will decide by majority. However, the CO of the branch will be able to veto. The same would apply for demotions. 

In the case of promotions to the rank of O-4 or above, the Branch HQ will be the one announcing the promotion in the Brach Mailing List.

For promotions to the rank of O-4 (and above), the candidates to promotion should meet the following requisites:

Proven leadership skills
Advanced skills in branch tactics
Organization
Communication
Be able to encourage and motivate his subordinates
Be able to promote activity

In case of exceptional duty and work above the call of duty, a CO can request to the Branch HQ about the promotion for a certain officer even if he hadn’t reached the required number of merits. In his request, he has to submit all the relevant information about the candidate and a detailed explanation why is he requesting this advance promotion.

All promotions of officers or to officer level will be announced in the Branch Mailing List by the unit CO so all members of the Branch are aware of who their  officers are.

Promotion for Command Officers

The promotion of Officers to Command level should be proposed by the Branch CO to the Imperial High Command once he has reached the required number of merits. The promotion will be approved by the Emperor. The same goes for demotions.

As in the case with officer levels, the same exception applies here.
 

Note: Every demerit will represent 2 less merits in the personal record.


Restrictions to Promotions

The responsible for awarding promotions is the unit CO (Fleet CO or Batallion CO). Only in case of leave or absence, the unit XO would be allowed to grant the promotions.

For the promotions to enlisted personnel, the unit CO can delegate in the Company/Line CO the responsibility to give the promotion. Promotions of officers are only handle by the unit CO. The unit CO is the responsible for approving all the promotions in the unit.

However, under NO circumstance, the officer handling the promotion will be allowed to grant a promotion to the same rank he has.

That is, if the Fleet CO is a Lieutenant Commander and the Line CO is a Flight Officer, if they want to promote Flight Sergeant Test to Flight Officer, the Fleet CO has to do it himself and can not delegate the promotion on the Line CO. The Line CO can make promotions up to Flight Sergeant.

Interim Procedure

Due to the fact that the Officer’s College is not ready yet, the promotions to Officer level (O-1) will be requested by the unit CO to the Branch HQ.

Since awards will be given more often now, there shouldn’t be a lot of exceptions to the promotion system.
