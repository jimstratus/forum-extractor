
# 🛡️ EID-Scimitar Strike Bomber Overview

The EID-Scimitar is a smaller, faster, and highly maneuverable bomber compared to its heavier EID-Hammer cousin.

Equipped with a formidable loadout of heavy lasers, missiles, and ion cannons, the Scimitar is capable of quickly striking fortified targets and escaping before heavy resistance can respond.

Its aerodynamic hull and moderate armor provide a balance between survivability and speed, ideal for hit-and-run bombing raids, planetary assaults, and supporting fleet engagements.

The EID-Scimitar is favored by elite pilots who value agility, precision, and tactical versatility over brute force.
