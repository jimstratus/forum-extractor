
# 🛡️ Lambda-Class Shuttle (LCS) Overview

The Lambda-class Shuttle (LCS) is the Imperial Republic Navy's most widely used short-range personnel and cargo transport. Recognized for its tri-wing design — a central stabilizer flanked by folding wings — the Lambda is equally adept at docking with capital ships or landing on planetary surfaces.

With a total length of 20 meters, it can carry up to 8 tons of cargo, 50 cubic meters of internal volume, and 10 passengers in addition to its three-person crew.

Armed with two laser cannons, four heavy lasers, and twin missile launchers, the LCS can fend off small threats and navigate contested airspace during extractions or VIP transports.

Its reinforced shielding and multiple jammer/sensor suites offer respectable protection during boarding operations, covert insertions, and shuttle runs across dangerous sectors.

Though primarily a workhorse, customized Lambda variants also serve roles as field command pods, medical evacuation shuttles, or elite infiltration craft.
