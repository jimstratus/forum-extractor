# 🦅 Imperial Republic Starfighter Data Sheet: EID-Eagle (Markdown Version)


# 🦅 EID-Eagle Starfighter Overview

The **EID-Eagle** is the Imperial Republic’s premier heavy strike and escort starfighter, developed in parallel to the lighter EID-Falcon. 
Designed for durability, firepower, and battlefield coordination, the EID-Eagle is equipped with a dual-cockpit configuration, reinforced armor plating, and powerful enhanced impulse drives.
Armed with an impressive array of laser, heavy laser, and ion weaponry, along with missile and torpedo launchers, it can dominate both atmospheric and space engagements. 
With its tractor beam system and heavy shielding, the Eagle can also conduct rescue, boarding, and recovery operations during fleet actions. 
Its silhouette evokes the regal strength of its namesake: wide-winged, lethal, and commanding. 
Preferred by elite strike wings and fleet command squadrons, the EID-Eagle serves as a cornerstone of Imperial Republic space superiority doctrine.
