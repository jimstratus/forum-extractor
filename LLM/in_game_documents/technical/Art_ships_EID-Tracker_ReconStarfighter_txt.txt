
# 🛡️ EID-Tracker Long-Range Recon Starfighter Overview

The EID-Tracker is the workhorse of the Imperial Republic's deep-space reconnaissance fleet, purpose-built for tracking enemy fleets, mapping unknown regions, and coordinating strike operations across vast distances.

Equipped with multiple advanced sensor arrays and jamming systems, the Tracker can detect, analyze, and evade threats across a wide operational theater.

While not a dedicated heavy fighter, it retains modest weaponry to defend itself if necessary, including lasers, ion cannons, and a pair of missile launchers.

Its dual-crew configuration allows for split duties: piloting and sensor/communication management, ensuring maximum operational efficiency on extended missions.

With strong shielding and reliable impulse drives, the Tracker is capable of operating alone for extended periods or leading smaller recon groups deep into contested space.
