
# 🛡️ Overmind-Class Battleship (OCB) Overview

The Overmind-Class Battleship (designation OCB) represents the pinnacle of the Imperial Republic Navy’s battleship design — a symbol of absolute command, relentless force projection, and enduring legacy.

Named in honor of Praetor Overmind, the legendary architect of the Republic’s naval supremacy, the Overmind-class is crafted to combine the best qualities of the Defender-class and Protector-class battleships while advancing beyond them in every category.

Stretching 2,200 meters from bow to stern, the Overmind dwarfs most other warships, its profile commanding fear and respect across the stars. It is heavily armored, shielded by 28,000-rated deflector fields, and armed with a devastating array of 50 laser cannons, 50 heavy lasers, 150 turbolasers, and 30 missile launchers, providing overwhelming firepower against fleets and fortified positions alike.

Reinforced by 10 ion cannons and 80 ion batteries, the Overmind can disable enemy shielding and systems with ease, softening targets for annihilation.

The ship’s enhanced speed of 55 MGLT — impressive for a vessel of its size — allows it to respond to threats and reposition faster than most heavy battleships, embodying the Republic’s doctrine of dynamic dominance.

Designed as a fleet command vessel, the Overmind carries 25 starfighter squadrons, 25,000 troops, and the capacity for 500 passengers, including high-level command staff, diplomats, and dignitaries.

The Overmind-class is not merely a ship; it is a declaration of Republic supremacy, a bastion of Order, Stability, and Freedom brought to the stars.
