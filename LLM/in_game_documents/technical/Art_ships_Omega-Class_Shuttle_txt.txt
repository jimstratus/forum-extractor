
# 🛡️ Omega-Class Shuttle Overview

The Omega-class Shuttle is an enhanced-capacity evolution of the classic Lambda-class workhorse, designed for missions requiring heavier passenger or cargo loads without sacrificing maneuverability or protection.

Maintaining the iconic tri-wing silhouette — central stabilizer and folding lateral wings — the Omega-class improves upon internal volume and transport capabilities, allowing 14 tons of cargo, 60 cubic meters of storage, and up to 20 passengers.

It sports the same rugged build but incorporates a stronger impulse drive (hyperspeed 1.00x, 80 MGLT sublight) and a slightly expanded missile payload.

Armed with twin laser cannons, four heavy lasers, and dual missile launchers, it can protect itself effectively during planetary landings or boarding actions.

Omega shuttles are often deployed for VIP convoys, rapid troop insertion teams, forward reconnaissance parties, and priority equipment drops — anywhere greater mass needs moving swiftly and safely.
