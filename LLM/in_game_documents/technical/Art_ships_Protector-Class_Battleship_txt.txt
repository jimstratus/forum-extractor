
# 🛡️ Protector-Class Battleship (PCB) Overview

The Protector-class Battleship (designation PCB) is the Imperial Republic Navy’s primary fast-attack capital warship. Designed to lead rapid-response strike groups, provide deep-space firepower, and offer flexible deployment across conflict zones, the Protector balances battlefield endurance with agility.

At 1,200 meters in length, it is significantly smaller than the Defender-class, but no less formidable. It carries 20 heavy lasers, 20 laser cannons, 50 turbolasers, and 30 ion batteries, giving it potent multi-role capabilities for fleet support, planetary bombardment, or ship-to-ship combat.

With a troop complement of 10,000, 10 squadrons, and a payload of vehicles and shuttles, it excels in supporting both space and surface operations. Its design emphasizes versatility: swift hyperspace insertion, strong shield endurance, and a robust sensor/jammer suite make it ideal for contested systems and perimeter security.

Often deployed in "Spear Vanguard" flotillas, Protector-class ships operate at the tip of the spear — clearing lanes for larger vessels, escorting convoys, and punishing pirate or insurgent fleets with speed and precision.
