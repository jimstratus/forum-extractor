
# 🛡️ Pathfinder-Class Transport Cruiser (PCTC) Overview

The Pathfinder-class (designation PCTC) is the Imperial Republic Navy’s premier mid-weight transport vessel, designed for rapid logistics missions, strategic supply drops, and planetary deployment operations where larger ships like the Horus-class would be too cumbersome.

At 750 meters in length, the Pathfinder-class is engineered for versatility — capable of planetary landing, orbital cargo drops, and troop deployment while under electronic warfare conditions.

With a 450,000-ton cargo capacity and a modular internal bay structure, the Pathfinder can quickly adapt to different mission profiles: medical supply transport, troop reinforcement, humanitarian relief, or forward base construction.

Lightly armed with 10 laser cannons, 10 heavy lasers, 5 turbolasers, and missile launchers, the ship relies primarily on escorts and speed to avoid prolonged engagements.

The Pathfinder’s ability to dock, land planetside, and quickly unload makes it a vital part of frontline operations, particularly in unpredictable or contested theaters of war.

Pathfinder groups are commonly organized into "Spearhead Convoys," blazing safe routes through unstable territories before heavier logistical fleets arrive.
