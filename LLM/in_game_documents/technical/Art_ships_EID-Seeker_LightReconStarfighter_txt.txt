
# 🛡️ EID-Seeker Light Recon Scout Overview

The EID-Seeker is the fastest and most agile of the Imperial Republic’s dedicated scout starfighters. Designed for rapid reconnaissance and tactical information gathering, the Seeker sacrifices heavy armor and firepower in favor of superior sublight speed and maneuverability.

Equipped with dual missile launchers, ion cannons, and enhanced sensor and jamming suites, the Seeker can dash into hostile territory, relay critical battlefield data, and withdraw before enemy forces can react.

Its compact, streamlined frame allows atmospheric flight and deep-space operations, making it a favorite among planetary scouts, forward observers, and special operations forces. Piloted by a single operator, the EID-Seeker embodies the principle of "speed is survival."
