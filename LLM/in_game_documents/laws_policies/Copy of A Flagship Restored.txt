Authors: Stratus, Quick
Title: A Flagship Restored
Location: Kuat
Synopsis: The Prince's Pride is currently located at the Kuat Drive Yards for extensive repairs and upgrades/overhauls as a result from the Battle of Coruscant (Palace Storm Scenario). During this time, many attempts are made to sabotage these efforts by various groups, and eventually a strike of the KDY employees and a political crisis will erupt. The combined political efforts of the Senate and members of the Throne are able to subside these efforts.
Character Development Goals: Captain Halcyon to bond with the crew and earn their respect and loyalty to his command and to the High Prince, as well as with the rest of the fleet. Lieutenant Jenna Deirdre to retrain and rebuild the legendary Phoenix Squadran and grow to take command of the fleet's squadrans. Lieutenant Fel...well...to keep him busy until we find something that he likes to do. (He wants to transfer to Army - which won't matter once the merger of the combined Navy/Army forces takes place).
Conflicts to present to players:
DIRECT CONFLICT
-Raids of anti-IR groups on the shipyards.
-Suspicious cargo or ships - (trying to planet explosives on the shipyards).
-
POLITICAL SIDE
-Strikes by KDY Employees
