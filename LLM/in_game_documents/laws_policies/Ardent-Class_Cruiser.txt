# 🛡️ Ardent-Class Cruiser (ACC) Overview

The Ardent-Class Cruiser (designation ACC) is the Imperial Republic’s premier medium assault cruiser — a fast, aggressive warship designed to spearhead rapid strike operations, planetary insertions, and fleet screening maneuvers.

At 750 meters in length, the Ardent sacrifices some endurance and raw firepower compared to larger cruisers in exchange for increased maneuverability, rapid-response capability, and battlefield adaptability.

Armed with a mixture of 30 laser cannons, 20 heavy lasers, 20 turbolasers, and 10 missile launchers, the Ardent is able to engage multiple classes of enemy ships simultaneously while supporting fighter wings and ground operations.

Its ability to perform planetary landings — rare among heavy cruisers — makes it uniquely suited to ground force delivery, assault spearheads, and emergency evacuation missions.

Carrying 6 squadrons of starfighters and 3,500 troopers, along with vehicles for rapid surface deployments, the Ardent often leads Vanguard Assault Groups into contested systems.

Highly respected for their aggressive tactics and swift battlefield command presence, Ardent-class ships symbolize the Republic’s philosophy of determined advance, relentless engagement, and fiery resolve.
