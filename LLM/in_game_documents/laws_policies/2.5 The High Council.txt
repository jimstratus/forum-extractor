Imperial High Council and Imperial Branches
All Recruits should familiarize themselves with the current staff of the Imperial High Council. Ignorance in the face of the Republic is unacceptable. 
The Supreme Ruler
The Republic itself is an absolutist force led by his majesty the Supreme Ruler. The Supreme Ruler is the supreme leader of the Imperial Republic and all aspects of Imperial life. Although he is responsible for the overall leadership of the Republic he does not directly oversee the command of units or fleets or governments. He directly commands the Imperial High Council, which in turn command the organizations, and units that compose the various branches of the Republic. Of course the Supreme Ruler may intervene in the day-to-day operations of the Republic at his discretion. If it were not for the Supreme Ruler the societies of the galaxy would have collapsed, as did the Republic.
The Executor
The Executor is a member of the Imperial Throne and in all rights and respects is the second in command of the Republic. The Chief of Staff’s responsibilities range from tending to personnel management, to ensuring the Branches and Sectors function efficiently and smoothly. The Chief of Staff will also conduct activities as directed by the Emperor.
The Sector Moffs and Sector Groups
The Sector Moffs are chiefly responsible for the leadership of their respective Sectors. Within their sectors they are not only responsible for the planets and systems but also the cultural, economic and military aspects of their Sector. Each Moff is the supreme commander of a Sector Group, which contains an Imperial Republic Armed Forces Fleet and an Imperial Republic Armed Forces Legion.
The Grand Minister and COMPNOR
The Commission for the Preservation of the New Order is a civilian organization that is responsible for the recruitment, education, and security of imperial citizens. The Select Committee administers COMPNOR; a group comprised of appointed individuals and COMPNOR department heads. The Grand Vizier leads both the Select Committee and COMPNOR Itself. COMPNOR currently has several active departments within its control including the Imperial Republic Security Bureau (IRSB) and the Sub-Adult Group.
Imperial Republic Intelligence Services
Imperial Republic Intelligence Services is the division responsible for investigating and eliminating all threats that originate from an external source (while the IRSB deals with security issues and internal threats). They operate covertly and report to the Director of Imperial Intelligence. The Director reports to the Minister of State, who then reports to the Supreme Ruler. Little of Imperial Republic Intelligence Services is publicly known and even less is known of their operations. Imperial Intelligence also assists in the creation and moderation of Imperial scenarios.
Imperial Republic Armed Forces
The Imperial Army is responsible for the defense and security of Imperial planets and when required, Naval ships. The Army is used for all land engagements, be it defending an Imperial planet or attacking an unfriendly world. The Imperial Army is composed of Corps, which in turn are comprised of two Divisions, each of which contains three Brigades. The Imperial Army includes branches such as the StormCorps, Mobile Armored Corps and the Scout/Recon units. The highest positions currently employed in the Imperial Army are that of the Corps Commanding Officer, Division Commanding Officer and the Line Commanding Officer.
Imperial Navy
The Imperial Navy is chiefly responsible for the policing, patrolling and defense of Imperial space. The Navy is also responsible for offensive actions and for the transport of army units into combat theatres. It consists of all capital ship units, logistics units and the Imperial Navy Starfighter Corps. The Imperial Navy is composed of Fleets, which in turn are comprised of two Squadrons, each of which contains three Lines. The highest positions currently employed in the Imperial Navy are that of the Fleet Commanding Officer, Squadron Commanding Officer and the Line Commanding Officer.
