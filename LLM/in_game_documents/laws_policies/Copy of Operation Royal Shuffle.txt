Phase 1 - Now to 6 months from now
1. Praetor Bryan Dugaan, High Minister of COMPNOR for Management, Policy & Operations, is promoted to High Minister of COMPNOR, Innovation, & Operational Excellence. In this role Bryan will provide thought leadership and his entrepreneurial spirit to drive Innovation and Operational Excellence through all divisions of the Imperial Republic. Bryan will also now assist me in the planning, implementation, and execution of COMPNOR’s Long-Term Strategy and Strategic Plans. 
2. Prince Marc Stratus, Grand Moff of Kuat System/Sector & President and Chief Operating Officer of Kuat Drive Yards; will move to Coruscant and be promoted to Grand Moff of the Kuat Sector, Chief Executive Officer of Kuat Drive Yards, and Sr. Minister of COMPNOR Initiatives. 
3. Ryan Hafele, Minister of Transportation; will fill in as General Manager of Kuat Drive Yards, until a suitable President & Chief Operating Officer is found. 
4. William Benson, Minister of Finance; will fill in as Moff of the Kuat System, reporting to Marc.
5. Intentionally Deleted

7. Micheal Orson, Minister of Strategy & Strategic Planning will now report to Bryan Dugaan.
8. Raphael Murieax, Sr. Minister of COMPNOR Shared Services is promoted to Sr. Minister & COMPNOR General Manager. Reporting to Ambassador Murieax will be Justice, Finance,  Labor, and Transportation. Operationally, the Imperial Republic Security Bureau, Dr. Ingraham, and Admiral Howe will also report to Ambassador Murieax; though officially they report to Prateor Dugaan.
9. Angela Ingraham, Sr. Minister of Education & Heritage and Valorumian Institute President,  will assume the role of IR Chief Financial Officer and lead the annual budgeting process for the entire IR and lead the Ministry of Finance. Effectively this means that Tom Winter, Karcye Tanex, Derrick Olsen, Minister of Education, and Damien Turane, Deputy Minister of Finance will report to Angela during this time.
-THIS SECTION DOES NOT YET APPLY 10. With the recent War Declaration against the Sith. I have asked Dr. Alexander Westin, Minister of Health, to serve until a candidate can be identified as Deputy Minister of Defense for Combat Medicine - COMPNOR Joint Services. With the projection of dealing with a lot of battlefield injuries for an extended period of time, it will beneficial to have Dr. Westin with his tremendous field experience working in Natural Disasters aftermaths to provide executive leadership to this function and develop a One-System approach to Battlefield Medicine. In Dr. Westin’s absence, the Ministry of Health will report directly to Executor Treyson, who will assume the duties and responsibilities of the Minister of Health.
11. Emily Marley, President & Chief Operations Officer of Santhe Corporation, will join COMPNOR as Executive-in-Residence. Emily Marley’s first assignment will be as Executive-Designate at KDY. Emily Marley will report to Marc Stratus.
12. Shannon Kline Principal Deputy Minister of Public Affairs will serve as Minister of Transportation in the Interim.
13. Michael Praxton, Deputy Minister of Public Relations & IR Press Secretary will assume overall leadership of the Ministry of Public Affairs as Acting Minister of Public Affairs, but will primarily focus on his current responsibilities, while he assumes the additional responsibility for providing executive leadership for the long-term strategy of the Ministry of Public Affairs. Diandra would become Deputy Minister of Public Affairs for Operations reporting to Michael, and overseeing the day-to-day operations of the Ministry of Public Affairs. This will not only be a tremendous help to the Ministry of Public Affairs, but also a tremendous learning opportunity for Diandra as she learns from one of our top media strategists, and will then be a stronger asset to HMPS. Michael Praxton and the Ministry of Public Affairs will report to Executor Treyson.

Barbara will begin her new career with the Imperial Republic by being loaned out to serve as the first and Interim Division Comptroller General for the Ministry of Defense.
15. Dr. Dan Vespucci, Dean of the Coruscant University's School of Public Policy, will join COMPNOR as Executive-in-Residence and loaned out to serve as the first and Interim Division Counsel for the Ministry of State. 
16. Dr. Adele Montgomery, President of Theed University will join COMPNOR as Executive-in-Residence and loaned out to serve as the first and Interim Division Comptroller General for the Ministry of State.
