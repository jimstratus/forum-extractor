The Phoenix, accompanied by the Royal Command Fleet left Coruscant in the middle of the night after the mysterious launch of a shuttle from the Imperial Palace. 
Dana was in the refresher inside the Emperor’s Suite, a multi-room quarters with a bathroom, bedroom, living room, foyer, office, and private communications center. The Super battlecruiser had five more such suites on the vessel, should the rare event happen in which the entire Throne and First Family was on board at the same time.
She was notified of the door chime when she was still in the refresher. The guards did not move to answer as the droid was handling things for her. They stayed in position as the droid admitted the person to the foyer. She pressed the intercom and listened in and recognized the voice of the Admiral of the Fleet, Grand Admiral Jon Tinkelle. 
The droid was about to send him away when she keyed the two way intercom and asked him to wait in the foyer and told her droid to make him comfortable. She would be out shortly. She looked at her attire that had been laid out for her, then got dizzy, and fell down to her knees. There was no way she could dress herself at this rate. She put a robe on, doubling up the layers, knowing Jim would not approve, but she did not have much of a choice. She then made her way out to the suite’s foyer. The look of shock on the Grand Admiral’s face brought a small smile to her face. Before addressing him she brought her loyal droid to her and whispered something to him. From what the Admiral could see they seemed to get into some sort of argument or disagreement. But this droid had been with her since she started with IRIS and was extremely loyal to her alone, though a few small subroutines had been added to prevent it from turning against the Royals, since it had security features of its own. Finally it said with a very reluctant voice, “As you command, Your Majesty.” then left the suite.
The Admiral had his hand up to cover his eyes and his head down. “Your Majesty.” he bowed. “May I ask….?”
“No, it is a private matter.”

“Of course. Perhaps I should come back later Your Majesty when Your Majesty is not indisposed.” 
“No, Admiral. You are already here. Say what it is you came to say.”
“As you wish, Your Majesty. The fleet has arrived at Kuat. The Royal Palace, Fleet Headquarters and the Marine Corps Garrison base has been alerted to our arrival. I have ordered General Hoffner to deploy her Royal Guard Marines attached to our fleet to secure the areas surrounding the Royal Palace and the Marine Corps base. They are ready and waiting for your arrival. The Princess Royal suggests we stop at the Marine base first and then go to the Palace for government and Royal family business.”
“Fine. I’ll need some more time to prepare. Is there anything else?”

“No, Ma’am”
“Then you are Dismissed.”
The Grand Admiral bowed, spun on his heels and left the room. He passed a 2-1B droid along with a cart of medical equipment, with the Empress’ protocol droid in their trail. It was a bit strange, he thought. He turned to see if they were entering the Emperor’s Suite but they turned the corner before the Suite’s entrance. He dismissed the thought and walked away. 
The protocol droid led them to an exterior wall on the side of the suite where an opening appeared out of nowhere and a grey alien hand ushered them in. The Noghri inside inspected the equipment and then allowed them to proceed with the door quickly closed behind them They had to keep this from the regular Royal Guards. Dana swore the Royal Guards to secrecy. She also ordered the 2-1B not to diagnose her but to simply give her a vitamin IV-drip. She did not want a diagnosis because once it happened, the droids, the Noghri, they would all be forced to act on it, perhaps even her. She promised them all she would get a thorough check up after the mission was completed. She had a full medical team dedicated to her on board the ship that followed her everywhere whenever she traveled. Some were on Kuat, some on Coruscant, and some that traveled with her. 
She finished her vitamin treatment, which took a while, and received help changing into her royal outfit from the two droids.She was finally ready, and moved to the hangar where her Horus-class Troop Carrier was waiting, and a EID-Falcon and EID-Eagle squadron had launched to escort it down. 
Horus Troop Carrier 29-921 landed at the Marine base landing pad without incident and the massive entourage disembarked, including Grand Admiral Tinkelle, who would have been in command if it hadn’t been for the fact that Lord Admiral Firecam was waiting for them at the bottom of the ramp. General Megan De`Soto had preceded them. He greeted the Queen of Kuat with a bow and led the way to the base as the group inspected went on an inspection tour. They were soon joined by two Paladins who had been re-routed from their current assignment. MCIS agents, IRSB agents, MPs and IRIS agents from multiple directorates were on site evaluating the evidence.
Princess Nicole had joined them as well, accompanying the Queen. The Queen made her observations in her datapad, cringing silently to herself a few times in pain, hoping nobody would notice. Unfortunately the Princess Nicole who was very powerful in the Force sensed her sudden shift in brain pattern and mood but said nothing at that time.. The other Force-sensitive Paladin thankfully was on the other side of the base and would either not notice, not identify her, or not be able to react at all. When it happened a second time the Princess suggested they move to the Palace, stating they had probably learned all they could for now until the investigators completed their analysis. The Queen agreed, finishing her notes on her datapad. They took the Carrier to the Royal Palace. As they walked from the hangar to Throneroom an incredible debilitating pain and dizziness overcame Dana and she collapsed. The Princess, who had sensed something was wrong before she collapsed, was already on her comlink. “I need a medical team to the Palace East Wing immediately.”
