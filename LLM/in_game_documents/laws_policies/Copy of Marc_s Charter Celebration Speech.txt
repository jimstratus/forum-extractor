I hope everyone enjoyed that delicious meal. I just want to take the opportunity to thank everyone that has worked to make this evening happen. I am delighted that we could have all of you here to celebrate this is very happy and exciting evening with my family and the Imperial Republic Leadership. 
This evening is in celebration of the Grand Revision of the Charter. Something that you could say has been in the making for over thirty-three years. We have been through it all, these past thirty-three years. We have seen both good and bad times. We have lived through economic prosperity and economic depression. We have seen our galaxy and our government change in our very own lifetimes.  
The Imperial Republic has survived for over thirty-three years and that is an amazing accomplishment that is directly attributable to many of the people in this very room.  But it isn’t the Imperial Republic’s continued success that we celebrate, it is the principles and ideals that the Imperial Republic was founded on, and that the Grand Revision of the Charter has guaranteed. Throughout history we have seen beacons of liberty and freedom fall prey to corruption, greed, and wars fought over territories, materialistic goods, and power rather than principles. The Sith Empire is waging a war against our very own way of life, a war against our very own existence. They intended to destroy the Imperial Republic, the moral epicenter of principled, moral thought and freedom in our galaxy. We celebrate tonight our principles and ideals, which have made the Imperial Republic. But we also remember that we are now at war with an enemy that will fight to the death, and that we have no option but to be the last one standing.  We can’t let this war divide our republic, for if we fracture the only outcome will be our ultimate defeat.  On behalf of the people of the Imperial Republic, I would like to thank all of the members of our Armed Forces for their commitment to service.
In a few moments, you will from the Supreme Chancellor, followed by the High Prince, the Executor, and then the Minister of Defense.
However, I would like to say a few words first
I have never gotten around to saying a job well done so I would like to recognize Baron Blake Lexington Tagge, Economic Advisor to the Executor and his team; Nathan Gilmore, General Counsel to the Executor; Rachel Love, General Counsel to the High Prince; Praetor Duggan; and the Executor for all of their hard work in the acquisition of Kuat Drive Yards. They will all be receiving a model crystal replica of the Prince’s Pride II.
