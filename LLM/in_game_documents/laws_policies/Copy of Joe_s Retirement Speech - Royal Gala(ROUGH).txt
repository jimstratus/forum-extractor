Joe listened attentively to both the Executor and the High Prince, nodding slightly in respect each time his name was brought up. It was a joyous event for him. He was being honored for his service to the Imperial Republic and its people. Joe found it hard to believe it had been over 3 decades, the Imperial Republic has blossomed and he was proud to have been able to give to its cause. He was anxious to finally be able to retire to his home planet of Corellia where he could focus on the other passions in his life.
“Loyal citizens today, I bring news with a heavy heart. After a long and rewarding career within the Imperial Republic I have officially decided to retire as Supreme Chancellor. Today marks another historic moment for the Imperial Republic. Tomorrow my successor will be announced to replace me as the President of the Imperial Republic Senate, the voice and living embodiment of the people of the Imperial Republic. While my successor will not be the second Supreme Chancellor ever, they will be the coveted President of the Senate, only the second that there has ever been. ascend the title and position in which I have held since the creation of this very office.” Joe nodded to the people around him. “It brings me tremendous honor to announce Prince Dene-Vye Cognatus as the new Supreme Chancellor.” He waves his hand towards Dene.
“Now now, even though Prince Cognatus has been an undying and loyal soldier, and even though he led the ground forces to attack the Royal Palace and destroyed much of the palace’s entrance, I have no doubt his ability to lead within the military will play heavily into his new role in which I have the utmost faith in him. I can only hope that he doesn’t destroy the Palace on his first day, as I would like to return for a free State Dinner or two. Though in reality, I will probably duck out after dinner, so that I can miss the speeches as they won’t be all about me.” A light chuckle could be heard flowing through the hall. “Prince Cognatus, there isn't much fighting here as there was in the Ministry of Defense, so I do ask you leave your warships and battle tanks behind as you ready yourself for a lifetime of paperwork, senatorial duties, and of course the occasional party.” He chuckled.
“A dear friend and colleague of mine, known almost universally to the rest of the Imperial Republic Government as the Dark Scrooge of Finance, Minister William Benson, was kind enough to present me with a gift earlier today. As I will no longer be the Supreme Chancellor, President and Chairman of the Senate, Most-High-Moff, Ambassador-in-Chief, and all; Bill was kind enough to present me a plaque with my brand new title on it, ‘President of Nil. You can always count on Bill’s humor to brighten up a teary-eyed day at work.”
“Enough poking fun though, Dene, my friend you have a challenging road ahead of yourself and I wish to bestow some wisdom that I had acquired during my three decades at the helm of the Imperial Republic.” (Enter Advice here…)  Do not expect to be best friends with everyone in the Senate, in the Media, in the Ministry of Defense, in foreign governments, in our Regional Oversectors, in the Ministry of State, or in COMPNOR. The Senators will try to rollercoaster you at every opportunity; as they are masters of politics. The Media will treat everything you tell them as a story, they need to be first at the scoop after all. The Ministry of Defense won’t be happy with galactic peace, as they become redundant. The foreign governments will be jealous of the Imperial Republic or just generally feel like you are the big bad bully on the playground. The Regional Oversectors will feel like you are meddling in their business. The Ministry of State will think they can do your job better than you. And COMPNOR, they will be busy missing me.”
