First year of the IR- Coruscant was made the capitol after the Corellian Sector's governor, Joe Quick, convinced them that it was their rightful place to be the capitol of yet another glorious government. James Stratus was elected leader of the Galaxy, Supreme Ruler of the IR, Layha Solo was appointed Grand Minister of COMPNOR, which oversees all the civilian aspects of the galactic government, Ieyena Cohean was commissioned as a Fleet Admiral and the first “Admiral of the Fleet”. Joe Quick was appointed Supreme Chancellor, and put in charge of the overall system of Sector governments, the legislative bodies, and the Ministry of State. Ieyena Cohean is informed that her fiancé (Greg) is presumed dead.

Year Two- With the assistance of Ieyena Cohean, the newly appointed Minister of Defense, the military was expanded, which convinced several systems to join the government because of the security it could offer. Layha was named 1st Executor of the Imperial Republic, the right hand of the Throne and an actual PART of the Throne. With Layha and James at the helm the rise of the Imperial Republic, the Security Bureau began.

Year Three- Due to diplomatic missions, the planets and systems of Hapes, Bilbringi, Yavin, Dathomir, Naboo, Tatooine, and several others were added to the Imperial Republic. Eric Jackson was named 2nd Executor of the Imperial Republic.

Year Four- Daniella Organa was appointed to the position of Deputy Minister of Defense and secretly given the command of the Imperial Republic's Special Operations force. In the same year, the Imperial Republic Security Bureau was completed and Grand Minster Layha Solo appointed two generals to lead the IRSB, General Damon Delyanie and Loke. Togorian War broke out.

Year Five- The IR engaged in battle.(Battle of Bilbringi) During this battle, Captain Lonewolf misguidedly informed his troops to fire upon two friendly transports, killing several citizens of the IR. He was court marshaled, and stripped of his position and was tried for treason, and was later executed. In this same year, Vice Admiral Cencori retired after the battle due to personal reasons. Daniella Organa and Admiral Eric Jackson were married.

Year Six- The Commission for the Preservation of History and Life was created and Myera Jacobson was placed in charge as High Minister. Earth was discovered. Ranea Nonstir was released from Kessle under the supervision of the IRSB and was promised by Executor Layha Solo-Katarn that with her assistants she could regain her freedom and a clean record. 

Year Seven- Oho Gomao was given command of the IRSB by Layha Solo and made General. Diego Cavaliero recieved the rank advancement from Rear Admiral to Vice Admiral. Auri came under the guidance and mothership of Daniella Organa-Jackson. Daniella Organa and Eric Jackson divorced.

Year Eight - Alanna Lightstar was promoted to Grand Admiral and given command of the Second Assault Fleet. The First Assault Fleet had been assigned to Victor Cencori earlier that year. Executor Layha Solo and Daniella Organa travel to Arrakis, there Daniella is infected with a virus. Executor Layha Solo and Kyle Katarn are married. Colette Miller became the assistant to the Executors. 

Year Nine- Daniella Organa remarried Executor Eric Jackson. During the Battle of the Pitates with space pirates in the system of Anobis, Myera Jackobson was killed and a wounded woman later known to be Myera Jackobson’s sister, was found in the pirate's abandoned base. At the end of year nine, the Phoenix Squadron, the first Commando squadron, was commissioned. 
Year Ten- Celebration of the IR's first Decade. Sightings of a secret society called the Shadow Kong are reported.
Year Eleven- Executor Eric Jackson and his son, Jacob were traveling to Tatooine when their ship was attacked ending in the death of the Executor and his son. Stricken with grief, his wife’s (Daniella Organa-Jackson) health began to decline. The Queen Mother of Hapes requested for her granddaughter to be kept on her home world of Gallinor. During a mission to rescue Auri (Daniella and Eric’s daughter) from the Shadow Kong, Kyle Katarn, husband of Executor Layha Solo-Katarn disappeared and was presumed to be dead.

Year Twelve- A new senator Stephen Roquen, was selected for the planet of Qua'tali. Jenov Raveshaw named Director of Imperial Republic Intelligence. Battlecreek named Deputy Director of Intelligence.
Year Thirteen- The Adar and Angel squadrons are created and began there train. Kyle Katarn, husband Of Executor Layha Solo-Kartarn, returns to Corascant.  IRSB reports of a Program started by the Shadow Kong called Kidstop. Overmind is found in suspended in hibernation aboard his ship THE STORM by Kaidlen Shan.

Year Fourteen; Daniella Organa-Jackson returned to Coruscant for medical treatment.  Ieyena Cohean began spying on the Throne to find information. Daniella Organa-Jackson is moved to Hapes by order of the High Prince. First Annual Minstery of Defense Galla. 
Year Fifteen- The presumed dead Executor Eric Jackson returns to Coruscant. Daniella Organa-Jackson death is announced…. Ieyena steps down from her commanding position of Minister of Defense. Ieyena Cohean named Chief of Special Operations and commissioned to rank of Command General. Admiral OVERMIND named Minister of Defense Vice Admiral Firecam named Interim Chief of Naval Operations. Brigadier General Cognatus named Interim Army Chief of Staff. Guinar Ndengin named Moff of Coruscant Sector. Vice Admiral Firecam named Chief of Naval Operations and promoted to Admiral.
Year Sixteen- Oho Gomao goes on mission and is presumed dead. Gomao secretly betrays Stratus and the Imperial Republic (manages somehow to keep Stratus from detecting this for a long time), and heads up the Des’Maric Criminal Consortium. The IRSB Head Quarters is infiltrated and collapses. All Agents are reported dead. Coral Dayton is appointed new IRSB General. Admiral Eric Jackson takes command of a special Forces unit. Martial Law declared on Corellia, Coruscant, Kuat.
Year Seventeen- Daniella Organa-Jackson’s body is reassembled and she is brought back to life. Admiral OVERMIND promoted to Fleet Admiral.
