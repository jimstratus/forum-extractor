Part 1
The Screen on the HoloNet flashes BREAKING NEW and switches to a human female reporter with long blond hair and green-gray eyes. "Good Evening this just in a riot has broken out over a labor dispute at two of the major Ship building yards. They are the Kuat Drive Yards and the Corellian Engineering Corporation. The  apparent   cause of the disputes is unclear at this time but we will keep you apprised of any new information as we get it. So far there has been only 50 injured as a result of the rioting and Security Forces are hard pressed to keep the situation under control. This Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network signing off."
Part 2
BREAKING NEWS flashes a crossed the HoloNet and fades to show Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network. "Good evening we have just received an update on the riots taking place at the Kuat Drive Yards. Our correspondent Zefel is at the scene and has more on the story."
The camera switches to the Kuat Drive Yards where Zefel a gray skinned humanoid with glowing blue eyes takes over. "Thank you Kayla, I'm here with one of the workers now. He name is Kay'lor Keltar and he tells me he is the union leader for Kuat Drive Yards. So Kay'lor what can you tell me of the current situation?" The camera panes over to Kay'lor a tall muscular human about 6ft high wearing dark blue coveralls and cap, that gave him an aura of authority. "Well Zefel it's like this we walked into work today and 150 of us fired and the rest of us were told that we should expect a decrease of ¼ our regular pay. As you can imagine it has not gone over to well. That's why we went on strike; unfortunately some of the boys have gotten a little out of hand." In the background you see workers holding signs and hear them shouting protests. Then a fight breaks out between an officer and some of the workers. The reporter yelp in surprise as the Union leader gets knocked into the camera and the feed goes dead.
The camera view returns to a concerned looking Kayla who recovers enough to wrap up the report. "There you have it the situation is quite serious and we hope it can be settled soon. Stay tuned for further updates. This Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network signing off." In the background can be hear someone cursing. 
Part3
BREAKING NEWS flashes a crossed the HoloNet and fades to show Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network. "Good evening we have just received an update on the riot taking place at the Kuat Drive Yards. At present we are unable to get a hold of our correspondent Zefel, but we are receiving from the IRSB is that the sector is off limits as they try to bring the situation in to control. We have received unconfirmed reports 15 fatalities and 60 seriously wounded. No word on how soon the situation will be under control only that the IRSB is currently working on the situation. As for the Corellian Engineering Corporation strike there has been no violence as a result as we had thought earlier. From what we have gathered the strike is over the loss of medical benefits for the workers families. The workers refuse to go back to work until the situation is resolved. We will continue to keep you informed of any updated as they come, this Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network."
Part 4 (I maybe spiting this into two parts)
BREAKING NEWS flashes a crossed the HoloNet to show Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network. “Good Evening I’m Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network. We have more on the ongoing crisis at the Ship Yards. It has been a week now since the trouble first started at the Kuat Drive Yards. What Started as a simple protest over unfair treatment of workers turned into a riot that has been going on for week now. We have received sketchy reports that some of the ships in the docks have been rigged with explosive. The workers are demanding their jobs back or they will blow up the Ship Yards with everything in them. With the body count rising, some have expressed concern that the situation is falling apart.” She pauses to look at her notes then continues. “On the side of the issue is the Corellian Engineering Corporation strike there has been no violence but it is causing damage just the same. From what we have gathered the strike is over the loss of medical benefits for the workers families. The workers refuse to go back to work until the situation is resolved. However every day they’re on strike is costing the Imperial Republic greatly as its affect are felt throughout. Some of the major trade Corporations waiting for repair and maintains for their shipping fleets are being forced to forgo it. This makes for a dangerous situation as they will have to risk using ships in need of repair or let many its customers starve. Which they can’t do because many of their customers are mining colonizes or other such worlds that rely on outside provisions to survive. That is why this such a bad situation not just because some beings lost their jobs or medical befits but because we all are suffering as a result. Already there are reports of food riots on Santex III out in the outer rim as thousands are starving because the trade vessel Nexus was destroyed due to equipment failure that had been scheduled to be fixed. The Situation look grave indeed, this Kayla Katorain for the Galactic News Network."
