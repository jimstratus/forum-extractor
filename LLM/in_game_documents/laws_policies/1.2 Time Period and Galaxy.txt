This Role-Playing Experience takes place before Star Wars: A New Hope. The Imperial Republic is still relatively young but is powerful and organized none-the-less. The Galaxy and the role-playing that occurs attempts to be as canon as possible with regards to the Star Wars universe; technology, organizations, races, etc, are all based on canon movies and literature. The only item that is not canon within this Star Wars Universe is the characters.
There is no Luke Skywalker, or Darth Vader. There is no Jabba the Hutt, or Boba Fett. We feel that this adds more depth and opportunity for players in the universe. This means the outcome of the great civil war is not certain. This opens the doors to an infinite number of possibilities.
