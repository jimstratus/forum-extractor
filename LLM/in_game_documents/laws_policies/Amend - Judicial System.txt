Section 4-The Judiciary
Article 4.0 - Judicial Powers
The judicial powers of the Imperial Republic shall be invested in its court system as outlined in the remainder of this section. The State as referenced in below sections shall be defined as the Galactic Government of the Imperial Republic.
Article 4.1 - Courts and Judicates of the Imperial Republic
Definitions of Judicate: A Judicate is a high court at the State or Galactic level with three or more judges called Judicators. Decisions made by Judicates require a majority vote of the Judicators serving on the Judicate.
Article 4.1.a - The Royal Supreme Court
The Royal Supreme Court shall consists of members of the Royal Family. This court is restricted to matters involving a member of the Royal Family or a member of the Throne. If a member of the court is summoned by said court, they may not participate in the judiciary powers allowed to the court. The Royal Supreme Court, consisting of the Royal Family, alone has the authority to call to convention the Supreme Electorate for consideration of removal of the Supreme Ruler, and may independently act concerning any member of the Throne.
Article 4.1.b -  Royal Throne Tribunal
The Royal Throne Tribunal shall consist of three members of the Throne, one being the Supreme Ruler or Regent, the other two selected from among the Throne membership by the Supreme Ruler for each convention. This body usually handles matters pertaining to Throne members other than the Supreme Ruler or Regent, but including agents of the Throne, members of the High Council, and other cases of high importance. This body may take original jurisdiction from any lower court. In the unlikely event that an Executor or Grand Vizier is summoned before this tribunal the Supreme Ruler shall select an agent of the Throne to sit on the body. The decisions made by the tribunal cannot be overruled by any other inferior court.
Article 4.1.c - The High Judicate
The High Judicate is, primarily, the highest government court. Though there are two higher courts, they are rarely used or called to session and generally defer matters to lower courts as they specialize in handling matters for specific circumstances only. The High Judicate handles high crimes committed by civilians or members of the Armed Forces, though generally military matters are brought first before a military court and later deferred to the High Judicate based on crimes or offenses committed, generally at the discretion of the military court. The High Judicate has the authority to take original jurisdiction on any matter if a petition is submitted by government officials or parties in question (granted that the case warrants their attention). 
Article 4.1.d - The High Council
The High Council shall have the authority to overturn the decisions made by any court system and handle a case themselves by majority vote except those of the High Judicate or higher courts. 
Article 4.1.e - Royal Judicate
The Royal Judicate shall have jurisdiction over the Royal Territories of the Imperial Republic. The Royal Judicate shall be divided into districts and regions based on population and sovereignty. The Royal Judicate shall consist of three members of the Great Houses of the Royal Territories, who shall be of sound mind, on a rotating basis. Decisions made by the Royal Judicate can be appealed to the Royal Imperial Throne or Royal Supreme Court. The Throne or the Royal Supreme Court may take original jurisdiction on cases brought before the Royal Judicate. Matters involving the Hapes Consortium and other Royal Territories shall automatically be elevated to the Royal Imperial Throne for determination on which Judicate or Court shall hear the case. 
Article 4.1.f Oversector and Regional Judicates
Oversector and Regional Judicates at the State (galactic) level shall deal with civil matters having jurisdiction within an oversector or a regions within an oversector. 
Article 4.1.g - Sector Judicate
A Sector Judicate is a civil court at the State-level, having jurisdiction over an entire sector. It cooperates and reports to the Sector Governor. Matters concerning the Sector Moff or Governor are generally brought to the attention of the Supreme Chancellor and generally brought before the High Judicate. 
Article 4.1.h - Courts of Local Governments
Each local government below the Sector-level shall have power by vote of the people to establish and maintain local courts comprised of inquisitors and inspectors, judges, and juries, where applicable, to handle violations of local laws and ordinances.
Article 4.1.i - Secret Tribunals
A secret tribunal may be convened when the subject-matter, person(s), or crimes concern State Security. The members of the tribunal shall be chosen by . The State Security Council, members of the Royal Imperial Throne, High Council or High Judicate may declare that a case must be tried in secret. Cases involving Intelligence operatives, operations, Special Forces or classified combat operations automatically require the case to be tried in secret, whether by a Secret State Tribunal or Secret Military Tribunal. 
Article 4.1.j - Military Tribunal
A military tribunal shall have jurisdiction over persons who are members of an enemy army, are held in military custody, and are accused of a violation of the laws of war. Military tribunals may be convened in secret if individuals or subject-matter concern State Security and a determination is made by the State Security Council, Supreme Chancellor, Minister of Defense, High Council, or other members of the Throne.
Military tribunals also may assert jurisdiction over people who are acknowledged to be non-combatants who are alleged to have broken civil or criminal laws in territories under military occupation by the Imperial Republic Armed Forces or Imperial Republic Territories under martial law. Judges in military tribunals shall be officers of the Armed Forces having a degree in military and/or civil law who are also licensed to practice law in that district, region, sector, or the Judge Advocate General of the Armed Forces. The convening authority shall also have authority to appoint civilian personnel with (an) equivalent law degree(s) that are duly licensed to practice law in that district, region, or sector.
Article 4.1.k - Uniformed Code of Military Justice
The Uniformed Code of Military Justice is a separate document that shall contain the laws, protocols, policies and procedures for handling crimes committed by members of the Armed Forces and Ministry of Defense personnel. The document shall be established and maintained by the Imperial Republic High Council in coordination with the Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Defense. Changes to the document can be made or authorized by the High Council. The High Judicate and higher courts shall have authority to declare any portion of the Code in violation of higher law as well as take original jurisdiction of any Military Court case and overrule any decision rendered by a Military Court. The Military Justice system shall have jurisdiction over all matters concerning  the Imperial Republic Armed Forces or any property thereof. High Crimes brought before a Military Court must be brought before the High Inquisitor to determine whether or not the case will be tried by the High Judicate or remain in the Military Justice system. The High Inquisition, the High Judicate, the High Council, the Royal Imperial Throne or the Royal Supreme Court may themselves decide to take jurisdiction or refer the case to a specific (higher) court.
Article 4.1.l - Appellate Courts
Courts with authority over appeals shall reside with each court level above the original court, and other courts created by the High Council, the Senate, or Executive Order.
Article 4.2 - Power to Pardon and Commute Sentence
Those with powers to pardon and/or commute the sentence of an individual of crimes committed against the Imperial Republic or its inhabitants are listed below. Those with the power to pardon may not pardon themselves or others with the same power or those of higher authority. The Supreme Ruler and Supreme Chancellor shall have galactic pardoning jurisdictions of all crimes, though the Chancellor will generally consult with the Throne before acting. Sector/Planetary Governors may pardon individuals of criminal crimes committed within their own sector/planet, unless it is a High Crime against the Imperial Republic. Grand Admirals shall have the authority to pardon individuals who committed civil crimes within a sector or territory of which they have legitimate governance in the absence of a Throne-appointed Regional Governor or during martial law;High Crimes can only be pardoned by the Throne.
Article 4.3 - Power to Arrest
Those with the power to arrest are obligated to hand custody of the arrested individual to the appropriate department that handles the type of investigation and offender in question, or by order of a court or government official possessing appropriate authority or jurisdiction of such matters. Those having diplomatic immunity due to government office or status can not be arrested by any organization unless specifically stated below. Others as indicated by legislation or executive order.
Article 4.3.a - The Royal Guard (HMRG/TRG/RG) and Palace Security Agency (PSA)
The Throne invests in the Royal Guard the authority to arrest and investigate anyone regardless of position or branch (with the exception of protectees, members and agents of the Throne, members of the High Council, and members of the Royal Family) when the safety of a protectee is in question. Those exempted from arrest except the Supreme Ruler may be arrested by warrant issued from any member of the Throne or in emergent situations by temporary warrant from the Commandant of the Guard. The Palace Security Agency is a sub-agency of the Royal Guard and shall have authority to make arrests within Palaces and other premises under their jurisdiction. They shall immediately remand anyone they arrest into the custody of the Royal Guard or other law-enforcement agency the Royal Guard shall name having jurisdiction after reviewing the reason for the arrest. The decision to transfer custody shall remain with the Commandant of the Royal Guard or his/her designee(s).
Article 4.3.b - Directorate of Counterintelligence (IRIS-DCI)
The IRIS-Directorate of Counterintelligence shall have the authority to arrest and investigate any persons in a law-enforcement, military, or intelligence, or security organization who they deem has compromised the security of the Imperial Republic, the Throne, the Royal Family, protectees of the Royal Guard, or their own department. In emergency situations or times of betrayal or treason they have the authority to take over investigations or security departments. They must obtain warrants from the Director, Supreme Chancellor or the Throne before arresting those exempt from arrest depending on the level of authority required to arrest the individual in question. 
Article 4.3.c - Imperial Republic Security Bureau (IRSB)
The Imperial Republic Security Bureau shall have the power to arrest and investigate any civilian personnel that they have good reason to take into custody in accordance with the laws of the Imperial Republic Charter and local sector, system, planetary, district, and municipality laws within the jurisdiction of the Imperial Republic. Warrants must be obtained from the Grand Minister of COMPNOR  to arrest members of the Select Committee. Likewise warrants must be obtained from the Supreme Chancellor for those with diplomatic immunity.
Article 4.3.d - Military Police and Military Criminal Investigative Service (MCIS)
The military police and Military Criminal Investigative Service (MCIS) shall have the power to arrest and investigate any members of the Armed Forces who have committed crimes while on duty or while on, in, or involving military property. They shall also have authority to arrest/investigate any personnel, civilian or military, who commit crimes on military vessels or facilities or other Ministry of Defense installations; or who obstruct or otherwise interferes with direct military operations. The Military Criminal Investigative Service shall consist of civilian personnel. The Director of the Military Criminal Investigative Service shall report to the Deputy Minister of Defense for Internal Affairs.
Article 4.3.e - Military Commanding and Executive Officers, capital warship commanders
Commanding Officers and Executive officers of units shall have the authority to arrest those under their command and require the Military Police and or MCIS to carry out the arrest and conduct an investigation for probable cause of committing a crime or violation of orders. Capital warship commanders and appropriate bridge officers may cause to have arrested and investigated any persons who commits suspicious activity aboard a vessel of the Imperial Republic Armed Forces.
Article 4.4 - Power to Detain
Ther authority to detain persons or vessels shall be vested in the same as those with authority to arrest. Additionally, the same authority shall be given to others authorized by statute or executive order. Others as indicated by legislation or executive order. 
Article 4.5 - Power to Issue Warrants
All individuals with the power to issue warrants for arrest and investigation are only entitled to issue said warrants within their sphere of authority and jurisdictions and where otherwise stated. Others as indicated by legislation or executive order.
The following entities are hereby entitled:
The Royal Supreme Court, the Royal Throne Tribunal, the High Council, the High Inquisition, the Senate, Senate Oversight Committees, the High Judicate, any Imperial Judicates, Royal Court, Military Court, Local Courts within their sphere of jurisdiction and authority. Others as indicated by legislation or executive order.
Individuals in the following positions are so authorized:
The Supreme Ruler, the Executor, the Supreme Chancellor, individual members of the High Judicate, the Chair and Vice Chair of COMPNOR, the Praetorian, the Lord Protector of the Imperial Republic, Agents of the Throne, the Inspector General of the Imperial Republic, the COMPNOR Inspector General, the State Inspector General, the Defense Inspector General (), members of the State Security Commission including division Chief Security Officers (CSOs), the Minister of Defense, Deputy Ministers of Defense and members of the Joint Chiefs of Staff of the Imperial Republic Armed Forces, the State Security Advisor, Grand Admirals, the Joint Chiefs of Staff of the Imperial Republic Armed Forces (individually), the Chief of Special Operations, the Director of Intelligence, the Deputy Director of Intelligence, the Assistant Director of Counterintelligence (IRIS), the Director-General of the Imperial Republic Security Bureau, the Commandant of the Royal Guard, members of the Royal First Family, 
Others as indicated by legislation or executive order.
Article 4.6 - High Crimes
High Crimes or offenses shall include: Conspiracy to commit Treason, Treason, High Treason, attempted or actual acts of terrorism, attempted or actual assassination of government or military personnel, espionage, murder, and conspiracy to commit murder. Crimes such as murder or conspiracy to commit murder are high crimes that are generally investigated by the lower level courts who have original jurisdiction. All others are generally investigated by higher courts.

Article 4.8 - The Power and Responsibility to Prosecute (Inquisition)
The power and duty to prosecute shall fall under the Office of the Inquisitor at the level of which laws were violated, as appointed by the executive office of that level of government. Others as indicated by legislation or executive order. The Inquisitor shall represent the level of government which is bringing charges against a party in the Court in which has primary jurisdiction except in cases in which a higher court decides to take the case or is ordered to do so by those having authority. 
Article 4.8.a - The High Inquisition and High Inquisitor(s) of the Imperial Republic
The High Inquisition is the organization or office responsible for prosecuting High Crimes against the Imperial Republic. The High Inquisition consists of four High Inquisitors, each responsible for carrying out the duties of a High Inquisitor within their level of assigned jurisdiction as prescribed below. The High Inquisition shall fall under the Ministry of Justice and be part of COMPNOR, though their duties and authority shall be for the entire Imperial Republic Government. High Inquisitors serve . All appointments to the office of an Inquisitor shall be approved by the Ministry of Justice after background checks are performed and they are cleared by the Imperial Republic Security Bureau. A separate background check and clearance is required with each re-appointment. They may be re-appointed in the same manner in which they are appointed as prescribed below. The High Inquisitors report to the Minister of Justice and also to the Throne and High Council. The High Inquisitors decide which level of Judicate will handle the legal proceedings or may defer the case and jurisdiction to a lower or local court. High Inquisitors shall perform or supervise the performance of other duties required by statute or Executive Order. The High Inquisitors shall make recommendations to the members of the Throne and High Council concerning appointments to state judicial positions and to positions with the Ministry of Justice and office of the High Inquisition including all other lower level Inquisitors and their deputies and other state court or judicate officers. High Inquisitors shall have the noble title of Lord or Lady. High Inquisitors shall not have authority to prosecute specific local crimes below the State level unless authorized to do so by the local-level equivalent to an Inquisitor for the specified locality. High Inquisitors shall not have authority over local Courts or law-enforcement agencies below the State level. In most instances, the High Inquisitors shall not provide legal advice to private citizens or organizations.
The High Inquisitor of the Imperial Republic is a member of COMPNOR, nominated by the Executor of the Imperial Republic and confirmed by the High Council (if active), otherwise the Senate. If the Senate is also not in session then the Supreme Ruler shall approve the nominee for the position. The High Inquisitor of the Imperial Republic represents the Imperial Republic Government in all legal matters, Court or Judicate trials and decides which charges on which an individual, group, or other entity are being prosecuted, in conjunction with the results of an investigation by the investigating law enforcement agency. The High Inquisitor of the Imperial Republic shall represent or supervise the representation of the Imperial Republic Government in the Royal Supreme Court of the Imperial Republic and all other courts, foreign and domestic, in which the Imperial Republic Government is a party or has an interest as may be deemed appropriate. 
The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of Defense is a member of COMPNOR, nominated by the Minister of Defense and confirmed by the High Council  (if active), otherwise the Senate. If the Senate is also not in session then the Supreme Ruler shall approve the nominee for the position. The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of Defense represents the Imperial Republic Government in all Judicate trials and decides with the Supreme Commander and the Minister of Defense, their deputies, which charges on which an individual, group, or other entity are being prosecuted, in conjunction with the results of an investigation by the investigating law enforcement agency. The High Inquisitor of the Ministry of Defense cannot hold an active military commission or rank while serving as High Inquisitor.  The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of Defense shall furnish advice and opinions, formal and informal, on legal matters to the Minister of Defense and their deputies, members of the Throne and High Council, and to the heads of departments and agencies of the Ministry of Defense, as provided or required by law. The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of Defense specifically handles high crimes against the Imperial Republic and ensures that Courts-Martial process and Uniformed Code of Military Justice is being administered and followed correctly within the Ministry of Defense, and decides, after conclusion of a General Court-Martial whether or not additional prosecution or charges are necessary at the State level in a non-military court.
The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of State  is a member of COMPNOR, nominated by the Supreme Chancellor and confirmed by the High Council  (if active), otherwise the Senate. If the Senate is also not in session then the Supreme Ruler shall approve the nominee for the position. The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of State represents the Imperial Republic Government and the Ministry of State in all Court or Judicate trials and decides with the Minister of State or their deputy, which charges on which an individual, group, or other entity are being prosecuted, in conjunction with the results of an investigation by the investigating law enforcement agency. The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of State cannot hold an active military commission or rank while serving as High Inquisitor.  The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of State shall furnish advice and opinions, formal and informal, on legal matters to the Minister of State and their deputies, members of the Throne and High Council, and to the heads of departments and agencies of the Ministry of State, as provided or required by law. The High Inquisitor for the Ministry of State shall decide whether the prosecution of an individual, group, organization or other entity should take place in a Secret Tribunal, though this authority is not restricted to the High Inquisition.
The High Inquisitor for the Regasterra is a member of COMPNOR and appointed by the Supreme Ruler of the Imperial Republic.  The High Inquisitor for the Regasterra represents the Imperial Republic Government and the Royal Family in all Royal Court or Judicate trials and decides with the Royal Family, which charges on which an individual, group, or other entity are being prosecuted, in conjunction with the results of an investigation by the investigating law enforcement agency. The High Inquisitor for the Regasterra cannot hold an active military commission or rank while serving as High Inquisitor.  The High Inquisitor for the Regasterra shall furnish advice and opinions, formal and informal, on legal matters to the members of the Throne and High Council, and to the Heads of Great and Nobles Houses of the Regasterra, as provided or required by law. 
Article 4.8.b - Removal of High Inquisitors
The High Inquisitors serve at the pleasure of the Throne and may be dismissed by the Throne at any time. If a High Inquisitor is charged with any crime at the State level, they are immediately suspended from their power and duties and a deputy is appointed by the Throne until the investigation and trial concludes. If found guilty of the crime in which they are charged with, the Throne shall decide whether or not to remove them from office.
Article 4.8.c - The Inquisition and State-level Inquisitors of the Class A Territories
Inquisitions shall exist for class A Territories for every level of government beginning with the sub-planetary level two, which shall be defined as two levels below the Planetary Governor (Sub-Prefect). Additional levels shall be afforded as requested by local government or as designated by higher level Inquisitors.  All state level Inquisitors and Inquisitions shall fall under the Ministry of Justice and be part of COMPNOR, though their duties and authority shall be for the entire Imperial Republic Government. Inquisitors serve for a five (5) year term. State-level Inquisitors shall be nominated by the governor of same level of government at which they serve, and must be members of COMPNOR. They must be confirmed by either the Senate Judiciary Committee or a designated member of the High Council. All appointments to the office of an Inquisitor shall be approved by the Ministry of Justice after background check is performed and they are cleared by the Imperial Republic Security Bureau. A separate background check and clearance is required with each re-appointment. They may be re-appointed in the same manner in which they are appointed as prescribed below after their term expires. 
The Inquisitors shall report to the Minister of Justice, their deputies, and also to the Throne and High Council. The Inquisitors decide which level of Judicate will handle the legal proceedings or may defer the case and jurisdiction to a lower or local court. Inquisitors shall perform or supervise the performance of other duties required by statute or Executive Order. The Inquisitors shall make recommendations to the governor of the level at which they serve concerning appointments to state judicial positions and to positions with the Ministry of Justice and office of the Inquisition including all other lower level Inquisitors and their deputies and other state court or judicate officers. Inquisitors shall not have authority to prosecute specific local crimes below the State level unless authorized to do so by the local-level equivalent to an Inquisitor for the specified locality. Inquisitors shall not have authority over local Courts or law-enforcement agencies below the State level. In most instances, the Inquisitors shall not provide legal advice to private citizens or organizations.
Inquisitors of the Imperial Republic represent the Imperial Republic Government in all legal matters, Court or Judicate trials and decides which charges on which an individual, group, or other entity are being prosecuted, in conjunction with the results of an investigation by the investigating law enforcement agency. Inquisitors of the Imperial Republic shall furnish advice and opinions, formal and informal, on legal matters to the executive and legislative government officials and bodies at the state level at which they serve,, and to the heads of executive departments or Ministries and agencies of the Imperial Republic Government, as provided or required by law.
Inquisitors of the state level serve at the pleasure of the Throne and can be dismissed by the Throne or High Council at any time. If an Inquisitor at the state-level is charged with any crime at the State level, they are immediately suspended from their power and duties and a deputy is appointed by the Ministry of Justice until the investigation and trial concludes. If found guilty of the crime in which they are charged with, the Ministry of Justice, Throne, or High Council shall decide whether or not to remove them from office.
Article 4.8.d - Local Inquisitions and Inquisitors
Local governments below the state level shall establish their own legal system and criteria for selection of prosecutors and investigating law-enforcement agencies. Local governments may request the assistance of state-level law-enforcement agencies when needed. The head of the state-level law-enforcement agency or their local deputy shall decide what assistance, if any, shall be provided upon request.
Article 4.9 - Creation and Dissolution of Special Permanent or Temporary Courts and Inquisitions
The Imperial Republic High Council and Imperial Republic Galactic Senate shall both independently or together have authority to create and/or authorize the creation of special permanent or temporary courts and/or Inquisitions at any level. Both the Senate and the High Council shall also have the authority to create special commissions and/or committees which may hold hearings to investigate issues brought to their attention. Some court types such as military tribunals will remain inactive except when activation is authorized by the Throne or either legislative body or their Judicial Oversight Committee.
Article 4.10 - Administrative Law
Each Ministry and other administrative agency of the Imperial Republic Government shall have Administrative Law Judges for the purposes of rulemaking, adjudication, and/or enforcement of specific regulatory policies within the purview of the agency of which they are appointed. The process for appointing and removing Administrative Law Judges shall be determined by internal policies of each agency as approved by COMPNOR or the High Council or Senate Judicial Oversight Committee.

The Treason Act -  Stuff from Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treason
High treason is criminal disloyalty to one's government. Participating in a war against one's native country, attempting to overthrow its government, spying on its military, its diplomats, or its secret services for a hostile and foreign power, or attempting to kill its head of state are perhaps the best known examples of high treason. High treason requires that the alleged traitor have obligations of loyalty in the state he or she betrayed, but this will usually be satisfied by being present in the state at the time of the offence, or being a citizen of the state if abroad. Foreign spies, assassins, and saboteurs, though not suffering the dishonor associated with conviction for high treason, may still be tried and punished judicially for acts of espionage, assassination, or sabotage, though in contemporary times, foreign spies are usually repatriated in exchange for spies of the mentioned nation held by another nation.[2] High treason is considered a very serious – often the most serious possible – crime, by the civil authorities. A conviction, by a Canadian court, for high treason, results in a mandatory life sentence (albeit with the possibility of parole after 25 years).
Whosoever, within New South Wales or without, compasses, imagines, invents, devises, or intends to deprive or depose Our Most Gracious Lady the Queen, her heirs or successors, from the style, honour, or Royal name of the Imperial Crown of the United Kingdom, or of any other of Her Majesty's dominions and countries, or to levy war against Her Majesty, her heirs or successors, within any part of the United Kingdom, or any other of Her Majesty's dominions, in order, by force or constraint, to compel her or them to change her or their measures or counsels, or in order to put any force or constraint upon, or in order to intimidate or overawe, both Houses or either House of the Parliament of the United Kingdom, or the Parliament of New South Wales, or to move or stir any foreigner or stranger with force to invade the United Kingdom, or any other of Her Majesty's dominions, or countries under the obeisance of Her Majesty, her heirs or successors, and expresses, utters, or declares such compassings, imaginations, inventions, devices, or intentions, or any of them, by publishing any printing or writing, or by open and advised speaking, or by any overt act or deed, shall be liable to imprisonment for 25 years.
A person commits an offence, called treason, if the person:

(a) causes the death of the Sovereign, the heir apparent of the Sovereign, the consort of the Sovereign, the Governor-General or the Prime Minister; or
(b) causes harm to the Sovereign, the Governor-General or the Prime Minister resulting in the death of the Sovereign, the Governor-General or the Prime Minister; or
(c) causes harm to the Sovereign, the Governor-General or the Prime Minister, or imprisons or restrains the Sovereign, the Governor-General or the Prime Minister; or
(d) levies war, or does any act preparatory to levying war, against the Commonwealth; or
(e) engages in conduct that assists by any means whatever, with intent to assist, an enemy:
(i) at war with the Commonwealth, whether or not the existence of a state of war has been declared; and
(ii) specified by Proclamation made for the purpose of this paragraph to be an enemy at war with the Commonwealth; or
(f) engages in conduct that assists by any means whatever, with intent to assist:
(i) another country; or
(ii) an organisation;
that is engaged in armed hostilities against the Australian Defence Force; or
(g) instigates a person who is not an Australian citizen to make an armed invasion of the Commonwealth or a Territory of the Commonwealth; or
(h) forms an intention to do any act referred to in a preceding paragraph and manifests that intention by an overt act.
A person is not guilty of treason under paragraphs (e), (f) or (h) if their assistance or intended assistance is purely humanitarian in nature.

CURRENT REQUIREMENTS for Amending the Charter
Article 1.0 - Amending the Charter

The Senate, High Council, Royal Family, and the Throne may propose any changes to the Imperial Republic Charter, except imposing term limits for members of the Throne. The responsibility to approve these proposed amendments rests with the High Council, Royal Family, the Throne, and a convention of the Supreme Electorate.
