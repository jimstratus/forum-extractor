The idea behind an Imperial Republic is an empire with a semi-Republican form of government in the way of the Landsraad. The logic behind this idea is that the Nobles of the Landsraad represent the people in their fiefdom, but are not elected by them. The only known existing micronations that are Imperial Republics are the nations of Imperial Republic of Shireroth and Tamisteon. Natopia had, for a time, been an Imperial Republic as well. The newest Imperial Republic is a different form of the system, led by Tamisteon.
With the instatement of the New Feudalism, the process of the Imperial Republic has changed slightly: now, the people choose by way of Feudal Contract who is to speak for them in the Landsraad.
In Shireroth, feudal contracts have fallen by the wayside since the Revival of October 2007 under Kaiser Mors V.
In Tamisteon the system is led by an Emperor, President, Senate, and Council.
