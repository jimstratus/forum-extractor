A[nother] Sith Among Us
The Dasurkan, awaken as a Sith is detected in the deep bowels of the old Jedi Temple’s Force Nexus. Mistaking the detection as that of the presence of a sith, and an old emergency protocol was activated (Omega Protocol) which was enacted when the Royal Guard became the Red Guard instituted by Lord Kennedy and the Dasurkan activate and mistakenly believe the Royal Guard or other forces have infiltrated the palace again and begin to appear everywhere and begin attacking the Royal Guards frantically searching for orders from a designated protector and most especially the High Prince  himself.
Meanwhile, the high prince and his guards fear the Dasurkan are after him, and begin attacking the Dasurkan and the High Prince goes in disguise as a Royal Guard. When he encounters a Dasurkan Centurion it stops and scans him, recognizes him and immediately transmits to the rest of the Dasurkan and shuts down, while the remaining guards droids retreat back to their posts.
High Prince Stratus calls a meeting of the protectors, paladins, and praetorian to inquire what triggered Omega Protocol. They decide to take a visit to Sanctuary to make their inquiries directly to the Dasurkan commander droids and discover the reason for the accidental activation of Omega protocol.
A Sith (while trying to avoid detection) was detected as she visited the former hideout of Lord Kennedy in the deep bowels of the former Jedi Temple which now was a secret military facility, near Sanctuary, guarded by the Dasurkan, with special Force detection equipment. 
The Sith is near, but who is it? What are their intentions? Are they part of the Sith Empire or the Imperial Republic? Will they reveal themselves? Will they oppose Stratus or swear allegiance to him and the New Order? Friend or Foe? Trustworthy or Deceiving? 
What will the Praetorian do? What will Stratus do? 
When Stratus commands the Dasurkan to cease hostilities and return to their posts he orders the Royal Guard to cover up the incident and report it as a training exercise that went bad and that is what is told to all who were not directly involved or affected. Those who came in contact with the Dasurkan are read in on their purpose and existance and sworn to secrecy under penalty of death or life imprisonment. The Dasurkan is one of the most heavily guarded secrets of the Imperial Republic, similar to Operation Paladin, the Darkstar Project, and Mount Dragoon. Stratus must head to Sanctuary to find out what is happening, (discovers Omega Protocol has been activated), and orders the droid commander to deactivate the protocol and investigates how it became reactivated in the first place.
Once Praetor Paladin figures out what is happening she comes clean to Stratus and meets him in Sanctuary, surrenders her weapon, and a private discussion takes place between the two. Meanwhile the military and all others that became active on Red Alert due to Omega Protocol are told to go down to yellow alert and that it was just a drill. Those who know the truth are moved or monitored, or if necessary, silenced to protect and safeguard the secret.
