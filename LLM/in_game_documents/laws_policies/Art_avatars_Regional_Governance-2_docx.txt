The Supreme Chancellor and Minister of State





His Excellency the Supreme Chancellor

Prince/Grand Duke Dene Vye Cognatus is the Supreme Chancellor of the Imperial Republic and Minister of State. As such he not oversees the Galactic Senate but all of Regional Governance. As Grand Duke he oversees all of Regasterra (by Proxy on behalf of His Majesty the Supreme Ruler)  (the Royal Territories of the Imperial Republic). He is married to the Princess Aurielle of the House Stratus, adopted daughter of His Majesty the Supreme Ruler, hence his title of Prince.
