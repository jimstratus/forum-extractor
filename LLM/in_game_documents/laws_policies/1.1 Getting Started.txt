The Imperial Republic has many things to offer an individual interested in a role-playing experience. This is not a game in the conventional sense but rather an ongoing experience. You begin by creating a character and you assume the role of said character in this environment. You may improve your character's abilities and potency through participating in scenarios and role-playing with the community. You create relationships with other players both in character and out of character. In the Imperial Republic you will embark on a career within the Imperial ranks. Demonstration of skill, dedication and activity will not go unnoticed and is the basis for promotions, awards and positions of authority within Imperial Republic. 
There are numerous items to be done within this Role Playing Game (RPG) community and within the Republic. They range from RPG scenarios (which may include combat or non-combat scenarios), volunteering for training fellow Imperial, assisting in institutions such as Diplomacy and Recruitment, creating and moderating scenarios, and of course interacting with your fellow Imperials. 
Interaction with other Imperials takes place on many mediums including mIRC (Internet Relay Chat- www.mirc.com) which houses channels for the Republic itself, sectors and some branches.
