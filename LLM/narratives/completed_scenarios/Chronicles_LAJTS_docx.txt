Prologue



The Galactic Alliance has fallen, just as the New Republic, Galactic Empire, and Galactic Republic before it. Only remnants of governments remain among small regions of the galaxy. Many attempts to unite the galaxy under a new government have been made; all have failed. It is time of terrible turmoil, a time of war. Sectors at war with sectors, planets at war with planets, cities with cities, and Houses at war with Houses. Among these houses on the planet of Kuat is the Royal House Stratus, which rules over the Kuat system, and the Noble House Badine, which desires to destroy the monarchy and turn the system into a communist state, under their sole authority and power. The Kingdom of Kuat, ruled by James Stratus I, who married the Royal Princess Adaline Hoffner of Hapes and assumed the Throne after the death of his father King Charles Stratus III is on the brink of collapse. The King refuses to rebuild the current palace into a protected fortress, out of tradition and stubbornness. A plot to overthrow the kingdom is about to unfold, and the Royal House Stratus is in grave danger.

Chapter 1: Insurrection on Kuat



Random quote from within the story shall go here

	The Royal Family of Kuat sat together at dinner in the Dining Hall, in observance of their daily tradition to dine together as an entire family. It was one of the few times the House Stratus met together due to their obscure duties in service to the Kingdom. Without warning, the blast doors separating the Dining Hall from the palace corridors opened and Colonel Keitel stormed in, a nervous and worried expression on his face. This was not unusual as the commander of the Kuati Blue Guard tended to get worked up on just about everything. Jim had a feeling however, that this time it was something serious. Jim’s father the King did not look pleased at the interruption, as this was sacred time with his family in which he had commanded no interruptions. Colonel Keitel quickly produced a datacard and handed it to the King. The King retrieved a datapad from his coat pocket and inserted the datacard. Jim watched his father for the “sigh” indicating the Colonel was worked up about nothing, instead he caught his father raise his eyebrows before handing the datapad with the datacard still intact to his mother the Queen. 

While James’ father was difficult to read, his mother was transparent, at least to young James. He studied his mother’s face as emotions of horror, sadness, and anger suddenly projected to her children, all of which were Force-sensitive and had begun trainings with Jedi Master Ric O’leary, one of the few remaining of the Order. His mother frequently forgot the adeptness by which her children could sense her feelings as the youngest began to cry and the others began to ask questions. Queen Adaline had been raised and trained in the way of the Falanassi, until her community had been infiltrated and hunted down. She had been rescued by the handsome King Charles from an Imperial Warlord. She had taught her daughters the way of the Falanassi and the young James too, at his insistence. 

“What is it, mother?” James asked in dismay. He was tired of his father and mother keeping things from them. There was a dark, deep secret lingering that he had been trying to pry out of them for weeks. His senses told him this issue was about to become blown wide open, and he was demanding of his parents to include him. 

His mother, ignoring Jim’s outburst, turned to her husband the King, “Charles, this is serious. Lives are at risk here. Our children’s lives. What will happen is bound to happen soon, and we must make sure…”

“It will not happen anytime soon” the King cut her off abruptly. 

“What will happen, Mommy?” the young Princess Nicole asked.

Without answering, mother and father, king and queen, exchanged a knowing look. “It is bed time my dears,” spoke their mother. She turned to the guardian-nanny droid, Z4-X2. “Zee Four, take the children to their rooms and assist them in preparing for bed. I will tuck them in once they are washed up and in their beds. The King and I need to talk in private.”

“Yes, of course, Your Majesties,” the droid replied, “I’ll have them in bed straight away.” The children had gotten down from the table and began to leave the Dining Hall. The Queen reached into her pocket and found a small control transmitting device. Pressing a familiar key, Z4-X2 immediately  looked up at the Queen and tweeted twice in acknowledgement before leaving the chamber. The droid activated its passive scanning system and powered up its hidden weapon system, transmitting to the hidden guardian droids in the children’s area of the palace to be on high alert. Jim noticed droids appearing from unknown hiding places that he had never seen before. They were designed differently from Z4 but seemed to offer some sort of salute as Z4-X2 passed. He could tell they were armed. He had a feeling that something bad would happen soon. Without speaking, he summoned his brother Marc to follow him back towards the dining hall. They slipped in quietly and invisible, using a technique his mother had taught him, to eavesdrop on the private conversation between their parents.

His mother looked right at him and sighed. She could see through the veil of invisibility, while his father could not. She made no motion for him to leave, but acted as if she wanted him to listen in and ignored his presence. Jedi Master Ric O’leary had just arrived, and while he could not sense young James while under Falanassi powers he could certainly sense Marc. He also could read Adaline well enough to know James was somewhere in the room as well. Marc had to hide behind a disposal device as he had not mastered the art of invisibility taught by his mother. “The room is now secure,” Queen Adaline announced. No one in the room would argue with her declaration, regardless of what scanners might say, as they knew better. She had decided it was secure, and thus it was. The Jedi Master, sensing wisdom in the Queen in her decision to allow the oldest of her children be present, remained silent. There was stubborn Hapan bloodline in her, and it became evident in times of crisis. She nodded to her husband who then nodded to their military advisor to report on his findings.

“Your Majesties, you should move your family to a secure location at once. Our Intel reports confirm high activity within close proximity, though the exact location of the Red Army we have been unable to determine. The communists could attack at any time now. It is imperative that we get you and your family to safety. This palace is old, nearly impossible to defend, and will crumble quickly when meeting their new and heavy weaponry. “

“Your Majesty refused the upgraded weapons and security system installation with the desire to preserve the home of the great King James I, may he rest in peace,” the old Jedi Master interrupted. He had regard for authority, but he was not one of politics or kind words. He boldly proclaimed what needed to be heard, and spared no expense in doing so as part of his teachings. “You are stubborn, Charles Stratus, and your stubbornness has already and will likely further place your wife and children in danger. You must rectify this at once.” The Jedi Master’s tone suggested it was a command. No one had ever dared spoken to a King of Kuat in such a manner, save Master O’leary. Without waiting to be dismissed, the Jedi Master, who was truly his own master, abruptly left the room in a huff.

“Thank you, Colonel, that will be all,” the Queen Adaline dismissed their military advisor. She turned toward the King and began to verbally rip him apart. “I know you and I can probably protect ourselves. I also know Master O’leary is right. You are a stubborn man. I know you like to be out in the open to show the people you are confident and fearless. But for heaven’s sake! Do not risk the lives of our children out of your own Stratus pride and stubbornness! We must think of them first Charles Stratus, and if you think otherwise I will take the children and leave this very instant!”

The King sighed and knew she was right. There would be no arguing with a Hapan woman, especially a mother of four children, in a situation such as this. “Alright Adaline, what do you want me to do?”

Without smiling or accepting his surrender, she ordered “for now we go to bed. Tomorrow, we get the children somewhere safe.”

***

Back in their room, Marc and Jim were still processing everything they had heard. Jim, much like his father, had too much faith in people and had not realized the magnitude of the situation they were currently in. Marc, on the other hand, was skeptical the entire time and knew something like this would happen. Master O’leary had taught them both to be prepared for any emergency, and had warned that such an attack would happen in the night. They kept travel packs and clothes near their beds, just in case.

What had the advisor said about an attack? Communists. He remembered learning about them in school with the royal tutor, and had recently heard about a group that had turned up six months ago. They called themselves the Red Party, but were more like terrorists trying to take over the government and overthrow the current monarchy. Apparently, they had gotten pretty out of control. His father had always considered it a joke, and weak, but now it had his mother and father’s staff worried. His father didn’t seem worried, but if he was, he didn’t show it. It was something to think about.

***

Camp Bravo: Red Party. 3 hours before midnight:

“The King still has a false sense of security. Now is the time to act. We will attack the palace and conquer tonight!”

Rallying behind their leader, the Red Party gathered their weapons and cheered.

***

The palace shook.

James and Marc bolted upright at the tremor of explosion. Voices and faint blaster fire echoed from a distance. Someone was crying in his sisters’ room.

He could hear his mother shouting orders. “Activate emergency defense programming. Get the children to my ship in twenty minutes. I’ll meet you there. If I don’t make it, activate program Alpha Five on the ship’s computer and get out of here.” He could sense her presence fading as she went to find his father.

James didn’t want to hear any more. He turned to Marc and started packing. “Pack all of your important stuff and find Zee Four. We’re leaving.”

As they finished grabbing their important things, his mother came in, handing them each a medium-sized bag. Both were full. “Take these with you. They are very important. See that they get aboard the ship.” She kissed their foreheads and ran out.

James opened one pocket to see data cards. Credits filled another pocket. James blinked, impressed. They must have been worth a fortune.

The door opened for Z4. “Come on. We must hurry to your mother’s ship.” The droid carried Ashlee in robotic arms as they ran toward a hidden exit of the palace. The escape landing pad.

When they reached the emergency blast doors, their mother and father were waiting. His father pressed on the keypad that opened the doors, and immediately alarms sounded. Footsteps came rushing toward the exit. The security guards took a body perimeter around the king and royal family. The king and queen drew blasters from their pockets and exchanged fire with the attackers. A guard near the king went down. The remaining guards moved to fill the gap, but were too late. A well-timed shot got through, and the king fell to the ground. His mother cried out, running to him.

“You were right,” he winced as he tried to talk, “I’m so sorry.”

“No! You cannot die now!”

“It’s my destiny…the children…safety. Go, now…” And King Charles Stratus, monarch of Kuat, was dead.

Ashlee’s cries became wails. Nicole and Marc started to cry. James wanted to join them, but he knew he couldn’t. He had to be the grown-up now. He had to be strong. And he would always remember this day.

His mother’s voice shouted through the haze of shock and blaster-fire. “Get the King’s body aboard my ship and get yourselves to safety!” She pointed to a cluster of guards. “You will come with me. The rest of you, follow us out of here until we get to hyperspace, and then get yourselves to safety. Zee Four, help me get the children aboard. We’re leaving.” She entered an emergency code that would sound the evacuation alarms, grabbed Ashlee from Z4 and ran to the ship. James, Nicole and Marc were right behind her.

As Z4 pulled their luggage onboard, James’s mother closed the ship’s ramp. The droid settled their family into the seats as the Queen started the ship. One of the guards served as her co-pilot, powering up the shields and weapons-systems. By now, the Red Party had reached the survivors, who were trying to escape. But their attack force was no match for the ship’s superior technology. His mother disabled them, but they didn’t seem to mind. Their goal was accomplished: the King was dead. The sole heirs of Kuat were fleeing. The government was theirs for the taking.

***

The Royal Dawn cleared the atmosphere. Seconds later, six security escort ships flanked it, on guard until the jump to hyperspace.

On board, James and his siblings were still seated, restrained, as the ship made its jump safely into hyperspace.

The Royal Dawn was a Hapan ship, a large, modified beauty given to his mother by the Queen of Hapes just for situations like this. Adaline walked in, surrounded by seven of the security guards—leaving her main co-pilot at the helm—and waited for everyone to be seated. “I am going to explain what has happened so that the children can understand, so please bear with me.” She gave each of her children an emotional hug. “My dears, last night we were attacked by a large group of very bad people with weapons. The palace wasn’t fortified for an attack, and we were not prepared. We had to leave before they could capture or kill us. Your father was killed trying to help us get to the ship. The Red Party has seized the palace, and we had to escape. It is only a matter of time before they take over all of Kuat.” Now the Queen turned to her guards. “We will emerge from hyperspace in ten minutes to do a systems check. After that, I am afraid we can make no stops. I do wish for all of you to be free to go find your families, or seek shelter or a new life, but I am afraid that will have to wait. The Red Party may still wish to capture me, or the children. So we will be heading somewhere they will not find us. Or, if they do, where they will not be able to harm us. The Queen of Hapes, my sister, will protect us. When we reach Hapes, you will be free to go, and I will personally pay your way. Otherwise, you are welcome to stay with us as personal guard or staff.”

There was a whisper among the officers, and they nodded as one stood. “Your Majesty, this has been a tragic day for all of us, but especially for you and your family. King Charles’s death was terrible. We, of the royal security, are with you and your family to the end. We do ask that we are able to go free to rescue our families from Kuat when we arrive at Hapes, but we will return.”

She nodded, crying. “Thank you. I will do everything I can to help you on your way.”

The ship’s intercom beeped. “Your Majesty, we have arrived out of hyperspace, and are ready to make the jump back into hyperspace to Hapes. The navigator has finished calculating. We are ready to go. All systems are fully functional.”

The Queen walked down the ship’s corridor to where the co-pilot was waiting. “Very good. Jumping to hyperspace in 10…” The ship jumped to hyperspace, en route directly to Hapes.

This would be a long trip. Hapes was far away, although they were prepared and well stocked to make it there without any problems.

***

James sat on his bed, staring at the wall and into nothingness. He couldn’t get it into his head that it had all been real. It had been so sudden, so instant, that he couldn’t believe it had happened. But it had. Everything was behind him. Home, friends, life. His father…

He had heard of Hapes before, heard stories of how only the females were dominant. Pirates, beautiful women, precious gems, among other things. And all to build a new life. A new name. He would be free to start a business, he hoped, since he wouldn’t have to become the king of Kuat.

He was scared. He missed his father. There was an empty space in his heart.

Marc walked in, saying nothing, and went to bed.

James needed rest too. He followed suit.

***

The chronometer of the Royal Dawn read thirty seconds, and the warning alarm beeped. James made his way into the cockpit and sat behind his mother .She turned around, saw him, and smiled. “Can I watch?” he asked.

She nodded. “Of course, but buckle your restraints. And if things get sticky, I will need you to stay quiet, okay?”

He was happy to be in the cockpit where he could watch everything that went on. He’d flown before with various teachers. Kuati children were encouraged to learn how to fly as young as possible.

The alert beeped again, and his mother pushed the release to make the jump out of hyperspace. The hyperdrive disengaged, and James could see what appeared to be Hapes, with traffic everywhere. Ships were going to and from the planet, ranging from merchants to politicians. He noticed that they were all on specific courses. Traffic regulation here was probably strict. The Royal Dawn was not on a specific course.

	The co-pilot pointed to the left. James looked over. “That’s the Star Home, personal ship to the Queen of Hapes. It’s the largest, most powerful ship in the Hapan military.” He said to James.

	The ship’s communicator beeped, and a harsh, accented voice broke through. “Royal Dawn, this is Star Home. You are in a restricted zone. Identify yourself and state your intentions.”

	James’s mother sat straight, every inch a queen, and flipped the comm. “Star home, Royal Dawn resquesting emergency landing at Royal Hapan palace with immediate audience with the Queen. Authorization code Five-Gamma-Zulu-Six. You may use my voice-print in your military records to identify me.”

	The communication’s officer on the other side snorted, but was cut off by another voice. Apparently, the ship’s computer had recognized Queen Stratus’s voiceprint and authorization code. “Royal Dawn, you have a valid authorization code, and emergency clearance has already been granted for you to land on Palace Pad Alpha-Two. A fighter escort is on the way.”

	“Roger that.”

	James could see eight Hapan fighters coming in escort formation. His mother took the ship’s controls and followed the fighters in. The Royal Dawn entered planet atmosphere. Massive, beautiful buildings were everywhere. In some areas, James could see small patches of green forests, lakes, rivers, and oceans. The ship sped past the main city to a remote, circular fortress. It was elegant, the most finely detailed of all the buildings there. The fortress-palace was on a small island surrounded by a lake, almost like a castle’s moat from ancient times.

	The fighter-escort followed the Dawn all the way to the landing pad. The pad itself was elegantly designed. The escort remained on patrol as they landed, and soldiers emerged from the palace, surrounding the ship. James had never seen so many guards before. The extreme amount of patrol indicated a high security alert. James’s mother cut the engines and motioned for them to follow. Guards formed an honor-formation for the Queen of Kuat as they descended the ramp.

	A veiled lady, surrounded by even more guards—these ones female—all but ran toward them. Normal ritual would demand that both queens walk to the center of the landing pad and bow to one another, but the queens ignored it. “Adaline, whatever happened?”

	His mother fell into the other queen’s embrace. “Elyse, it was horrible. We were attacked. Charles was killed. We barely escaped with our lives.” She pulled back, bowing now in formality. “My security officers are the only loyal servants of the palace. My family and staff seek shelter among you, and I would help in assisting their rescue of their families.”

	The Queen of Hapes let her sister cry on her shoulder. “I will give you anything you need. I want you and your family to come live in the palace. You and your children will be under my protection at all times. Your staff may stay with you. I will do whatever is in my power to help them bring their families to safety. But for now, come inside, and get some rest.” She looked up at the sky. “It looks like it’s going to rain.”

	***

	The light turned on, and Jamie De’Soto, Jim Stratus’s personal guard, walked into the room. “Time to get up, sleepy-head.” She sounded like she could be his mother. “You don’t want to keep your mother waiting. Today is special.”

	Today was his sixteenth birthday, and his mother had planned a huge celebration at the palace. They’d been on Hapes for four years now. Jim’s mother had fallen ill, and there didn’t seem to be any doctors who could help. In all likelihood, she was dying.

	She was still training him in the ways of the Force, and the old Jedi Master from Kuat had come to Hapes to complete Jim’s training. He was gaining an education in politics to please his mother.
