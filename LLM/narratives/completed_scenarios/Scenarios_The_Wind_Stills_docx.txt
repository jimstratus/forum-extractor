Following the Arrest of General Cognatus



In the Throneroom of the Royal Palace of Coruscant, the Royal Throne met with the members of the Senate Judiciary Committee to hear their case. Also present were lead prosecuting and defense counsels representing the case of General Cognatus, now marked a traitor of the government. The High Prince had not yet spoken with the General since his arrest, though had stared him in the eyes and with all his might and power, broke as many barriers as he could and read into the mind of Cognatus. He had known him for many years. He felt strongly that he had not betrayed him, unlike many others whom he had placed high trust in. He was inclined to release him and clear him of charges using his authority as the Supreme Ruler. The defense, including the Grand Admiral spoke for such action, though the rest felt that he was not an impartial party. Throughout the proceedings the Executor and Supreme Chancellor remained silent, as they would not ever speak out against the Supreme Ruler in such a setting, but would counsel with him in private, though most of the time he was able to simply read their thoughts, and they his, as much as they had worked together over the years. He knew they agreed with the issue at hand. The case would not only need to stand full trial, but would need to be deferred to the High Inquisition, where a strong impartialty existed.



After hearing many different points of view, the High Prince held up his hands to silence all present. He then leaned to the side to hear Executor Treyson's whispered wisdom and judgement, and then leaned to the opposite side to do the same for the Chancellor. He nodded after each of them had spoken. They were now all in agreement. He cleared his throat. "The decision of the Throne has been made. General Cognatus will stand trial. We defer this case to the High Inquisition of the Imperial Republic, the date of trial to be set by the High Inquisitor. You are all dismissed."



Everyone left and the Royal Guard began assisting in their departure. The members of the Throne remained and discussed it in further detail, privately, though the decision had been officially rendered. This gave them all the opportunity to further talk it out and strengthen their decision and unity.





The End of Turbulence, the Trial of Dene Vye Cognatus

 

It did not take long before the letters of the law were affected, and Command General Cognatus, relieved of duty and under house arrest (a mercy granted to him by the Supreme Ruler), waited for the guard's announcement that it was time. He was tired and weary, someone uncertain of the future, but he knew the truth, and would stand by it, even if it meant his life. He had not fought all those years for nothing, he believed in the system, had fought for the system, and now would see it in action on a personal level.



The signal from the guard came, and he nodded and followed them, completely unarmed, but also, free from any restraints. There were certain levels of trust that the High Prince had bestowed upon him even now, to show his faith in him. No restraints and no ysalamiri. His lightsaber was, however, now in the possesion of the High Prince, and Dene knew that it would be returned to him eventually, regardless of the outcome, because he knew Stratus, he had served him loyally for many years. 



He was lead into the courtroom of the High Inquisition, where at a tall podium sat the Tribunal of Inquisitors, the High Inquisitor seated in the center, with the other two at each of his sides. They were not smiling, though they did not avoid his gaze. Their faces were solemn, their lips were shut, and they did not move. The IR Attorney, representing the government of the Imperial Republic, including the Ministry of Justice, and the three security agencies that had pressed charges against him. On the other side was a defense attorney, representing and defending the General, a trusted friend and advisor of Dene's. Showing his support and seated next to the General was Grand Admiral OVERMIND himself, the Minister of Defense. While the High Inquisition did not utilize a jury of any kind, jury-like seating of prestige was still to the side, and was generally used for official attendees of the Imperial Republic government, that were occasionally called upon during such proceedings, though they had no authority over the ruling. In this particular case, however, in these prestigious seats sat the members of the Imperial Republic High Council, and in the center and high seats, the members of the Throne itself. Their faces were also solemn.





The Bothan in the center, the High Inquisitor Hosk Buw'atu, was the first to speak. "General Cognatus, throughout these proceedings you will be addressed by your proper rank and title. This Inquisition is reminded by the Throne, that while you are currently relieved of duty, you still hold the rank of Command General, the positions of Chief of Army Operations and High Councilor, though both of these are currently suspended, until this court should come to a ruling deciding whether or not you shall retain that rank and those positions. Are you ready to hear the charges which have been brought forth against you?"



Yes, My Lord High Inquisitor. I am ready to hear the charges.



"Command General Dene Vye Cognatus, you are hereby charged with one count of High Treason, one count of Treason, three counts of murder, violation of direct orders from your commanding officer, the commander-in-chief, as well as orders from the Throne. How do you plea?"



"I plea not guilty, to the first five charges, My Lords and Lady, and to the sixth and final charge, I plea guilty with reservations."



"Very well, the trial shall now begin. We shall hear first from the Imperial Republic Attorney, to present the evidence of these charges that are brought before you. You will then be given a chance to defend yourself against these charges and provide any evidence in support of your actions that will assist in clearing you of these charges. Let us then proceed."
