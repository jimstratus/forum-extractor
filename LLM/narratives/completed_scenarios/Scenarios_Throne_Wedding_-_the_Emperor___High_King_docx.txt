37 IRY - Transgalactic Partnership



Earlier that day.... in the private executive residential suites of the Royal Palace, High Prince James Stratus paced nervously around the room, waiting nervously for Dana to get home. He had summoned the key COMPNOR officials to hear firsthand a report on the outcome and process of the Outer Rim Transgalactic Partnership, some awards he needed to hand out and congratulations that were in order, as well as a special appointment for Praetor Dugaan. Technically the Transgalactic Partnership had not yet been officially been stamped with the "Emeperor's Seal" though it had been given Throne approval by Praetor Dugaan on the Supreme Ruler's behalf. The Supreme Ruler needed to make sure the seal was affixed to the document one way or another, though he did not see the need to do it personally, he felt someone else should have that honor, and it was high time that individual be inducted into the Order anyway, to sign and seal things in His Majesty's name.



But at the moment those thoughts were just a distraction. He was trying to distract from the fact that he was actually nervous. James Stratus was not a nervous being, the last time he was nervous was, well decades ago to say the least. The only time the man ever got nervous truly were in matters of the heart, true, he worried over political situations and military conflicts and wars and economic crisis, but he worried about them, he did not get nervous. He was a natural born leader, in fact the galaxy's Supreme Leader. But at the moment this leader was pacing nervously, in dress uniform, walking up and down the executive residential suite main hall, until he felt the presence of the love of his life enter the residence through a secret tunnel as she always did, unnoticed, as her job in Imperial Republic Intelligence required her to keep a low profile in terms of work movements, only when did she go out socially (publicly) with the Supreme Ruler though she usually wore a lot of cover and changed her look and was so surrounded by guards it was near impossible for cameras or anyone to catch a glimpse of her no matter the angle, including aerial, as the Royal Guard had aerial drones covering her as well. She was a Royal Guard protectee, and he knew she hated having a protection detail, and having to take undercover cross-trained team of Royal Guard/IRIS agents with her everywhere was not her idea of being independent IRIS Senior Agent in Charge. He also knew how much she loved her job. He was very nervous about what the significance of this entire conversation would lead.



Dana wore a black overcoat and dark boots and special combat gear, no rank insignia except a small all-access ID chip at her waste required inside the Palace should someone fail to recognize her and try to stop her if they happen to see her. Though her senses were very well honed and she was very good at sensing Jim's presence in the residence, their home (though she was not a Jedi or Force-sensitive in the traditional sense, she had a unique direct link to his mind from learning from the Aiing-Tii with him and his allowing her to do so. In spite of all this, she was not expecting Jim home in the middle of the day, knowing his daily schedule as she received a copy of it in the morning from his Chief of Staff though often details and specifics were left out. She nearly jumped out of her boots when she crashed into him, neither of them seeing each other, both looking down while they were walking, herself reading a datacard report, him, also reading a datacard, notes he had prepared of what he was about to say.
