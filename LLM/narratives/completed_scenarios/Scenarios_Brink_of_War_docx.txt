From: Lan Klone, Underdirector, IRIS-DOO



Re: Emerging Intelligence Activity on Brolsam; Threat to SR



Dear Sirs,



We have intercepted a transmission originating from Brolsam sent to Supremacy Trade Station which was relayed to grid coordinates 113,238 on Corusca City, Coruscant. The transmission mentions the Crown Shuttle specifically with usage of planted explosives. Attached with the transmission was the Supreme Ruler’s daily schedule and planned travel, including confidential data concerning the specifications of His Majesty’s shuttlecraft and Palace Hangar Bay Alpha One. Because of this we have reason to believe it is a credible threat to not only the Imperial Republic but also to His Majesty the Supreme Ruler himself. We recommend the Royal Guard be placed on high alert and that further investigation is taken on Coruscant while we investigate the origin of the transmission.
