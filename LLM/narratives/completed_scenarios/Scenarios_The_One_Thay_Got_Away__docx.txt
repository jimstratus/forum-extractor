I want spy ships in the system, hiding, scanning, ready to run and flee on a moments’ notice. I want boots on the ground, Military Intelligence would be good. Not just at Mon Cal, but at other strategic locations, listening in at their listening posts.



Since we have been at peace time for so long, throughout the galaxy in every sector the sector fleets are deployed/spread out throughout their respective sectors, to patrol their individual sections and to also show a sign of military strength to all of the populated planets by putting ships around each as well as patrols around the unpopulated areas and borders, therefore they are spread out thin. This puts the Imperial Republic Fleet at both an advantage and disadvantage should the imperial republic come under attack. The good news is we should not be able to be caught by surprise due to having covered such a large amount of “ground”/territory. We are also able to see more of what is going on and respond to it quicker as most issues do not require a full fleet/task force, and those that do can or will have to wait until the entire fleet can be assembled, though in the sector they are not spread out too far that it would take too long, for most sectors. For oversectors that is another story altogether, but also the reason for needing to be spread out.



The stubborn admiral not realizing the shipyards are endanger abandons his post and takes the remainder of the fleet into battle - this time against orders (“to save his people/men”) even after being informed that a member of the Grand Admiralty was on their way to that location. Not long after he leaves his post, terrorists (or so it seems) strike at the Mon Cal shipyards and render it offline and critical. IRSB believes they will try again to finish the job of destroying the shipyards completely. IRIS, after looking into the attack, come up with nothing so far, though they do not give up, the perpetrators covered their tracks well. However, they discover strange activity on the planet, non-IR traffic and communications to and from the planet going off-world to what appears to be a set of coordinates in space, which according to the map is just dead space within IR territory, not far from the Mon Cal system. Whatever they are, how they and the other group got passed our patrols into IR territory is a interesting question, one that needs to be solved, but right now we need to focus on what is about to happen.



The Mon Cal system will come under attack, we will have no fleet there. Hopefully our garrison will still be intact. Our fleet will likely be destroyed or heavily damaged and crippled. We will have to wait until a backup fleet sent by DMoD/MoD arrives at Mon Cal to retake it, either the nearest sector fleet (though that may not be wise, if we don’t know if they have another one closeby), or a Grand Admiral’s task force, the closest one. We will also want to consider reconfiguring our fleet deployments for war and bring them (each fleet) close together safety in numbers “so all her ships surround her flag.”
