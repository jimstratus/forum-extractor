Palace Storm 

Return from the Shadows

Revised: 30 August 2008

 

Background: Kennedy persuades Layha to replace some royal guard personnel, specifically the ones that oversee personnel (hiring/firing, etc.) RG units/personnel with new training slowly begin to replace the old ones, who are either let go, offered retirement, or disappear and are mysteriously replaced without any mention.

 

Intro: Royal Family notice difference in royal guard before they "disappear" from them and are uncomfortable with the guard. Jim likewise notices difference upon his return. Layha notices but thinks nothing of it for her mind is clouded by her master. The dark side clouds most the situation from notice. Other Jedi, who are leaders of the IR (HC members, etc) notice changes as well, some are weary of it, others think nothing of it, at least at first. Upon the Royal Family's arrival at Coruscant, most of the Royal Guard from all over the IR will be there as all of the protectees will be there at once, including HC members.

 

Climax events:

-OM gets upset about (something - doesn't matter - he can pick) and goes to Throneroom seeking the High Prince. He is refused by the secretary, which is very unusual. He demands entrance and is refused by the royal guard as he attempts to enter himself with his own key. He senses something wrong between HMPS and the royal guard and in general senses alarm. OM "works on a document"  on his holopad right there that he wanted to "review with the Prince" and gives a datacard to the secretary to place in the Prince's file for later review and requests a prior authorization code be attached to it for easier approval.

-HMPS secretary reads the encoded message on her terminal from OM containing a CODE 7 ALARM and sends it through the proper channels to be transmitted secretly to its intended recipients. The secretary then hides in a secret compartment of her office from the guard.

 

Confrontations:

OM vs Royal guard - perhaps he insists on seeing the Prince, sensing something wrong, and fights with the guard

Kaidlen vs. guard - She is attacked in her room while resting, (having sensed the attack just prior and is prepared). She kills the initial combatants and escapes through the hidden tunnel underneath the bed and meets Noghri warriors guarding the tunnels who she warns of the guards' attack.They travel with her through secret passageways. She or OM ends up saving the HMPS secretary's life. Kaidlen ends up joining OM in allying with him against the guard and their search for the Royal Family. The royal floor has a huge lockdown and they have to fight off many armed guards before they can attempt to unlock blast doors or hotwire them.

Colonel Bremen gets CODE 7 alarm message first and alerts the rest of PSA secretly and they wait for some type of signal to attack the guard and prepare themselves. They are the first to arrive on the Throneroom floor and join OM and Kaidlen and follow their orders.

IRSB/IRIS emergency armed response teams head through secret tunnels from their bases to the palace. Automated weapons begin firing at them and their directors disable the palace's underground weapons systems with emergency override codes.

General Cognatus is on Coruscant inspecting the army, having been there following Jim's return to meet with him. He recieves Code 7 alarm message and he calls for a Military Red Alert and gathers a strike force of Army ground troops and loads in shuttles - travels to Palace to "Storm the Palace" and provide safety. He is unable to get initial clearance from the automated shields to allow him passage into the palace with his troops through the hangar. 

MEANWHILE Revan heads toward the palace having returned from a trip and notices the army assault force preparing to storm the palace. He confronts Gen. Cognatus and demands an explanation. While in conversation he gets a delayed Code 7 alarm message and then understands, and immediately issues emergency override codes for the palace shields to allow Dene's troops to land. He joins Dene and they strike from the opposite end.

 

I have no idea where Layha or her involvement will be. She will have to help decide that.

 

Note that Martial Law can be declared by military leaders (with branch leader/MoD approval) in most systems. Coruscant requires Throne approval. Follow the order of succession should the throne be unreachable.

 

Royal Family will be trapped and will team up together and the noghri will be with them, some with Revan, some with Kaidlen. (Perhaps Jim will get into lightsaber fight with Kennedy and OM & Revan will show up at the end (having been to late but will be able to provide any needed medical or other assistance). We will likely be going with the Sith betrayal option with Kennedy being behind it all somehow.

 

We have lots of options on who, what, when, where, how, etc. All suggestions and feedback welcome. Lots of in-betweens to plan.
