Major Frederick Downsend, Attache to the Chief of Army Operations was in the Senate building on the Executive level. He walked alongside with Jonus Quell’ter, Personal Aide to the Supreme Chancellor. Since General Cognatus was recently working with the Senate on getting special funds for the Army, the two aides saw a lot of each other as late, working together on this proposal. It was not uncommon for them to exchange notes or items for review by either of their superiors. As they were about to leave, Major Downsend paused and turned back around and handed to Jonus a very small package. "Oh, I was wondering if you could put this in the Chancellor's office. I am not sure what it is myself but its from my boss to yours, something private they've been working on that is apparently classified, so don't open it. But if you could drop it off in his office for me that would be great."



"Sure thing Downsend. No prob. Catch ya later."



The two parted their seperate ways and the Major, once out of sight, hurried out of the building as fast as he could.

More alarms sounded at the Palace Situation Room. 



"Your Excellency," Colonel Bremen, who was first to react. "There appears to have been an explosion in one of the Senate offices on the executive level. I don't have an exact location, but IRSB Rapid Response is on its way to investigate. There is the possibility this could be linked, of course, but one never knows." He eyed the Director of the IRSB, who was actively shouting orders into his comlink.



Let me know as soon as you find something out, Colonel. If this is linked, I need to know about it." The Chancellor turned around and spoke so that everyone in the vicinity could hear. "A suggestion was made that some sort of public address should be given. I feel that this is necessary, and as the acting representative of the Throne on duty this responsibility falls on my shoulders, to assure the people that everything is under control, give or take. What I am going to tell them...I am not sure yet. I do have some decisions to make, however, regarding these issues we are facing."



The Chancellor paused as everyone looked at him. He was the ranking Throne member on Coruscant, and therefore had the full authority of the Throne, though they had not seen him exercise any of this authority as of yet. They could tell he was faced with some big decisions, though like any normal authority figure, he did not like to make decisions without enough information in front of him to base them on. "Get Channel One ready, I will make my address in 20 minutes. I expect to have more answers before then." He said sternly.
