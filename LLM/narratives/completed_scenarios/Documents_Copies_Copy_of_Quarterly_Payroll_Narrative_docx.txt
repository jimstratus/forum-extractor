Payroll Process Narrative



Quarterly Tasks: 



1. Process the Additions to the Payroll and make sure that all of their information is correct and that the data is pulling correctly from the additions page.

2. Verify the Membership of the Imperial Republic High Council, the Royal Family, the Throne, and the Management of KDY and IR Holdings, Inc. from COMPNOR.

3. Send Letter to the Minister of Defense, the Minister of State, the Grand Minister of COMPNOR, and the High Prince to get a list of active members over the current pay period.

4. Have the Minister of Defense, Minister of State, High Prince, Executor, Grand Minister of COMPNOR, sign-off on the applicable rank chart pages, verifying that they are correct.

5. Have the High Prince and Executor Sign-Off on the Pay-grades per rank.

6. Obtain written details about any bonus compensation or adjusted pay for an individual in writing.

7. Determine the amount of Salaries.

8. Perform the calculations for the High Council, Royal Throne/Family, & KDY/IR Holdings Adjustments.

9. Have the High Prince and Executor Sign-off on Adjustment calculations.

10. Perform adjustments.

11. Determine Monthly Compensation.

12. Calculate Quarterly Compensation.

13. Calculate Bonus.

14. Add Bonus amount to Quarterly Compensation Payable to determine the Total Compensation payable.

15. Have the Preparer, the Preparer's Supervisor, and a member of the Throne Sign-off on the Total Compensation Payable.

16. Then distribute the pay.
