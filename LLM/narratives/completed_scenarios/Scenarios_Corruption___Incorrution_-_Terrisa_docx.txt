CORUSCANT - ROYAL PALACE



Diandra walked past Terrisa’s office and peeked inside. Noticing her deputy getting ready to leave, she decided to stop at the threshold. “Heading out?” Diandra asked.



Terrisa looked up from her purse and smiled at Diandra. “Ah, Lady Devin! Indeed I am! Heading out for dinner.”



Diandra tried to hide a look of shock on her face. “Really? That's a rare sight! Give Director Klone my best regards.”



Terrisa laughed a little, at which Diandra tilted her head slightly out of confusion. “He’s far too busy right now for dinner, what with all his duties.” She flashed Diandra a wide grin. “I'm actually heading to the shopping district to dine with a couple of friends at their restaurant.” 



“Is that so? Don’t let me hold you up then. Have fun! Enjoy yourself!” Diandra said. Before walking away, she added, “If you do happen to see your husband later tonight, let him know I need the IRIN report by tomorrow afternoon.”



Terrisa smiled and nodded. “Certainly! I'll make sure he does it.”

CORUSCANT - SHOPPING DISTRICT



“Is this the first time you’ve had dinner outside of Royal Palace since you’ve arrived on Coruscant?” Abigail Sorrel, Terrisa’s childhood best friend, asked.



Terrisa sat at a table with the co-owners of the Sorrel Family Tavern, a human couple around Terrisa’s age. Though the restaurant wasn’t closed, there were a few tables that were occupied, mostly attributed to the two royal guardsmen standing watch by the entrance of the establishment. Several of the patrons gave occasional glances to the three as they conversed, though the looks on their faces seemed more like surprise than anything else.



Terrisa drank a bit of her Corellian Ale before responding, “It is. I’ve been too busy to explore beyond the walls of the Palace, and my husband’s still getting situated with his new office, so we still haven’t had dinner together.” 



Abby rested her hand on one of Terrisa’s hands. “And you said you have a son now?”



Terrisa smiled. “Yes. Kalen. He’s only a few months old right now.”



Michael Sorrel, Abby’s husband, laughed. “They’re a handful, aren’t they?”



Abby kicked her husband under the table and gave Terrisa a quick smile. “I’m glad you decided to settle down and start a family after so long, Risa.” 



Terrisa smiled back at her friend and pushed her chair out. “If you’ll excuse me, I need to use the ladies’ room.” She stands from her seat and makes her way to the back of the restaurant. The two guardsmen by the doorway, noticing this, follow behind her.



As the wife of the Director of Intelligence, she was taught to always be alert and had honed her observation skills over the years. When Terrisa exits the restroom, she notices quickly that her bodyguards are not in sight. Instantly she knows something is wrong, as they would never leave their posts. Uncertain of her friends’ involvement, and not wanting to put them in danger, Terissa finds a discreet exit and quickly leaves. She brings her comlink to her head but hears static. They are jamming communications. Whoever they are, they aren’t messing around. Before she has a chance to make a plan or get out in plain view to hail a taxicab she is hit from behind with something, and falls to the ground, out cold.
