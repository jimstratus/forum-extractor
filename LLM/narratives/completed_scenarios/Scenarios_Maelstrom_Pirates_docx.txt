Slate Commor

Captain of the Maelstrom



Sex: Male

Species: Human

Home Planet: Coruscant

Age: 59, appears 45



Physical Description

Slate has all the appearance of a distinguished gentleman. He has a square jaw and a salt-and-pepper beard, matching his graying brown hair. His eyes are dark brown, almost maroon, and although his face is beginning to wrinkle, he only looks more handsome with age. Slate has a tall, muscular build, and works hard to keep his body in top shape.

Personality

Slate turned to piracy not for the gold, but for power and the joy of killing. He has an explosive temper. A man with just enough Force sensitivity to make his life easier, he is a criminal mastermind. Dark, dangerous, and manipulative, it’s wise to stay on his good side.

History

Born into a happy home, Slate had a fondness for the dark side of life at an early age. He started small, killing animals and insects under the guise of science. At the age of eight, his experiments led to an accident that killed his parents, sending him to a series of foster homes who never kept him for long. One foster parent reported that Slate “had an aura about him” that “unsettled” them. 

At the age of eighteen, Slate ran away from his foster home and joined a pirate crew. Little is known about his life until he re-emerged twenty years later as first mate of the Dreadnaught. He spent the next five years planning mutiny, gathering a crew loyal to him and him alone. When the time was right, he killed the captain and re-Christened the Dreadnaught into the Maelstrom.

According to sources, Slate has spent the past 16 raiding ships and preparing for “something big”. 

Adra Delys

First Mate and Pet Jedi of the Maelstrom



Sex: Female

Species: Human

Home Planet: Tatooine

Age: 30, appears 25



Physical Description

Adra has a striking face and deep brown eyes. Her hair is flaming red and falls to her waist, usually pinned up in buns or horsetails. She is medium height, lithe, and agile. 

Personality

As beautiful as she is cruel, Adra is Slate’s on-again, off-again lover and chief lieutenant. Fiercely devoted to her captain, she is a fully trained Sith. Whatever compassion she once had has been lost to heartbreak and pain. All that remains is a powerhouse of malice and obsessive worship of Slate. 

History

Adra was born into an abusive home, where she spent her prime years in constant fear for her life. At the age of ten, her father died, and she was passed to her uncle, who saw the Force potential in her and chose to keep her as a tool for his own gain.

Five years later, she met Slate Commor, who recognized her as a kindred spirit, and killed her uncle. Promising her Jedi training and a life of freedom, he convinced her to leave with him. 

True to his word, Slate sought a teacher for her—one who wouldn’t hesitate to train a broken spirit, and one who wouldn’t take issue with Slate’s influence corrupting her. Adra mourned their separation, and promised herself she would join Slate’s crew as soon as she was fully trained.

When her training was complete, Slate sought her out and seduced her, securing her loyalty to his cause. She joined the crew of the newly re-Christened Maelstrom, and has served Slate ever since as his lover and pet.

Klio “the Geist” Jadir

Pirate of the Maelstrom



Sex: Male

Species: Human

Home Planet: Unknown

Age: 40, appears 40



Physical Description

Klio is a scarred and twisted mass of a human being. An escaped slave, he has wild blue eyes and a fierce smile. Scars litter his body, particularly his throat, from a slave collar. He wears them with pride, daring the world to enslave him again. He is lean and appears almost frail, but is substantially stronger than he looks.

Personality

Volatile and unpredictable, Klio resents authority. He is loyal to Slate only because of the role Slate played in his escape. Sometimes referred to as “the Geist”, Klio walks with a hunch and is known to leap on enemies, digging his fingers into their eyes and ripping them out. Klio is among the most dangerous members of the Maelstrom.

History

Klio was born into slavery on an unknown planet outside the rule of the Imperial Republic. Passed from master to master, he fostered a grudge against authority, and vowed to escape and kill everyone who had ever whipped him.

When he met Slate Commor at the age of 25, his vows came true, and Slate bought him from his then-master, ordering Klio to kill the man immediately afterwards. Freeing him under the promise of loyalty, Slate aided him in his search for former masters, teaching him how to use a weapon and prolong the kill.

A jabbering wreck, Klio followed Slate out of gratitude, and has worked under him for so long that he has forgotten Slate is technically a former master. Most of the crew avoids him, so he seeks his primary companionship in Slate and Adra, who find his mannerisms endearing.
