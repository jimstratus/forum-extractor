Ender's Escape Scenario

Timeline

 

The assassination of Mikolo’s father, as well as Mikolo’s recent assignments, prompts him to re-evaluate his core beliefs and question Nathan’s motives.

Mikolo discovers Nathan’s cooperative plot with other companies to expand Vashon’s borders. These plans involve a risky tactic – a border conflict with the IR. He also discovers Nathan’s involvement in the assassination of his (Mikolo’s) father.

Mikolo devises an intricate plan to leave Vashon, destroy all that Nathan has built, and hinder the border conflict with the IR. Most of his team agrees and they set the plan in motion. Only the pilot, Tom Perkins, disagrees. When Tom threatens to expose Mikolo’s escape plan, he is killed.

Teage, an undercover operative for the IR, discovers Mikolo’s plan to destroy ZenTech. He has been monitoring Nathan’s activities and watching for other questionable activities. He approaches Mikolo, informing him of the loose ends and offering to help Mikolo in exchange for his life. Teage offers the team safety within the IR’s borders as refugees and citizens of the IR. In turn, Mikolo must provide assistance in fending off Vashon’s border assault. Further negotiations result in the following terms: Mikolo is required to bring to the IR border fleet one of ZenTech’s Cooperative Ion Fleets: CIF Hinder. Mikolo is also to aid the IR in the diagnosing and using of these ships. Teage is required to aid Mikolo in any way he can and to help Mikolo smuggle in and liscence his own private freighter, as well as a cache of ZenTech’s equipment. Teage is also required to guarantee the team’s freedom at any cost once in the IR’s borders. Teage makes contact with the IR to inform them of their current situation. Teage is then transferred to the team as their new pilot.

The escape does not go as smoothly as they hoped for. Both Mikolo and Kaci are injured in the escape. Mikolo loses consciousness. A few of the explosives set to destroy ZenTech do not go off.

Mikolo regains consciousness. After a short recovery, the team is inderdicted by the IR at the border. Teage and Mikolo offer further explanation as they are interrogated by the IR.

CIF Hinder is delivered to the IR.

Teage aids Mikolo and the team in smuggling in their personal freighter and other goods of a personal nature.
