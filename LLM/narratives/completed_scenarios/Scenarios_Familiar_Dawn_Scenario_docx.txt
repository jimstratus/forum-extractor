Familiar Dawn

Return from the Shadows

Revised: 19 August 2008



Synopsis: Members of the Royal Family each have a Force vision, in which they recognize a set of procedures they each must do (each unique to them). They recognize the need for both speed and subtlety. Marc gathers Noghri warriors and mobilizes them. Nicole and Ashlee travel to Kuat to the Family’s secret and hidden Force temple in Mt. Dagoon. Kaidlen Shan goes on a mission to Kuat, also having a vision/dream, and meets the Royal Family there. High Prince James Stratus returns from the shadows, making his first appearance at Mt. Dagoon, his second at a rendezvous with the Royal Fleet, and his third at Coruscant, where he meets with the Throne. Layha steps down and returns the command of the Imperial Republic and all its inhabitants and forces to James Stratus. Scenario ends. Ender's Escape scenario involving IR military and outer-rim forces takes place starting in step 4 as seperate scenario running simultaneously and connected.

Dreams/Visions:



1. Marc’s vision (Takes Place while meditating at Mt. Dagoon)

See’s himself among the Noghri gathering warriors. Sees Throne chamber at Palace where Jim used to sit and his office. Sees his siblings gathered at Mt. Dagoon with Noghri warriors in anticipation of someone important’s arrival.

2. Nicole’s vision (takes place in Jedi temple on Corellia while she is engaged in exercises with a friend/mentor)

See’s herself inside the Temple library looking for a certain datacard only a member of the Royal Family can access. Sees herself making contact with special agents and being smuggled to Kuat where she meets someone she knows inside Mt. Dagoon.

3. Ashlee’s vision (takes place on board her private yacht while traveling with a friend)

Sees herself traveling to a smuggler base nearby where Stratus Industries has contacts. She discovers a library of datacards at her friendly contact’s base and discovers one only she is able to decode with a family code. (She will recognize on the card a SISAFET contact). See’s herself being smuggled to Kuat and sends her friend to another place with her ship.

4. Kaidlen’s vision (takes place while sleeping at her Coruscant apt).

Sees herself going to Kuat to inspect Naval Headquarters and other military operations. Sees herself obtaining Layha’s permission to go, telling Layha that the Force is sending her there and she does not know why. Sees herself traveling to Kuat and staying at the Stratus Palace after inspecting the military base of operations. Discovers familiar Noghri who lead her to Mt. Dagoon through a series of underground tunnels, and meets someone waiting there she knows well.

5. Layha’s vision (Takes place on Coruscant while in office)

Sees Jim arriving at Coruscant with the Royal Fleet

Background Information

Mt. Dagoon – Stratus Family Force Temple built inside the mountain nearest the Kuati Royal Palace. The mountain has secret passageways from the Palace underground and to an emergency bunker/hangar. Only James, Marc, Nicole, and Ashlee know how to get to it. Kaidlen and Layha only know of its existence outside the immediate Royal Family. Mountain/Temple/Base is a fortress of it’s own with undetectable weapons and shields and a hangar for emergency landing or escape.



SISAFET - Stratus Industries Security Agency Family Emergency Travel – A secret department of the family’s corporation/enterprise used to smuggle them out/in to places in event of security failure or emergency.



IRPA – Imperial Republic Port Authority – has registration info on all ships privately owned and regulates control of travel (All ships file “flight plan” per se like our own Air Traffic control system in the USA) Notices when ships arrive late or not at registered destination. All ships upon arrival and departure of any major system are first cleared through IRPA.



Royal Fleet Before James left, he set up hard coded/hidden orders in the ships’ computers that the Fleet commander and ship captains know of. When activated by remote signal, they are to jump to the pre-designated rendezvous points until locating the unique beacon call of the Dawn Treader.



Noghri affiliation to the Royal Family – The Noghri race have an unchallengeable allegiance to the royal family as a result of a great love they have for them for liberating them from Sith oppression and saving their race from extinction. They are a backup and private guarding Force (“in the shadows”) to members of the House Stratus and sometimes close friends. Four clans are each assigned to a family member to protect, the Overclan being dormant, having formerly protected James until he left – though a few close associates volunteered and insisted on going with him to the Shadows.



IRIS – Imperial Republic Intelligence Service



IRSB – Imperial Republic Security Bureau
