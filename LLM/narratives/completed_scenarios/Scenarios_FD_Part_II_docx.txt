The Dawn Treader soared through hyperspace. "It's going to be different, isn't it? Being back in the center of things, amongst many changes." It was the voice of Captain Megan De`Soto, longtime captain of the Prince's Royal Guard. She had been with James Stratus since the foundation of the Imperial Republic.

 

"Yes Megan, it certainly will be different. Though I dare say it will probably not be pleasant. There is much trouble ahead. The Dark Side of the Force surrounds the Imperial Republic. Something is about to unfold, and when it does, it could change the entire circumstance of the government."

 

A gravely voice spoke from the back. "The Noghri will of course be ready to provide whatever assistance you require." It was the voice of one of his two Noghri guards that had chosen to accompany him on the long journey. He had traveled only with the two royal guards, the two most loyal to him whom he trusted above all, and two stubborn Noghri who had always been at his side on previous missions.

 

"This is it! Prepare for reversion to realspace. Buckle in! There's no telling what to expect." The Dawn Treader reverted to realspace and the screen began beeping with all sorts of alerts. The ship had been disguised and its transponders modified so that it would not be recogized. It was running on stealth mode so that it would not be detected immediately. There were a few surprises installed as well. Immediately he felt the presence of several powerful beings nearby. He looked at the screen. Both Command Fleets were in the system. That would explain one of the presences he felt, Admiral OVERMIND. He could also feel...his family..they had gathered at Kuat. They had responded to the images he sent them in a vision.

 

"Alright, let's execute this code sequence before we are discovered, it's only a matter of time now. We must be precise exactly in every way for this to work, or else we're fried gungan." James Stratus activated the transmitter and a series of code sequences were transmitted, afterwhich the Dawn Treader simply vanished from view. The cloaking device activated. "Increase engine power! We must do this fast!" The Prince piloted the ship himself, with Megan as his copilot. The ship bursted forward and soared past the three fleets guarding the system. As they approached the planet, the shield opened up at a timed sequence in a specific place, where the Prince had transmitted for it to open. It was unlikely such an opening in the shield for a brief period would be detected, but it was possible. The experts maintaining the field were just that, experts. Luckily all his failsafe security codes were still in place and active. They were hidden well. He was not heading for the Palace. This must be quick and timely, he thought. I will have to decloak to transmit to the mountain to open a portal to recieve me before I am noticed. He held his breath, and pressed a series of buttons. The ship decloaked, and the transmitter began. A very small port opened in the mountain and he zoomed in as the portal closed behind him. He was the only one who had the codes and knowledge on how to get inside the mountain from outside the Palace. The ship landed, and the Royal Family, as well as a contigent of Noghri warriors with weapons at the ready, waited for the ship to land. They waited anxiously as the boarding ramp descended.

Jim Stratus ran down the ramp, disposing of all "pleasantries" or formalities, and hugged each of his family members. "It's so good to see all of you again! We have so much to talk about! I am glad that you all recieved the messages I sent to you through the Force, otherwise you would not be here."

 

Marc's eyebrows raised, understanding, while Nicole frowned. "You mean that was you? But how did you---?" she asked but decided not to finish the question. "Can you feel it, Jim? The change in the Force? The dark side rising?"

 

Jim nodded. "There is little time to waste. We must act quickly before it is too late. I must return to Coruscant. I must return to the Throne before it becomes too late. Layha knows I have returned. Undoubtedly the Sith may also. Either way they shall know soon enough. You know what we have to do..." his voice trailed off. They all nodded. There was a protocol for one to take an extended leave of absence from the Throne, just as there was a protocol for one to return. Since the Royal Family as a whole had the ultimate power, (though that was unknown to most - as it was hidden in the charter, and their power was only valid when asbolutely united and unanimous - and when it concerned mostly matters of the Throne.) They had to approve by unanimous voting in front of authorized witnesses the return of a person from leave of absence to the Throne, when it involved the top.

 

Some familiar individuals descended the boarding ramp, following behind Jim. His two loyal guards, his two beloved Noghri friends, who also guarded him. Then a lone woman followed behind them all, slightly younger than James to say the least. She caught his eye and he gave her a slight smile. "You may join us and witness this, if you'd like," he spoke someone softly to her. Those present wondered who she was of course, but James offered no explanation and they did not ask. He turned to Kaidlen, and rejoiced at seeing her again. He gave her a big hug. "Kaidlen....it is so wonderful to see you again and still well. Would you please act as an official witness of what is about to take place?"

 

She nodded. "Of course, Sire. It would be my pleasure. Who will be your second witness?" A minimum of two authorized witnesses, besides the immediate Royal family, was required.

 

"Captain De`Soto. You will join Kaidlen as a witness."



"Yes mi'lord."

 

They walked into a large conference chamber. They took their places, with James at one of the ends of the table, and Marc at the other. As this motion invovled James, Marc would preside over these precedings.
