# Like Unto The Romans - Plot Outline of Established Events

## Initial Incident
- A COMPNOR scientific team (Interstellar Explorers) sent to Almania to investigate special energy-emitting gems is murdered in Stonia, the capital city.
- Lieutenant Riloan Cotar of the Stonia Police Force investigates the deaths.
- Evidence suggests the killings were carried out by the Albatross (Almanian Army Elite forces).

## Intelligence Response
- IRIS Directorate of Operations loses track of the Explorer team.
- Underdirector Lan Klone orders agents to infiltrate Stonia's police force.
- The infiltration team confirms 11 Imperials were murdered (9 COMPNOR personnel, a military security officer, and a representative from Princess Ashlee's company).
- Terrisa Klone reports findings to IRSB Director Applebee.

## Royal Family Involvement
- Princess Ashlee Stratus-Gourdine, who commissioned the team to investigate the gems, becomes concerned when communication ceases.
- She contacts Grand Admiral Taftican requesting investigation.
- Unknown to Ashlee, the team included a DCI agent and a fleet security officer close to Admiral Taftican.

## Military Escalation
- Grand Admiral Taftican confirms the murders and reports directly to High Prince James Stratus.
- Stratus orders immediate military action:
  - Mobilization of the 5th Assault Fleet
  - Complete blockade of the Almanian system
  - Preparation for a ground invasion

## Parallel Events
- An assassination attempt occurs against High Prince James Stratus.
- Praetor Kaidlen Shan, Commandant of the Royal Guard, protects the High Prince.
- Shadow Guards pursue the would-be assassin.
- Heightened security measures implemented across Coruscant.

## Political Developments
- Supreme Chancellor Joesefus Quick announces his resignation.
- High Prince Stratus promotes Admiral Jared Quinn to Grand Admiral.
- Stratus announces the "Stratus Doctrine on Civil Safety" declaring that any attack on an Imperial Republic citizen will be considered an act of war.

## Princess Ashlee's Journey
- Despite her brother's objections, Princess Ashlee insists on traveling to Almania.
- High Prince arranges for Admiral Kelly to escort her aboard the *Scarlet Angel*.
- As the *Scarlet Angel* enters the Almanian system, it's ambushed by unknown enemies using gravity-well generators.

## Intelligence Response to Royal Threats
- IRIS-DCI detects potential threats against multiple royal family members (codenames Bysen and Apollo).
- Simon Morris, Bureau Chief of IRIS-DCI, assigns additional protection to Princess Ashlee.
- Investigation into the connection between the Almania murders and the assassination attempt begins.

## Current Crisis Points
1. The *Scarlet Angel* with Princess Ashlee aboard is under attack by unknown forces.
2. The High Prince has declared the intention to invade Almania.
3. The assassination attempt against the High Prince remains unsolved.
4. The Supreme Chancellor has resigned, creating a power vacuum.
5. The properties and significance of the Almanian gems remain unexplored.


# Like Unto The Romans - Plot Development Outline

## 1. The Ambush on the *Scarlet Angel*
- The *Scarlet Angel* with Princess Ashlee and Commodore Travis Gourdine aboard has been ambushed by unknown forces
- Immediate action: Admiral Kelly must defend the ship and protect the royal passengers
- The identity of these attackers must be revealed: Possibilities include:
  - Almanian allies who learned of the Imperial Republic's planned invasion
  - A third party with interest in escalating the conflict
  - Traitors within the Imperial Republic who want to destabilize the government during the Chancellor transition

## 2. The Assassination Attempt on High Prince James Stratus
- The perpetrators and their motives need to be uncovered
- Investigation by IRIS-DCI, possibly led by Simon Morris and Lan Klone
- Potential connection to the Almanian situation
- Security measures at the palace need to be reviewed - possible infiltration of security forces

## 3. The Invasion of Almania
- Grand Admiral Taftican's task force is preparing for full-scale invasion
- Need to show the military operation from multiple perspectives:
  - Grand Admiral Taftican commanding the fleet
  - Marine forces on the ground
  - Local resistance on Almania, possibly from Riloan Cotar's perspective
  - Diplomatic fallout from the invasion

## 4. The Transition of Power in the Imperial Republic
- Chancellor Quick has resigned, creating a power vacuum that needs to be filled
- High Prince Stratus must select a new Chancellor
- Potential candidates and political maneuvering from various factions
- The timing of this transition during a crisis creates additional tension

## 5. The Mysterious Gems of Almania
- The original mission was to investigate special energy-emitting gems with potential lightsaber applications
- These gems could be a valuable resource worth killing for
- Exploration of their properties and significance to both the Imperial Republic and Almania
- Possible Force-related properties that interest both Jedi and potential Sith adversaries

## 6. The Intelligence Operations
- IRIS and DCI operations need to coordinate to uncover the truth behind both the murders and assassination attempt
- Lan Klone and Terrisa Klone working together across agencies
- Infiltration agents on Almania discovering critical intelligence
- Revelation of potential double agents or traitors within the Imperial Republic

## 7. The Personal Dimension: Princess Ashlee's Friend
- The fate of Princess Ashlee's close friend who was part of the murdered expedition
- Her personal quest for answers and justice while potentially caught in an ambush
- The relationship between the royal siblings during crisis

## 8. Military Response and Strategic Planning
- Grand Admiral Quinn's new role in organizing the military response
- Coordination between multiple fleet elements and ground forces
- Strategic challenges of invading a sovereign world
- Possible diplomatic alternatives that emerge during military preparation

## Character-Focused Continuation Points

### High Prince James Stratus
- Dealing with multiple crises simultaneously: assassination attempt, Almania situation, and governance transition
- Making difficult decisions about military action and appointing a new Chancellor
- Personal concern for his sister who may be in danger
- Possible reflection on the implications of the "Stratus Doctrine" he just established

### Princess Ashlee Stratus-Gourdine
- Immediate survival situation aboard the ambushed *Scarlet Angel*
- Her determination to find out what happened to her friend
- Tension with her brother over his protective instincts
- Working with her husband, who as a Commodore has military expertise

### Admiral/Grand Admiral Jared Quinn
- Adjusting to his sudden promotion to Grand Admiral
- Organizing the military response to Almania
- Balancing his loyalty to the High Prince with strategic military considerations
- Leading his team into a new type of engagement

### Grand Admiral Tura Taftican
- Executing the blockade and preparing for invasion
- Dealing with the sudden arrival of Princess Ashlee in a dangerous situation
- Political implications of the military action
- Possible discovery that there's more to the Almanian situation than initially appeared

### Lan Klone and Terrisa Klone
- Investigating the link between the murders on Almania and the assassination attempt
- Uncovering potential conspiracies within the Imperial Republic
- Working across agency boundaries to assemble the full intelligence picture
- Personal relationship dynamics during a crisis situation

### Lieutenant Riloan Cotar (Almanian perspective)
- Caught between loyalty to his planet and recognition of his leader's wrongdoing
- Potential to become an asset to the Imperial Republic
- Perspective on the blockade and impending invasion from the Almanian side
- Knowledge about the mysterious gems that others might not have

### Kaidlen Shan
- Enhanced security operations after the assassination attempt
- Coordinating with intelligence services to prevent further attacks
- Personal devotion to protecting the High Prince
- Tactical planning for future public appearances under heightened threat

## Next Steps in the Story

1. **Crisis aboard the *Scarlet Angel***: Show the battle that ensues when the ship is ambushed, focusing on Admiral Kelly's tactical response and Princess Ashlee's experience of being caught in combat.

2. **Investigation of the Assassination Attempt**: Follow Kaidlen Shan and intelligence operatives as they interrogate captured assailants or gather evidence.

3. **Military Mobilization**: Show Grand Admiral Quinn beginning to assemble forces for potential action against Almania, and Grand Admiral Taftican implementing the blockade.

4. **High Prince's Decision-Making**: Show James Stratus working with advisors to develop a comprehensive response to the Almanian situation while also considering Chancellor candidates.

5. **Intelligence Breakthrough**: Have Lan and Terrisa Klone discover critical information about the connection between events on Almania and Coruscant.
