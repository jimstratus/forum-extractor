The Praetorian Order

Legal Brief prepared by Rachel Love, J.D. - Imperial Republic General Counsel

[DRAFT]

IRIN # G349933



Founded by the Supreme Ruler, the Praetorian Order was created to serve as a dual purpose, both as an Order of Honors and Trust, and second of great position and power. Those appointed to this order are granted both the title and position of Praetor, which is both a rank and title. On the Rank Chart it is the highest High Command Rank in the HC-series, (HC-6), and directly below the Throne (rank series, RT, meaning Royal [Imperial] Throne). A Praetor is also a title, which could fall between a Prince (heir to the Kingdom) and a Duke in the Peerage system, though below the Grand Duke) in the Regasterra. Praetors are the most senior agents of the Throne, and have the authority to act on behalf of any member of the Throne. Generally they only take action when on assignment or within their assigned realm or aspect of influence (which will be explained in a later paragraph), though they have the authority to act independently in any aspect of the Imperial Republic Government should they witness something that requires action or that is brought to their attention. The Supreme Ruler has entrusted that they will use best judgement in all matters, whether to defer to their colleagues or the appropriate jurisdictions or to act of their own free will, or to escalate and seek guidance from the Throne, especially in delicate or matters of extreme importance.



According to the Prime Directive C405047, Praetors are essentially Agents of the Throne and High Ministers which oversee functionality and operations of super-ministries of the Imperial Republic Government. Praetors are Officers of the Prime Directive. Together with the Executor they make up the Prime Council and have the following special powers: -Execute the Prime Directive Weld and use an Ultimate Authorization Code -Issue orders/commands on behalf of the Throne in person or via official communique



Each Praetor is assigned what is known as an aspect of oversight or realm of influence within the imperial Republic. The Supreme Ruler may appoint additional Praetors and create new areas of oversight or realms of influence, or create Praetors-at-Large. While each area has its own leadership such as Directors, Ministers, etc., who report not to the Praetorian but to their chain of command or in many cases the Throne directly, the Praetorian have oversight authority within their areas of oversight and may exert that authority as representatves or agents of the Throne at any time, independently of the Throne, so lone as not to make drastic changes such as replacing the Throne’s appointed leaders, though they may make arrests and press charges with cause.



The areas of influence currently are as follows:

His Majesty’s Special Forces (Paladins/Black Ops)

Imperial Republic Armed Forces

COMPNOR

Ministry of State

Lord Protector of the Imperial Republic (Lord Praetor/Senior Praetor)
