Individuals (for lack of better term – suggestions welcome)



Supreme Ruler = Lord of the Guard.

- Appoints Commandant.

- Designates protectees and titles and attachment types.



Commandant – Appointed by Supreme Ruler.

- Oversees personnel and coordinates multi-branch affairs.

- Assigns attachments to protectees

- Coordinates with Chief Lord of the Guard in designation of protectees and attachment types but can independently do so if situation requires

- Is protected by the same guard attachment of Supreme Ruler or independent depending on assignment

- Reports directly to the Supreme Ruler



Authoritative Protectee – A designated protectee with direct authority over attachment assigned and can issue orders including arrest and mission assignments. Answers to the Supreme Ruler. Assigned own attachment.



Protectee – A person designated under protection of the Royal Guard. May be assigned own attachment or share with another protectee as determined by commandant and Supreme Ruler.



Attachment Leader – Reports directly to the commandant and the Authoritative Protectee if protectee is designated such. Appointed by Commandant in coordination with the Authoritative Protectee if applicable. Can be changed by Authoritative Protectee with Commandant approval.
