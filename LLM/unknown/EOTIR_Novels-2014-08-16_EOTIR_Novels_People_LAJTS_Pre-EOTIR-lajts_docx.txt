Ep. XOXOXOXOXOXOXOX



200ABY



Many attempts of galactic unification have failed. Remnants of various alliances and sectors rule over themselves in varying styles of government. The Galactic Alliance is fallen, and the Jedi and Sith are scattered across the galaxy. 



A sovereign monarchial system of government is established in the Kuat Sector under the reign of King James Stratus I (the first) and Queen Adaline Hoffner Sratus of Hapes, the King’s wife. Noble Houses and fiefdoms are formed on Kuat from among those which are tied by blood, allegiance, and values, though all remaining under the rule of the Royal House Stratus. Young princes and princesses are born in the Royal House, and brought up to become royal heirs. 



212ABY



Disagreement and unrest arise among the fiefdoms of Kuat, establishing a split in loyalties among the fiefdoms of Kuat establishing a large rebellion united behind the House Badine against the Royal House Stratus. King James Stratus I is killed and the Royal House Stratus flees to Hapes for refuge.
