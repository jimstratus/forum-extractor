The galactic government, the Imperial Republic, funds many research organizations and purposes through government grants, loans, donations, contribution, and of course, direct funding used for specific tasks or missions they have such organizations perform, governmental or third-party. Nebular Enterprises is one of those such information organizations. Nebular Enterprises set up one such expedition using funds from the government to explore the ‘outer-regions’ of known space, to go a little beyond. Since they are not sure what lies there, they have outfitted a capital ship (civil-class of course, but modified), a Maurader-class Corvette, with a capacity of carrying 200 people, being approximately 200 meters long. This ship, christened the Knowledge Seeker, was modified to have weaponry and shielding higher than the normal standard and legal boundaries as well as an armed security team with military background/training placed aboard. The reasons for this was to protect the expedition and better prepare them for what could lie ahead; the unknown. The specific mission of this expedition was to discover or attempt to discover new solar systems not charted and to look for inhabitable planets (or inhabited planets) and study species and life forms on them, so as to expand the government’s map of charted space. As the entire crew and expedition team consists of approximately 200 people, some key characters are Ryan, the ship’s captain and main pilot, he is a male Human who loves women, a skilled pilot and ship expert (building, repairs, make, upgrades, etc.), very and sometimes overly enthusiastic, however unprofessional, somewhat ignorant yet handsome, and in an emergency situation he can take complete control due to his military background; Shay, the lead scientist/expedition leader with some piloting skills and takes the role of copilot, thinks of Ryan as cute but annoying (likes him deep down but does not realize it, or want to think about or admit it), strikingly beautiful, feminine, has skills in solving problems, making predictions, professional and diplomatic; Marcha, a drall (short alien race), who is proud, an ancestor to a former Governor-General of a solar system, who’s primary interests are archaeology and history, she often exaggerates, and is clumsy; Ephraim, a male drall, who is interested in puzzles/mysteries and solving them, he loves history and archaeology, and is very specific, exact, precise, stubborn, and has a good memory. The rest of the crew consists of scientists, security teams, and the ship’s crew. For the security of the mission, Nebular Enterprises also included aboard this capital starship an entire squadran of starfighters to protect it. After continuous arguments of the destination coordinates from the key members of the crew, many events lead up to coordinates in the navigational computer being changed and changed and messed up from the original plan. When the ship comes out of hyperspace, they find themselves in an asteroid belt between Jupiter and Mars, and in an unknown solar system far far away. They navigate through the asteroid field but the ship is damaged where the hyperdrive fails and the fuel is also now very low. They send out probes and discover a small planet of ocean with land of significant sizes around. The probe’s data also shows the planet to have strange metallic objects of obsolete technology and power orbiting this planet, which appears to be inhabited. The expedition leaders decide that this planet will be a good place to land, as the raw materials needed for repair of their ship (or their only hope for repair) would be there, and they could have a chance to study the life forms on that planet. They head for the planet (which also happens to be Earth). They find a ‘desert’ portion of land that is semi-remote from civilization and set course for there. Meanwhile, astronauts in the International Space Station can see the Knowledge Seeker outside their window views as a large spacecraft-object. They look at it in awe and fear and contact the planet through their radio waves. As radio-wave-technology is old but still detectable and open-(not encrypted), Ryan is able to pick up the frequencies and hear the conversations. The languages are interpreted by a protocol droid which figures out what they are saying. The planetary governments are having their astrologists be silent and making up excuses or reasons for sudden increases in security measures. Before entering the atmosphere of the planet, Ryan sends a messenger-probe droid back to Imperial Republic space asking for a rescues mission and help. The Knowledge Seeker lands in the desert of Arizona, on the North American continent, and the local Air Force and Army Bases immediately send out troops and craft to investigate. The crew of the Knowledge Seeker see a military force of some kind approaching outside the ship so they make repairs to their shields as much as possible and activate them, while they decide what to do. There are arguments aboard the ship as to how to approach the people, whether to leave the ship or not. Finally, they decide to make a holo-image appear of them outside the ship with an audio broadcast that in several languages and repeated recordings instruct the American troops that an emissary will be unboarding the ship, and that they are to send for ‘appropriate ambassadorial personnel’ to greet them. They also warn them that if the American troops open fire anywhere near the ship that the ship’s auto-defense computers will activate and fire back, and the security teams aboard the ship will also respond.
