SECURITY

Senior staff will be protected by the Royal Guard at all times in and out of the Palace, and will be protectees of the Guard. Junior staff will have Royal Guard and Palace Security protection while on duty, and Security Bureau protection while off duty, and will be monitored by the Royal Guard while off duty. All staff will be provided with military escort when traveling with the Principal or when a emergency transport is needed when seperated from the Principal in a mission- or job- critical circumstance. Military escort will be provided when stranded outside IR territory and IRSB or Royal Guard escort when stranded within IR territory, or military escort in some situations where the Principal requests it and arranges it with the local military commander.
