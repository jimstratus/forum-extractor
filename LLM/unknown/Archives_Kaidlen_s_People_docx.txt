Sentient Species: Nekasan, Nekoisan

Homeworld: Nekoisa

Capital: Mrraanshai

Tech Level: Space going

Government: Hereditary monarchy

Location: Unknown Regions, further out past Csilla, towards the galactic edge 

Terrain: grassy plains and forests



Nekasan characteristics: Bilateral; humanoid; catlike eyes; fanged eyeteeth; striped or spotted patterned skin; sparse body hair (like that of true humans), with heads covered with real hair, 

males tend towards denser hair; low set, pointed ears; no tails; retractable claws on fingers and toes;  females tend to be 1.6 to 2 meters tall, males from 1.8 to 2.6 meters tall; 

plains dwellers. Nomadic clans, except for Royal family.



Nekoisan characteristics: Bilateral; humanoid; catlike eyes; fanged eyeteeth; striped or spotted patterned skin; a dense, downy feeling body fur, with heads covered with real hair, males 

tends towards dense facial and body hair along with the denser body fur: high set, catlike ears; tails; retractable claws on fingers and toes; females tend to be 1 to 1.9 meters tall, males 

from 1.2 to 2 meters tall; forest dwellers. City builders, along tribal groupings.



Nekasans and Nekoisans look similar to each other, with the main noticeable differences being with the ears, and that the Nekoisans have tails. Nekasans tend to run to lighter colorations, while Nekoisans tend to be darker. Both species have a wide variety with coloration. However, a white coloration for either species is extremely rare, and usually only found within the Royal bloodlines. Eye colors tend to be green, gold, greengold, blue, amber, and grey. Neither species is naturally fertile with the other. With medical intervention, the two species are capable of producing offspring, but it is highly frowned upon. The resulting offspring are usually sterile, and are called Nekia. Nekia traits are usually decided upon by their parents.



Royal Nekasans and Nekoisans both are distinguished by their white hair, and light skin colors. They also tend to have bright, light green eyes.



Nekasans and Nekoisans have an average lifespan of about 200 years.



Child: 1-19 

Young Adult: 20-24

Adult: 25- 99

Middle Age: 100-149

Old: 150-179

Venerable: 180+



The Peace Accord- 

There is a peace treaty that exists between the Nekoisans and the Nekasans. It has existed for thousands of years. Every 500 years, a member of each Royal family is mated to their counterpart from the other family. The mating is expected to be consummated. However, only when the mating happens to be a love match does offspring occur. 

This is the other reason for why matings between the two species is frowned upon.
