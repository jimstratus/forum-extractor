[Military Intelligence] Vice Admiral Alun Tringad to Admiral / Director of Military Intelligence**

Because of this tremendous successes I am pleased to promote him to the rank of Admiral and appoint him Director of Military Intelligence.

J. Stratus: Thank you, Grand Admiral Quinn. And Congratulations to you all.



Recently the Ministry of Defense as can see has undergone some reorganization s. As part of that reorganization the Minister of Defense has also reorganized the Internal Affairs Division including the leadership of the same. Now if Minister of Defense himself would please come back to the dias. Thank you, Admiral.

You may have all notice me address Admiral Quinn a moment ago by his military rank and not his current civilian one, which frankly outranks his military one. There is a reason for this. You all know Grand Admiral Jared Quinn to be a military man, a soldier. Perhaps he will always be a soldier in his own mind as well. When he was appointed Minister of Defense, he opted, or rather requested to maintain and/or keep his military rank for the duration of his service in the Imperial Republic Armed Forces, the Ministry of Defense, even as Minister, which by nature was designed to be a civilian role. However, because of a precedent created by the very first Minister of Defense, Fleet Admiral Cohean (later Councilor, then Executor Cohean), every Minister of Defense that followed has requested this privilege to follow them as well and I have allowed it, seeing no better way then a military officer to lead our military. And lead us he has, through times of turbulence and strife, Grand Admiral Quinn, who has also been serving as a full member of the Imperial Republic High Council, has played both administrator and soldier; and we have come here this evening primarily to honor him and one of his colleagues. 

Grand Admiral Quinn, for services to the Ministry of Defense and the Armed Forces in the line of duty, as Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces I hereby promote you to the rank of Lord Admiral, effective immediately. Congratulations.



If we could get Grand Admiral Firecam to join us at the dias,Thanks.

Grand Admiral Locke Firecam,, Deputy Minister of Defense for Operations, and not too long ago was pulled from being “operational” himself to do administrative (desk) work, not too unlike the Minister of Defense himself, though Grand Admiral Firecam is mostly stationed at one particular headquarters whereas Lord Admiral Quinn moves from Coruscant to various headquarter locations. However, not long after giving Grand Admiral Firecam this more “comfortable” assignment, I pulled him from his desk and made him operational again and tasked him with rooting out corruption from within and seeking out and annihilating a pirate base.  He is also a member of the Imperial Republic High Council, as well as an Agent of the Throne, having served as the Throne Regent during the Battle for Coruscant. However, it is because of his most recent actions that I, the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces do hereby promote him to the rank of Lord Admiral, effective as of midnight tonight.

Congratulations, Admiral.
