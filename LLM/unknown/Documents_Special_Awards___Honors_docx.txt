Order of the Emperor's Seal

The Supreme Ruler may invest a recipient with membership within The Order of the Emperor's Seal at his discretion, as recognition for exceptional dedication, loyalty, ability, and the application of those traits.

Order of the IR Seal

A member of the Royal Imperial Throne may invest a recipient with membership within The Order of the Imperial republic Seal at their discretion, as recognition for loyalty beyond the call of duty, and dedication to the ideals of the New Order.

Order of King Charles

King Charles Stratus was the brave and dedicated King of Kuat who sacrificed his life to save his family during the Great Rebellion on Kuat so his (the Royal Family) family could flee to Hapes. Those who are awarded/inducted into this order shall be those whose actions display bravery and sacrifice in service to the Imperial Republic.

Order of Charon

Charon, the first to become an Imperial Republic Knight, displayed true loyalty to the Imperial Republic during the confusing Battle for Coruscant and was one of the first to happen upon Darth Radeous and challenge him, and gave his life in service to the Imperial Republic.Those who are presented/awarded with/inducted into this order shall be those whose actions display true loyalty to the Government of the Imperial Republic and the Royal Imperial Throne.

Order of Tavria or Treyson (or Tavria Treyson)?

The second Grand Minister of COMPNOR (and later an Executor of the Imperial Republic), whose tenure has shown massive changes, growth and improvements to the betterment of the whole of the Imperial Republic Executor Treyson even took part in the Grand Revision of the Imperial Republic Charter, which was a very long and difficult task. Executor/Duchess Tavria Treyson has contributed a great deal to the infrastructure of the Imperial Republic. Those who are inducted into this order and thus awarded will be those who have contributed much to the betterment of the Imperial Republic.
