Ceremony:



My Lords, Ladies, and Gentlemen. We are gathered in this Royal Hall to join in hand Princess Aurielle Jackson-Stratus and retired General and present High Councilor Dene Vye Cognatus. His Majesty the Supreme Ruler has asked that I conduct this ceremony. Official witnesses includes High Prince Stratus, Supreme Chancellor Quick, Praetor Shan, and Grand Admiral Quinn.



This ceremony of Royal Union shall be administered and governed by the marriage laws of the this planet of Coruscant.. 



Does Your Highness, Princess Aurielle Jackson-Stratus, take Dene Vye Cognatus, to be your lawfully wedded husband, to have and to hold from this day forward, for better or for worse, for richer or for poorer, in sickness, and in health, to love and to cherish, now and forever?



I Do So Affirm.



Do you Dene Vye Cognatus, take Her Highness Princess Aurielle Jackson-Stratus, to be your lawfully wedded wife, to have and to hold, from this day forward, for better or for worse, for richer or for poorer, in sickness, and in health, to love and to cherish, now and forever?

Then by laws of Coruscant, and the power invested in me by His Majesty the Supreme Ruler and as a member of the High Judicate, I hereby pronounce you husband and wife, legally and lawfully wedded before these witnesses and the Force itself. As is proper and by the laws and traditions of the Royal Houses of Regasterra, I declare you, Dene, Prince of Kuat, and legal member of the Royal House Stratus.
