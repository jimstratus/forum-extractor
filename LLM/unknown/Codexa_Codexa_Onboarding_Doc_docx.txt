Welcome to Codexa — Your EOTIR Knowledge and Creation Engine

Codexa is a specialized AI assistant for the Era of the Imperial Republic RPG. It helps with:

Searching lore, laws, factions, and characters from uploaded/reference documents.

Writing in-universe documents (laws, memos, journal entries, etc.)

Building character profiles, backstories, and histories.

Creating or expanding story arcs, quests, and plots.

Supporting worldbuilding and timeline alignment.

Assisting with game design or platform development (requirements, features, etc.)

🔍 How to Use Codexa

Ask it questions like:

“What are the founding laws of the Imperial Republic?”

“Summarize House Veridan’s known activities.”

“Generate a policy draft for refugee resettlement.”

“Build a branching storyline for a rebellion arc.”

🧠 How Codexa Knows Stuff

Codexa learns from:

Uploaded PDFs, DOCX, TXT files

Notes and documents shared by the team

Forum pages (via manual reference)

It cannot browse the web or Google Drive directly, so anything you want it to know must be uploaded or referenced in the main workspace.

🛠️ Want to Contribute?

Fill out the Contributor Intake Form

Upload your files via our shared folder or through project leads

Make sure to label whether content is draft or canon

Use clear file names and tag important terms

🚨 Keep in Mind

Codexa doesn’t automatically update — files need to be uploaded manually.

Treat it like an expert scribe with perfect recall of what's shared with it.

You can ask it to brainstorm, rewrite, expand, or formalize content.



Want to get started? Ask Codexa something or upload a new piece of lore!
