Ceremony:



We are gathered together as friends and family, great Houses of the Regasterra, for this Royal Union of two of the Great Houses, and to unite the fair and beautiful Duchess Tavria Treyson of Maires with the handsome and clever? Prince Marc Stratus of Kuat. His Majesty the Supreme Ruler has asked that I conduct this ceremony with the consent of Her Majesty the Queen of Hapes, both of whom grace us with their presence on this special occasion. Official witnesses includes both Their Majesties, as well as Grand Admiral Jared Quinn, Her Royal Highness Princess Ashlee Stratus-Gourdine, Captain Travis Gourdine, Her Royal Highness Princess Nicole Stratus-McFayden and Commander Scott McFayden.



This ceremony of Royal Union shall be administered and governed by the marriage laws of the planet Maires, as agreed by the Royal Heads of the Two Houses, and the two participants and protectors of this union. 



Does Your Highness, Prince Marc[us] [Kenneth] Stratus of both Kuat and Hapes, take Her Grace the Duchess Tavria Treyson of Maires, to be your lawfully wedded wife, to have and to hold from this day forward, for better or for worse, for richer or for poorer, in sickness, and in health, to love and to cherish, now and forever?



I Do So Affirm.



Does Your Grace, Duchess Tavria Treyson of Maires, take His Royal Highness Prince Marc [Kenneth] Stratus of Kuat and Hapes, to be your lawfully wedded husband, to have and to hold, from this day forward, for better or for worse, for richer or for poorer, in sickness, and in health, to love and to cherish, now and forever?

I Do So Affirm.



Then by laws of Maires, and the power invested in me by Their Majesties the Queen of Hapes and the Supreme Ruler, I hereby pronounce you husband and wife, legally and lawfully wedded before these witnesses and the Force itself. As is proper and by the laws and traditions of the Royal Houses of Regasterra, I declare you, Tavria, Princess of Kuat, and legal heir and legal member of the Royal House

 Stratus.



(turning to the Prince) Your Royal Highness, you may kiss the Princess, your bride.
