Ceremony:

We are gathered together as friends and family, great Houses of the Regasterra, for this Royal Union of two of the Great Houses, and to unite the fair and beautiful Duchess Dana Lance of the Relephon Moons  with the handsome and powerful High Prince James Stratus of Kuat. His Majesty the Supreme Ruler has asked that I conduct this ceremony with the consent of Her Majesty the Queen of Hapes, and the Royal Supreme Court, all of whom grace us with their presence on this special occasion. Official witnesses includes both Her Majesty, as well as Executor Tavria Treyson, Lord Admiral Jared Quinn, Lord Admiral Firecam, Praetor-Admiral OVERMIND, Praetor-General Kaidlen Shan, Lady Selina Vadcasta, Lord & Lady Klone, Lady Diandra Devin, Her Royal Highness Princess Ashlee Stratus-Gourdine, Rear Admiral Travis Gourdine, Her Royal Highness Princess Nicole Stratus-McFayden and Commander Scott McFayden.



This ceremony of Royal Union shall be administered and governed by the marriage laws of the planet Kuat, as agreed by the Royal Supreme Court, the Royal Heads of the Two Houses, and the two participants and protectors of this union. 



Does Your Royal Highness, the Sovereign High Prince James [Taylor] Stratus, of both Kuat and Hapes, take Her Grace the Duchess Dana Lance of the Relephon Moons, to be your lawfully wedded wife, to have and to hold from this day forward, for better or for worse, for richer or for poorer, in sickness, and in health, to love and to cherish, now and forever?



I Do So Affirm.



Does Your Grace, Duchess Dana Lance of the Relephon Moons, take His Royal Highness, the Sovereign High Prince, James [Taylor] Stratus, of Kuat and Hapes, to be your lawfully wedded husband, to have and to hold, from this day forward, for better or for worse, for richer or for poorer, in sickness, and in health, to love and to cherish, now and forever?



I Do So Affirm.



Then by laws of Kuat, and the power vested in me by Their Majesties the Queen of Hapes and the Supreme Ruler of the Imperial Republic and their Royal Highnesses of the Royal Supreme Court, I hereby pronounce you husband and wife, legally and lawfully wedded before these witnesses and the Force itself. 



As is proper and by the laws and traditions of the Royal Houses of Regasterra, and in accordance with the traditions and laws of Kuat, Your Grace, Duchess of the Relephon Moons, Dana, I do crown you Queen of Kuat, and declare you to be a legal heir to the Throne of Kuat and legal member of the Royal House Stratus, and you, my Lord Sovereign High Prince James, I do crown King of Kuat.



(turning to the King) Your Majesty, you may kiss the Queen, your bride.

Supreme Chancellor: “My Lord, we your loyal subjects know that you have for a long time avoided having a certain title, even when you took full command and rule of the Galactic Empire you did not take this title. You have always told those whom have served alongside you that the reason you must remain “just a Prince” is because you are not married. Your Majesty is now married, and King of Kuat. It is therefore proper and fitting, that I, Prince Dene vye Cognatus, Grand Duke and Supreme Chancellor of the Imperial Republic, in accordance with the Charter laws and traditions, crown you Emperor of the Imperial Republic, and High King and Emperor of the Royal Territories of the Imperial Republic. As per the charter you shall always be the Supreme Ruler by position, but by title you shall henceforth be referred to as Emperor, Your Majesty. Do not worry, Your Majesty, we will continue to preserve the values, ideals, and structure as set forth by the Charter and Supreme Law, and honor our founding parents’ wishes concerning the government, including Your Majesty’s own as one of the key founding parents. The time is now appropriate due to your recent nuptials and with the blessing of the Supreme Court and Your Majesty to crown Her Majesty Queen Dana Stratus of Kuat, Empress of the Imperial Republic and appoint her a member of the Royal Imperial Throne by the Supreme Command and authority of the Royal Supreme Court. Though she will not by right have Charter authority, she will be an Agent of the Supreme Ruler, like the other Throne members, thus shall she be an auxiliary, or supporting member. Long Live Your Majesties, Emperor and Empress.
