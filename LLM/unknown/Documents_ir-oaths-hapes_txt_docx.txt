Imperial Republic Government Oath of Office

I _____, do solemnly swear that I will support and defend the Charter of the Imperial Republic against all enemies, foreign and domestic, that I will bear true faith and allegiance to the same, that I will protect and defend the Sovereignty of the Imperial Republic, the High Prince and Throne, and the Royal Family as the guardians of our liberty, that I take this obligation freely, without any mental reservation or purpose of evasion, and I will well and faithfully discharge the duties of the office on which I am about to enter. 



Oath of Office of the Queen Mother of Hapes

I, Phoenix Solo-Katarn, do solemnly swear that I will support and defend the Charter of the Imperial Republic (as it pertains to the Hapes Cluster) against all enemies, foreign and domestic, that I will bear true faith and allegience to the same, that I will protect and defend the Sovereignty of the House Stratus and the Supreme Ruler. I solemnly swear to protect and defend the Hapes Cluster and all its people, and serve as Queen Mother of Hapes and fulfill the obligations charged to me with this office, and those of the High Prince, and promise to uphold peace between Hapes and the Imperial Republic, and that I will serve my people with all my might, that I take this obligation freely, without any mental reservation or purpose of evasion, and I will well and faithfully discharge the duties of the office on which I am about to enter. 



Pronouncement of the Queen Mother by the Supreme Ruler

Princess Phoenix Solo-Katarn, having sworn and oath to me, the Royal Family, and the people of Hapes, I, High Prince James Stratus, by the authority which I hold of the Sovereign Authority of the Royal Family and Throne of the Imperial Republic, I hereby pronounce you Queen Mother of Hapes, and invest in you all the authority entitled you by virtue of the office of which you now possess, and I do so in front of these many great and noble witnesses representing the Royal House Stratus, the Government and Ministry of Defense of the Imperial Republic, the Nobility and Government of Hapes, and the many witnesses of this great Hapes Consortium who are the very people of Hapes on this, the first day in the 26th Year since the formation of the Imperial Republic.
