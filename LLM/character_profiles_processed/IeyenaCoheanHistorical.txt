Posted 19 June 2006 - 06:52 PM 
 
Ieyena stood in the middle of a landing platform in the 
pouring rain. She watched as the ship-her mother had been directed to deliver here- landed a 
kilometer or two away. She watched as her mom began to descend the ramp. Her mother was a 
tall and gracefull woman who was strong in the ways of the force.
 
Suddenly, out of nowhere, an explosion errupted from the top of the ramp. Pieces of her 
mother's ship flew in every direction. A wave of compressed air knocked her to the ground as 
she saw her mother's body fly into the air and land fifteen meters away.
 
Screaming, she ran towards the debris and her mother, pounding wind and rain forcing her 
back. As she reached her mother's side, she realized that it was too late: her mother was 
already dead. Tears stung her eyes as she held her mother's limp body in her arms. Her 
companion rushed after her.
 
"Vivy, you've got to get away from here; it's not safe. I'll take you up in the another ship, but if 
the Jedi hunters could track your mom here, they can track you. We've got to get back to Greg." 
He helped her up slowly, then quickly led her away from the platform.
 
{Flashback}
 
{Four Years Earlier}
 
"It's my life, and I'm not going to go back to that stupid academy! Especially after what they did 
to Daniella!" Ieyena screamed in her mother's face.
 
"You're only eighteen, and as long as you're my daughter, you won't betray your family like this! 
Besides, it's not like the acedemy had a choice. They-" Ieyena's mother yelled back.
 
"No choice?! They had every opportunity to protect her from that jerk father of hers! What right 
did they have to return her to a man who didn't care whether she lived or died, just because he's 
her father?! And as for being your daughter, maybe I don't want to be anymore!"
 
"You don't mean that!" Ieyena's mother's face looked stricken with fear, as if she knew her 
daughter could-and would-leave her.
 
"I mean every word of it! I'm joining CorSec whether you like it or not! And you can't stop me!" 
Ieyena turned, grabbing the luggage she had packed, and ran out the door. As she turned the 
corner, she knew her life would never be the same again. She wanted to go back to her family, 
but after what she had just said, she knew she couldn't. CorSec would have to be her family 
now.
 
{End Flashback} 
Tears still filled her eyes as she sat in the shuttle, she and her father had built. She thought of 
the events that had led up to this terrible fate.
 
{Flashback}
 
{Four Hours Before}
 
Ieyena sat behind a large desk in her father's office, feeling akward and out of place in the 
place where she had spent most of her childhood at home, and stared at the hollow images of a 
little girl she wasn't sure she knew anymore. She ran her fingers through her short blonde hair, 
she was still not used to the change. Her hair had always been down to her waist and in black 
curls. Her eye color had been changed too, in hopes that no one would be able to recognize 
her. Hearing footsteps, she looked up as a tired-looking old man walked through the door and 
immedietly knew it was her father.
 
"'Yena? My baby girl? Is that you? What have you done to yoursef?" he asked, running to her 
and scooping her up in his arms; just like he had when she was just a little girl. Tears filled his 
eyes as he searched for words to say to her, "How-Why?"
 
"I can't stay, Daddy; I just came to say I was wrong. You and Mamma were right all along. 
CorSec was the wrong place for me, they tried to make me betray the Jedi-I just couldn't." 
Ieyena said as she began to loose her composure.
 
"Shh. It's okay, honey, as long as I know you're safe everything's going to be fine."
 
"Daddy, I have to go. They can't find me here. They can't know that you and Mamma know, I've 
got a friend waiting outside. He's going to take me back to Greg; don't worry, Greg will take care 
of me."
 
"That Jedi friend of yours? If he's with you, then I know you'll be safe. I still have that ship we 
built together, if you want it...it's yours. You and he can take it, no one will be able to trace it."
 
Ieyena smiled, looking up at the man she had always admired. The man who had no Force 
ability at all, but was still willing to protect his wife and daughter who did. "Thank you, Dad," she 
said, regaining her composure, "I knew I could count on you. I love you, Dad," she paused, "But 
where's Mom? I want to say goodbye to her."
 
"Oh, she's flying one of the ships over to Tolsen's Shipyard for me, it was a delivery that was 
late. You know...same old, same old." he reached over to his desk and grabbed a datapad 
with the information on it, he handed it to her, "Here, this is all the information you need to find 
her. Now stay safe," he said and gave her a kiss on the cheek and watched her leave his life, 
possibly forever.
 
{End Flashback}
 
Ieyena leaned back in the co-pilot's seat of her new ship. A single tear trickled down her 
cheek, "Mom, I hope you forgave me." she whispered.
 
Ieyena took one last quick look around her new ship before she turned back to the ramp that 
extended from her ship to the docking bay floor. Her pace quickened as she desended the ramp and noticed a tall figure awaiting her. His own pace quickened as he hurried to lessen the gap 
between them. The two met at the end of the ramp, his arms quickly encircled her in a hug.
 
"When we heard about your mother, we feared the worst," Greg said, gazing down at her, 
concern evident in his dark brown eyes.
 
"I'm okay, but I wouldn't have been if it wasn't for Alec." she said with a faint smile.
 
Greg turned and looked over at the man he had sent to escort Ieyena, "Thanks for taking care 
of Vivy,"
 
"Anything for you, General," Alec replied.
 
The group turned and headed to the tunnel at the end of the docking bay with Greg's arm still 
circling Ieyena's shoulders.
 
"So, where do we go from here?" Greg asked with a mischievous smile on his face.
 
Ieyena looked at him and grinned back, without saying a word; they both understood what 
needed to be done.
 
"Now if you'll both excuse me, there are other things I need to attend to," Alec said, turning to 
the two of them as they reached Greg's office.
 
"Thank you, you're dismissed," Greg replied.
 
Alec turned and walked away, Greg and Ieyena entered the office. Ieyena sat down in a chair 
near the corner of Greg's desk and waited for the doors to seal behind them before speaking.
 
"Are you sure they fell for Ieyena's 'execution'?" she asked, looking at Greg, concern finally 
showing in her mannerisms.
 
"Yes, I'm pretty sure; you're going to have to stay at Base Ops. for awhile, and you won't be 
able to return to your old Group. But the good thing is, I won't have to spend as much time 
worrying about you anymore."
 
Ieyena rolled her eyes and stuck her tongue out at him, "Thank's 'daddy'," she said 
sarcastically, "I don't need you babysitting me too. I'm a big girl, I can take care of myself; plus, 
you can't afford to just have me sit around, there's got to be some opperation you need another 
person for."
 
"Well, yeah, we have a new client. I can't give you much information now, but it seems rather 
promising."
 
"Okay, so what are you waiting for?" Ieyena asked.
 
"Well that's just it. He's still technically respectable and wants to be very careful on how he 
establishes his illegal ties."
 
"So what can I do to help? There's got to be something we can do to convince him that we're 
the group that can handle his 'needs'," 
"I'm sure there is, Ieyena, but right now we're just waiting for him to make the next move."
 
"Great, so I'm still stuck here,"
 
"For the time being," Greg said with a smile, betraying the fact that he wasn't upset about it at 
all.
 
Ieyena stood and walked over to him, a smile on her face, "I'm sure that this was no accident." 
she said.
 
He smiled as he slipped his arms around her waist, "I just don't want to loose you again."
 
She leaned her head against his shoulder, and let her mind drift back to when she had lost him 
before.
 
{Flashback}
 
{Several Years Ago}
 
The darkness filled the room around her as two beams danced, cutting throught it. It was a 
smooth pattern, the practices that had been so deeply ingrained in her consciousness, she 
could preform them in her sleep. She allowed herself to drift into the calm stillness of the Force 
that surrounded her. Her mind was clear and open while her eyes were closed to the images 
around her.
 
Suddenly, another beam added itself to the practice, turning it into a duel of abilities. She 
opened her mind and reached out to sense who her opponent was, her concentration 
immediately broke, realizing that it was Daniella.
 
Her eyes sprang open and a smile crossed her face as she deactivated her lightsabers. Daniella 
did the same, "So, who are you thinking about?" Daniella asked with obvious teasing in her 
voice.
 
"No one, I was just practicing," Ieyena said.
 
"Yeah, sure you were." Daniella replied sarcastically, "I bet you were thinking about Greg."
 
"Oh really, and why do you say that?"
 
"I've seen the way you look at him."
 
"How? Like I look at every other person?"
 
"Yeah, if you stareat every other person every chance you get,"
 
Ieyena turned and proceeded out the door with Daniella following behind, teasing her 
mercilessly.
 
"Come on, I'm your best friend. You have to tell me!"
 "Yeah, just like you told me about you and Cory." Ieyena retorted sarcastically, trying to hide the 
fact that it still annoyed her.
 
They were walking down the path that headed to the garden, near the compound where their 
sleeping quarters were located. Ieyena finally broke down, she turned to Daniella as they 
walked, "Fine. It was Greg..."
 
"And...?"
 
"And what? I answered your question."
 
"In the briefest way."
 
"So, you never said I had to tell you everything." as she said this, Ieyena looked forward 
to see Greg and several other people heading toward them. As they drew closer, Ieyena 
recognized Daniella's father standing just behind Greg. The air left her lungs, she knew what 
was happening.
 
"Daniella, run." she hissed, but Daniella could only stand there in shock.
 
"Ieyena, don't!" Greg cried as Ieyena pulled one of her lightsabers from her belt.
 
"I'm not going to let him take her. You know what kind of a man he is. He doesn't deserve to 
have a daughter like her!"
 
Daniella's father grinned, they had stopped a few feet in front of them. The group contained four 
Namibian guards, Daniella's father and Greg.
 
"Ieyena, the council has agreed. She needs to go home." Greg continued, helplessly.
 
"I'm not going to let him take her. It's not a 'home' she'd be going to, it'd be a prison. I'm not 
going to let it happen." she said spitefully. Daniella turned, trying to run as two Namibian guards 
reached for her wrists. Ieyena attempted to push them away, but was tackled by the other 
two guards. She struggled, screamign as she watched her best friend be torn away from her. 
Daniella's screamed echoed in her ears.
 
"Stop it! Let me go!"
 
"Greg, stop them! Help me!" Ieyena shouted, "Let me go!" she attempted to rip away from the 
guards. Greg seemed immobile, as though some invisible force kept him in place. She tried to 
use the Force to escape, but she was unable to. It was as though someone had placed a wet 
cloth over her and she couldn't use her powers.
 
Daniella's father grinned evilly as his daughter was taken away. The guards holding Ieyena 
released her only when Daniella and her father were departing in the landspeeder that had been 
parked thirty meters away. She broke away and raced after the speeder. She ran for several 
kilometers until she finally collapsed on the ground, sobbing uncontrollably, the speeder now out 
of her sight.
 
She felt a hand rest on her shoulder and immediately knew it was Greg, "There was nothing I 
could do." he said softly. 
She stood, "You're such a liar," she spat, punching him in the face with all the strength she 
could muster, knocking him to the ground.
 
Cory ran up to them, panic stricken, "What happened? Where's Daniella?"
 
"Why don't you ask your friend over there." Ieyena snarled, turning and stomping away.
 
{end of flashback}
 
Ieyena's thoughts returned to the pressent and she looked up at Greg,"I'm so sorry that I broke 
your noes."She apologized.
 
"Just don't ever do it again."He said smiling. Then lenning his in he softly kissed her on the lips. 
Ieyena leaned back with a smile on her face.
 
"I think we should be getting back to work," she wispered softly.
 
"In just a moment."he replied. He lenned is and kissed her with more passion this time.
 
{few weeks later}
 
They were gathered in a small room on the ship in the middle of nowhere. Ieyena paced slightly 
back and forth, anxious to meet their new 'business partner'. She knew this was going to be one 
of the most important transitions for Greg's operation. If they could nail this account, then they 
would be guaranteed more protection than they had ever hope for. And a legitimacy they had 
never had.
 
"Sit down, you're making me nervous." Greg said.
 
"I'm sorry, its just I know how important this account is. And I really want it to go well, but there's 
so many things that could go wrong." she said, sitting down at the table next to him.
 
"Don't worry. I have a feeling this is all going to work out." he replied with a smile.
 
The door swished open and several figures walked in. She recognized one of them, it was Alec, 
Greg's most trusted partner. Behind him was a tall, commanding figure who seemed to see 
more than was possible. And next to him, was another man she could not place.
 
Greg stood up and she followed his example, and walked over to the group. "Greg, Vivy," Alec 
said, "I would like you to meet James Stratus, founder of Stratus Industries, and his second in 
command, Eric Jackson."
 
Ieyena realized why they had looked familiar to her. These men were powerful and well known 
throughout the galaxy, which was unusual in these times, considering the unrest and lack of 
organization that existed. She was surprised that such influencial individuals would be seeking 
out assistance of their kind.
 
"It's a pleasure to finally meet you," Stratus said as he extended his hand and shook Greg's and 
then Ieyena's.
 "Please be seated," Greg said, "We have much to discuss."
 
They sat down and the conference began.
 
{two weeks later}
 
Ieyena sifted through the piles of datapads that were piled on her small desk. She absolutely

She didn't even look up as the door to her tiny office hissed open. "What do you want now, 
Greg?"
 
"Nothing! Can't a guy just see his favorite person in the world and not want something?" Greg 
asked.
 
"If that were true, you wouldn't be wanting me to go with Stratus and another one of his frumfy

"How did you know that's what I wanted?"
 
She looked up, "Word spreads fast. You asked Alec first. He tells me a lot, you know," Ieyena
