FATE-005 - Force Levels
The Force Level system defines the ranks and progression of Force-sensitive individuals within the universe of the Imperial Republic. This structure provides clarity and consistency for roleplay, training, advancement, and classification of characters.
🔮 Force Level Hierarchy
🧠 Notes
None: Cannot become Force-sensitive; biologically or spiritually inert to the Force.
Potential: Often identified through testing or visions; eligibility for initiation.
Novice: Beginning of formal training.
Padawan: Paired with a mentor; often sent on supervised missions.
Knight: Represents mastery of foundational Force techniques and combat readiness.
Senior Knight: Optional honorific distinction for long-serving or decorated Knights.
Master: May train others; holds leadership or advisory roles.
Senior Master: Reserved for the most senior figures of the Order. This level is optional.
Grand Master: Symbolic head of the Order. Typically, only one exists at a time.
This system is faction-neutral but can be reskinned per tradition (e.g., Jedi/Sith variations).
