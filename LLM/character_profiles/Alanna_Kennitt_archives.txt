**Name** : Alanna Kennitt  
**Age** : 47  
**Species** : Human  
**Home** : Gala  
  
**Appearance** : 5'3", tan, and lithe. Alanna has stringy auburn hair cropped above the shoulders, and green-tinged hazel eyes. She wears a prosthetic leg from the lower left thigh down, although the disability does not prevent her from everyday life. She is deeply scarred from years of battle experience.  
  
**Personality** : Sarcastic, cynical, impertinent. A recluse, Alanna hates social conventions and frivolity. She prefers to be working exercises on the Outer Rim. Years of military training has taught her to curb her tongue, but Alanna will never be considered "tolerant". Her opinions are known by everyone who works with her, and subordinate officers take care not to offend them. Alanna has little patience for error, and leans toward particularity in the workplace.  
  
**Goals and Ambitions** : To Be Determined

**Education** : Graduate of the Eric T. Jackson College of Military Science, and James T. Stratus School of Military Leadership.
