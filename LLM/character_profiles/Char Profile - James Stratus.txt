Biography:
The oldest of four, Prince James T. Stratus II was born to King James I and Queen Adaline Hoffner Stratus, then monarchs of Kuat. His siblings were also born several years apart each, Nicole, Marc, and Ashlee. They were raised in the Kuati Royal Palace where they and all lived together. When Jim was only 14 years old (Nicole 12, Marc 8, Ashlee 6), a civil uprising began on Kuat in which his father was killed. He managed to escape with his mother, and brother and sisters.
They traveled to Hapes where they were welcomed by the Queen of Hapes, who was Queen Adaline Stratus’ sister (their aunt). She invited them to live in the Royal Palace with her. When they were teenagers, Jim ran into one Layha Solo on Hapes and they became very good friends. They shared most of the same beliefs and interests, and they often talked of politics and the wars of the past, etc. As they grew older, the Queen Stratus, James' mother, got ill and died. The Queen of Hapes, having been completely pro-female, her sister now dead, adopted the sisters and reluctantly took in James as well as a member of the Hapan Royal Family.
When James became older, she gave him a large, very, very large sum of money, and asked him to leave Hapes. She did not want him to be around "as an heir", or known to have done this. She told him she would give him anything he wanted, so he asked for some jewels, more money, and some good-armed heavily equipped ships with all kinds of technology. His aunt, the Queen of Hapes, just wanting to get rid of him gave him everything he asked and sent him on his way. Jim Stratus moved to Corellia and established Stratus Industries (SI). He used the money he had to invest and buy out many production and research organizations and small corporations, personally reorganized them all with new SOP's and agendas, and they quickly started producing a vast amount of products and generated large amounts of income.
He then established Stratus Intelligence, (also SI), which led his organization to be more of a fringe type, but well respected and feared. He finally managed to get enough credits to purchase the Centerpoint Space Station from the organizations and governments controlling it. It was in this process that he met Josefus Quick the elected leader of that system, which had declared neutrality from the crumbling and corrupt New Republic. With his large amount of previously acquired assets from the Queen of Hapes who was also giving him a constant income to keep him silent and away from Hapes. Ieyena Cohean, who at this time was working in the fringe, signed a contract with him and did some smuggling and fringe-type work for Stratus Intelligence, (which helped keep CorSec off her back seeing as they had an arrest warrant for her.)
CorSec came to Stratus for advice on revising their intelligence agency, and organization structure. He helped them reorganize it, and they paid him heavily for it. Remnants of the Empire, came and recruited him into the military. As a result of his leadership skills and capabilities, and extensive military and officer training, they gave him a military command of an entire task force. A coup began, individuals were assassinated, and the Imperial government faltered and began to fall apart. Jim, then Admiral Stratus, with his military authority and economic influence seized control of the last remnants of the Empire. His first major act was to move Centerpoint Station to a hidden system and with orders for special projects to be done, leaving two of his most trusted organization officers in charge. He recognized the falling and corruption of the government of the New Republic and the galaxy, and witnessed its economic collapse, and chaos begin. He traveled to the Corellian System, where he proposed a New Order, a new political party and a new government, which would result in the merging of the five newly formed governmental factions into one.
With the help of Josefus Quick, Jim Stratus is able to get the support of the Corellian system, as well as all Imperial systems under his control. Together they travel throughout the galaxy getting more systems to their cause. Stratus assigned Josefus Quick a Star Destroyer and sent him on many diplomatic missions. Quick is able to gather more systems to their cause. While rallying other systems’ support, Jim was reunited again with Ieyena Cohean and Layha Solo, (friends). Jim is introduced to one of their friends, Daniella Organa. The four of them embarked on a mission together to Hapes in an attempt to unite Hapes under the New Order. With all of the women on his envoy, and his and Quick's own political pull and diplomatic skills they are able to convince the new Queen of Hapes, to form a New Republic. With the balance of power of the Hapes Consortium supporting the motion and military support, they are able to then form a New Order, seize the remaining Republic systems that have not withdrawn or joined up, and the Capital Coruscant System, where they rebuild new government buildings (having specially moved the others to a hidden planet for study), including a new Royal Palace. Stratus is elected the leader of the New Order, and with the people's support, declares himself the Supreme Ruler and the Imperial Republic is established.

Position Descriptions and Responsibilities:
Below are the details and descriptions of what such personnel would do.
Fleet Executive Officer (XO): The Fleet Executive Officer is normally the second in command of a fleet and performs executive tasks and assists the Commanding Officer where needed. However, since the Commanding Officer is the Supreme Ruler, the Executive Officer of this fleet has a lot more duties and is solely responsible for the duties the CO would normally perform over the fleet.
Fleet Command Coordinations Officer (CCO): This person has the duties of the CCO plus assists the XO and takes on the duties a normal XO would perform.
Fleet Wing Commander: This person is in command/responsible for charge of all fighter squadrans of the fleet.
Royal Guard Commander: This person has a lot of duties. This person follows the Supreme Ruler everywhere, has the knowledge and skills of all other positions on the Prince's personal staff and can perform in all functions. Can act on behalf of the Supreme Ruler when situation merits. Responsible for coordination of protection of the Supreme Ruler's guard and security forces.
Health Supervisor: This person's job is to monitor the Supreme Ruler's vitals, perform frequent checkups, and make sure that he is eating well, in shape, taking the right medications if any, and any other medical needs a personal physician would perform.
Policy Advisors & Political Officers: These personnel advise the Supreme Ruler on policies in existance or ideas for new ones or help him write new ones or manage existing ones. They are from each major political commission and act as messengers, councilors, advisors, and representatives between the bodies. They work hand-in-hand with those divisions leaders, the Supreme Ruler, and the political officers on the Supreme Ruler's staff.
Office Secretaries: The Office Secretaries’ responsibilities are to maintain schedules, coordinate events, assist with planning, passing on messages, etc. to and from the Supreme Ruler's office from the Palace and wherever the Supreme Ruler may be (travelling aboard the Prince's Pride, etc.
Military Aide(s): These persons' responsibilities are to keep the Supreme Ruler updated on current military issues and events and advise him on decisions regarding such, as well as monitor the military leadership and act as messenger and liaison as required.
Intelligence Officer: These persons' jobs are to make sure the Supreme Ruler is provided with the most current information as it comes in and as necessary and are direct liaisons from Imperial Republic Intelligence.
Communications Director: This person is solely responsible as a spokesperson for the Supreme Ruler in communicating with the press, writing or assisting writing speeches, documents, and acting as liaison to the Ministry of Public Affairs.
