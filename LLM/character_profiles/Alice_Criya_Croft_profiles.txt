**Full Name** : Alice Criya Croft

**Age** : 36

**Species** : Human

**Home** : Balmorra

**Appearance** : Alice has brown eyes and blonde, wavy hair falling just below her shoulders. At 5'7", she is on the slender side of average, and possessed of a willowy build. There is a deep scar running along the curve of her shoulder blade. It is never seen in public.

**Personality** : Thorough to a point where she is almost cold, Alice is a decisive thinker. She is motivated by a need for galactic justice, and sees her position as a means toward achieving this. Outspoken, professional, and efficient, she has little patience for error. Alice is not naturally attuned to the feelings of others, and can appear brash upon first impressions. For a politician, she is remarkably closed-minded and judgmental. Her good opinion, once lost, is likely lost forever. However, she does her best to keep these traits to herself.

Her dry humor and quick logic makes her a gifted public speaker. Alice is a passionate believer in equality and the good of all. She exists in a constant state of progress, engaging in multiple projects to keep herself busy. Alice is uncomfortable with "free time", and prefers to fill idle space with work or reading. Chaos and anarchy unsettle her, on the best of days. Many refer to her as an "over-achiever". Her desire for justice makes her moral compass immobile, and her hatred for criminal activity will never be compromised.

**Goals and Ambitions** : Justice. Alice has seen the darker side of life, and chose to take up arms against it. She is an advocate of the death penalty, and skeptical that a criminal might change. For her, life exists as a chance to purge the galaxy of evil. Often cynical, Alice believes that if the world will not help itself, _she_ will have to help it.

**Career** : Appointed Senator of Balmorra, Imperial Republic Senate.

**Education** : Graduate of:

Joesefus A. Quick School of Law

School of Communication

School of Languages & Cultures

**Property** : To Be Determined.

**Family** :

Tyron Croft (Father, Deceased)

Avis Croft (Mother, Deceased)

Emory Croft (Brother, Deceased)

Verity Croft (Aunt)

**Background** : Born to a supportive family, Alice was raised to believe in the innate goodness of the galaxy. Her parents stressed the importance of responsibility, and the value of law. In her early teenage years, however, a pirate attack cut short her family vacation. Avis and Emory were killed in the struggle, leaving Tyron crippled and Alice a shadow of the carefree child she had once been. Despite her attempts to come to terms with the incident, her faith shattered one year later when her father died of his injuries. Following the death of her brother-in-law, Verity opened her home to Alice, and the two came to share a deep bond. Her aunt supported her through school, and continues to support her career in the Senate.
