This evening we gather to share our sincere gratitude, well-wishes, and farewells with Supreme Chancellor Joe Quick, as he prepares to embark on his next journey in life. The Supreme Chancellor and I have had the experience and pleasure of working together for many years, in many different capacities, from my time as a news reporter covering the Imperial Republic Senate; to my time at the Ministry of Public Affairs working to bring the message of Joe and other leaders to the people; to my time collaborating with Joe as part of the Imperial Republic Throne and the Imperial Republic High Council.
He has provided exceptional leadership to the Ministry of State and Senate; was instrumental in the founding and development of the Imperial Republic over the last three decades; and was a key architect of the original charter. The Imperial Republic Charter is the greatest constitutional document in history, elevating the freedom of individuals over the tyranny of the galactic despot. The Imperial Republic came about in a time of blight and despair in a fading galaxy, but Joe refused to accept that the decay of civilization was inevitable. He believed the galaxy and its people could do better. During his tenure as Supreme Chancellor, he has artfully elevated the profile and stature of the Imperial Republic, causing many of our allies to refer to our way of government as decadent Imperial Republic liberalism. Joe has elevated the Imperial Republic and the galaxy to a state of near perpetual peace, which has driven a legacy of prosperity and has led to a rise in the level of wealth and happiness in the galaxy, which were unfathomable to previous generations that lived through the Dark Ages of the Galactic Empire. While Joe has no shortage of critics in the media, he is well respected by his peers across the galaxy, and recognized in the senate as an apt statesman and a non-partisan consensus builder. He was secure in his faith that his vision and commitment for the Imperial Republic was just and right.
He has been a valued partner to COMPNOR and has been a fervent supporter of Imperial Republic Culture and the development of talent across the Imperial Republic. He has masterfully coached our Ambassadors, Moffs, and Ministers and helped to build the bench strength of our team. Joe was also a big proponent and supporter of the continued diversity of the Imperial Republic. He recognized that while not every member planet of the Imperial Republic was equal in terms of size or stature; to the Imperial Republic they were all equally important. I know that we will all miss the cool-headed and diplomatic frame of mind and presence of the Supreme Chancellor in any future crisis. We wish you the best of luck in your retirement, and it has been a pleasure and honor working with you.
We are not a one generation government, we do not intended to let the Imperial Republic be a one generation society. Whether we like it or not things are changing: the changing of technologies; the changing of cartography as more and more systems and planets join the Imperial Republic; and the changing of our Supreme Chancellor. We must look as far into the future as we can, to ensure all the best talents are ready to lead our people and our government into that future. We can’t opt-out of the technology race and stand to the side watching as a bystander. We can’t opt-out of our battle for freedom against a Sith enemy intent on destroying the very moral fabric and Imperial Republic way of life. Our dedicated and steadfast Champion of Freedom and Liberty is stepping down from the front lines; we must all join together to take up the mantle that Joe is leaving behind. Together as an alliance of people not planets, with the same values, the same passion and desire for freedom and liberty, and the same hope and faith that our legacy to the galaxy can and will be a better and safer place for our future generations to live in safety and peace.
As the torch passes from one era to the next, we stand at a crossroads contemplating what the future will bring. Like every citizen, I wish our new Supreme Chancellor and his staff the best of luck on their new endeavor. I hope that their coming years of service are blessed with the success of galactic peace and prosperity. Our citizens expect their Supreme Chancellor and the Senate to find consensus and solutions to the great issues of our time. Solutions which make this government and its citizens better positioned for the present and the future. Many issues await their great discourse before the Senate in our future, and our new Supreme Chancellor will need to navigate the consensus of the citizens, to arrive at the solutions that best serve both the citizens and the interest of the Imperial Republic. As many of you will recall, last year we celebrated the revision of our charter, where we strengthened the voice and safeguarded the rights of our citizens. Our new Supreme Chancellor will be tasked with protecting this renewed Imperial Republic dream that we all want.  As trying times and obstacles await us in the future, the legacy of Joe Quick remains a steadfast guide for our future.
