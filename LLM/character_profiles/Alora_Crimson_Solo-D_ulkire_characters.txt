**Flaws:**

Rebellious, emotional, sometimes doesn’t think things through. Can be selfish and stubborn. Is naïve and gullible as well.

**Physical Characteristics:**

Alora has her fathers golden hair and her mothers blue eyes. She is slender for her age and has already begun blossoming into a young woman. Her hair is long and slightly wavy, and she is tall for her age. Hapan Royal Armband is on her Left Upper-Arm. Her hair turns strawberry blonde in summer time due to her mothers natural red locks.

**Biography:**

Princess Alora was born the first of three siblings. Being the eldest meant many things. First, she was spoiled beyond belief, for she had no other sibling until she was of five years old. And second, she was very bossy, and knew her place rather well. She was a ladies princess, very much like her mother, she enjoyed the spoils of royalty, the boys, the “friends”, the noble life of a royal. She despised dirt, playing around, or anything other than wearing flashy gowns and looking pretty.

When she started her training that every Hapan Princess went through, she was not very interested. However, she had no choice, and was constantly scolded by her master on how to better her skills. She was decent, she knew that much. But she never really enveloped her true ability in the art. 

Her two younger brothers were the victims of many temper tantrums, and many commands to get things done the way she wanted. In all actuality, she truly had a good heart, but was misguided by her mind. She loved her little brothers, but loved herself much more. By the time she was around thirteen, she had developed a very good skill at manipulating people. She was also very like her mother was when she was her age—nosey. She had to know the goods on everyone, and was the first to know if anything went down between their circle of friends. This gave her many friends, and many enemies. Many classmates endured the results of her gossip with much struggle to get back to their rank in the popularity pole. 

She became even more tempered as hormones took part in this stage of life. She missed many classes in her athletic studies, and was usually in turn, reading a book somewhere near the palace courtyard. She loves nature, and finds herself daydreaming most of the time. She'd rather read a good book, than learn how to do a back flip and kick someone in the torso in the process. She felt that was for her bodyguards to know, and not her. But her parents, mainly her father, began to have more say in this matter. Her father came from a Mystril background. His mother was part of the royal court of Emberlene. And his aunt was a mystril shadow guard. Not only was it in his wife’s culture for women to be leaders and physically adept to kick anyones butt if needed, but it was also ingrained in his. He would not have his daughter disgrace such heritage.

Thus she was assigned a personal escort to make sure she was where she needed to be. This royally aggrivated Alora to no ends. She was coming up on her sixteenth birthday party, and scheming was the majority of her time spent. When the night came, she escaped the summer cottage her family lived in during the season, using what skills she had to evade getting caught. It was around this time she realized she could somehow hear some peoples thoughts if she focused hard enough. She knew the force ran in her family, but was never inquisitive enough to figure it out. And it wasn’t Hapan ritual to test every royal blood born in the palace for midi-chlorians.
