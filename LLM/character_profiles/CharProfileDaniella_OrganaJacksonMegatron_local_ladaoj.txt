Faults/Flaws:

Daniella hates the life of a Government officer, and resents herself for taking on the position again after her rebirth. Eric and Auri and Laston are HUGE weaknesses for her. Daniella is struggling to find herself with being a Jedi and the disease inside of her, and it sometimes causes severe depression and feelings that she isn't needed in the galaxy, and this effects her work.



She also is very headstrong and has a tendency to hate people easily for teir attitues, and once you lose her good graces, Daniella finds it extremely difficult to forgive.

Six Months: Daniella's parents begin nasty divorce, leading to even nastier custody battle over young Daniella. Father wins by bribing judge, and Daniella's mother is shamed. Father becomes Senator. Daniella's force abilities are discovered. Daniella is sent to the planet (Insert name here ... Quetalre was the spelling I last knew) for training.



Early Childhood: Daniella advances steadily through the training programs offered, dispite her father taking her for occational weekend visits and beating the snot out of her. She befriends Ieyena, Cory, (Ieyena's Boyfriend), etc. while in training.



Age 11: Daniella becomes apprenticed to (Insert Name Here).



Age 16: Daniella is removed from the school by her father, taken to Naboo to help his political persona. He beats her almost to death when the Queen Mother of Hapes sends Prince Mikael to retrieve her and bring her safely to Hapes.



A few months later, Daniella runs away with Cory Heiss. She lives as a bounty hunter.



Age 19/20: Daniella comes home to find Cory seemingly murdered by a force hunter, and she flees for her life with the help of Ieyena and her father. She is gravely injured from the force hunter's attack. She recovers on Nar Shadda and is returned to Hapes.



Age 22: Daniella to this point has been living on Hapes, per her grandmother's wishes. She has also been looking to locate her mother. She eventually finds her, and Chelsea, working for the Queen of Naboo. See Chelsea Organa's History (when it's put up).



Age 23: Daniella leaves the confines of Hapes to explore the world of Coruscant. She lives on her own, sustained by her remaining earnings from the bounty huntress days.



Age 25: Daniella is recruited by her adopted Uncle, James Stratus, to assist in creating the Special Operations Force. When the program is completed and initiated, James Stratus gives her the rank of (Insert Rank Here) and the title of Chief of Special Operations, or CoSpecOps (Because it's oh so cool!!!).



Age 26: To keep Daniella out of suspicion for her involvement in the Government, she is given the title of Deputy Minister of Defense.



Age 28: Daniella finds her brother Michael, presumed dead from the war won by the rebels (James Stratus, and Co) is very much alive. His daughter is abandoned on Naboo with Queen Chelsea when Daniella bannishes him. Daniella decides to adopt Cielo, who becomes the modern day Aurielle, or Auri. Shortly after adoption, Daniella and Eric Jackson marry on Hapes after a long engagement. They seperate in the same year. Although Eric believed the divorce final, Daniella never signed the paperwork and their marriage is still legal.



Age 29: Daniella gives birth to Eric's second son. She is told he died at birth, when in reality he was taken in by Jade and Mikael. 



Age 30: Daniella travels to Arrakis with Layha. She is infected with a viris that boosts her force abilities, but it weakens her severely.



Age 31: The weakened Daniella returns to Coruscant. She and Admiral Jackson reconcile their differences and resume the married life. 



Age 33: Aurielle Organa-Jackson was kidnapped from school, sending Eric and Jacob on a rescue mission that costed them their lives. Daniella's health reaches an alarming low. The Queen Mother sends Daniella to Gallinor in the hopes that she will recover.



Age 36: Daniella returns to Coruscant. She begins to delve into the darker sides of the force, and temporarily resigns from her duties to give her time to study. This is done in secret. Not even Stratus was informed of her plans.



Age 38: Daniella, using the deep, dark side of the force she studied for two years, uses her remaining energy to bring to life her beloved husband, Eric Jackson. He finds her moments from death, and she was able to tell him about their son and that he would recieve her powers.
