**History**  
In the two years prior to the creation of the Imperial Republic, Alexander worked for the Governor-General of Corellia, Joesefus Quick, as an intern for his office. After the formation of the IR, Alexander attended the University of Corellia, earning his Bachelor in Public Administration. Upon graduating, he immediately started working towards his Master's Degree in law, deciding to follow in the footstep of his father, who was a planetary judge for Corellia. After 5 years of studying and hard work, Alex graduated from the University of Corellia's School of Law. Alexander first started working as an attorney for the Corellian government. After 10 years of service, he was granted a position as a planetary judge for Selonia. In 27 IRY, Alexander stepped down from his position and decided attend the Stratus University's Joesefus A. Quick School of Law on Corellia to obtain his Doctorate in Law. Upon graduating, he decided to join the Imperial Republic's Regional Governance. Initially, he was assigned to work in the Corellia Oversector as Vice Mayor of Drall. As he rose through the ranks, he was reassigned to the Coruscant Oversector upon his promotion to System Governor and was appointed the new Governor of the Coruscant system. He took a leave to return to college to earn his Doctorate in Public Administration from the Treyson College of Public Administration on Coruscant. He made his return to Corellia in 32 IRY when was was named Grand Moff of the Corellia Oversector.
