**Background/History:**

Alun grew up on Kuat, born into a well-off family that owned and operated a shipyard in orbit. Donaa, Alun's mother and heiress to the shipyard fortune, worked for her father in running the company. Just before Alun's birth, his father was executed by members of House Badine at the conclusion of the Kuati civil war for being a loyal naval officer on the side of House Stratus. William had lead a diversionary attack that helped ensure that the Stratus Royal family escaped.

Alun and his older sister, Lynn, were very close growing up, and were being groomed to be the next generation of leaders for the family business. They were taught from an early age to distrust the ruling House Badine, and that the House was responsible for their father's murder.
