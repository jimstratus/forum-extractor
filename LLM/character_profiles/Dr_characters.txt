**History**

Justice Elouna Joffuro, MD, PhD, JD was appointed by Executor Treyson to the High Inquisition two years ago. Prior to that she has served a dedicated life in public service to government agencies across the galaxy, including the Ministry of Justice, the Imperial Republic Security Bureau, the Ministry of Health, and the prestigious Valorumian Institute. Dr. Joffuro brings to the bench years of experience in solving murders through the use of Forensic Science and brings that extensive knowledge and understanding to the High Inquisition to ensure that only sound science is introduced, to ensure a fair and balanced trial takes place. As being one of only 81 board certified members of the Galactic Academy of Forensic Sentietology, Dr Joffuro has worked on thousands of planets across the galaxy, investigating mass graves, natural disasters, terrorist attacks, and countless other horrific scenes. In addition she has worked as a lawyer at the Ministry of Justice and as a Pathologist for the Ministry of Health and the IRSB.
