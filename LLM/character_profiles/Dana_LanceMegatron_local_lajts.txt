ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

History:

Lived on the peaceful planet Melida/Daan with her older brother, Mother and Father. They had a farm on the outskirts of the city Garth. Dee was 13 when the pirates came like shadows in the dead of night. They slaughtered her family before her eyes and kept her alive to be sold into slavery. For ten years Dee lived the life of a slave, and was sold three times before she came on board the ship Tarris. There she met a boy about the same age, Gavin. They became fast friends and secretly plotted their grand escape to freedom. After years of carefully laying out the plan they executed it on Dee's 23rd birthday. It began by sabotaging the ships main drives. This caused Tarris to drop out of hyperspace and into enemy territory. The Kathol Rift Monks had attacked the now crippled pirate ship. Gavin was caught and tortured before being killed for having a hand in the destruction of the ships drives. Dee escaped, but with the help of a monk named Marek. Marek took Dee back to their compound and after a brotherhood vote, decided to train her in there ways. Years of training past, but Dee was determined to destroy the pirate faction. She became extremely skilled in wilding blades of all sizes. Also her skills to track people where amazing and Marek and the brotherhood allowed her to conduct her own missions. Near her 30th year, Marek sent a message to her asking her to return to ops base. She had been on the outer rim territories holding down the fort will Brother Mathes fought with the crude pirates near Rakata Prime. Dee returned and was introduced to a man named James. For the next few years Jim and Dee became partners. At first the friendship was on rocky terrain and Dee was skeptical of the man and his abilities. She was soon proven wrong and with time allowed Jim in. They became great friends and she entrusted him with her life. She never truly understood his background until the day he asked her to return with him to Coruscant. She was reluctant to go, but with the guidance of Marek she said yes and followed Jim to Coruscant. This is where this story ends and a new one begins.
