**Name:** Danroth D’Illige

**Birth name:** Richter Renholm

**Age:** 29

**Rank:** Corporal

**Home:** Berea

**Position(s):**

IRSB Public Safety Officer

**Previous Position(s):**

Law Enforcement - Various

**Martial Status:**

Widowed

**Known Relatives:**

Marisa Renholm-Elsan, wife, deceased

Rosalia Renholm, daughter, deceased

Orion Renholm, father, deceased

Milia Renholm-D’Illige, mother, deceased

**Personality:**

Cool, Graceful, Confident, Stylish. Although he has lived a pampered life, it also has left him without a moral. If it gets him where he wants to be he’ll get there. He has low regard for other people’s lives and if he needs to get rid of someone he will.

**Flaws:**

Due to his past he has many enemies and although he has changed his identity people are still looking for him. 

Since his early childhood Danroth is known for taking things that aren’t his. It doesn’t matter if this is something large or something small. People who don’t pay close attention to there stuff around Danroth will find it missing. 

Although Danroth is trying to change his old ways, this doesn’t always work out and remembering his past and his wife and daughter can bring out violent tempers within him. This usually comes out through throwing with objects or hurting people.

**Physical Characteristics:**

Aged 29 and standing tall at 5 feet 8 inches, Danroth has the stature of a leader. His blond hair and green eyes complete the mystery that covers him. His hair make him stand out in any crowd. On his right shoulder features a black symbol tattoo, which reminds him of his past. Another link with his family is his ring, which is gold with three small red rocks in a triangular pattern. This ring was given to him by his father on his 20th birthday. Danroth is usually dressed in dark green shirt, black pants and a green jacket. Danroth's light build make him look like a rookie, but this is only appearance. 

**Biography:**

Being the son of a director of a corporation has it’s advantages. During his childhood nothing was to good for Richter. He lived his life like a king. Due to this Richter was known as somewhat of a thief by taking everything even if it wasn’t his. This continued as he grew up and became worse and worse. Although still protected by his father and the Renholm Corporation, Richter had business outside the company mainly business that wasn’t legal. Extortion, Smuggling, where but some of the minor contributions. This grew larger and larger until Richter was running a fully outfitted crime organisation somewhat under the cover of the Corporation. The violence he sometimes used earned Richter the nickname “Hell’s Blonde”. His father didn’t knew all of it, but he knew of Richter using his business for something else. Running a crime organisation anywhere doesn’t only attract the law, but also other criminals. In exchange for usage of Richter’s resources and remaining in command of this operation. Richter added his small crime operation to The Syndicate, a large criminal operation scattered across the galaxy. Life was good for Richter this way and he finally married the girl of his dreams, Marisa and they had a daughter. As usual everything didn’t completely turn out as planned. His father found out about his affairs and around his back The Syndicate had him and Richter’s mother taken out. This didn’t go well with Richter and somewhat of a feud started. Richter pulled the short straw and saw his entire life go the pieces. Barely escaping the aftermath, Richter had seen his wife and daughter brutally murder, his friend and companions die, the Renholm Corporation crushed. There was nothing he had left but his life. At that time he swore to revenge on The Syndicate. To stay out of there hands he had changed his name taking on the name of his grandfather. Now Danroth he left behind the mess on Berea and moved towards the centre of the galaxy, hoping for better times.
