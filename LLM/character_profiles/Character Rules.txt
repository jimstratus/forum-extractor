Era of the Imperial Republic RPG | Character Rules
CAT = Character Administration Team
Senator Character CAT-004
All players with a primary character that is a citizen of the Imperial Republic or living in the Royal Territories (Regasterra), including the Hapes Cluster, must register a “Senator Character” using the registration form (it can be found inside IRIN or if you aren’t eligible for an IRIN account please contact an IRIN for an alternate link for registration). We highly encourage registration via IRIN. If you meet the criteria requiring you to have a Senator Character, you should also have an IRIN account. Please see this post about how to get one or message an admin on Chat (Discord). You do not have to create Senator Characters for secondary characters with one exception:
If your primary character is not an Imperial Republic citizen (or resident) but your secondary character is then you would make a senator character for the secondary character in place of the primary character.
A Senator Character is simply the name of a senator NPC from the planet (homeworld) that your main character is from and that you will NPC in the Senate forums when laws are being proposed, to discuss votes, or if/when your Senator is assigned a Committee then you will NPC them making posts (with your regular player character login) in that Senate forum. That is it. All you really need is a name, your main character;s homeworld planet (see your primary character’s profile) and if they were elected or appointed to the Galactic Senate seat on their planet (some planets may elect or appoint or both, but senators are only one or the other). We have found a Star Wars Name Generator website that may be helpful for you in the process of naming your Senator Character. You will find the link here.
