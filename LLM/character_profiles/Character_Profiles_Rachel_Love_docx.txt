History

Rachel was born an only child to a mother and father on the planet of Commenor. She had the normal life of attending school, partying, talking to boys, not really studying just having fun. She always wished she would have had a brother or sister growing up, as she always kind of felt lonely, as she could only invite friends over or hang out on the weekends. Her parents were strict believers in the philosophy that school nights were for nothing else but homework. That was a philosophy that she came to hate real quick, if you could imagine. Her favorite holodramas as a child were the ones about lawyers and court cases, so it came as no surprise to her parents when she declared to them her intention of becoming a lawyer. 



Once she was ready to attend university, she had made her decision to attend Coruscant University. At Coruscant University, she took her courses very seriously and excelled in them. She caught the attention of Dr. Dan Vespucci, the Dean of the Public Policy School at Coruscant University, who recommended her to his friend, in the Senate as an intern. After that summer internship, she returned to Coruscant University for her last year, there and graduated with honors, making her the first in her family to graduate from college.

 

With letters of recommendation from Dr. Vespucci and the Senator, Rachel was accepted into the prestigious law school at the University of Chandrilla, where she soon graduated in the top half of her class. After clerking for the High Inquisition and working at two different law firms, Rachel has worked in a number of different positions within Government, and has been advised through the most recent parts of her career under the guidance of both the Praetor Bryan Dugaan and Prince Marc Stratus, both respected lawyers in their own right. Last year, her name was submitted for consideration to the High Prince, to become the next General Counsel, His Majesty's Personal Service. The High Prince approved the recommendation, and Rachel Love joined the prestigious HMPS.



In the past year, while serving in His Majesty's Personal Service, Rachel has had the opportunity to work on the Charter Revision with the High Prince, Executor, High Minister, and other senior officials.
