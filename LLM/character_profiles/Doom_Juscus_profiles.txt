**Flaws:**

On the outside Doom looks like the perfect example of what a human is. But what flaws him is not on the outside but what lies on the inside. There are really only two things he hates about his shot life, one small thing is that he used to be overweight. Overweight is not a good word, maybe fat, or super fat. He was picked on when he was a little boy. Doom likes to think that this is actually a good thing because it is what made him into what he is today. He started working out everyday to make it so people did not say mean things and bully him. In his teenage years he was of good appearance, but strength training persisted as an everyday task. Doom did not stop when noone picked on him but continued because of how much he liked it. Strength was like a delicous drink or food to him, he always wanted more. His physical characterstics were pristine when he graduated from school, and after a friend joked with him about joining the military he looked towards the imperial army. His other flaw which most do not know and he does not talk about is the death of his parents. Right before he thought about joining the army his father and mother were assasinated from a bomb placed on their speeder. This event hardened Doom's heart into what it is today. He is neither immoral or moral, he is just there. He takes what he has learned and put it into practice and bases his values and skills on what he has learned through the military. Due to these two flaws, he does not like to deal with "unhealthy" military personel and does not acutally like to befriend civilians who are overweight. Doom is also prone on attacking an individual's parents when the parents use their connections to help them.

**Physical Characteristics:**

Hair Color - Brown

Eye Color - Blue

Height - 6'2''

Weight - 195lbs

Due to the military and extensive strength training throughout his everyday life, Doom is a pristine example of what a human should strive to be physically. He was sustained a leg wound during a battle he faced in the army. Doctors feared he woudl be unable to walk but all that has come from it is a nasty scar that runs half way down the right side of his right thigh.

**Biography:**

At the start of his military career he showed great skills and leadership. After grade school he joined the military academy in Coruscant. His instructors and trainers always told him how far he would go in the army. Upon completion and at the podium of his graduation, he shocked many of his friends and officers when he did not accept his commision. He was then started as a Recruit instead of a 2nd LT, a harsh choice to some but understandable reasons to others. Since then he has accrued six years experience in the imperial army. He has advanced quickly to the rank of Corporal in four years, a feat that normally takes six. Here is a brief history of his experience through his 6 years in the army.

Year 1 - Graduated Combat Training. Promoted to E-2. Assigned to a scout squad.

Year 2 - Promoted to E-3. Permanent duty change to a heavy weapon squad. E-Web gunner.

Year 3 - Wounded during battle. Permanet duty change to a sharpshooter squad.

Year 4 - Promoted to Corporal. Permant duty change to a line squad. Promoted to squad secondary leader. Squad consisted of a SGT, himself, and 8 E-2/E-3s.

Year 5 - Promoted to Sergeant. Promoted to squad leader.

Year 6 - Promoted to Staff Sergeant. Promoted to platoon sergeant.

After jumping around due to "gaining as much experience as possible" according to his commanders, Sergeant Juscus has found a good platoon that he has grown to almost be normal with. His Sergeant back when he was a corporal put it differently, " They probaly don't know what do do with you. I mean you just soak up everything they tell you and use it when [stuff that comes out when you poo] hits the fan." He has stayed in the same Light Infantry Platoon for almost three years. He is not used to getting to know the people around him because he likes to learn what he can and move forward. However, due to unknown chance he has stayed with the same unit for quite some time. Some officers see the fast progress he has made as a nonroutine happening that should not take place. Citation of inexperience were thrown at him behind his back by these officers, and yet those who have served with him no a different story. Only by his actions has he been able to go so far.
