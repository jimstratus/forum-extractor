History
Born on the Hapan Planet of Maires, Tavria's early childhood was spent with her cousin Revan, but lost her cousin after he moved away to live with his Great Uncle. Tavria grew up on her family estate on Maires, during the reign of House Treyson (the House that claims the right , which continues to this day). She spent her childhood with her family and with her people on Maires and saw the horrible abuses men had to live with in Hapan Society. Her mother, Elizabeth, sent her off to school on Hapes to become a well educated young lady, that Elizabeth hoped would one day come to do great things in the galaxy. After her time on Hapes, she traveled heavily throughout the Cluster seeing the latest discoveries and innovations and making connections with people on all of the worlds in the Cluster, and became a Certified Galactic Accountant. Her mother allowed her to take control of Maires' communications infrastructure, to which she modernized it and reworked it so that Maires would only have one news station/press bureau, which she ran. Tavria also helped Maires update in other areas to become more advanced in technology like Hapes and Charubah. 
When Tavria was twenty, Revan returned to Maires to visit what was left of his family. She spent a lot of time with Revan and tried to get to know her changed cousin better, as he nursed his ailing mother. She grew really close to her cousin in that year, and then saw him disappear again after his mother died. Her mother then ruled over Maires. Tavria spent time getting close to her father and mother. She left after awhile to live with her Grandmother and Aunt of Minor House Astopone on Hapes. Her mother and father died in an assassination plot by the Queen Mother. She appointed a trusted friend to be the Maires representative to the Royal Court, in her place, and left the daily affairs of Maires to the Royal Governor of Maires, who updated her daily of any new events on the planet. She left the Hapes Cluster and searched out the exiled Hapan Prince, since she no longer wanted to serve the Queen. 
After joining the IR she spent time on the HMPS, High Council, served as the Second Executor and as the Supreme Chancellor before retiring to Naboo, yet retaining her High Councilor position. During her first stint in the IR she went on the Togorian expedition among others. She decided to retire after a kidnapping and Layha and Revan had returned from their galactic escapades, so she knew the IR would be in good hands while she took a break. With her experience in governmental affairs and the realm of public affairs and foreign affairs, she became a very attractive candidate to have on any board of director for a company or for an organization, and she was happy to do it. After a few years in retirement on Naboo in which she pursued other educational, business, and advisory ventures, in addition to her High Council duties, she returned full time to the IR to serves as the Grand Minister of COMPNOR.
