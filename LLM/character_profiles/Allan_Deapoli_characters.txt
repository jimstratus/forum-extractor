History

Allan Deapoli was born on Naboo 20 years before the Imperial Republic's inauguration, Allan Depaoli joined the Imperial Republic Navy and served in it for 10 years, ending his military career as Captain of a Cruiser protecting the Chommell sector. He was raised in on a planet that supported peace and his family was very peaceful, though they believed in protecting themselves. Unlike most military personnel, Allan became interested in politics and law, and decided to obtain a law degree at the best known academy of law, on Coruscant. After finishing his degree he returned to Naboo and became a lawyer, though there was little use for them on Naboo, and ended up serving more clients in the sector than the planet itself. Although not Force sensitive, he had a great talent for discerning truth. It was part of the culture of his people, as truth meant everything to them. He later became elected a judge of the people, and his talents were discovered by one Revan Centurion, then an Executor of the Imperial Republic. He was appointed by then Executor Centurion as the IR Attorney for the Coruscant Sector, and a year after that as a member of the High Inquisition upon the death of a previous Inquisitor. He was one of the youngest and least experienced members of the High Inquisition, but later became one of the most respected members of the High Inquisition.
