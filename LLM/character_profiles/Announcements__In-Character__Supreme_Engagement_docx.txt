37 IRY - Transgalactic Partnership

 

For Immediate Release to the Kuati People



“My fellow Kuatis,



It is with great happiness and joy that I announce my engagement to the lovely Dana Lance. Miss Lance was born in the Hapes Cluster, though much of her life was spent traveling in the Outer Rim, Unknown Regions, and outside of Imperial Republic territory. Most recently, she has been faithfully serving the Imperial Republic as a government employee in a classified position under the Ministry of State. Before entering into this union of marriage and becoming my Queen, she will have to resign from government service in order to take her place on the Thrones of Kuat, the Royal Territories of Regasterra, and the Imperial Republic. The wedding date will be announced by the Office of the Supreme Ruler shortly. The wedding ceremony will be performed by His Excellency the Supreme Chancellor as Minister of State. Any inquiries or wishes of congratulations should be sent to Mr. Maurice Jandreau, Communications Director of His Majesty’s Personal Staff.”



High Prince James Taylor Stratus

Sovereign Prince of the Kingdom of Kuat

Supreme Ruler of the Imperial Republic

37 IRY - Transgalactic Partnership

 

For Immediate Release



“His Majesty the Supreme Ruler of the Imperial Republic, High Prince James Stratus of Kuat, has proposed marriage to Miss Dana Lance of the Imperial Republic, who has accepted the proposal. Miss Lance, who was born in the Hapes Cluster, is a government employee working in a classified position for the Ministry of State. The engaged couple has been dating for the last 11 years. A wedding date has yet to be set, but is expected to be announced in the coming weeks. It has been decided that His Excellency the Supreme Chancellor as Minister of State will perform the marriage ceremony and oversee a special coronation ceremony for the Emperor and Empress of the Royal Territories and the Imperial Republic. The coronation ceremony on Kuat will immediately follow and will be presided over by the senior members of the Royal First Family.”



Maurice Jandreau

Communications Director

His Majesty’s Personal Staff
