Imperial Republic Government

Kingdom of Kuat of Regasterra

His Royal Highness the Prince Regent of Kuat

 

For Immediate Release to the Kuati People



“My fellow Kuatis,



It has been an honor and a privilege to serve you as your Regent these past few decades on behalf of my brother the King of Kuat. I have recently re-married and joined in union to the Duchess of Maires and Executor of the Imperial Republic, Tavria Treyson. As her duties are far more demanding than my own, as well as being headquartered on the Imperial Republic Capitol of Coruscant, I am relinquishing my authority as Regent to the next ranking member of the Royal First Family, with the consent of my older brother the High Prince, to my dear sister Princess Nicole Stratus-McFayden. I will be moving to Coruscant with my wife Tavria and taking a short sabbatical from public service to celebrate our honeymoon after which I will serve in whatever capacity I am appointed and will continue my duties as a member of the Royal First Family.



I’d like to thank my advisors, business associates, friends and you loyal citizens of Kuat who have stood by me during my reign and expect you to do the same for the new Regent, the Princess Nicole. And so for now I must bid you farewell, and May the Force be with us all.”



Prince Marcus Kenneth Stratus

Prince Regent of the Kingdom of Kuat

Royal First Family of the Imperial Republic

For Immediate Release to the Kuati People



With my brother stepping aside from the Kuati Throne to be able to live with his wife and spend  time with her, it now befalls upon me to announce his replacement as your sovereign. I am pleased to announce that I have appointed the Princess Nicole to rule over Kuat as Regent on my behalf. I grant unto her the authority of the Kuati Throne, and governance of the Kuat Sector.



High Prince James T. Stratus

Sovereign Ruler of the Kingdom of Kuat

EXECUTIVE OFFICES



For Immediate Release to the General Public



Effective 25 Üffpor 35 IRY



I, Nicole Stratus-McFayden do tender my resignation as CEO of Stratus Industries so that I may answer the call to serve as Regent of the Kingdom of Kuat on behalf of the Sovereign High Prince James Stratus. The Board of Directors of Stratus Industries will announce my replacement within the next 30 standard days. Until then, Chief Operations Officer and Vice President Skye Bishto will be Acting CEO in my stead until the Board of Directors names my replacement. I will continue to serve on the Board of Directors and stay involved in the overall supervision of the officers of Stratus Industries with the other board members.



Princess Nicole Stratus-McFayden

Chief Executive Officer, Stratus Industries

Board Member, Stratus Industries Board of Directors
