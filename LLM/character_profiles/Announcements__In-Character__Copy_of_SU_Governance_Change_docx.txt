Imperial Republic Government

Commission for the Preservation of His Majesty’s New Order

Office of the Grand Minister

Her Excellency the Duchess of Maires

 

For Immediate Release to the General Public

 

I am pleased to announce that this morning by Executive Decree, the charter of Stratus University has been amended to reflect a change in governance. Rather than being governed by the Ministry of Education it will now be governed by an Independent President and Board of Trustees to serve as the “body politic and corporate” of the university. The Board of Trustees will include the Executive Leadership of COMPNOR, the President of the University, and 2 members of the royal family. 



Dr. Tony Henson, the current Chancellor, is named as the first independent President of Stratus University. Dr. Henson along with Executor Tavria Treyson (Chairman), Praetor Bryan Dugaan (Vice Chairman), Senior Minister Blake Lexington Tagge IX (Secretary), Senior Minister Grant Kline (Treasurer), Senior Minister Raphael Murieaux, Senior Minister Angela Ingraham, Princess Nicole Stratus, and Prince Marc Stratus will serve as the Board of Trustees.



The University Leadership team will consist of Dr. Tony Henson (President), Dr. Rebecca Mackenzie (Executive Vice President & Chancellor), Dr. Casey Latimer (Provost & Senior Vice Chancellor of Academics & Admissions), Dr Jodie Stinson (Vice Chancellor of University Services

& Coruscant Branch), Dr Ivory Capons (Vice Chancellor of Kuat Branch), Dr Jennifer Luthy (Vice Chancellor of Corellia Branch), and Dr Krister Briscoe (Vice Chancellor of Obroa-skai Branch).



COMPNOR will also be recommending to the Imperial Republic Senate, that five additional Super-Regional Universities be created under the Government Universities Program to serve the populations of their respective regions in alignment with the COMPNOR regional structure. The proposed Universities would primarily support the following super-regions: A - Coruscant/Capitol, Kuat, Regasterra; B - Outer Rim North/South, Deep Core, Core Worlds, Unknown; C - Bright Jewel, Braxant, Allied Tion, Tingel Arm; D - Frontier, Expansion, Outlaw, Wild Space; and E - Corellia, Colonies, Hapes, Mid Rim. These Universities would each have their own separate Board of Trustees.
