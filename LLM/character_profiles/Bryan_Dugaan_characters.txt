**History** :

Bryan was born into a wealthy and prominent minor aristocratic family on Corellia, that of his mother, and only child. He was considered one of those elite effete snobs, by some Corellians. His father was from the ancient royal family of Anaxes, whose throne they were deposed from centuries upon centuries ago. He was very aware of his surroundings as a child and constantly observed people in his surroundings. During his time working at the Corellian Memorial Hospital & Research Center he was also working for Corellian Security and IRSB as a snitch, reporting information he gathered from patients, that had participated in illegal activities. After working at the Hospital & Research Center for a few years, he found out that his true calling was in law enforcement, and he applied to Corellian Security and got his first Law Enforcement job, and just took off after that.
