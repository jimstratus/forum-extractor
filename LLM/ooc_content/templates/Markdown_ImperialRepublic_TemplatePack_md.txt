
# 📦 Imperial Republic Project Template Pack

This pack includes both **in-universe** and **out-of-character (OOC)** document templates.

---

## 🧾 In-Universe Templates

### 1. 📄 Document Summary Sheet

```markdown
# 📄 Imperial Republic Document Summary Sheet

**Document Title:**  
**Document ID:**  
**Issuing Authority:**  
**Date (IRY):**  
**Classification Level:** [Public | Confidential | Secret | Supreme Secret]  
**Document Type:** [Law | Directive | Treaty | Mandate | Order]  
**Status:** [Draft | Active | Superseded | Archived]

## 🔍 Summary
(Brief 2-5 sentence summary of the document's purpose)

## 🗂️ Key Provisions
- Provision 1
- Provision 2
- Provision 3

## 🖋️ Signatures
- [Title] [Name], [Position]
```
