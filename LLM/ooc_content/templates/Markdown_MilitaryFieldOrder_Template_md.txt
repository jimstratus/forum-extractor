# 🪖 Military Field Order

**Order ID:** FO-MOD-IRY36-059  
**Commanding Officer:** Grand Admiral Rhivarn Dekel  
**Date:** IRY 36.5  
**Sector:** Bormea  

## 🎯 Objective
Secure orbital space over Verity Prime and establish control nodes in system’s major communication relays.

## ⚔️ Orders
1. Deploy Task Force 12 under Admiral Kel Morven.
2. Authorize full blockade protocols.
3. Use non-lethal measures unless directly fired upon.

## 🛡️ Rules of Engagement
Engagements must be approved by fleet command unless in response to direct provocation. All actions to be logged with COMPNET.

## 📌 Remarks
Operation sanctioned by the Supreme Ruler. High Council oversight deferred.
