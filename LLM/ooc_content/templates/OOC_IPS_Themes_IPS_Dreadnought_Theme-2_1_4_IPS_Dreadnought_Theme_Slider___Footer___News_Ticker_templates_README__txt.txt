###############################################################################################################


	What are these? 
		A custom "Pages" templates to show record feed in this theme Slider, Footer, News Ticker.
	Pages?
		https://invisionpower.com/features/apps#pages


		*************	
		Installation: 	
			All you have to do is installing these templates and then creating a block with the newly installed templates :) 

		How to install the templates?
			Go to ACP > Pages > Templates > Upload Templates > ---> Upload the templates that you want for example the slider and footer template.

		*************
		Done. :)
			Now How does it work?

			It is very easy, You just need to create a block with a specific key provided in this theme settings
			for example Edit this theme and go to Swiper Slider Tab, scroll down to end you can see a setting named "Article slider" that provided with a guide how to to enable :) 

			See? Very easy, Dont message me how to install.
			Just kidding, If you have any questions or help installing it, feel free to message anytime.
			
			But really, its very easy :)
