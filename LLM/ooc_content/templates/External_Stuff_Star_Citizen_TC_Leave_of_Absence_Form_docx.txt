Welcome Service Members; you are reminded that if you choose to leave without notifying your next chain of command you will be placed in an AWOL status. If you are unfamiliar of the term, refer to this definition. (AWOL adjective or adverb: absent from the armed forces without permission; broadly : absent or missing). So it is important and your responsibility that you submit a 'Leave of Absence' so the staff understand of your departure and return. This is a mandatory request for all members who hold a staff position so a suitable replacement during your leave can be found. For all others of the general populace it is encouraged but we understand if you can't do so.
