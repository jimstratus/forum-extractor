IRIN Document (CODEX) Defense Order D128917 - WORKING DRAFT



The Military Criminal Investigative Service is herewith granted full operational and investigative authority to carry out their mission and purpose within and throughout the Imperial Republic Armed Forces and the Ministry of Defense, its personnel and assets. Should a subject of investigation within their jurisdiction within the Ministry of Defense personnel, its assets, or properties, or as otherwise defined by their Standard Operating Procedures, or Imperial Republic Law,  they are therefore authorized to act upon, investigate, including interfere with military operations with cause, including relieving personnel of duty, up to, and including, the arrest of any persons with cause or civil or military warrant, in the pursuit of their ongoing investigations or conclusions theirof. All of these actions taken by MCIS shall be done under the authority of the Minister of Defense.



Therefore it is so ordered and so my consent is hereby given for the Military Criminal Investigative Service to operate under my authority

-Lord Admiral OVERMIND

Minister of Defense



Whilst this document is a working document, or draft, it is so ordered that Defense Order D128917 shall be in full force and effect until further notice, such as a countermand order be issued.
Countersigned - High Prince James Stratus
                       Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces
