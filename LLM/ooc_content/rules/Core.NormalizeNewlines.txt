Core.NormalizeNewlines
TYPE: bool
VERSION: 4.2.0
DEFAULT: true
--DESCRIPTION--
<p>
    Whether or not to normalize newlines to the operating
    system default.  When <code>false</code>, HTML Purifier
    will attempt to preserve mixed newline files.
