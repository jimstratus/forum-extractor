Omega Protocol

Executive Order E819502



In case of wide spread treason among the Armed Forces, the Security Bureau or the Royal Guard, this protocol shall be enacted activating the secret heavy battle droid Centurions that are hidden inside the Palace within the walls, in the ships of the Command fleets, and a full armament and army inside Sanctuary. 



Can be activated only by the Praetorian, the Throne, the Director of IRIS and Assistant Director of IRIS-DCI. 



Centurions everywhere will emerge from their hidden places and secure the facilities, ships, palaces, buildings, etc., of the areas they are assigned and stationed, and terminate all individuals branded and detected as traitors and continue this pursuit until countermanded by two Praetorians or one member of the Throne.
