A hypothetical galactic government that is a constitutional autocracy would have the following wartime protocols:

The government would have a strong central authority, led by an autocratic leader who holds immense power and is not limited by a constitution or other checks and balances.

The leader would have the power to declare war and make decisions related to military strategy and deployment of forces.

The government would have a large standing military that is well-equipped and trained to defend the galaxy against external threats.

The government would have a system of conscription in place, allowing them to quickly mobilize a large number of citizens in the event of war.

The government would also have a system of emergency powers in place, allowing them to take control of resources, communication, and transportation in order to effectively respond to the war.

The government would also have a system of censorship and propaganda to control the narrative of the war and maintain public support for the government's actions.

The government would also have a system of espionage to gather intelligence on enemies and potential threats to the galaxy.

The government would also have a system of alliances, treaties, and diplomatic relations with other galactic powers to protect its interests and maintain peace in the galaxy.

The government would also have a system of war tribunals to prosecute war criminals, spies and traitors.

The government would also have a system of post war reconstruction to rebuild and restore the galaxy after the war.

It's worth noting that this kind of government would be highly centralized and would have a strong control over the citizens and their rights, this could potentially lead to some negative consequences like oppression, human rights violation, etc.
