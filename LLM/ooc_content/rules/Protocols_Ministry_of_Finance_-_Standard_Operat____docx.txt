Ministry of Finance - Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Manual

 

Purpose:

 

The Purpose of this SOP Manual is to provide procedural guidance and direction to various units and staff who use the services of the Ministry of Finance. It contains IR-wide technical operating procedures for processing and recording financial accounting transactions. It supplements other IR and COMPNOR Directives to ensure a smooth financial management program.

Section 1: General Responsibilities

 

A. Financial Management Policy - The Ministry of Finance is responsible for developing, promulgating, and implementing the Imperial Republic's Financial Management Policy for Use by the Imperial Republic and its Affiliates.



B. Budgeting - All units and staff divisions will be responsible for preparing and supporting their budgetary requests. All submissions for future funding requirements or quarterly apportionments should be handled in accordance with issued policy and local directives.



C. Procurement - All purchases must be made in accordance with local instructions and acquisition regulations. The Ministry of Finance shall maintain copies of Warrants for Designation of Procurement Officers, any changes or corrections must be submitted to the Ministry of Finance as they occur.



D. Accounting & Disbursing - The Ministry of Finance is solely responsible for the payment of all commercial and government bills for the Imperial Republic Government; and for maintaining all accounting records and submissions of report for all units in the Imperial Republic.



 

 

Section 2: Authority



The Ministry of Finance has the authority and/or regulatory authority over the Economy and all Financial Industries of the Imperial Republic, and all Financial aspects of the Corporate Sector. They are all responsible for the Financial Management policies of the Imperial Republic as a whole.



Section 3: Officers of Finance



The following are considered to be duly authorized officers of the Imperial Republic, Class B in regards to Financial Matters in their respective spheres of influence,



A. Minister - The Chief Executive/Financial Officer of the Ministry of Finance 

B. Principal Deputy Minister & Treasurer - The Chief Operating Officer and Treasurer of the Ministry of Finance

C. Deputy Minister for Imperial Republic Accounting & Controller - Responsible for the accounting of the entire IR government

D. Deputy Minister for Central Banking/Economic Affairs & Asst. COMPNOR Treasurer - Responsible for coining the Currency of the Imperial Republic and managing the economy

E. Deputy Minister for Monetary Policy & Asst. State/Defense Treasurer - Responsible for handling all Financial Policy matters both internally and externally of the IR

F. Deputy Minister for Investigations & Enforcement - Responsible for the Collection of Taxes and Investigation & Enforcement of all Financial Policy and Regulations.

G. Deputy Minister for Economic and Financial Management & Assistant IR Treasurer/Controller - Responsible for the IR's revenue collection & cost-saving strategies



Section 4: Financial Agreements

 

 

For any Financial Agreement/Transaction (of any kind) to be valid, the following requirements must be met:



1. One Class A officer must sign/approve the agreement;

2. One Class B officer must sign/approve the agreement;

3. One Class C officer must sign/approve the agreement;

4. The Divisional Head of the Requesting Agency must sign/approve the agreement;

5. The Divisional Head of the Requesting Agency can not be the class B or C officer that signed/approved of the agreement.

6. One Class D Officer can act as a Class A Officer if no Class A Officers are available, as certified by a Class C Officer.

7. The Class D Officer can not be the the Divisional Head of the Requesting Agency.

Section 6: Routine Correspondence



Communication from staff or a Commanding officer of a unit to the Ministry of Finance, depends on the nature of the Communications



A. For routine accounting items, direct communication with the Ministry of Finance is authorized and permitted.



B. For those of a non routine nature or that impact the unit's operational capabilities, communication will be via the Chain of Command.
