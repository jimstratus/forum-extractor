Command Staff Billet Descriptions 
The purpose of this page is to advise potential applicants the responsibilities, duties, account-abilities and knowledge/education level a member must obtain in 
order to be efficient in the office he or she is applying for. Like with any career, this is no different and a member must be able to fulfill the duties inscribed here on 
in to be a functional component to the Terran Confederation. Careful consideration and interviewing is conducted to find the best possible candidate and each 
application is taken seriously. A formal reply will be issued when you apply to a position stating its receipt and those whom do not fit the criteria will be denied 
either by formal letter or through post-process interviewing. The accepted applicant will under-go a formal command review and final appointment letters upon 
acceptance of office and posted next business day. 
Space Marshal  - O11 
Duties & Responsibilities 
1: Operate and Maintain the Terran Confederation community in its entirety. 
2: Communicate with Flag Officers and or Staff in providing services to the members of the community. 
3: Plan & Assist in battle strategy with other Flag Officers / Staff for in game theater level operations. 4: Maintain staffing levels on all levels of the community. (Appointments & Resignations) 
5: Oversight on all aspects and levels of content within or out of the community. 
6: Maintain & Enforce all rules, regulations, terms of use and so forth within and out of the community. 7: Conduct & Impose judgement on members who create infractions through a 'Officer Review Board', a session with other Flag Officers. 
Account-abilities of Office 
1: Ensure Flag Officers & Staff operate at acceptable levels of efficiency as well as being an efficient manager. 
2: Conduct and Attend Joint Command Meetings at least once per month. A representative can be placed if unavailable. 3: Abide by the (Staff Officer Command Position Appointment Agreement) and ensure staff abides by them as well. 
4: Award member(s) the 'Medal of Honor' by direction of the President, if eligible.

Chief Executive Officer - Confederation Express Corporation 
The Chief Executive Officer ("CEO”) is responsible for leading the development and execution of the Company’ s long term strategy with a view to creating shareholder value. The CEO’ s 
leadership role also entails being ultimately responsible for all day-to-day management decisions and for implementing the Company’s long and short term plans. The CEO acts as a direct liaison 
between the Board and management of the Company and communicates to the Board on behalf of management. The CEO also communicates on behalf of the Company to shareholders, 
employees, Government authorities, other stakeholders and the public. 
Duties & Responsibilities 
1: Assist in the operation and maintenance of the Confederation Express in its entirety. 
2: Communicate with Flag Officers and or Staff in providing services to the members of the community. 
3: Lead, in conjunction with the Board, the development of the Company's strategy. 
4: Ensure the Company is appropriately organized and staffed and to have the authority to hire and terminate staff as necessary to enable it to achieve the approved strategy. (Appointments & Resignations) 
5: Oversight on Confederation Express aspects and levels of content within or out of the community. 
6: Maintain & Enforce all rules, regulations, terms of use and so forth within and out of the community. 
7: Advise CO/XO of infractions by members if necessary, correct matter if possible without Flag Staff involvement. 
8: Lead and oversee the implementation of the Company's long and short term plans in accordance with its strategy. 
9: Ensure that expenditures of the Company are within the authorized annual budge of the Company. 
10: Assess the principal risks of the Company and to ensure that these risks are being monitored and managed. 
11: To Ensure effective internal controls and management information systems are in place. 
12: To ensure that the Company has appropriate systems to enable it to conduct its activities both lawfully and ethically. 
13: Act as a liaison between management and the Board as well as Government Officials. 
14: To communicate effectively with shareholders, employees, Government authorities, other stakeholders and the public. 
Account-abilities of Office

2: Request that special meetings of the Board be called at least once per month. 
3: Abide by the (Staff Officer Command Position Appointment Agreement) and ensure staff abides by them as well. 
4: Refer for awards for eligible members in division to CO/XO. 
5: To keep abreast of all material undertakings and activities of the Company and all material external factors affecting the Company and to ensure that processes and systems are in place to ensure that the CEO 
and management of the Company are adequately informed.
