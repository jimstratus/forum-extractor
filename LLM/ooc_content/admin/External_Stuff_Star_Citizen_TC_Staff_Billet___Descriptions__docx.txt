Command Staff Billet Descriptions


The purpose of this page is to advise potential applicants the responsibilities, duties, account-abilities and knowledge/education level a member must obtain in order to be efficient in the office he or she is applying for. Like with any career, this is no different and a member must be able to fulfill the duties inscribed here on in to be a functional component to the Terran Confederation. Careful consideration and interviewing is conducted to find the best possible candidate and each application is taken seriously. A formal reply will be issued when you apply to a position stating its receipt and those whom do not fit the criteria will be denied either by formal letter or through post-process interviewing. The accepted applicant will under-go a formal command review and final appointment letters upon acceptance of office and posted next business day.
