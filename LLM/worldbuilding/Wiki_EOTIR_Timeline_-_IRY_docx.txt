The Imperial Republic is Formed [0 IRY/0 UFY]

It was a time of darkness, a time of chaos, a time of of uncertainty. There were those who fought for order, there were those who fought for peace, and there were those who fought for both. The Galactic Alliance had fallen, and war plagued every system, as despots, tyrants, opportunists, nobles, reformers and revolutionists alike all raced against each other in the quest for power. Eventually factions emerged, many collapsing almost immediately, others merging together to form larger factions, and eventually the United Factions for Order and Peace was formed, a coalition of factions establishing peace treaties and trade agreements between each other, regulated by a Supreme Council of Faction Leaders and Representatives. The time came for reform, a better government, and the members of the Supreme Council recognized that, though agreement and conformity were yet to be found. After almost a year of much debate, several key faction leaders and other key council members including Josefus Quick, Ieyena Cohean, Layha Solo, Daniella Organa, Myera Jacobson, Sean Kennedy, and Igor Murashkin, formed together and proposed the creation of a New Order, with James Stratus the leader behind the proposal. At first many were against it, until eventually everyone agreed that the New Order would be the best course of action to pursue. The council voted, and Lord Prince James Stratus of the Imperial Remnant and Kuati Region was appointed the Supreme Ruler of the Imperial Republic, and the United Factions for Order and Peace were dissolved and merged into His Majesty's New Order, the Galactic Government of the Imperial Republic.

First year of the IR [1 IRY]

Coruscant was made the capitol after the Corellian Sector's governor, Joe Quick, convinced them that it was their rightful place to be the capitol of yet another glorious government. James Stratus was elected leader of the Galaxy, Supreme Ruler of the IR, Layha Solo was appointed Grand Minister of COMPNOR, which oversees all the civilian aspects of the galactic government, Ieyena Cohean was commissioned as a Fleet Admiral and the first “Admiral of the Fleet”. Joe Quick was appointed Supreme Chancellor, and put in charge of the overall system of Sector governments, the legislative bodies, and the Ministry of State. Ieyena Cohean is informed that her fiance, Greg, is presumed dead.



Year Two [ 2 IRY]

With the assistance of Ieyena Cohean, the newly appointed Minister of Defense, the military was expanded, which convinced several systems to join the government because of the security it could offer. Layha Solo was named the first Executor of the Imperial Republic, the right hand of the Throne and an actual PART of the Throne. With Layha Solo and James Stratus at the helm the rise of the Imperial Republic, the Security Bureau began.

Year Three [3 IRY]

Due to diplomatic missions, the planets and systems of Bilbringi, Yavin, Dathomir, Naboo, Tatooine, and several others were added to the Imperial Republic. Eric Jackson was named 2nd Executor of the Imperial Republic.



Year Four [4 IRY]

Daniella Organa was appointed to the position of Deputy Minister of Defense and secretly given the command of the Imperial Republic's Special Operations force. In the same year, the Imperial Republic Security Bureau was completed and Grand Minster Layha Solo appointed two generals to lead the IRSB, General Damon Delyanie and Loke. Togorian War broke out.

Year Five [5 IRY]

The IR engaged in battle.(Battle of Bilbringi) During this battle, Captain Lonewolf misguidedly informed his troops to fire upon two friendly transports, killing several citizens of the IR. He was court marshaled, and stripped of his position and was tried for treason, and was later executed. In this same year, Vice Admiral Cencori retired after the battle due to personal reasons. Daniella Organa and Admiral Eric Jackson were married.



Year Six [6 IRY]

The Commission for the Preservation of History and Life was created and Myera Jacobson was placed in charge as High Minister. Earth was discovered. Executor Layha Solo and Kyle Katarn are married.



Year Seven [7 IRY]

Year Eight [8 IRY]

Year Thirty-Seven [37 IRY]

Wedding of High Prince James T. Stratus and Dana Lance. James T. Stratus. 

James T. Stratus and Dana Stratus are coronated and crowned King & Queen of Kuat. The Supreme Ruler of the Imperial Republic, now married, becomes the High King of the Regasterra, dispenses the title of High Prince and assumes the title of Emperor of the Regasterra and the Imperial Republic (the position remains Supreme Ruler, the title changes from High Prince to Emperor due to marriage). His wife/spouse by virtue of marriage is coronated and crowned and granted the title of Empress and appointed to the Royal Imperial Throne, becomes a member of the Royal First Family, the Royal Supreme Court, and an Agent of the Supreme Ruler, (unlike the other members of the Royal Family who are Agents of the Throne, the Empress is an Agent of the Supreme Ruler, and other than the Executor and the Supreme Chancellor, is the only one to hold that position or title and authority, though does not have the same authority of the Executor or the Chancellor independently, but only acts as an Agent or representative of the Supreme Ruler, as duly authorized, whereas the Executor and Supreme Chancellor are entitled (and expected) to act and exercise their Throne authority independently by virtue of their position. The law states that a member of the Throne must remain on Coruscant at all times. The Empress, while a member of the Throne, is not factored into this law, as it refers to the members of the Throne with independent authority. The Supreme Ruler may with permission from the RSC grant the Empress authority to represent the Throne on Coruscant and allow the other members to leave the planet for a specified period of time only.
