The Hapan Throne
The House Hoffner or Hoffner Dynasty ruled the Hapes Consortium for many years. Queen Mother Hoffner, sister of the Queen of Kuat, Adaline Hoffner-Stratus, (mother to James, Marc, Nicole, and Ashlee, wife to King Charles.)
--------------------------------
(will fix stuff below copied from chat to make into above official document timeline explanation/wiki-article )
While Layha Solo's family has claim to the Throne of Hapes, the House Stratus also has claim, even more so, as the House Hoffner (Jim's mother) held the Throne of Hapes when Jim was first born, and his mother's sister was Queen Mother of Hapes when he was born, (his mother of course was the Queen of Kuat, she left Hapes to marry the King of Kuat, Jim's father). 
Which is why it was important when Layha left the "known galaxy" to appoint and crown Phoenix Solo as Queen of Hapes, it bridged the gap between both royal bloodlines. she is the daughter of James Stratus and Layha Solo.
